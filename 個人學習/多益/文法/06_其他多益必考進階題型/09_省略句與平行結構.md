# 09_省略句與平行結構

## 核心概念

多益文法題中，「平行結構」與「省略句」是 Part 5、Part 6 常見的進階考點，核心邏輯都圍繞著「對等連接詞（and / or / but / nor）前後的成分必須一致」。

### 一、什麼是平行結構

當 and、or、but 連接兩個或多個對等成分時，這些成分在**詞性、時態、語態**上必須完全對等。

- 詞性平行：The manager is **honest and reliable**.（形容詞 + 形容詞）
- 動詞平行：The clerk **filed the documents and updated the database**.（過去式 + 過去式）
- 動名詞平行：The team enjoys **brainstorming and presenting** new ideas.（V-ing + V-ing）
- 不定詞平行：The firm plans **to expand its market and to hire** more staff.（第二個 to 常可省略：...**to expand its market and hire** more staff.）

### 二、對等連接詞不只有 and

- **and**：單純並列
- **but**：轉折對比，前後語意相反但詞性仍需一致：The proposal is **innovative but costly**.
- **or**：選項並列：Employees may submit the form **by email or by fax**.
- **not only...but also**、**either...or**、**neither...nor**：相關連接詞（correlative conjunctions），兩側結構必須對稱：The director **not only approved the budget but also revised** the timeline.

### 三、什麼是省略句

英文為避免重複，常在對等結構中省略與前文重複的部分，這種現象稱為「省略句（ellipsis）」。常見型態：

1. **省略重複的主詞/助動詞**：Mr. Lin reviewed the contract and (Mr. Lin) signed it.
2. **省略重複的動詞（比較句中常見）**：Ms. Wu works faster than her colleague (works).
3. **對等子句中省略重複主詞+be動詞**：The report, when (it is) finalized, will be sent to the board.（分詞構句其實是省略「主詞+be」的結果）
4. **as / than 引導的比較從句中省略動詞**：The new system processes orders more efficiently than the old one (processes orders).

### 四、平行結構與省略句的關係

省略句之所以能成立，正是因為被省略的部分和前面的成分「結構平行」，讀者能自動補回。因此看到 and/or/but/than/as 時，第一步永遠是回頭找「前面對等的成分是什麼詞性/時態」，再決定空格要填什麼。

## 常見陷阱

- 誤把不同時態的動詞並列：~~The assistant confirmed the reservation and cancels the meeting.~~ 應改為 confirmed...and cancelled。
- 動名詞與不定詞混用：~~The policy aims at reducing costs and to improve efficiency.~~ 應統一為 reducing...and improving。
- 相關連接詞前後結構不對稱：~~The plan is neither realistic or affordable.~~ neither 應搭配 nor，而非 or。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

*   **align** (v. 及物/不及物) 使一致、對齊 | Department heads must *align* their goals with the company's overall strategy.
*   **coincide** (v. 不及物) 同時發生、一致 | The product launch will *coincide* with the annual trade show in October.
*   **concise** 形容詞 簡潔的 | The CEO praised the report for being both accurate and *concise*.
*   **coherent** 形容詞 條理一致的、連貫的 | A *coherent* marketing message helps customers remember the brand.
*   **consistency** 名詞 一致性 | Maintaining *consistency* across branches is a top priority for the franchise.
*   **counterpart** 名詞 對應的人/物 | Our Taipei office coordinated closely with its *counterpart* in Tokyo.
*   **juxtapose** (v. 及物) 並列、對照 | The presentation *juxtaposes* last year's figures with this year's projections.
*   **symmetry** 名詞 對稱 | The designer valued visual *symmetry* in the new packaging layout.
*   **truncate** (v. 及物) 截短、省略 | The summary *truncates* the lengthy legal clauses into a single paragraph.
*   **abbreviate** (v. 及物) 縮寫、簡化 | Job titles are often *abbreviated* on internal directories to save space.
*   **elliptical** 形容詞 省略的 | The memo's *elliptical* wording confused several new employees.
*   **implicit** 形容詞 隱含的、未明言的 | There is an *implicit* agreement that overtime will be compensated.
*   **contrast** (v. 及物/不及物) 對比、形成對照 | The bold headline *contrasts* with the plain body text.
*   **correlate** (v. 不及物) 相關聯 | Employee satisfaction scores *correlate* closely with retention rates.
*   **uniform** 形容詞 一致的、統一的 | All branches must follow a *uniform* pricing policy.
*   **symmetric** 形容詞 對稱的 | The negotiation reached a fairly *symmetric* outcome for both sides.
*   **duplicate** (v. 及物) 重複、複製 | The system automatically flags any *duplicate* entries in the database.
*   **synchronize** (v. 及物) 使同步 | Managers must *synchronize* their schedules before booking the conference room.
*   **compress** (v. 及物) 壓縮、精簡 | The editor *compressed* three paragraphs into a single sentence.
*   **interchangeable** 形容詞 可互換的 | In this context, the two terms are largely *interchangeable*.
*   **cohesive** 形容詞 有凝聚力的、連貫的 | A *cohesive* team delivered the project two weeks ahead of schedule.
*   **synopsis** 名詞 摘要 | The analyst attached a one-page *synopsis* to the full report.
*   **superfluous** 形容詞 多餘的、不必要的 | The editor removed several *superfluous* clauses from the contract draft.

### 片語

*   **on par with** 與...同等 | The new intern's performance is already *on par with* that of senior staff.
*   **in tandem with** 與...同步進行 | The marketing rollout will proceed *in tandem with* the product launch.

## 📚 補充秒殺公式

1.  **and/or/but 前後看詞性**：只要看到對等連接詞，先圈出它連接的兩個成分，再判斷空格該填什麼詞性，而非直接翻譯猜答案。
2.  **時態一致原則**：and 連接兩個動詞時，除非語意明確有先後差異，否則兩個動詞時態應一致。
3.  **相關連接詞成對記憶**：not only...but also／either...or／neither...nor／both...and，這四組前後結構必須完全對稱，選項若破壞對稱可直接刪除。
4.  **省略句還原法**：遇到看似不完整的句子，先在腦中把省略的主詞/動詞補回去，再判斷空格詞性。
5.  **比較句省略動詞**：than / as 之後若動詞與主句相同，常被省略，只留主詞或受詞，看到 than 後面直接接名詞不要誤判為受格錯誤。
6.  **分詞構句＝省略句的變形**：When (it is) reviewed, When (they are) combined 這類結構，考的其實是「主詞+be動詞被省略」的觀念。
7.  **動名詞 vs 不定詞平行陷阱**：句中已出現一個動名詞，and 後方若接選項多為動名詞，反之亦然；不可混用兩種形式。
8.  **but 表轉折仍要詞性一致**：but 雖然語意相反，文法上仍要求前後詞性/句構對等，不要被語意對立誤導成可以不對稱。
9.  **三項並列看逗號規律**：A, B, and C 這種三項並列，A、B、C 三者詞性必須一致，只要其中一項不同就是錯誤選項。
10. **省略 to 的不定詞並列**：第一個不定詞出現 to V 後，第二個並列的不定詞常省略 to，只留原形動詞，例如 decided to cut costs and (to) improve quality。
11. **找主詞代動詞的省略點**：句中若有逗號後接分詞或形容詞卻找不到主詞，通常是主詞子句中「主詞+be動詞」被省略。
12. **相似詞性選項先排序再刪去**：平行結構題常見四個選項為同一單字的不同詞性變化，先分析空格在句中的語法角色，再對應到正確詞性。

## 💪 練習題（15 題）

**1.** The consultant reviewed the contract, revised the payment terms, and ______ the final draft to the client.
(A) sends (B) sent (C) sending (D) to send

**2.** The new manager is both approachable and ______, which makes her popular among the staff.
(A) decisive (B) decisively (C) decision (D) decide

**3.** Employees may submit their expense reports either by uploading the file online or ______ them to the finance office in person.
(A) delivered (B) delivering (C) delivers (D) to deliver

**4.** The proposal was praised for being innovative but ______ costly for a company of this size.
(A) prohibitively (B) prohibitive (C) prohibit (D) prohibition

**5.** Rather than hire additional staff, the department decided to reorganize existing roles and ______ workflow across teams.
(A) streamlines (B) streamlined (C) streamline (D) streamlining

**6.** The report, when ______ by the finance committee, will determine the budget for next quarter.
(A) approve (B) approved (C) approving (D) approves

**7.** The two branches operate under a uniform pricing policy, ______ the regional headquarters in Singapore.
(A) as does (B) as it does (C) so as (D) as they were

**8.** Neither the marketing director ______ the sales team anticipated such a sharp increase in demand.
(A) or (B) but (C) nor (D) and

**9.** The client not only requested a full refund ______ also demanded an official apology in writing.
(A) and (B) but (C) or (D) so

**10.** Compared to the previous model, the updated printer runs faster and produces documents more ______.
(A) accurate (B) accuracy (C) accurately (D) accuracies

**11.** The HR director spent the morning interviewing candidates and ______ the afternoon reviewing résumés.
(A) spends (B) spent (C) spending (D) to spend

**12.** The audit found several procedures to be redundant, outdated, or simply ______ with current regulations.
(A) inconsistent (B) inconsistency (C) inconsistently (D) inconsist

**13.** Once ______ by both parties, the agreement will take effect immediately.
(A) sign (B) signed (C) signing (D) signs

**14.** The design team's goal is to simplify the layout, reduce printing costs, and ______ readability for customers.
(A) improves (B) improved (C) improving (D) improve

**15.** The finance department's quarterly figures were more favorable than ______ of the previous year.（第一空）Analysts therefore expect the board to approve the expansion plan and ______ additional funding for the coming fiscal year.（第二空）
第一空：(A) that (B) those (C) it (D) one
第二空：(A) releases (B) released (C) release (D) releasing
