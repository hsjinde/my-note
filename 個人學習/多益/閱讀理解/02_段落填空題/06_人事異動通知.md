# 06_人事異動通知

## 📝 答題策略 🌟
1.  **公告類文章先抓「事件本身」**：人事異動、新人歡迎信這類文章，第一句通常就點出「誰、發生什麼事、何時生效」，抓到這條主線再回頭看空格。
2.  **主動與被動的判斷**：留意空格動詞的主詞是「人」還是「事」，人事異動公告常見「A 被調往 B」的被動語態。
3.  **現在完成式 + for/since**：文章中若出現「for the past year」「since + 過去時間點」，通常暗示現在完成式。
4.  **句子插入題**：確認插入句與前後句的主題是否連貫，尤其留意人稱代名詞與時間副詞的呼應。
5.  **詞性題**：留意空格前後是否有 be 動詞、介系詞，判斷該填形容詞、名詞還是動詞原形。

## 短文一：員工調職公告

To: All Staff
From: General Manager's Office

Effective September 15th, Mr. David Huang ______(1) from our Taipei branch to the Kaohsiung branch to strengthen the regional sales team. Mr. Huang has served as senior account manager here for four years and built strong relationships with our key clients. ______(2). All ongoing projects under his supervision will be handed over to Ms. Wendy Lai, who ______(3) as his direct backup for the past year. A farewell gathering ______(4) this Friday afternoon in the main conference room, and everyone is welcome to attend.

### 題目 1
(A) transfers
(B) transferred
(C) will be transferred
(D) has transferred

### 題目 2
(A) We are confident that his experience will greatly benefit the Kaohsiung team.
(B) The company plans to open a new branch in Taichung next year.
(C) All employees must complete the updated expense report by Friday.
(D) The building's air conditioning system will be upgraded next week.

### 題目 3
(A) served
(B) serves
(C) has served
(D) had served

### 題目 4
(A) will be held
(B) holds
(C) held
(D) is holding

## 短文二：新進員工歡迎信

Dear Ms. Karen Lopez,

Welcome to Sunrise Innovations! We are delighted that you ______(5) our team as a Marketing Coordinator starting next Monday. Your first day will begin with an orientation session covering company policies and workplace procedures. ______(6). Please bring a valid form of identification so that the HR office can issue your employee badge. Your mentor, Mr. Alan Reed, will show you around the office and help you become ______(7) with our daily operations. Should you have any questions ______(8) your first week, feel free to reach out to us anytime before your start date.

### 題目 5
(A) join
(B) joined
(C) will be joining
(D) have joined

### 題目 6
(A) You will also receive a laptop and system access during this session.
(B) The marketing department relocated to a new office last year.
(C) Employees must submit expense reports within a week of travel.
(D) Our quarterly newsletter features updates from every department.

### 題目 7
(A) acquainted
(B) acquaint
(C) acquaintance
(D) acquaintances

### 題目 8
(A) during
(B) while
(C) for
(D) since
