# 02_倒裝句

## 核心概念
倒裝句 (Inversion) 是將「助動詞或 be 動詞」移到「主詞」前面，改變一般語序的句型。多益考的倒裝句主要分為兩大類：**否定副詞倒裝**與**條件句 If 省略倒裝**（假設語氣）。

## 1. 否定副詞倒裝 🌟

當句子以**否定或半否定的副詞/副詞片語**開頭時，主詞與助動詞的位置必須互換 (倒裝)。

**公式**：`否定副詞 + 助動詞/be + 主詞 + 動詞`

| 否定/限制副詞 | 意思 | 倒裝範例 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Never** | 從不 | **Never** *have* I *seen* such dedication. |
| **Rarely / Seldom** | 很少 | **Rarely** *does* the CEO *attend* such meetings. |
| **Hardly (ever)** | 幾乎不 | **Hardly** *had* she *left* when it started raining. |
| **Not only...but also** | 不只...還... | **Not only** *did* they *finish* on time, but they also exceeded the goal. |
| **Not until** | 直到...才... | **Not until** the report is submitted *will* the payment be released. |
| **Under no circumstances** | 在任何情況下都不 | **Under no circumstances** *should* you *share* your password. |
| **On no account** | 絕對不可以 | **On no account** *are* employees *allowed* to... |
| **Only + 副詞片語** | 只有... | **Only then** *did* they *realize* the problem. |

## 2. 條件句倒裝 (If 省略) 🌟
請參考 [[04_假設語氣]] 的考點 1。

當假設語氣中 `If` 被省略時，助動詞移到句首，形成倒裝：

| 原句 | 倒裝句 |
| :--- | :--- |
| If **had** p.p. (過去) | **Had** + S + p.p., S + would have p.p. |
| If **should** V (未來) | **Should** + S + V, ... |
| If **were** (現在) | **Were** + S ..., S + would V |

*   *Should* you **need** any assistance, please contact us. (最常考)
*   *Had* I **known** earlier, I would have acted differently.

## 3. Only + 副詞片語 開頭的倒裝

當 `Only` 加上副詞片語放在句首時，主要子句必須倒裝：
*   **Only after** the meeting **did** she **realize** the issue.
*   **Only by** working together **can** we **achieve** this goal.
*   **Only when** you **submit** the form **will** the process **begin**.

> [!note]
> 注意：`Only + 主詞` 開頭時**不倒裝**！
> - **Only he** *knew* the truth. (只有他知道，主詞是 he，不倒裝)

## 4. So/Neither/Nor 的附和倒裝

*   **So + 助動詞 + 主詞**：表示「...也是如此」(肯定附和)
    *   A: I enjoyed the conference. B: **So did** I. (我也是)
*   **Neither/Nor + 助動詞 + 主詞**：表示「...也不」(否定附和)
    *   A: I don't like long meetings. B: **Neither do** I. (我也不喜歡)

## 📝 多益秒殺解題技巧
1.  看到 `Never, Rarely, Seldom, Not only, Only + 副詞片語` 開頭 ➔ 確認後面是否有**倒裝** (助動詞在主詞前)。
2.  看到 `Should + 主詞 + V`, `Had + 主詞 + p.p.`, `Were + 主詞` 的句型 ➔ 這是**假設語氣倒裝**，後面主句記得填對時態 (would/would have)。
3.  **閱讀測驗**：倒裝句型判斷助動詞位置，是分析長句的好工具。

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **地方副詞倒裝（非否定副詞）**：地方副詞片語放句首時，也可能倒裝，但主詞若是**代名詞則不倒裝**。
    *   **Here comes** the manager. (地方副詞 + 動詞 + 名詞主詞，倒裝)
    *   **Here he comes.** (主詞是代名詞 he，不倒裝)
2.  **完全倒裝 vs 部分倒裝**：be 動詞或一般動詞開頭的倒裝為「完全倒裝」(動詞整個移到主詞前)；含助動詞的句型多為「部分倒裝」(只移助動詞)。選項考的通常是**部分倒裝**，注意不要把原形動詞也一起移到句首。
3.  **陷阱：否定副詞後方誤用一般直述語序**：若選項只有動詞而無助動詞倒裝形式，多半是錯誤選項，因為否定副詞開頭**必須**倒裝。
4.  **So + 助動詞 + 主詞 vs So + 主詞 + 助動詞**：前者表示「附和」(也是如此)，後者 (不倒裝) 表示「的確如此、確實」，兩者意義不同，選擇時看語意需求。
    *   A: He is very diligent. B: **So he is.** (的確如此，不是附和句)
5.  **倒裝句中主詞與動詞的數一致性別忘記**：倒裝時仍要注意主詞單複數是否與助動詞一致，例如 `Rarely do they complain`（they 複數用 do，非 does）。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

### 副詞 / 副詞片語
*   **rarely** (adv. 很少地) | **Rarely does** she miss a deadline.
*   **seldom** (adv. 不常地) | **Seldom is** the office this quiet.
*   **hardly** (adv. 幾乎不) | **Hardly had** the meeting begun when the fire alarm rang.
*   **under no circumstances** (phr. 在任何情況下都不) | **Under no circumstances should** you disclose client data.
*   **not until** (phr. 直到...才) | **Not until** the contract is signed **will** the deal proceed.
*   **in no way** (phr. 絕不) | **In no way does** this affect our schedule.

### 動詞 / 語意相關字
*   **exceed** (v. 超過) | The results **exceeded** all expectations.
*   **submit** (v. 提交) | **Not until** the form is **submitted** will the application be processed.
*   **disclose** (v. 揭露) | **Under no circumstances should** employees **disclose** confidential data.
*   **acknowledge** (v. 承認、確認收到) | **Only after** reviewing the file did she **acknowledge** the mistake.

### 片語
*   **so far** (phr. 到目前為止) | **So far**, the project has gone smoothly.
*   **no sooner...than** (phr. 一...就...) | **No sooner had** he arrived **than** the meeting started.

## 💪 練習題（5 題）

**1.** Rarely __________ such a rapid increase in productivity within a single quarter.
(A) we have seen
(B) have we seen
(C) we saw
(D) did we seeing

**2.** __________ received his promotion than he was assigned to lead a new team.
(A) No sooner had he
(B) He had no sooner
(C) No sooner he had
(D) Had he no sooner

**3.** Should any issues arise during the installation, __________ our support team immediately.
(A) contacting
(B) contact
(C) to contact
(D) contacted

**4.** A: "I haven't finished the quarterly report yet." B: "__________."
(A) Neither have I
(B) So have I
(C) Neither I have
(D) So I haven't

**5.** 請閱讀以下短文並選出最適合填入空格的答案。

Not until the final inspection was complete ______(1)______ the factory allowed to resume production. Only after every safety measure had been verified ______(2)______ the manager sign off on the report.

(1)
(A) was
(B) is
(C) the factory was
(D) had

(2)
(A) does
(B) did
(C) do
(D) done
