# 10_語態轉換與行為者省略

## 核心概念
本章聚焦兩件事：(1) 主動句轉換成被動句的**完整操作步驟**（不是只看 be + p.p. 的公式，而是練習「動作」本身），(2) 被動句中 **by + 行為者要不要保留**的判斷原則。多益閱讀與填空題常要求考生從一段主動敘述反推被動句該怎麼寫，或是從一個被動句的空格判斷後面該不該接 by 子句，這正是本章要解決的問題。

### 1. 主動轉被動的三個步驟
把一句主動句轉成被動句，永遠依照固定順序操作：

1.  **把主動句的受詞搬到被動句的主詞位置**
2.  **動詞改成「be + p.p.」，be 動詞的時態要跟原本主動動詞的時態一致**
3.  **原本的主詞移到句尾，加上 by，變成「by + 行為者」（如果需要保留的話）**

*   主動：The finance team **submits** the quarterly report every month.
*   被動：The quarterly report **is submitted** by the finance team every month.

時態必須完整保留，不能因為轉成被動就簡化：
*   主動（過去進行）：The auditors **were reviewing** the accounts.
*   被動：The accounts **were being reviewed** by the auditors.
*   主動（未來完成）：The board **will have approved** the budget by then.
*   被動：The budget **will have been approved** by the board by then.

**只有及物動詞才能轉被動**：occur、happen、arrive、remain 等不及物動詞後面沒有受詞，無法轉換成被動句，這點在 03_被動語態 已提過，此處作為轉換步驟的第一道檢查關卡——沒有受詞就不必談步驟 1。

### 2. by + 行為者何時可以省略
被動句中的「by + 行為者」不是每次都要寫出來。多益考的重點是：**當行為者不明確、不重要，或讀者本來就知道是誰時，直接省略**。常見情況：

*   **行為者是不特定的人（someone/people/they/one）**：這種泛稱本來就不需要具體點名。
    *   The office **was renovated** last month.（不用寫 by workers/by someone）
*   **行為者從上下文就能明顯推知**：句子或段落已經表明是誰在做，重複寫出反而累贅。
    *   The proposal **was rejected** at yesterday's meeting.（讀者已知是由委員會/主管在會議中決定）
*   **強調的是動作或結果本身，而非誰做的**：商業文件中常見，重點在「事情發生了」。
    *   The shipment **was delayed** due to a customs inspection.
*   **一般性慣例、規定或制度性描述**：這類句子行為者本來就是抽象的「制度」，不需具體點名。
    *   Employees **are paid** on the last business day of each month.

### 3. by + 行為者何時不可省略
反過來，以下情況**一定要保留 by + 行為者**，省略會讓句意不完整或改變原意：

*   **行為者是句子的資訊重點，或是意外/特殊的人**：省略就失去了句子想強調的訊息。
    *   The merger agreement **was reviewed by an outside legal team**, not by the in-house counsel.（對比兩種行為者，缺一不可）
*   **行為者是具體、獨特、無法從上下文推知的個人或機構**：
    *   The keynote speech **was delivered by the company's founder**.
*   **句子本身的功能就是要「歸功」或「歸咎」某個對象**：
    *   The delay **was caused by a shortage of raw materials**.

### 4. by 與 with 的行為者混淆
多益也愛考「工具/材料」與「行為者」的介系詞混用：
*   **by + 行為者（人或機構，執行動作的一方）**：The report **was written by** the intern.
*   **with + 工具/材料（用什麼東西完成動作，不是誰做的）**：The package **was wrapped with** recycled paper.
*   一句話中兩者可以同時出現：The contract **was signed by the CEO with a fountain pen**.（by 表示誰簽的，with 表示用什麼簽）

## 📝 多益秒殺解題技巧
1.  看到主動句要轉被動的題目，先找受詞——沒有受詞（不及物動詞）就不能轉被動，直接刪除該選項。
2.  轉換時務必核對 be 動詞的時態是否與原句動詞時態一致，這是最常出錯也最常考的細節。
3.  空格後面如果接的是「介系詞 by + 具體機構/人名」，通常代表這個行為者是句子想強調的重點，不能亂省略掉這段。
4.  空格後面如果直接接句號或轉折語氣（表示行為者不重要/不明確），優先選不帶 by 子句的被動句。
5.  看到 by 後面接的是材料、工具、方式（紙、筆、機器等），要小心可能該用 with 而非 by。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

### 轉換與行為者相關動詞
*   **transform** v. 轉換、轉化 | The team **transformed** the manual process into an automated workflow.
*   **convert** v. 轉換、改變 | The firm plans to **convert** the warehouse into office space.
*   **rewrite** v. 重寫 | The editor was asked to **rewrite** the press release before publication.
*   **restructure** v. 重組 | The company will **restructure** its sales division next quarter.
*   **rearrange** v. 重新安排 | The assistant had to **rearrange** the schedule after the client canceled.
*   **reorganize** v. 重新組織 | Management decided to **reorganize** the entire filing system.
*   **modify** v. 修改 | The engineer **modified** the design after receiving customer feedback.
*   **alter** v. 更動、變更 | The contract terms **were altered** shortly before signing.

### 行為者不明確／泛稱的表達
*   **anonymous** adj. 匿名的 | The complaint was submitted by an **anonymous** employee.
*   **unspecified** adj. 未指明的 | The budget cut was attributed to **unspecified** reasons.
*   **unidentified** adj. 身份不明的 | An **unidentified** caller reported the security breach.
*   **undisclosed** adj. 未公開的 | The deal was reportedly financed by an **undisclosed** investor.
*   **on behalf of** phr. 代表 | The lawyer signed the document **on behalf of** the client.
*   **collectively** adv. 集體地 | The decision was made **collectively** by the department heads.

### 歸功／歸咎相關動詞與片語
*   **attribute A to B** phr. 將 A 歸因於 B | The sales increase was **attributed to** the new marketing campaign.
*   **credit A with B** phr. 將功勞歸於 A | Analysts **credited** the CEO with the company's rapid recovery.
*   **stem from** phr. 源自於 | The dispute **stemmed from** a misunderstanding about delivery terms.
*   **originate from** phr. 起源於 | The idea for the product line **originated from** a customer survey.
*   **be blamed for** phr. 因...而被責怪 | The contractor **was blamed for** the construction delay.
*   **take credit for** phr. 居功、認領功勞 | The manager should not **take credit for** the entire team's effort.

### by / with 介系詞辨析相關單字
*   **draft** v. 起草 | The contract was first **drafted** by the legal department.
*   **wrap** v. 包裝 | All fragile items are **wrapped** with protective foam.
*   **seal** v. 封緘 | The envelope was **sealed** with wax before shipping.
*   **compile** v. 彙整 | The annual report was **compiled** by three different departments.
*   **assemble** v. 組裝 | The furniture is **assembled** with a set of included tools.
*   **certify** v. 認證 | The financial statements were **certified** by an independent auditor.
*   **endorse** v. 背書、認可 | The proposal was **endorsed** by the entire executive committee.

### 重要性與焦點相關形容詞
*   **noteworthy** adj. 值得注意的 | The improvement in customer retention was particularly **noteworthy**.
*   **negligible** adj. 微不足道的 | The impact of the price change was considered **negligible**.
*   **decisive** adj. 決定性的 | The chairman's vote proved **decisive** in the final outcome.
*   **incidental** adj. 附帶的、非重點的 | The extra cost was treated as **incidental** to the main project.

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **轉換三步驟**：受詞變主詞 → 動詞改 be + p.p.（時態不變）→ 原主詞變 by + 行為者（視需要保留）。三步驟缺一，轉換就不完整。
2.  **不及物動詞不能轉被動**：轉換題第一步一定要先確認原動詞後面有沒有受詞，沒有受詞的句子直接排除被動選項。
3.  **時態一致原則**：被動句的 be 動詞時態必須完全對應主動句原本的動詞時態，簡單式對簡單式、進行式對進行式、完成式對完成式。
4.  **泛稱行為者必省略**：someone、people、they、one 等泛稱主詞轉被動時，句尾一律不寫 by + 行為者。
5.  **上下文已知則省略**：段落前文已交代是誰做的動作，被動句中可以直接省略 by 子句，避免累贅重複。
6.  **強調結果、非行為者，則省略**：商業書信中常見「事情發生了」的語氣，不需具體點名是誰做的。
7.  **行為者是句子重點時必須保留**：若行為者是特定機構、特定個人，或句子明顯要對比/強調是誰做的，by 子句不可省略。
8.  **歸功／歸咎語境必留 by**：attribute to、caused by、credited with 這類句子，行為者本身就是資訊核心，絕不能省。
9.  **by 用於行為者，with 用於工具或材料**：分辨方法是問「後面接的是『誰』還是『用什麼東西』」。
10. **by 與 with 可同句並存**：一句話可以同時交代「誰做的」和「用什麼做的」，兩個介系詞分工不衝突。
11. **篇章題常見雙重考點**：同一封信或備忘錄裡，有些句子該保留 by（強調責任歸屬），有些句子該省略 by（強調事件本身），須逐句依語境判斷，不能套用同一規則。
12. **省略不代表句子不完整**：被動句省略 by 之後仍然是文法完整的句子，考生常誤以為被動句「一定要」有 by 子句而選錯。

(A) delivered (B) was delivered (C) delivers (D) was delivering

**5.** After months of negotiation, the merger agreement ______ , though the press release did not mention which party made the final concession.
(A) was finally signed (B) was finally signed by the two CEOs (C) finally signs (D) finally signing

**6.** The wooden crates ______ with reinforced straps to prevent damage during transport.
(A) secured (B) were secured (C) secures (D) were securing

**7.** According to the internal memo, the delay in shipment ______ a shortage of packaging materials at the supplier's factory.
(A) was caused (B) was caused by (C) caused (D) causes

**8.** The invoice was mistakenly sent to the wrong client, but the report simply notes that the error ______ during last week's system migration.
(A) occurred (B) was occurred (C) occurs (D) is occurred

**9.** The original blueprint ______ several times before the construction team finally approved the final version.
(A) revised (B) had been revised (C) revises (D) is revising

**10.** The company's rapid turnaround this year ______ the new CFO, whose cost-cutting measures reportedly saved millions.

(A) stamped (B) was stamped (C) stamps (D) was stamping

(A) handled (B) are handled (C) handle (D) are handling

(A) must certify (B) must be certified (C) must certifying (D) must certified

(A) was rejected (B) rejected (C) rejects (D) was rejecting
