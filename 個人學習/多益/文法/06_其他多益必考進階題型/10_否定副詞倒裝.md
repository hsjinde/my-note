# 10_否定副詞倒裝

## 核心概念

本章專門深入「否定/限定副詞倒裝」，聚焦 [[02_倒裝句]] 尚未詳細處理的子題：**Little 開頭倒裝**、**否定受詞前置倒裝**（Not a single word did she say）、**Barely/Scarcely...when**、**No longer / Nowhere / By no means / At no time** 等次常見否定副詞、以及 **So + 形容詞 + that** 倒裝（與 So 附和倒裝不同）。若你還沒讀過 [[02_倒裝句]] 的 Never/Rarely/Seldom/Not only/Not until/Under no circumstances/Only 系列，請先讀那篇，本章不重複那些公式。

## 1. Little 開頭的倒裝（常與認知動詞連用）

`Little` 作為半否定副詞，意思是「幾乎不、一點也不」，放句首修飾 know、expect、realize、imagine 等認知動詞時，主詞與助動詞倒裝：

**公式**：`Little + 助動詞 + 主詞 + 動詞`

*   **Little did** the manager **know** that the client had already signed with a competitor.
*   **Little did** they **realize** how much the renovation would cost.

> [!note]
> 不要跟 `little` 當形容詞（很少的，修飾不可數名詞，如 little time）搞混，那種用法不倒裝。

## 2. 否定受詞前置倒裝（Not a single / Not one + 名詞）

當 `Not a single + 名詞` 或 `Not one + 名詞`（作受詞）被移到句首強調時，主詞與助動詞倒裝：

**公式**：`Not a single/one + 名詞 + 助動詞 + 主詞 + 動詞`

*   **Not a single complaint did** the department **receive** during the trial period.
*   **Not one employee did** the supervisor **dismiss** despite the budget cuts.

這與 Not only 系列不同：這裡倒裝的觸發點是「被前置的否定受詞」，而不是句首副詞。

## 3. Barely / Scarcely...when、No sooner...than 的辨析

`Barely` 和 `Scarcely` 表示「幾乎不」，常搭配 `when`（`No sooner` 搭配 `than`，已於 [[02_倒裝句]] 教過，此處只做對比）：

**公式**：`Barely/Scarcely + had + 主詞 + p.p. ... when + 子句（過去式）`

*   **Scarcely had** the shipment **arrived when** customs flagged it for inspection.
*   **Barely had** the negotiations **begun when** one party walked out.

> [!note]
> `No sooner...than` 與 `Barely/Scarcely...when` 意義相近（一...就...），但連接詞不可混用：no sooner 只能接 than，barely/scarcely 只能接 when。

## 4. No longer / Nowhere / By no means / At no time

這些是多益常考卻常被忽略的否定副詞（片語），置於句首一律倒裝：

*   **No longer**（不再）：**No longer does** the firm **outsource** its customer service.
*   **Nowhere**（哪裡都不）：**Nowhere else could** the team **find** a supplier with such reliability.
*   **By no means**（絕不）：**By no means should** the contract **be** altered without legal review.
*   **At no time**（在任何時候都不）：**At no time was** the manager **informed** of the delay.

## 5. So + 形容詞/副詞 + that 的倒裝（強調句型，非附和句）

當 `So + 形容詞/副詞` 被移到句首以加強語氣，後面接 `that` 子句時，主要子句倒裝。這與 [[02_倒裝句]] 提到的「So + 助動詞 + 主詞」附和句（也是如此）**意義完全不同**，考試常混淆兩者：

**公式**：`So + 形容詞/副詞 + be動詞/助動詞 + 主詞 + that ...`

*   **So significant was** the drop in revenue **that** the board called an emergency meeting.
*   **So quickly did** the team **respond that** the client extended the contract on the spot.

對比附和句：A: The results were impressive. B: **So were** the client's reviews.（附和：客戶的評價也是如此，無 that 子句）

## 📝 多益秒殺解題技巧

1. 看到 `Little` 開頭且後面接 know/expect/realize 等動詞 → 確認倒裝（助動詞在主詞前）。
2. 看到 `Not a/one + 名詞` 開頭且該名詞在原句中應為受詞 → 這是受詞前置倒裝，後面必須倒裝。
3. `Barely/Scarcely...when` 與 `No sooner...than` 不可混搭連接詞。
4. `So + 形容詞 + that` 開頭時先確認有沒有 that 子句：有 → 強調倒裝句；沒有 that 子句、只是回應對話 → 通常是附和句 So + 助動詞 + 主詞。
5. 這些否定副詞倒裝多半只倒裝「助動詞」（部分倒裝），不要把後面原形/過去式動詞也搬到句首。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

### 否定/限定副詞與片語
*   **little** (adv. 幾乎不) | **Little did** the intern *realize* how demanding the role would be.
*   **nowhere** (adv. 任何地方都不) | **Nowhere** *could* the auditor *find* discrepancies in the ledger.
*   **by no means** (phr. 絕不) | **By no means** *is* the deadline *negotiable*.
*   **at no time** (phr. 在任何時候都不) | **At no time was** the supervisor *aware* of the breach.
*   **no longer** (phr. 不再) | **No longer does** the company *rely* on paper invoices.
*   **not a single** (phr. 一個...也沒有) | **Not a single** applicant *met* the qualifications this round.
*   **not once** (phr. 一次也沒有) | **Not once did** the vendor *miss* a delivery deadline.
*   **so...that** (phr. 如此...以致於) | **So** urgent *was* the request **that** the team worked through the weekend.

### 動詞（常與倒裝句搭配使用）
*   **dismiss** (v. 解僱、駁回) | **Not one employee did** the manager *dismiss* during the restructuring.
*   **compromise** (v. 妥協、危及) | **By no means should** quality *be compromised* for speed.
*   **overturn** (v. 推翻) | **Nowhere in** the policy **could** the committee *overturn* the decision.

### 名詞 / 形容詞
*   **backlash** (n. 強烈反彈) | The proposal triggered a *backlash* from shareholders.
*   **restructuring** (n. 重組) | The *restructuring* affected two divisions.
*   **negotiable** (adj. 可協商的) | The salary is **by no means** *negotiable*.
*   **disruptive** (adj. 造成干擾的) | **So disruptive was** the outage **that** operations halted for hours.
*   **unprecedented** (adj. 前所未有的) | **Never had** the firm faced such an *unprecedented* loss.

## 📚 補充秒殺公式

1.  **Little vs. A little**：`Little`（幾乎不）為半否定，句首會倒裝；`A little`（一點點）為肯定量詞，不觸發倒裝。看清楚有沒有 `a` 是判斷關鍵。
2.  **受詞前置倒裝的辨識法**：先找出「被前置的名詞在原句中扮演什麼角色」——如果它是受詞（如 Not a single complaint 是 receive 的受詞），主詞動詞就必須倒裝。
3.  **Barely/Scarcely 只搭 when，No sooner 只搭 than**：這是固定搭配，選項若把 when/than 互換即為陷阱。
4.  **So + 形容詞 + that 與 Such + a + 名詞 + that 的轉換**：意義相同但詞性要求不同，So 後面接形容詞/副詞，Such 後面接（a/an）+ 名詞，Such 開頭時整句仍需倒裝（Such was the impact that...）。
5.  **By no means / At no time / Nowhere 屬於「完全否定」而非「部分否定」**：與 rarely/seldom（部分否定，還是有發生機率）語意不同，寫作題常考語意選字。
6.  **倒裝句中的時態一致性**：Barely/Scarcely...when 句型固定用過去完成式(had p.p.) + 過去簡單式(when 子句)，不可用現在完成式替換。
7.  **No longer 可放句中也可放句首**：句中 `The firm no longer relies on...`（不倒裝）；句首 `No longer does the firm rely on...`（倒裝），選項常考兩種位置的正確搭配動詞形式。
8.  **not a single 之後接單數名詞 + 單數動詞**：即使語意上是「完全沒有」，倒裝後動詞仍需與該單數名詞的文法數一致。
9.  **陷阱：否定副詞倒裝句中誤用非助動詞的完整動詞形式**：例如 `Little did he knew`（錯誤，did 後面應接原形 know，而非過去式 knew）。
10. **So + 形容詞 + that 中形容詞需為表語形容詞**：常考 significant、urgent、severe、disruptive 等描述程度的形容詞，選項中若給出不能作表語的形容詞即可排除。
11. **多題型交叉**：閱讀測驗中若看到句首是這些否定副詞開頭且動詞放在主詞前，不要誤判為文法錯誤，這是正確的倒裝語序。

(B) found
(C) they found
(D) had they find

**3.** __________ the technician sat down when the emergency alarm went off.
(A) Barely
(B) Barely had
(C) Barely did
(D) Barely was

**4.** __________ does the logistics team rely on manual tracking sheets since the new system launched.
(A) No longer
(B) Not longer
(C) No more longer
(D) No long

**5.** By no means __________ the client's request unreasonable, given the scope of the project.
(A) is
(B) was the manager consider
(C) the manager considers
(D) considered

**6.** At no time __________ of the security breach until the audit revealed it.
(A) the CEO was informed
(B) was the CEO informed
(C) the CEO informed
(D) did the CEO informed

**7.** Nowhere in the contract __________ a clause permitting early termination without penalty.
(A) exist
(B) exists
(C) does exist
(D) existed there

**8.** So urgent __________ the request that the entire team stayed past midnight to finish it.
(A) was
(B) is
(C) it was
(D) has been

**9.** Not once __________ a delivery deadline in over five years of partnership.
(A) has the supplier missed
(B) the supplier has missed
(C) the supplier missed
(D) missed the supplier

**10.** 第一空：Scarcely had the auditors reviewed the first ledger ______(1)______ they discovered irregularities in the accounts.

(D) then

**12.** Not a single employee __________ from the mandatory training session this quarter.
(A) exempted
(B) was exempted
(C) has exempt
(D) exempts

**13.** By no means __________ this decision be reversed without approval from the regional director.
(A) can
(B) is
(C) does
(D) will be

(A) was
(B) is
(C) has
(D) did
