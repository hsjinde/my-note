# 08_強調句型

## 核心概念

強調句型 (Cleft Sentence) 是把句子中「主詞、受詞、時間副詞、地方副詞」等某個成分抽出來，放進 `It is/was ... that ...` 的框架裡，藉此突顯這個成分。多益考的重點不是造句，而是**辨識**：句子挖空的位置，到底是強調句的一部分，還是長得很像但其實是別的句型（名詞子句主詞 it、虛主詞 it、形容詞子句）。

## 1. 強調句型的基本公式 🌟

**公式**：`It is/was + 被強調的成分 + that (人用 who/whom 亦可) + 句子剩餘部分`

*   原句：**The finance director** approved the budget last Friday.
*   強調主詞：**It was the finance director that** approved the budget last Friday.
*   強調受詞：**It was the budget that** the finance director approved last Friday.
*   強調時間副詞：**It was last Friday that** the finance director approved the budget.

判斷口訣：把 `It is/was` 和 `that` 拿掉之後，剩下的字重組起來要能還原成一個**完整、通順**的句子。若拿掉之後句子不完整（例如缺主詞或缺受詞），代表這裡不是單純的強調句，而可能是名詞子句。

## 2. 動詞時態與 be 動詞的選擇

強調句中的 `is/was` 只反映「說話當下強調的時間點」，不受被強調成分本身的單複數影響（因為 `It` 是虛位主詞，恆用單數 be 動詞）：

*   **It is** the two supervisors **that** signed off on the report. (雖然強調 two supervisors，但 be 動詞仍用 is，不是 are)
*   **It was not until 2023 that** the company launched its overseas branch. (常考：`not until` 強調句)

## 3. 強調句 vs 名詞子句主詞 it vs 虛主詞 it 的辨識 🌟

這是本章最容易混淆、也最常考的地方：

| 句型 | 拿掉 It is/was...that 後 | 範例 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **強調句** | 剩下的部分是一個**完整句子** | It was the client **that** rejected the proposal. → The client rejected the proposal. (完整) |
| **虛主詞 it (形容詞子句/不定詞)** | `It` 本身就是句子唯一的主詞，`that` 引導的是**名詞子句**當受詞或補語 | **It is** essential **that** every applicant submit the required documents. (essential 是形容詞，that 子句是名詞子句，不能拿掉重組成強調句) |
| **代名詞 it** | `it` 單純指代前文提過的某個東西 | The report is on the desk; **it** is the version **that** the manager approved. (it 指 report，that 引導形容詞子句修飾 version) |

判斷技巧：強調句拿掉 `It is/was ... that` 之後，句意跟原句幾乎一樣；虛主詞句型如果硬拿掉，會變得語意不通或缺主詞。

## 4. 強調疑問句 (It + be + 疑問詞成分 + that)

強調句也能用在特殊疑問句：

*   Who approved this contract? → **Who was it that** approved this contract?
*   What caused the delay? → **What was it that** caused the delay?

## 5. 強調地方/原因副詞

*   強調地方：**It was in the Taipei office that** the negotiation took place.
*   強調原因：**It is because of the shipping delay that** customers have filed complaints.

## 📝 多益秒殺解題技巧
1.  看到 `It is/was ___ that` 的空格，先確認空格後面拿掉 `that` 之後的部分是否**完整**——完整就填「被強調的名詞/代名詞/副詞片語」，不完整（例如後面接形容詞 + 名詞子句）就要考慮是虛主詞句型。
2.  強調句中的 `that` 前面若被強調的是「人」，可換成 `who`；但多益選項通常直接給 `that`，不必糾結換成 who/whom。
3.  題目若出現 `It is/was + 形容詞（essential, important, advisable...）+ that`，優先判斷為虛主詞 + 名詞子句，而非強調句，子句動詞常用**原形動詞 (subjunctive)**。

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **還原測試法**：把 `It is/was` 與 `that` 刪除，看剩下部分能否組成完整句子；能，才是強調句。
2.  **be 動詞恆單數**：強調句的 is/was 不隨被強調成分的單複數變化，只看句子描述的時間點是現在還是過去。
3.  **強調受詞時，主詞絕不能省略**：`It was the report that the manager reviewed.` 中，`the manager` 不可省略，否則句意不完整。
4.  **`It is/was not until... that...` 是強調句與倒裝句的橋樑**：這個句型也可以改寫成倒裝句 `Not until... did...`，兩者意義相同，是常見的同義句轉換考法。
5.  **強調句中被強調的部分絕不能是動詞或整個子句**：只能強調名詞、代名詞、副詞（片語），不能強調動詞本身。
6.  **虛主詞 it + be + 形容詞 + that 子句，動詞常為原形**：例如 `It is essential that every employee attend the briefing.`，屬於慣用語氣，與強調句無關。
7.  **強調疑問句的語序**：`疑問詞 + was/is + it + that...`，疑問詞永遠擺最前面，`it` 緊接在 be 動詞後面。
8.  **強調句可以強調的成分不含補語**：主詞補語（如形容詞）不能被強調句框住，只有名詞性/副詞性成分可以。
9.  **看選項判斷「代名詞的格」**：強調主詞用主格代名詞（It was **she** that...），強調受詞則用受格（It was **her** that they promoted），但正式多益考題傾向用完整名詞而非代名詞，選項出現代名詞時要留意格位是否正確。
10. **與分裂句 (pseudo-cleft) 區辨**：`What caused the delay was the shortage of parts.` 是另一種強調句型 (Wh-cleft)，與 `It is/was...that` 句型效果相同但結構不同，多益偶爾混合出現，看到 `What + S + V + was/is...` 也算強調句的變體。
11. **強調句不影響主要子句的動詞一致性**：即使強調的是複數名詞，`that` 後面的動詞仍須與該名詞的單複數一致，例如 `It is the employees that were notified.`
12. **常與強調句混淆的假受詞句型**：`I find it difficult that...` 中的 it 是假受詞，同樣不是強調句，注意句型結構整體判斷，不要只看到 it...that 就直接視為強調句。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

### 強調/凸顯相關動詞
*   **emphasize** (v. 及物) 強調 | The manager wanted to *emphasize* the importance of meeting the deadline.
*   **underscore** (v. 及物) 強調、突顯 | The report's findings *underscore* the need for better logistics planning.
*   **highlight** (v. 及物) 凸顯、標示重點 | The consultant chose to *highlight* three key risks in her presentation.
*   **underline** (v. 及物) 強調 | The CEO's remarks *underline* the company's commitment to sustainability.
*   **accentuate** (v. 及物) 使更顯著 | The new packaging design *accentuates* the product's premium quality.
*   **spotlight** (v. 及物) 使受矚目 | The award ceremony *spotlighted* employees with outstanding sales records.
*   **pinpoint** (v. 及物) 精確指出 | The audit team managed to *pinpoint* the exact source of the accounting error.
*   **single out** (phr. v.) 特別挑出 | The director decided to *single out* one branch for its exceptional performance.
*   **differentiate** (v. 及物) 使有區別 | The marketing team worked to *differentiate* the brand from its competitors.
*   **draw attention to** (phr.) 引起對...的注意 | The email was sent to *draw attention to* the upcoming policy change.
*   **call attention to** (phr.) 提請注意 | The safety officer wanted to *call attention to* the faulty equipment.
*   **bring to light** (phr.) 揭露、使明朗化 | The internal review *brought to light* several billing discrepancies.

### 強調/凸顯相關形容詞、副詞
*   **precisely** (adv. 精確地、正是) | *Precisely* what the client wanted was a faster turnaround time.
*   **exactly** (adv. 確實地) | This is *exactly* the kind of proposal the board has been waiting for.
*   **genuinely** (adv. 真誠地、確實地) | The manager was *genuinely* surprised by the team's rapid progress.
*   **undeniably** (adv. 不可否認地) | The new system is *undeniably* faster than the one it replaced.
*   **undoubtedly** (adv. 無疑地) | This quarter's results are *undoubtedly* the strongest the company has posted.
*   **solely** (adv. 單獨地、僅僅) | The decision rests *solely* with the executive committee.
*   **exclusively** (adv. 專門地、獨家地) | This discount is offered *exclusively* to long-term corporate clients.
*   **primarily** (adv. 主要地) | The delay was caused *primarily* by a shortage of shipping containers.
*   **distinct** (adj. 明顯不同的) | Each product line has a *distinct* target market.
*   **significant** (adj. 重大的、顯著的) | The merger had a *significant* impact on the company's stock price.

### 相關名詞與片語
*   **pivotal** (adj. 關鍵性的) | The finance director played a *pivotal* role in securing the loan.
*   **paramount** (adj. 至為重要的) | Customer satisfaction remains *paramount* to the company's long-term strategy.
*   **central** (adj. 核心的、中心的) | Data security is *central* to the firm's IT strategy this year.
*   **crux** (n. 核心、要點) | The *crux* of the dispute was who should bear the shipping costs.
*   **none other than** (phr.) 不是別人正是 | The keynote speaker was *none other than* the company's founder.
*   **first and foremost** (phr.) 首要地 | *First and foremost*, the manager thanked the logistics team for their effort.
*   **above all** (phr.) 最重要的是 | *Above all*, the client valued transparency throughout the negotiation.

## 💪 練習題（15 題）

**1.** It was ______ that discovered the discrepancy in the shipping invoice, not the accounting department.
(A) discovering (B) supervised (C) supervision (D) the warehouse supervisor

**2.** It was the marketing director, not the sales manager, ______ approved the revised advertising budget.
(A) who (B) whom (C) which (D) whose

**3.** It is ______ that every applicant complete the background check before receiving a job offer.
(A) essence (B) essential (C) essentially (D) essentialize

**4.** ______ was it that finally persuaded the board to approve the overseas expansion plan?
(A) That (B) Which (C) What (D) Whom

**5.** It was not until the quarterly report ______ that the executives realized how severe the losses had been.
(A) reviewed (B) reviewing (C) review (D) was reviewed

**6.** It was the shortage of raw materials, rather than a lack of demand, ______ delayed production last month.
(A) that (B) what (C) so (D) it

**7.** It is the client's feedback, not the internal survey results, ______ the design team relies on most heavily.
(A) which is (B) that (C) who (D) whom is

**8.** It was in the regional office in Taichung ______ the merger agreement was finally signed.
(A) where (B) which (C) that (D) there

**9.** It was because of the sudden currency fluctuation ______ the company postponed the overseas purchase.
(A) why (B) so that (C) for that (D) that

**10.** 第一空：It was the logistics team ______ successfully coordinated the emergency shipment over the holiday weekend. 第二空：It is now common practice for the company to ______ such urgent orders to a dedicated response unit.
(A) that / assign (B) who / assigning (C) whom / assigned (D) which / assignment

**11.** It is the newly revised compliance policy that ______ every department to submit monthly reports.
(A) require (B) requires (C) requiring (D) to require

**12.** It was during the emergency board meeting ______ the CFO reviewed and rejected the proposed budget increase.
(A) when (B) so (C) that (D) which

**13.** It is not the price but the delivery time ______ most customers complain about.
(A) what (B) so (C) whom (D) that

**14.** 第一空：It was the customer service representative ______ handled the complaint calmly and efficiently. 第二空：It is precisely this kind of professionalism ______ the company hopes to promote across all departments.
(A) who / that (B) whom / what (C) which / so (D) that / which is

**15.** It was only after the internal audit was completed ______ the accounting errors came to light.
(A) when (B) that (C) then (D) so
