# 12_數字時間金額表達慣用語

## 核心概念

多益商業文件（memo、email、合約、報告）中充滿數字、時間、金額的固定表達方式。這些片語考的不是複雜文法，而是**慣用搭配的精準度**——同樣表達「大約」，approximately 與 roughly 用法相同但語域不同；同樣表達「期限」，no later than 與 by the end of 結構不同；同樣表達「總計」，a total of 與 in total 詞性不同。掌握這些固定搭配，才能在 Part 5/6 快速選對答案。

### 1. 表達「大約 / 精確」的用語

- **大約**：approximately、roughly、about、around 後面直接接數字，不加 of。
  例：The renovation cost **roughly** $50,000.
- **精確**：exactly、precisely 強調數字沒有誤差。
  例：The shipment arrived **exactly** on schedule.

### 2. 表達「期限 / 截止」的用語

- **no later than + 時間點**：不遲於某時間，強調最終期限，常搭配 must / should。
  例：Reports must be submitted **no later than** Friday.
- **by + 時間點** vs **within + 一段時間**：by 接時間點（by June 1），within 接一段長度（within two weeks）。
- **at the latest** 通常放句尾，語氣比 no later than 稍口語。

### 3. 表達「總計 / 累積」的用語

- **a total of + 數字 + 名詞**：a total of 過去以數字開頭修飾名詞，是形容詞化用法。
  例：**A total of** 200 employees attended the seminar.
- **in total** 是副詞片語，放句尾或句中修飾整句。
  例：The company hired 50 new staff **in total** this year.
- **cumulative / aggregate** 強調隨時間累加的總數，常用於財務報告。

### 4. 表達「上限 / 下限」的用語

- **cap / ceiling** 名詞表示上限，也可作動詞（to cap spending at）。
- **threshold** 表示門檻，跨過此值即觸發某條件。
- **at most / at least** 分別表示「最多」「最少」。

### 5. 金額與帳務相關用語

- **payable** 表示「應付的」，常見 accounts payable（應付帳款）。
- **installment** 分期付款；**subtotal** 小計；**invoice** 發票／請款單。
- **disbursement** 支出撥款，語域較正式，常見於財務報表。

## 📝 多益秒殺解題技巧

看到空格前後有數字或時間，先判斷詞性：需要形容詞（修飾名詞）還是副詞（修飾動詞/句子）？例如 a total of 後面一定接名詞，in total 則放句尾當副詞。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

### 精確度與程度

*   **exact** adj. 精確的、確切的 | Please provide the *exact* figure by tomorrow.
*   **exactly** adv. 精確地 | The meeting started *exactly* at nine o'clock.
*   **roughly** adv. 大約地 | The project will take *roughly* six months to complete.
*   **precisely** adv. 精確地 | The invoice amount was calculated *precisely* to the cent.

### 期限與時間範圍

*   **deadline** n. 截止期限 | The submission *deadline* has been moved up by one week.
*   **timeframe** n. 時間範圍 | We need to finish the audit within a tight *timeframe*.
*   **timely** adj. 及時的 | We appreciate your *timely* response to the invoice.
*   **punctual** adj. 準時的 | Vendors are expected to be *punctual* with deliveries.
*   **expire** (v. 不及物) 到期、失效 | The contract will *expire* at the end of this fiscal year.
*   **expiration** n. 到期、屆滿 | Please renew before the *expiration* date on the license.
*   **extend** (v. 及物) 延長 | The board voted to *extend* the payment period by 30 days.
*   **extension** n. 延期 | The vendor requested an *extension* on the delivery deadline.
*   **interval** n. 間隔 | Invoices are issued at regular *intervals* of 30 days.
*   **duration** n. 期間、持續時間 | The lease *duration* is set at twenty-four months.
*   **span** n. 時間跨度 | Revenue doubled over a five-year *span*.
*   **at the latest** 片語 最晚 | Please confirm your attendance by Friday *at the latest*.
*   **in advance** 片語 提前 | Payment must be made two weeks *in advance*.
*   **ahead of schedule** 片語 提前於計畫 | The construction project finished *ahead of schedule*.
*   **behind schedule** 片語 落後於計畫 | The shipment is currently three days *behind schedule*.
*   **on time** 片語 準時 | The supplier delivered the parts *on time* this quarter.

### 總計、累積與統計

*   **cumulative** adj. 累積的 | The *cumulative* effect of the discounts reduced overall profit.
*   **aggregate** (adj./n.) 總計的；總數 | The *aggregate* sales figure exceeded projections.
*   **sum** n. 總額 | The company paid a substantial *sum* for the merger.
*   **subtotal** n. 小計 | Check the *subtotal* before adding tax to the invoice.

### 帳務與金額

*   **installment** n. 分期付款 | Customers may pay in twelve monthly *installments*.
*   **invoice** n. 發票、請款單 | The accounting department has not received the *invoice* yet.
*   **payable** adj. 應付的 | The balance is *payable* within thirty days of receipt.
*   **disbursement** n. 支出、撥款 | All *disbursements* over $10,000 require managerial approval.
*   **allocate** (v. 及物) 分配 | The finance team will *allocate* the budget across departments.
*   **allocation** n. 分配額 | Each division received a fixed budget *allocation* this year.
*   **increment** n. 增額 | Salaries rise in small annual *increments*.
*   **fluctuate** (v. 不及物) 波動 | Currency exchange rates *fluctuate* daily.
*   **fluctuation** n. 波動 | Price *fluctuations* made forecasting difficult this quarter.

### 上限、門檻與週期

*   **threshold** n. 門檻 | Orders above the *threshold* qualify for free shipping.
*   **ceiling** n. 上限 | Management set a spending *ceiling* for the marketing campaign.
*   **cap** (n./v.) 上限；設上限 | The company decided to *cap* travel expenses at $2,000 per trip.
*   **quarterly** adj. 每季的 | The board reviews performance on a *quarterly* basis.
*   **fiscal year** n. 財政年度 | The new budget takes effect at the start of the next *fiscal year*.

## 📚 補充秒銷公式

1.  **a total of + 數字 + N**：a total of 是形容詞片語，必須放在名詞前面，不能單獨放句尾；若要放句尾修飾整句，改用 in total。
2.  **no later than 後面只接時間點**：不接時間長度，例如可說 no later than Friday，不可說 no later than two weeks。
3.  **by 接時間點，within 接時間長度**：看到「一段時間」（two weeks、a month）優先考慮 within；看到明確日期或事件則用 by。
4.  **approximately / roughly / about / around 直接接數字**：不加介系詞 of，也不與 exactly 混用於同一句。
5.  **at the latest 放句尾**：等同於句首的 no later than，兩者語意相同但位置相反，考題常互換測試理解。
6.  **cap 與 ceiling 互換測試**：cap 可當動詞（to cap X at Y），ceiling 通常只作名詞，考題常考詞性判斷。
7.  **expire 為不及物動詞**：後面不可直接接受詞，若要表「使⋯到期」需用 invalidate 或改用被動語態。
8.  **payable 修飾名詞時放在名詞後**：如 the amount payable，屬於後位形容詞用法，與一般形容詞前位修飾不同。
9.  **cumulative 與 aggregate 語意相近**：cumulative 強調隨時間逐步累加，aggregate 強調把各部分加總為一個整體數字。
10. **installment 常與 in / by 搭配**：pay in installments（分期支付）或 pay by installments，皆可，但不可用 pay with installments。
11. **threshold 常搭配 above / below / exceed**：exceed the threshold、below the threshold 為固定搭配，不用 over the threshold 表達正式書面語。
12. **fluctuate 為不及物動詞，fluctuation 為名詞**：句中若空格前有冠詞（a/the）或介系詞，選 fluctuation；若空格獨立作動詞，選 fluctuate。

## 💪 練習題（15 題）

**1.** The new safety guidelines will ______ effect at the start of the next fiscal year.
(A) take (B) do (C) make (D) have

**2.** All expense reports must be submitted no later ______ the last business day of the month.
(A) as (B) that (C) than (D) from

**3.** ______ 300 clients attended the annual product launch event.
(A) A total of (B) Totally (C) In total (D) Total

**4.** The renovation budget was set at a strict ceiling, and no department is permitted to exceed the ______.
(A) threshold (B) span (C) installment (D) interval

**5.** Because the shipment arrived three days ______ schedule, the client requested a partial refund.
(A) ahead of (B) behind (C) in (D) on

**6.** The finance director asked the team to ______ the remaining budget across the four regional offices before year-end.
(A) allocate (B) allocation (C) allocated (D) allocating

**7.** The membership card will ______ automatically unless renewed within thirty days.
(A) expiration (B) expire (C) expiring (D) expires

**8.** Currency exchange rates have ______ significantly over the past quarter, complicating our cost projections.
(A) fluctuation (B) fluctuated (C) fluctuating (D) fluctuate

**9.** Payment for the equipment is ______ within sixty days of the invoice date.
(A) payable (B) paying (C) payment (D) pay

**10.** 第一空：Customers who order in bulk may pay ______ twelve monthly installments, and the first payment is due ______ before shipment.
(A) in (B) on (C) at (D) by
第二空：(A) advance (B) advanced (C) in advance (D) advancing

**11.** The report's ______ figures show a steady increase in revenue over the last five fiscal years.
(A) cumulative (B) cumulate (C) cumulatively (D) cumulation

**12.** Contractors are advised to submit their bids at the ______, since the deadline will not be extended.
(A) last than (B) latest (C) at the latest (D) more late

**13.** The subtotal listed on the invoice does not include tax; the ______ amount will be calculated separately.
(A) sum (B) span (C) installment (D) duration

**14.** 第一空：Although the survey was conducted within a limited ______, researchers managed to collect responses from over a thousand employees ______ two weeks.
(A) timeframe (B) deadline (C) ceiling (D) cap
第二空：(A) by (B) within (C) since (D) until

**15.** Delivery vendors are contractually required to remain ______ at all times, as late shipments result in financial penalties.
(A) punctual (B) punctuality (C) punctually (D) punctuate
