## Part 7：篇章閱讀（2 篇）

### 文章一：合資企業新聞稿

**Global Horizon Manufacturing and Andes Materials Group Announce Joint Venture**

Global Horizon Manufacturing, a leading producer of industrial components, today announced a new joint venture with Andes Materials Group, a raw material supplier based in Chile. The partnership, named the Horizon-Andes Alliance, will focus on securing a stable supply of copper and aluminum for Global Horizon's expanding production lines.

Under the terms of the agreement, Andes Materials Group will supply raw materials at a fixed exchange rate for the first three years, protecting both companies from currency fluctuations. In exchange, Global Horizon Manufacturing will invest in upgrading Andes' processing facilities in Santiago, allowing the supplier to increase output by 25 percent.

Analysts note that the joint venture reflects a broader trend among manufacturers seeking to reduce their exposure to volatile commodity markets through long-term international partnerships. The companies expect the agreement to lower material costs by approximately 12 percent over the next five years.

The joint venture is expected to take effect on October 1, pending final approval from regulatory authorities in both countries. A detailed financial report on the partnership's projected impact will be released to shareholders next quarter.

### 文章二：貿易條款協商信

Dear Mr. Takahashi,

We are writing in response to your letter dated June 3 regarding the recent increase in import tariffs affecting our trade agreement. As you noted, the new tariff rate imposed on textile imports has raised our overall shipping costs by nearly 18 percent, which was not anticipated when we signed the original contract last year.

After reviewing our financial projections, we would like to propose renegotiating the pricing clause of our agreement to reflect these additional costs. Specifically, we suggest adjusting the unit price by 8 percent, with the remaining difference absorbed by our company in order to preserve the long-term stability of our partnership.

We understand that this proposal may affect your budget planning for the coming fiscal year, and we are prepared to discuss alternative arrangements, including a temporary reduction in order volume, if that would better suit your company's needs.

We value the trust we have built over the past six years and are committed to finding a solution that keeps both companies financially sound. Please let us know your thoughts by July 15 so that we can finalize the revised terms before the next shipment is scheduled.

Sincerely,
Trade Relations Department
