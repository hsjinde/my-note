# 01_比較級與最高級

## 核心概念
比較級 (Comparative) 用於比較兩者，最高級 (Superlative) 用於比較三者以上。多益常考「比較結構的對稱性」、「倍數比較」以及「特殊比較句型」。

## 1. 形成規則

| 類型 | 原級 | 比較級 | 最高級 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **短形容詞** (1音節) | fast | **faster** | **the fastest** |
| **-y 結尾** | easy | **easier** | **the easiest** |
| **長形容詞** (2音節以上) | efficient | **more** efficient | **the most** efficient |
| **不規則變化** | good / well | **better** | **the best** |
| **不規則變化** | bad | **worse** | **the worst** |
| **不規則變化** | many / much | **more** | **the most** |
| **不規則變化** | little | **less** | **the least** |
| **不規則變化** | far | **farther/further** | **the farthest/furthest** |

## 2. 基本句型

*   **比較級**：`A + 比較級 + than + B`
    *   This method is **more efficient than** the previous one.
    *   The new office is **larger than** the old one.
*   **最高級**：`A + the + 最高級 + in/of + 範圍`
    *   This is **the most important** decision of the year.
    *   She is **the best** performer **in** the department.
*   **同等比較**：`as + 原級 + as`
    *   This product is **as reliable as** the leading brand.

## 3. 多益必考：特殊比較句型 🌟

### 倍數比較
`數詞 + times + as + 原級 + as` 或 `數詞 + times + 比較級 + than`
*   The new model is **three times as fast as** the old one.
*   Revenue is **twice as high as** last year's. (不是 twice higher than)

### 比較級 + and + 比較級 (越來越...)
*   The market is becoming **more and more competitive**.
*   The deadline is getting **closer and closer**.

### the + 比較級, the + 比較級 (越...越...)
*   **The more** you practice, **the better** you become.
*   **The earlier** we start, **the sooner** we finish.

### no more... than / no less... than
*   She is **no less capable than** her colleagues. (她的能力不亞於同事，甚至可能更好)

## 4. 比較結構的對稱性 (多益陷阱) 🌟
比較的兩端必須是同類的事物！
*   ❌ The sales of this quarter are higher than **last quarter**.
*   ✅ The sales of this quarter are higher than **those of last quarter**. (sales 對比 sales)
*   ✅ This quarter's sales are higher than **last quarter's**. (所有格對比)

## 📝 多益秒殺解題技巧
1.  看到 `than` ➔ 前面一定需要**比較級** (more + adj. 或 adj.-er)。
2.  看到 `the + 形容詞` 在句首，後面又有 `the + 形容詞` ➔ 這是「越...越...」句型，選**比較級**。
3.  看到 `as ___ as` 中間的空格 ➔ 選**原級**形容詞/副詞。
4.  **對稱性陷阱**：比較兩個名詞時，看看兩邊是否是「同類」，必要時需用 `that of` 或 `those of` 來替代已提到的名詞。
5.  看到 `in` + 範圍 (in the company, in the world) 或 `of all` ➔ 前面需要**最高級**。

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **than any other + 單數名詞**：表示「比其他任何一個都...」，用來**間接表達最高級**，注意 other 後面的名詞必須是**單數**。
    *   This model sells better **than any other product** in the lineup. (雖然文法上是比較級，語意等於最高級)
2.  **one of the + 最高級 + 複數名詞**：固定句型，最高級後面接的名詞必須是**複數**。
    *   She is **one of the most experienced managers** in the firm. (不可寫成 manager 單數)
3.  **less / least 表示「越來越少 / 最少」**：容易和 more/most 搞混，看清楚語意是「遞減」還是「遞增」。
    *   This plan is **less effective than** the previous one. (較沒效果，不是「較有效」)
4.  **the second/third + 最高級**：多益偶爾考「第二、第三...」的最高級變化，一樣要加 the。
    *   This is **the second largest** market in the region.
5.  **陷阱：more 與 -er 不可重複使用**：長形容詞用 more，短形容詞字尾加 -er，兩者**不可同時出現**。
    *   ❌ **more easier** ➔ ✅ **easier**（或 more easy 為誤用，一律用 easier）

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

### 形容詞
*   **efficient** (adj. 有效率的) 高效的 | This method is **more efficient than** the old one.
*   **reliable** (adj. 可靠的) 值得信賴的 | This brand is **as reliable as** the leading one.
*   **competitive** (adj. 競爭激烈的) 有競爭力的 | The market is becoming **more competitive**.
*   **substantial** (adj. 大量的) 顯著的 | The company reported **a substantial increase** in profit.
*   **considerable** (adj. 相當大的) 可觀的 | There was **a considerable difference** between the two proposals.

### 副詞
*   **significantly** (adv. 顯著地) 大幅地 | Sales rose **significantly higher than** projected.
*   **relatively** (adv. 相對地) | The new system is **relatively faster** than the old one.
*   **increasingly** (adv. 日益地) | Customers are **increasingly demanding** better service.

### 名詞
*   **margin** (n. 差距、利潤) | The company won by a narrow **margin**.
*   **counterpart** (n. 對應的人/物) | Our sales figures exceeded those of our **counterpart** in Europe.
*   **benchmark** (n. 基準、標竿) | The new product set **a benchmark** for quality.

### 片語
*   **outperform** (v. 表現優於) | This model **outperforms** all competitors.
*   **surpass / exceed** (v. 超越、超過) | Revenue **surpassed** expectations this quarter.
*   **on par with** (phr. 與...同等水準) | Our service is **on par with** industry standards.
*   **far and away** (phr. 遠遠地、最...) | She is **far and away** the best candidate.

**1.** The new marketing strategy has proven to be much __________ than the previous one.
(A) effective
(B) more effective
(C) most effective
(D) effectively

**2.** Of all the candidates interviewed, Ms. Chen was considered __________ qualified for the position.
(A) more
(B) most
(C) the most
(D) much

**3.** This year's customer satisfaction score is higher than __________ of last year.
(A) it
(B) that
(C) those
(D) one

**4.** The more thoroughly employees are trained, __________ they perform on the job.
(A) good
(B) better
(C) the better
(D) best

**5.** 請閱讀以下短文並選出最適合填入空格的答案。

Since adopting the new inventory system, our warehouse has become far ______(1)______ than it was under the old manual process. In fact, it now processes orders faster ______(2)______ any other branch in the region.

(1)
(A) efficient
(B) more efficient
(C) most efficient
(D) efficiently

(2)
(A) than
(B) as
(C) that
(D) of

**6.** In daily operation the refurbished units are nearly as ___ as the brand-new ones from the same series.
(A) reliable (B) more reliable (C) most reliable (D) reliably

**7.** This year's turnout at the trade fair was significantly ___ than the organizers had projected in March.
(A) high (B) higher (C) highest (D) as high

**8.** The Kaohsiung facility is the ___ heavily automated plant in the group's entire manufacturing network.
(A) more (B) much (C) most (D) many

**9.** The delivery times offered by the new courier are noticeably shorter than ___ of the previous provider.
(A) it (B) that (C) those (D) them

**10.** The sooner the replacement parts clear customs, ___ the assembly team can resume the second shift.
(A) the earlier (B) earlier (C) earliest (D) as early

(A) fewer and fewer (B) few and few (C) fewest (D) less and less

**12.** The revised warehouse layout is ___ more efficient than the floor plan drawn up three years ago.

(A) broad (B) broader (C) broadest (D) as broad

**14.** The annual training budget is no ___ important than the capital equipment budget in the board's view.
(A) less (B) least (C) little (D) lesser

**15.** The plant produced twice ___ many finished units this quarter as it did during the same period last year.
(A) so (B) as (C) too (D) very
