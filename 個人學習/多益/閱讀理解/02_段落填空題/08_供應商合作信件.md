# 08_供應商合作信件

## 📝 答題策略 🌟
1.  **先判斷信件角色與立場**：是「發出合作邀約的一方」還是「回覆並提出協商條件的一方」，立場不同會影響語氣與句子插入題的邏輯方向。
2.  **時態要對照信中的時間線索**：over the past decade、before joining the firm 等片語分別對應現在完成式、過去完成式，須抓住句中的時間錨點。
3.  **句子插入題看「這封信要往哪裡走」**：合作邀約信常見「稱讚→提議→說明具體條件」的順序；協商信常見「感謝提案→提出疑慮→列出具體修改項目」的順序。
4.  **假設語氣／建議動詞（ask/require + that）題**：ask that、require that 後面的子句動詞須用**原形動詞**（虛擬語氣），不受主詞單複數或時態影響。
5.  **比較級題留意「than」是否出現**：句中若有 than，通常需要比較級而非原級或最高級。

## 短文一：供應商合作邀約信

Dear Mr. Navarro,

We at Aurora Textiles have long admired the quality of the fabrics your factory ______(1) over the past decade. We are writing today to propose a long-term supply partnership between our two companies. ______(2). Under this arrangement, we would commit to a minimum order volume each quarter in exchange for priority production scheduling and a discounted unit price. Our procurement director, who ______(3) previously with several overseas manufacturers before joining our firm, believes this partnership could benefit both parties considerably. We would like to discuss the terms further ______(4) a phone call or an in-person meeting at your facility.

### 題目 1
(A) produces
(B) has produced
(C) produced
(D) will produce

### 題目 2
(A) Specifically, we would like to become one of your primary buyers for the coming year.
(B) Our design team recently attended a textile trade show in Milan.
(C) Your factory was originally established as a small family business.
(D) We recently switched to a new accounting software system.

### 題目 3
(A) works
(B) worked
(C) had worked
(D) has worked

### 題目 4
(A) via
(B) despite
(C) among
(D) unless

## 短文二：採購條件協商信

Dear Ms. Alvarado,

Thank you for your proposal regarding a long-term partnership between our companies. We are interested in moving forward, but before signing the agreement, we would like to renegotiate a few of the procurement terms. ______(5). Given the current volatility in raw material costs, we would ask that the unit price ______(6) fixed for only the first six months rather than the full year, after which it can be reviewed. We would also like all shipments to undergo a quality inspection ______(7) leaving your facility, in line with our standard procurement policy. With these two adjustments, we believe the revised agreement would be considerably ______(8) favorable to both sides than the original draft.

### 題目 5
(A) Specifically, we have concerns about two of the terms outlined in your draft: pricing and quality inspection.
(B) Our factory has operated in this region for over thirty years.
(C) We were impressed by the quality of your latest product catalog.
(D) Our accounting department recently upgraded its invoicing software.

### 題目 6
(A) is
(B) was
(C) be
(D) will be

### 題目 7
(A) before
(B) despite
(C) although
(D) since

### 題目 8
(A) more
(B) most
(C) much
(D) very
