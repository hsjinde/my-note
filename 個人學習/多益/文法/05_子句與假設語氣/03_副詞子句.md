# 03_副詞子句

## 核心概念
副詞子句 (Adverb Clause) 是由「從屬連接詞 + 完整子句」構成，在句子中扮演「副詞」的角色，修飾主句的動詞，說明時間、原因、條件、對比、目的、結果等關係。多益最常考「連接詞意思的辨別」以及「連接詞 vs. 介系詞的詞性判斷」。

## 1. 常見副詞子句連接詞分類 🌟

### 時間 (When)
| 連接詞 | 中文 | 範例 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **when** | 當...時 | *When* the meeting **ends**, I will call you. |
| **while** | 當...的同時 | *While* he **was presenting**, I took notes. |
| **before** | 在...之前 | Please review the report *before* you **submit** it. |
| **after** | 在...之後 | *After* the project **was completed**, they celebrated. |
| **as soon as** | 一...就... | I will inform you *as soon as* a decision **is made**. |
| **until** | 直到...為止 | The office will be closed *until* renovations **are done**. |
| **by the time** | 到了...的時候 | *By the time* you **arrive**, the meeting will have started. |

> [!warning] 時間副詞子句不用未來式
> 在時間副詞子句中，用**現在式**代替未來式！
> - ❌ When he **will arrive**, I will tell him.
> - ✅ When he **arrives**, I will tell him.

### 原因 (Because)
| 連接詞 | 中文 | 範例 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **because** | 因為 (最強調原因) | *Because* the deadline **was moved**, we worked overtime. |
| **since** | 由於 (已知原因) | *Since* you **are** the expert, please guide us. |
| **as** | 因為 (語氣較弱) | *As* the office **was** full, the meeting was moved. |
| **now that** | 既然 (現在已經...) | *Now that* the issue **has been resolved**, we can proceed. |

### 條件 (If)
| 連接詞 | 中文 | 範例 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **if** | 如果 | *If* you **have** any questions, please contact us. |
| **unless** | 除非 (= if not) | *Unless* you **submit** the form, you won't be registered. |
| **provided (that)** | 只要 (條件較嚴格) | *Provided that* all conditions **are met**, the deal is final. |
| **as long as** | 只要 | You can use the lab *as long as* you **follow** the rules. |

> [!tip]
> `unless` = `if ... not`，不要雙重否定！
> - ❌ Unless you *don't* submit... (錯誤)
> - ✅ Unless you *submit*... (正確)

### 對比/讓步 (Although)
| 連接詞 | 中文 | 範例 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **although / though / even though** | 雖然、儘管 | *Although* the budget **was tight**, the project succeeded. |
| **while / whereas** | 然而、相較於 (表對比) | Sales *increased*, *while/whereas* costs *decreased*. |

### 目的 (So that)
| 連接詞 | 中文 | 範例 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **so that** | 為了 (目的) | She worked overtime *so that* the team **could meet** the deadline. |
| **in order that** | 為了 (正式) | We upgraded the system *in order that* users **can** access it faster. |

## 2. 連接詞 vs. 介系詞 (多益重點比較) 🌟
請參考 [[04_連接詞]] 中的對照表格。

| 語意 | **連接詞** (後接完整子句 S+V) | **介系詞** (後接名詞/V-ing) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 雖然/儘管 | although, even though | despite, in spite of |
| 因為 | because, since | because of, due to, owing to |
| 直到 | until | until (可接名詞，也可接子句) |

## 📝 多益秒殺解題技巧
1.  **判斷空格後方的結構**：
    *   後面是**完整子句 (S+V)** ➔ 選**連接詞** (although, because, since, when...)。
    *   後面是**名詞或 V-ing** ➔ 選**介系詞** (despite, because of, due to...)。
2.  看到 `unless` ➔ 子句不加 not，`unless` 本身已含否定。
3.  **時間副詞子句**：看到 when/before/after/as soon as 引導的子句，若主句是未來式，子句用**現在式**（不用 will）。
4.  看到 `while/whereas` 後面子句意思與主句相反/對比 ➔ 這是「對比連接詞」，而非「當...時」。

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **even if vs. even though**：`even if` 表示「即使 (假設語氣，尚未發生)」；`even though` 表示「即使、雖然 (已經是事實)」，兩者常混淆考。
    *   **Even if** it rains, the event will proceed. (假設)
    *   **Even though** it rained, the event proceeded. (事實)
2.  **once 作連接詞**：意思是「一旦...」，引導時間/條件副詞子句，後面同樣不用 will。
    *   **Once** the contract **is signed**, the deal will be finalized.
3.  **in case (that) / in the event that**：意思是「以防萬一」，語意接近 if，但強調預防措施。
    *   Bring an umbrella **in case** it **rains**.
4.  **副詞子句的省略 (Elliptical Clause)**：當副詞子句主詞與主句主詞相同，且子句動詞為 be 動詞時，可省略「主詞 + be 動詞」。
    *   **While** *(he was)* waiting for the bus, he read a book.
    *   **Although** *(it is)* small, the office is efficient.
5.  **as if / as though**：意思是「彷彿、好像」，常接假設語氣 (過去式表與現在事實相反)。
    *   He talks **as if** he **were** the manager. (但他不是經理)

## 🔤 相關單字和片語
*   **provided** (conj.) 只要、假使 | You may leave early, **provided** you finish the report.
*   **once** (conj.) 一旦 | **Once** approved, the budget cannot be changed.
*   **whereas** (conj.) 然而、反之 | Some prefer email, **whereas** others prefer calls.
*   **owing to** (片語，介系詞) 由於 | The flight was delayed **owing to** bad weather.
*   **due to** (片語，介系詞) 由於 | The meeting was postponed **due to** a scheduling conflict.
*   **in spite of** (片語，介系詞) 儘管 | **In spite of** the rain, sales remained strong.
*   **renovation** (n.) 翻新、整修 | The store closed until **renovations** were complete.
*   **overtime** (n./adv.) 加班 | Staff worked **overtime** so that deadlines could be met.
*   **finalize** (v.) 完成、定案 | **Once** the design is **finalized**, production begins.
*   **postpone** (v.) 延後 | The launch was **postponed** because the samples were delayed.
*   **in the event that** (片語) 萬一、如果 | **In the event that** the server fails, contact IT immediately.
*   **as long as** (片語) 只要 | You may work remotely **as long as** you attend meetings.
*   **come into effect** (片語) 生效 | The new policy will **come into effect** once it is approved.
*   **budget** (n.) 預算 | **Although** the **budget** was tight, the project succeeded.
*   **compliant** (adj.) 符合規定的 | **Provided that** the report is **compliant**, it will be accepted.

## 💪 練習題（15 題）
1.  ______ the new system is more efficient, employees still find it difficult to use.
    (A) Because
    (B) Although
    (C) Despite
    (D) So that

2.  ______ the shipment arrives before Friday, the store will not run out of stock.
    (A) Unless
    (B) Provided that
    (C) Because of
    (D) In spite of

3.  The manager acted ______ she had already made her final decision.
    (A) as if
    (B) even if
    (C) so that
    (D) unless

4.  短文填空：All staff must complete the safety training (1)______ they begin working on the factory floor. (2)______ the training is finished, supervisors will issue the required safety equipment.
    (1)
    (A) despite
    (B) before
    (C) because of
    (D) in spite of
    (2)
    (A) Once
    (B) Unless
    (C) Due to
    (D) Whereas

5.  ______ the storm, the delivery team managed to arrive on time.
    (A) Although
    (B) Despite
    (C) Even though
    (D) While

**6.** ___ the merger has been finalized, the two IT teams will begin consolidating their ticketing systems.
(A) Now that (B) In spite of (C) Owing to (D) Regardless

**7.** You may keep the loaner laptop ___ you return it before your contract with the agency ends.
(A) as long as (B) in case of (C) due to (D) aside from

**8.** The vendor shortened its lead time by six days ___ the retailer could restock ahead of the holiday.
(A) so that (B) such as (C) as well as (D) owing to

**9.** Revenue from the online channel doubled last year, ___ foot traffic in physical stores declined again.
(A) while (B) despite (C) therefore (D) because of

**10.** The product launch will proceed on schedule ___ the second supplier misses its shipment deadline.
(A) even if (B) in spite of (C) as though (D) so as to

**11.** ___ the third-party safety inspection was completed, the crew resumed work on the upper deck.
(A) After (B) Despite (C) During (D) Because of

**12.** The system will issue a confirmation number as soon as the payment ___ through the banking network.
(A) will clear (B) clears (C) cleared (D) is clearing

**13.** ___ the capital budget has already received board approval, the department may proceed with procurement.
(A) Given that (B) Owing to (C) Instead of (D) Apart from

**14.** Attendance at the open house was ___ large that the organizers had to open a second exhibition hall.
(A) so (B) such (C) too (D) very

**15.** The operations manual was translated into three languages ___ every regional team could follow one procedure.
(A) in order that (B) in order to (C) so as to (D) such as
