# 07_會議通知與議程

## 📝 答題策略
1.  **未來時間線索**：留意 next Wednesday、before the meeting 等未來時間副詞，判斷空格動詞應為未來式或未來式被動。
2.  **被動語態判斷**：會議、議程、通知等多半是「被安排」「被發送」的對象，先確認主詞是動作執行者還是承受者。
3.  **連接詞/轉折詞題**：判斷前後兩句的邏輯關係（讓步、因果、對比），再選出對應的從屬連接詞。
4.  **句子插入題**：插入句必須同時承接前一句的主題，並自然引出下一句的內容，不能只符合其中一句。
5.  **代名詞指代**：確認空格代名詞所指的對象是單數還是複數、是「事」還是「物」，避免只看字面意思。

## 短文一：會議召開通知

Notice of Quarterly Planning Meeting

The quarterly planning meeting ______(1) in the third-floor conference room next Wednesday at 2 p.m. The purpose of this meeting is to review the second-quarter results and outline priorities for the coming months. ______(2). Attendees who wish to raise additional topics should notify the coordinator no later than Monday morning. ______(3) the meeting is expected to run for only one hour, please keep your afternoon schedule flexible in case the discussion extends further. A finalized agenda ______(4) to all participants by email before the meeting.

### 題目 1
(A) will be held
(B) held
(C) holds
(D) has held

### 題目 2
(A) A tentative agenda has already been drafted by the project leads.
(B) The company's annual report will be released next spring.
(C) Employees are reminded to submit expense reports by Friday.
(D) The main lobby will be closed for renovation this week.

### 題目 3
(A) Although
(B) Because
(C) In spite of
(D) Due to

### 題目 4
(A) will be circulated
(B) circulates
(C) circulated
(D) was circulating

## 短文二：會議議程異動說明

Update on Meeting Agenda Changes

Dear Team,

Please note that the agenda for tomorrow's department meeting ______(5) since it was first circulated last week. Due to a scheduling conflict, the budget review session originally planned for 10 a.m. will now take place after lunch. ______(6). The venue remains the same, but the meeting will now ______(7) until 4 p.m. instead of ending at 3 p.m. If you have any concerns about how ______(8) affects your afternoon workload, please contact the meeting organizer directly.

### 題目 5
(A) has been revised
(B) is revising
(C) revised
(D) will revise

### 題目 6
(A) As a result, the morning session will instead focus on the marketing update presented by Ms. Tanaka.
(B) The company was founded by three engineers two decades ago.
(C) Employees are reminded to renew their parking permits this month.
(D) The cafeteria menu will feature a new set of dishes next week.

### 題目 7
(A) run
(B) runs
(C) running
(D) ran

### 題目 8
(A) this
(B) these
(C) those
(D) that
