# 02_完成式

## 核心概念
完成式主要用來表達「在某個時間點之前，已經完成、持續或有經驗的動作」。
公式：`Have / Has / Had / Will have + p.p. (過去分詞)`

## 1. 現在完成式 (Present Perfect)
*   **公式**：`has / have + p.p.`
*   **用法**：
    1.  **經驗**：從過去到現在曾經做過的事。(常搭配 ever, never, before, once, times)
    2.  **持續**：從過去持續到現在的動作或狀態。(常搭配 for + 一段時間, since + 過去時間點)
    3.  **完成**：剛剛完成或尚未完成的動作。(常搭配 just, already, yet)
*   **多益必考關鍵字**：
    *   `lately` / `recently` (最近)
    *   `over/in/for the past/last few years` (在過去幾年來)
    *   `so far` / `up to now` (到目前為止)

## 2. 過去完成式 (Past Perfect)
*   **公式**：`had + p.p.`
*   **用法**：表達「過去的過去」。當兩個發生在過去的動作，先發生的用過去完成式，後發生的用簡單過去式。
*   **例句**：The train **had left** before I **arrived** at the station. (火車先開走，我才到)
*   **多益考點**：常搭配 `by the time + 過去式子句` (到了...的時候)。
    *   By the time he *arrived*, the meeting *had ended*.

## 3. 未來完成式 (Future Perfect)
*   **公式**：`will have + p.p.`
*   **用法**：表達「在未來的某個特定時間點之前，將會完成的動作」。
*   **多益考點**：常搭配 `by + 未來時間` 或 `by the time + 現在式子句` (代替未來)。
    *   We **will have finished** the project *by next Friday*.
    *   *By the time* you *read* this, I **will have left**.

## 📝 多益秒殺解題技巧
看到 `for + 一段時間`、`since + 過去時間`、`over the past...` ➔ 秒選 **現在完成式**。
看到 `by the time + 過去式` ➔ 秒選 **過去完成式**。
看到 `by + 未來時間` 或 `by the time + 現在式` ➔ 秒選 **未來完成式**。

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **since 子句本身用過去式**：`since + S + 過去式動詞`，主句才用現在完成式。
    *   She has worked here *since* she **graduated** (不是 has graduated) from college.
2.  **have/has been to vs. have/has gone to**：`been to` 表示「去過（已回來）」，`gone to` 表示「去了（還沒回來）」。
    *   He **has been to** Tokyo three times. (去過，人在這)
    *   He **has gone to** Tokyo. (現在人在東京，還沒回來)
3.  **just, already 位置在 have/has 之後**，yet 則放句尾（用於疑問句或否定句）。
    *   We **have** *already* **submitted** the form. / Have you finished the report *yet*?
4.  **現在完成式不可與明確過去時間點並用**：ago, yesterday, last year, in 2020 只能搭配過去簡單式，不可搭配現在完成式。
    *   ❌ I *have finished* it *yesterday*. → ✅ I **finished** it yesterday.
5.  **not until + 過去式，主句用過去完成式**：強調「直到...才」的句型常考過去完成式。
    *   He **had not left** the office until his manager **arrived**.

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

### 現在完成式常用副詞
*   **so far** phr. 到目前為止 | **So far**, the team has completed 80% of the project.
*   **lately** adv. 最近 | Sales have improved **lately**.
*   **yet** adv. （否定/疑問）還（沒） | The shipment has not arrived **yet**.
*   **already** adv. 已經 | We have **already** signed the contract.

### 過去完成式相關
*   **prior to** phr. 在...之前 | **Prior to** the merger, the two firms had operated separately.
*   **beforehand** adv. 事先 | He had prepared the slides **beforehand**.

### 未來完成式相關
*   **by then** phr. 到那時 | **By then**, we will have shipped all the orders.
*   **no later than** phr. 不晚於 | The report must be finished **no later than** Friday.

### 動詞（常考不規則動詞 p.p.）
*   **complete → completed** v. 完成 | The audit has been **completed**.
*   **submit → submitted** v. 提交 | She has **submitted** her resignation.
*   **arise → arisen** v. 出現、發生 | Several issues have **arisen** since the launch.
*   **undergo → undergone** v. 經歷 | The company has **undergone** major restructuring.

## 💪 練習題（5 題）

**第 1 題**
The firm _____ its logo three times since it was founded in 1998.
(A) changes
(B) changed
(C) has changed
(D) will change

**第 2 題**
By the time the inspector arrived, the workers _____ already _____ the faulty wiring.
(A) have / fixed
(B) had / fixed
(C) will have / fixed
(D) has / fixed

**第 3 題**
We _____ the new software by the end of this quarter.
(A) implement
(B) implemented
(C) will have implemented
(D) had implemented

**第 4 題**
Ms. Lopez _____ to Berlin twice this year for client meetings.
(A) has been
(B) has gone
(C) went
(D) is going

**第 5 題（短文填空）**
Since the company (1) _____ its new safety policy last spring, workplace accidents have decreased significantly. By the end of this year, management (2) _____ a full review of the policy's effectiveness.

(1)
(A) introduces
(B) introduced
(C) has introduced
(D) will introduce

(2)
(A) conducts
(B) had conducted
(C) will have conducted
(D) has conducted
