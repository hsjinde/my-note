# 01_綜合練習一

## 📝 答題策略 🌟
篇章閱讀題（Part 7）文章長、題目多，善用以下技巧可提高效率與正確率：
1.  **先看題目再讀文章**：快速瀏覽問題，抓出關鍵字（人名、日期、金額、地點），再回文章中定位答案，避免逐字精讀。
2.  **主旨題看標題與首尾句**：文章的目的通常在開頭或結尾點出，標題（如 Notice, Memo, Advertisement）也透露文體與目的。
3.  **細節題用關鍵字定位**：題目中的專有名詞、數字、日期是最佳搜尋線索，直接回文章對應段落找答案。
4.  **同義詞替換**：選項常將文章原句改寫成同義詞或換句話說，不要只找「一模一樣的字」，要理解語意後比對。
5.  **推論題用消去法**：文章沒有明講的題目，需根據上下文邏輯推斷，先刪除明顯錯誤或文章未提及的選項。
6.  **字義題看上下文**：多益字義題考的是「該字在此句中的意思」，須代入前後文判斷，而非該字的最常見字義。

## 文章：電子郵件

From: customerservice@greenvalleyhotel.com
To: r.thompson@fastmail.com
Subject: Your Reservation Confirmation

Dear Mr. Thompson,

Thank you for choosing Green Valley Hotel for your upcoming stay. This email confirms your reservation for a Deluxe Room from July 15 to July 18. Check-in time begins at 3:00 P.M., and check-out is by 11:00 A.M. As requested, we have arranged a room on a higher floor with a view of the garden.

Please note that our hotel is currently undergoing renovations to the lobby area, which may cause some noise during the day. We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and have arranged for a complimentary breakfast voucher for each day of your stay as a token of appreciation.

If you need to modify or cancel your reservation, please do so at least 48 hours before your check-in date to avoid a cancellation fee. Should you have any special requests, such as an early check-in or airport shuttle service, kindly contact our front desk at least one day in advance.

We look forward to welcoming you and ensuring you have a comfortable stay.

Best regards,
Customer Service Team
Green Valley Hotel
