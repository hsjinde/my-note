# 04_連接詞

## 核心概念
連接詞用來連接單字、片語或子句。多益中最常考的是「對等連接詞」與「從屬連接詞」，特別是表示「相反、因果、條件」等語氣的功能詞。

## 1. 相反的功能詞 (影片重點) 🌟
表示「雖然、儘管、但是」的字詞，是多益必考重點，要注意它們的**詞性**差異！

| 語氣 | 連接詞 (後接 **完整子句 S+V**) | 介系詞 (後接 **名詞/V-ing**) | 副詞 (單獨使用，常配合逗號) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **相反/讓步** (雖然/儘管) | **although, even though, though**<br>*Although* it rained, we went out. | **despite, in spite of**<br>*Despite* the rain, we went out. | **however, nevertheless**<br>It rained; *however*, we went out. |
| **因果** (因為/由於) | **because, since, as** | **because of, due to, owing to** | **therefore, as a result** |

## 2. 對等連接詞 (FANBOYS)
用來連接對稱的結構 (名詞連名詞，子句連子句)。
*   **F**or (因為), **A**nd (和), **N**or (也不), **B**ut (但是), **O**r (或), **Y**et (然而), **S**o (所以)

## 3. 相關連接詞 (成雙成對)
考題常考固定搭配，看到前面就要選後面。
*   **both** A **and** B (兩者皆)
*   **either** A **or** B (兩者之一)
*   **neither** A **nor** B (兩者皆非)
*   **not only** A **but (also)** B (不只 A 還有 B)

## 📝 多益秒殺解題技巧
1.  **判斷空格後方的結構**：
    *   如果空格後是**完整的句子 (有主詞+動詞)** ➔ 選 **連接詞** (Although, Because)。
    *   如果空格後是**名詞詞組 / V-ing (沒有動詞)** ➔ 選 **介系詞** (Despite, Due to)。
2.  **陷阱題**：選項同時出現 `Although` 和 `Despite`，直接看後面有沒有動詞就能秒殺！

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **while 的雙重身分**：`while` 可表「當...時候」(時間) 或「然而、對比」(讓步/對比)，後面固定接**完整子句**，判斷語意即可。
2.  **so that / in order that vs. so...that**：`so that`(以便，目的) 與 `so + adj/adv + that`(如此...以致於，結果) 外型相似但用法不同，注意空格前後結構。
3.  **whether vs. if**：兩者都可表示「是否」，但 `whether...or not` 才是正確搭配，`if...or not` 為常見錯誤用法。
4.  **連接詞 + 完整句 vs. 介系詞 + 名詞** 是所有連接詞題的核心判斷依據：先看空格後面「有沒有主詞+動詞」，再決定選連接詞還是介系詞。
5.  **not only...but also 的插入語序**：當 `not only` 放句首時，主詞和動詞需**倒裝**，如 `Not only did sales increase, but also profits rose.`

## 🔤 相關單字和片語
### 從屬連接詞
*   **although / even though** 連接詞 雖然、儘管 | *Although* the budget was limited, the team completed the project.
*   **because / since / as** 連接詞 因為 | *Since* the client was unavailable, the meeting was postponed.
*   **while** 連接詞 然而／當...時 | *While* sales grew in Asia, they declined in Europe.
*   **unless** 連接詞 除非 | *Unless* you register early, you may not get a seat.
*   **once** 連接詞 一旦 | *Once* the contract is signed, the project can begin.

### 介系詞（易混淆對照）
*   **despite / in spite of** 介系詞 儘管 | *Despite* the rain, the event proceeded as planned.
*   **because of / due to / owing to** 介系詞 因為 | The flight was delayed *due to* bad weather.

### 對等與相關連接詞
*   **not only...but also** 片語 不僅...而且 | The plan is *not only* cost-effective *but also* efficient.
*   **either...or** 片語 兩者之一 | You can *either* call us *or* send an email.
*   **as a result** 副詞片語 因此、結果 | Sales dropped; *as a result*, the company cut costs.

## 💪 練習題（15 題）

**1.** ___ the manager was on vacation, the team continued to meet the deadline.
(A) Despite (B) Although (C) Because of (D) Due to

**2.** The company will proceed with the merger ___ the shareholders approve the proposal.
(A) unless (B) despite (C) because of (D) in spite of

**3.** ___ increasing competition, the firm managed to maintain its market share.
(A) Although (B) Despite (C) Even though (D) While

**4.** Employees may choose ___ to work remotely or in the office on Fridays.
(A) either (B) both (C) neither (D) not only

**5.** ___ the new software reduces processing time, it also improves data accuracy.（第一空）As a result, the company decided to adopt it company-wide, ___ the initial training cost.（第二空）
第一空：(A) Despite (B) Not only (C) Because (D) While
第二空：(A) although (B) despite (C) because (D) so that

**6.** The outdoor awards ceremony was moved indoors ___ the sudden thunderstorm that afternoon.
(A) because (B) because of (C) even though (D) whereas

**7.** The prototype passed every safety inspection, ___ the launch was postponed for an additional review.
(A) so (B) for (C) yet (D) nor

**8.** The renovation will not only reduce annual energy costs ___ also improve overall employee comfort.
(A) and (B) but (C) or (D) nor

**9.** The mandatory training program covers ___ workplace safety and personal data protection.
(A) both (B) either (C) neither (D) whether

**10.** Shipping delays affected three regions; ___, the product launch date was pushed back by two weeks.
(A) despite (B) although (C) therefore (D) whereas

**11.** Neither the site supervisor ___ the technicians were informed of the revised inspection schedule.
(A) or (B) nor (C) and (D) but

**12.** The main office will remain closed on Monday ___ scheduled maintenance of the ventilation system.
(A) due to (B) because (C) since (D) so that

**13.** ___ the vendor agreed to lower its unit price, the procurement team still selected a different supplier.
(A) In spite of (B) Even though (C) Despite (D) Regardless of

**14.** The agenda was circulated a full week in advance ___ every participant could prepare questions.
(A) so that (B) in order to (C) such as (D) as well as

**15.** Sales in the northern region rose by twelve percent, ___ southern sales remained flat for the same period.
(A) whereas (B) despite (C) moreover (D) therefore
