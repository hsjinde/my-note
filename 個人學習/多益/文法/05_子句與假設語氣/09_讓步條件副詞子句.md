# 09_讓步條件副詞子句

## 核心概念
讓步副詞子句（雖然、儘管）與條件副詞子句（如果、除非、只要）在多益文法題中經常混出，因為兩者都描述「一種情況成立/不成立時，主句結果會不會改變」。讓步子句強調「即使某條件成立，結果依然不變」；條件子句強調「結果的成立取決於某條件」。本章聚焦 [[03_副詞子句]] 未深入處理的細部辨析：`even if` vs. `even though`、`unless` vs. `in case` vs. `only if`、`no matter + wh-` 系列、`granted (that)` / `on condition that` 等正式條件片語、以及 `notwithstanding` / `albeit` 等書面轉折詞。

## 1. 讓步 vs. 條件的本質差異
- **讓步（Concession）**：承認某情況為真或可能為真，但結果不受影響。核心詞：although, even though, even if, no matter how/what/who, granted (that)。
- **條件（Condition）**：結果的發生與否，取決於該條件是否成立。核心詞：if, unless, provided/providing (that), in case, only if, on condition that。

> [!tip] 判斷技巧
> 讓步子句的中文通常可以代換成「不管...都」；條件子句的中文則是「只有...才」或「要是...就」。

## 2. even if vs. even though 深度辨析
| 詞 | 語意基礎 | 用法 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **even if** | 尚未發生的假設 | 強調「就算發生了那件事」，結果依然不變，帶有條件句的不確定性 |
| **even though** | 已經是事實 | 強調「雖然事實如此」，結果依然不變，帶有讓步句的確定性 |

- **Even if** the client cancels the order, the factory will keep production running. (尚未取消，只是假設)
- **Even though** the client cancelled the order, the factory kept production running. (已經取消，是事實)

## 3. unless / in case / only if 三者比較
- **unless**（除非）= if...not，語意上等同否定條件，子句本身不再加 not。
  - **Unless** the invoice is corrected, the payment will not be processed.
- **in case**（以防萬一）：描述一種預防性措施，主句動作是為了應付子句可能發生的情況，語意上不等於 if。
  - Keep a backup generator **in case** the power supply fails.
- **only if**（只有...才）：把條件的唯一性提到最前面，常放句首並倒裝主句助動詞。
  - **Only if** all signatures are collected will the contract be considered valid.

> [!warning] in case 不是 if
> `in case it rains, bring an umbrella` 意思是「以防下雨，先帶傘」（不論下不下雨都先準備），不是「如果下雨才帶傘」。若要表達「如果下雨」，應用 `if it rains`。

## 4. no matter + 疑問詞／whatever 系列
`no matter how/what/who/when/where` 引導讓步子句，等於 `however/whatever/whoever/whenever/wherever`，語意為「不管...」，常考與 whatever 系列的替換。
- **No matter how** busy the manager is, she always replies to client emails within a day.
  - = **However** busy the manager is, ...
- **No matter what** the outcome is, the team will present the findings honestly.
  - = **Whatever** the outcome is, ...
- **No matter who** signs the contract, the terms remain the same.
  - = **Whoever** signs the contract, ...

## 5. 正式條件片語：granted (that) / on condition that / provided (that)
- **granted (that)**（姑且承認、就算）：帶讓步意味的條件詞，常用於書面辯論、報告語氣。
  - **Granted that** the new system is expensive, it will save labor costs in the long run.
- **on condition that**（在...條件下）：比 `provided that` 更強調「附帶條件」，常見於合約、協議語境。
  - The bank approved the loan **on condition that** the company submit quarterly financial statements.
- **provided (that) / providing (that)**：語意等同 `if`，但語氣更正式、條件更明確，常見於規章條款。

## 6. 書面轉折／讓步副詞：albeit / notwithstanding / nevertheless / nonetheless
這些詞常出現在 Part 5/6 的正式商業文書中，需與連接詞、介系詞區分詞性：
- **albeit**（雖然，接形容詞/分詞片語，非完整子句）：The proposal was approved, **albeit** reluctantly.
- **notwithstanding**（介系詞，儘管，可放名詞前或後）：**Notwithstanding** the delay, the shipment arrived intact. / The delay **notwithstanding**, the shipment arrived intact.
- **nevertheless / nonetheless**（副詞，然而、儘管如此，銜接兩個獨立句子，前面通常用分號或句號）：The budget was cut significantly; **nevertheless**, the project met its deadline.

## 7. lest 與 for fear that（否定目的／預防）
- **lest**（以免，之後子句用 should + 原形動詞，或直接用原形動詞，正式英文）：Double-check the figures **lest** the report **contain** errors.
- **for fear that**（因為擔心）：She backed up all files **for fear that** the server **might** crash.

## 📝 多益秒殺解題技巧
1. 看到空格後面是「完整子句 (S+V)」→ 優先考慮連接詞（even if, even though, unless, granted that...）；後面是「名詞/動名詞」→ 考慮介系詞（notwithstanding, despite）。
2. `even if` 帶有「尚未發生的假設」語感，`even though` 帶有「已發生的事實」語感 —— 看子句時態與上下文事實與否來判斷。
3. `unless` 後面絕對不能再加 not（雙重否定）。
4. `no matter how` 後面直接接形容詞/副詞，不能接完整名詞片語；`no matter what` 後面才能接完整名詞。
5. `only if` 放句首時，主句要倒裝（助動詞提前）。
6. `albeit` 後面接的是形容詞、分詞或片語，不是完整子句；`notwithstanding` 是介系詞，後面接名詞。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

### 讓步／條件連接詞與片語
*   **granted (that)** (conj.) 姑且承認、就算 | **Granted that** the plan is risky, the potential return justifies it.
*   **on condition that** (片語連接詞) 在...條件下 | The supplier agreed **on condition that** payment be made in advance.
*   **only if** (片語連接詞) 只有...才 | **Only if** the budget is approved can the project begin.
*   **no matter how** (片語連接詞) 不管多麼 | **No matter how** tight the schedule is, quality must not suffer.
*   **no matter what** (片語連接詞) 不管什麼 | **No matter what** happens, the deadline stays the same.
*   **no matter who** (片語連接詞) 不管是誰 | **No matter who** reviews the file, the conclusion will be identical.
*   **suppose / supposing (that)** (conj.) 假設、萬一 | **Supposing that** the deal falls through, we need a backup plan.
*   **even so** (adv.) 即使如此 | The audit was thorough; **even so**, minor errors were missed.
*   **whether or not** (片語連接詞) 不論是否 | The event will proceed **whether or not** it rains.
*   **for fear that** (conj.) 因為擔心 | The manager sent a reminder **for fear that** staff **might miss** the deadline.
*   **in any case** (片語) 無論如何 | **In any case**, the report must be submitted by Friday.
*   **at all costs** (片語) 不惜代價 | The firm must protect client data **at all costs**.

### 相關名詞：條款、備案與例外
*   **precaution** (n.) 預防措施 | Employees are asked to take **precautions** against data leaks.
*   **stipulation** (n.) 規定、明文條件 | The contract's **stipulation** requires monthly progress reports.
*   **loophole** (n.) 漏洞 | Lawyers identified a **loophole** in the agreement.
*   **exemption** (n.) 豁免 | Small businesses may qualify for a tax **exemption**.
*   **override** (v. 及物) 推翻、優先於 | A signed contract will **override** any verbal agreement.
*   **fallback** (n.) 備用方案 | The team kept a **fallback** supplier ready.

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **even if vs. even though**：`even if` = 尚未發生的假設；`even though` = 已發生的事實。看時態與上下文判斷何者已成立。
2.  **unless 不加 not**：`unless` 本身已含否定，句意「除非」= `if...not`，子句內不可再出現 not。
3.  **in case ≠ if**：`in case` 表示「預先防範」，主句動作先於子句情況發生；`if` 才是單純條件。
4.  **only if 倒裝**：`Only if + 子句, 主句助動詞 + 主詞...`，放句首時主句需部分倒裝。
5.  **no matter + wh- 詞可與 -ever 系列互換**：no matter how = however；no matter what = whatever；no matter who = whoever。
6.  **granted (that) 語氣**：帶有「姑且承認」的味道，常見於正式報告開頭承認對方論點，再轉折反駁。
7.  **albeit 詞性**：`albeit` 後面接形容詞、分詞或介系詞片語，不能直接接完整子句，是連接詞中的特例。
8.  **notwithstanding 位置彈性**：可以放在名詞片語前（Notwithstanding the risk...）或後（The risk notwithstanding, ...），是唯一可後置的介系詞之一。
9.  **nevertheless / nonetheless 是副詞不是連接詞**：不能直接連接兩個子句，前面須用句號或分號斷開，或用 and 連接。
10. **lest 後面接原形動詞或 should + 原形**：屬於較古典的正式用法，多見於書面警告語句。
11. **on condition that vs. provided that**：語意接近，但 `on condition that` 更強調契約式的附帶條件，`provided that` 較廣泛適用於一般規則說明。
12. **for fear that 與 lest 語意相近**：兩者都表達「為了避免...」，`for fear that` 後接的子句常用 would/might，`lest` 則接原形或 should。

## 💪 練習題（15 題）

**1.** ______ the merger takes longer than expected, the two firms have already begun integrating their IT systems.
(A) Even though (B) Even if (C) In case of (D) Unless

**2.** The vendor will not release the shipment ______ full payment has been confirmed by the finance office.
(A) whereas (B) granted that (C) even though (D) unless

**3.** The logistics team keeps an extra truck on standby ______ one of the delivery vehicles breaks down.
(A) only if (B) in case (C) unless (D) if

**4.** ______ will the board approve the acquisition can the two companies begin merging their operations.
(A) Even if (B) In case (C) Only if (D) Whether

**5.** ______ how complicated the negotiation becomes, the legal team must finalize the contract by Friday.
(A) Whereas (B) No matter (C) Although (D) Even

**6.** ______ the proposal involves considerable risk, the investment committee decided to move forward with it.
(A) Granted that (B) In case (C) Only if (D) Unless

**7.** The supplier agreed to lower its prices ______ the retailer place orders exclusively through the new online portal.
(A) notwithstanding (B) on condition that (C) albeit (D) lest

**8.** The audit uncovered several irregularities, ______ minor ones that did not affect the final figures.
(A) unless (B) granted that (C) albeit (D) in case

**9.** ______ the objections raised by two board members, the merger agreement was signed as scheduled.
(A) Even though (B) Unless (C) Provided (D) Notwithstanding

**10.** Revenue fell sharply during the recession; ______, the company managed to avoid any layoffs.
(A) unless (B) nevertheless (C) granted that (D) in case

**11.** The training manual will be revised ______ what feedback the pilot group provides after the workshop.
(A) even if (B) unless (C) no matter (D) provided that

**12.** The IT department scheduled the system upgrade for midnight ______ minimize disruption to daytime operations.
(A) for fear that (B) lest (C) in order to (D) notwithstanding

**13.** The safety officer double-checked every valve ______ a leak occur during the overnight shift.
(A) even though (B) granted that (C) whereas (D) lest

**14.** The client accepted the revised timeline, ______ she remained concerned about the added cost. ______ the concerns, she signed the amended contract the same afternoon.
第一空：(A) unless (B) granted that (C) although (D) whether
第二空：(A) Unless (B) Notwithstanding (C) Only if (D) Even if

**15.** The contract remains valid ______ or not either party formally requests a renegotiation before the renewal date.
(A) unless (B) whether (C) granted (D) provided
