# 03_電子郵件與備忘錄組合

## 📝 答題策略
1.  **辨識文體與寄件雙方關係**：先確認這是電子郵件還是備忘錄、寄件人與收件人的身份與立場，有助於判斷文章語氣與主旨方向。
2.  **主旨題看開頭段落**：商業郵件與備忘錄通常在第一段就點出「為何寫這封信／備忘錄」，先讀第一段再決定要不要細讀全文。
3.  **細節題鎖定專有名詞與數字**：訂單編號、金額、日期、期限都是細節題常考的線索，直接回文章對應位置核對。
4.  **推論題留意「暗示但未明說」的資訊**：正確答案必須是文章合理延伸的結論，而非文章直接寫出的句子，也不能超出文章範圍過度推論。
5.  **字義題代入上下文**：先找出該字所在的句子，再判斷其在此語境下的意思，而非直接套用該字最常見的字典釋義。
6.  **備忘錄類文章留意行動要求**：備忘錄常在結尾提出具體要求（如「請於某日前完成」），這類句子常是細節題或推論題的答案來源。

## 文章：電子郵件

From: j.nakamura@brightsteel-supply.com
To: purchasing@harborline-manufacturing.com
Subject: Delay in Shipment Order #4471

Dear Ms. Carter,

I am writing to inform you that Order #4471, the batch of steel components scheduled to arrive at your facility this Thursday, will be delayed by approximately five business days due to a mechanical breakdown at our shipping port. We understand that this delay may affect your production schedule, and we sincerely apologize for the inconvenience.

To minimize the impact on your operations, we have arranged for the remaining stock in our regional warehouse to be shipped separately by air freight at no extra cost to your company. This partial shipment should arrive within two business days and should cover roughly 40% of your original order, allowing your assembly line to continue running while the rest of the shipment is in transit.

We also noticed a discrepancy between the invoice total and the quantity listed on the packing slip for your last order. Our accounting team is currently reviewing the matter and will send a corrected invoice by the end of this week.

Please let us know if the partial shipment arrangement works for your production needs, or if you would prefer an alternative solution.

Sincerely,
Jun Nakamura
Brightsteel Supply Co.

### 題目 1（主旨題）
What is the main purpose of this email?

(A) To request an extension on a payment deadline
(B) To notify a client about a shipment delay and proposed solution
(C) To cancel an existing order due to a shortage of materials
(D) To announce a new pricing policy for future orders

### 題目 2（細節題）
What has Brightsteel Supply Co. arranged to reduce the impact of the delay?

(A) A full refund for the delayed order
(B) A discount on the client's next order
(C) A partial shipment sent by air freight at no extra cost
(D) An extended warranty on the steel components

### 題目 3（推論題）
What can be inferred about the invoice discrepancy mentioned in the email?

(A) It was caused by an error in Ms. Carter's payment.
(B) It is currently being investigated by Brightsteel's accounting team.
(C) It has already been resolved with a refund.
(D) It will not affect the current shipment in any way.

### 題目 4（字義題）
The word "discrepancy" in paragraph 3, line 1, is closest in meaning to

(A) agreement
(B) inconsistency
(C) improvement
(D) delivery

## 文章：內部備忘錄

TO: All Department Staff
FROM: Finance Department
DATE: March 3
RE: Updated Expense Reimbursement Procedure

Starting next Monday, all reimbursement requests for business-related expenses must be submitted through the new online reimbursement portal instead of the paper forms previously used. This change is intended to reduce processing errors and shorten the average waiting time for reimbursement, which has recently grown due to a backlog of paper submissions.

Employees must attach a clear photo or scanned copy of the original receipt to each request; requests submitted without a receipt will be automatically rejected by the system. In addition, any expense exceeding NT$3,000 will require prior approval from a department supervisor before submission, and the approval must be uploaded together with the receipt.

The Finance Department will continue accepting paper forms submitted before this Friday, but any request received afterward must go through the new portal. A short training session on how to use the portal will be held in the main conference room this Thursday at 2:00 P.M.; attendance is optional but strongly encouraged for staff who frequently submit reimbursement requests.

We appreciate your cooperation in helping us streamline this process.

### 題目 5（主旨題）
What is the main purpose of this memo?

(A) To announce a reduction in the annual travel budget
(B) To introduce a new procedure for submitting reimbursement requests
(C) To explain why a recent reimbursement was rejected
(D) To request feedback on the current expense policy

### 題目 6（細節題）
According to the memo, what will happen to a reimbursement request submitted without a receipt?

(A) It will be processed at a reduced amount.
(B) It will be automatically rejected by the system.
(C) It will be forwarded to a supervisor for review.
(D) It will be delayed until the next processing cycle.

### 題目 7（推論題）
What is suggested about the paper reimbursement forms?

(A) They will no longer be accepted under any circumstances.
(B) They caused a backlog that slowed down processing time.
(C) They will still be required in addition to the online portal.
(D) They were discontinued due to a shortage of paper supplies.

### 題目 8（字義題）
The word "streamline" in the last sentence is closest in meaning to

(A) complicate
(B) simplify
(C) delay
(D) duplicate
