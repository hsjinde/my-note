# 05_動狀詞補充句型

## 核心概念
除了不定詞、動名詞、分詞的基本用法外，多益還會考「動名詞的邏輯主詞」「獨立分詞構句」「完成式動狀詞」等進階句型。這些句型雖然出現頻率較低，但屬於高分關鍵題型，常讓中階考生誤判。

## 1. 動名詞的邏輯主詞 (Possessive + V-ing) 🌟
動名詞本身沒有人稱變化，但若要指出「誰」做這個動作，會在動名詞前面加上**所有格**（正式）或**受格**（口語）。
*   **正式（多益書信體常用）**：We appreciate **your** *attending* the workshop.
*   **口語**：We appreciate **you** *attending* the workshop.
*   **注意**：多益單選題若同時出現所有格與受格選項，兩者文法上都可接受，但正式書面文體優先選**所有格**。

## 2. 獨立分詞構句 (with + O + 分詞) 🌟
`with` 引導的分詞片語，用來補充說明「伴隨的狀態」或「背景描述」，常見於描述辦公室情境或會議場景。
*   受詞後方接的動作若為受詞**主動**發出 ➔ 用 **V-ing**
    *   She walked into the room **with** her assistant *carrying* the documents.
*   受詞後方接的動作若受詞是**被動**承受 ➔ 用 **p.p.**
    *   He finished the report **with** all the data *verified*.

## 3. 完成式動狀詞 (Having p.p. / to have p.p.) 🌟
表示動作發生在**主要動詞之前**，強調時間先後順序，是分詞構句與不定詞的進階變化形式。

| 形式 | 用法 | 範例 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Having p.p.** | 完成式分詞構句，動作先於主句 | **Having reviewed** the contract, the lawyer approved it. |
| **to have p.p.** | 完成式不定詞，表示「之前已經...」 | She claims **to have finished** the project ahead of schedule. |

*   **Having submitted** the application, he waited for the response. (先繳交，才等待)
*   The manager seems **to have made** a wrong decision. (似乎「已經」做錯了決定)

## 4. 動名詞的被動與完成式 (being p.p. / having been p.p.)
動名詞也有被動與完成式的變化，多益閱讀題中偶爾出現。
*   **being p.p.（被動）**：He complained about **being ignored** during the meeting.
*   **having been p.p.（完成被動）**：**Having been informed** of the change, she adjusted her schedule accordingly.

## 📝 多益秒殺解題技巧
1.  看到 `所有格/受格 + V-ing` 作受詞 ➔ 這是動名詞的邏輯主詞，判斷句子仍以 **V-ing** 為核心，不要誤選其他詞性。
2.  看到 `with + 受詞 + ___` ➔ 判斷受詞是「主動」還是「被動」，分別選 **V-ing** 或 **p.p.**。
3.  看到分詞構句或不定詞想表達「動作發生在主要動詞之前」➔ 秒選 **having p.p.** 或 **to have p.p.**。
4.  空格前是介系詞 (about, for, of) 且句意牽涉被動 ➔ 考慮 **being p.p.** 這個動名詞被動形式。

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **claim / seem / appear + to have p.p.**：這類動詞表達「宣稱／似乎」已經完成的事，是多益閱讀常見句型，勿與一般不定詞混淆。
2.  **regret + V-ing vs. regret + to V**：`regret doing`（後悔做了...，事情已發生）與 `regret to say/inform`（很遺憾要告知...，正式用語）意義不同，考題常以此設陷阱。
    *   We **regret to inform** you that your application was not successful.
3.  **without + V-ing**：`without` 是介系詞，後方一定接 **V-ing**，表示「沒有做某事的情況下」。
    *   He left the office **without** *notifying* his supervisor.
4.  **worth 系列進階**：`be worthy of + V-ing/N` 與 `be worth + V-ing` 意思相近但文法結構不同，worthy 後面需要介系詞 of。
5.  **分詞構句的否定形式**：否定詞 `not` 放在分詞**前面**。
    *   **Not knowing** the answer, she remained silent.

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

### 完成式動狀詞常見動詞
- **claim** 及物動詞 宣稱 | He **claims to have completed** the certification last year.
- **seem** 不及物動詞 似乎 | The data **seems to have been** miscalculated.
- **regret** 及物動詞 後悔；遺憾 | We **regret to inform** you of the delay.
- **acknowledge** 及物動詞 承認 | She **acknowledged having made** an error in the report.

### with 獨立分詞構句常見搭配
- **carry** 及物動詞 攜帶 | He entered **with** a folder **carrying** important documents.
- **verify** 及物動詞 核實 | The invoice was approved **with** all figures **verified**.
- **attach** 及物動詞 附加 | She sent the email **with** a file **attached**.

### 其他重要片語
- **without + V-ing** 片語 沒有做...的情況下 | You cannot enter the lab **without** *wearing* proper gear.
- **be worthy of + V-ing/N** 片語 值得... | This achievement **is worthy of** *recognition*.
- **being p.p.** 動名詞被動 被... | She disliked **being interrupted** during presentations.

We greatly appreciate ___ willingness to work overtime during the busy season.
(A) you
(B) your
(C) yourself
(D) yours

**題目 2**
The director walked into the boardroom with the annual report ___ under his arm.
(A) tuck
(B) tucking
(C) tucked
(D) to tuck

**題目 3**
___ the necessary approvals, the project manager proceeded with the construction plan.
(A) Obtain
(B) Obtaining
(C) Having obtained
(D) To obtain

**題目 4**
The CEO seems ___ the merger agreement without consulting the board of directors.
(A) sign
(B) signing
(C) to have signed
(D) signed

To: All Department Heads

We regret (1) ___ you that the annual budget meeting originally scheduled for Friday has been postponed. (2) ___ the current review of departmental expenditures, we believe a short delay will allow for more accurate reporting.

(1)
(A) inform
(B) informing
(C) to inform
(D) informed

(2)
(A) Consider
(B) Considering
(C) Considered
(D) To consider
