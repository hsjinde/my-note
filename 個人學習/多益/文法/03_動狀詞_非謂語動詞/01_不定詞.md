# 01_不定詞

## 核心概念
不定詞 (Infinitive) 的形式為 **`to + 原形動詞`**，在句子中可作名詞、形容詞或副詞使用。多益常考「接不定詞還是動名詞」的辨別，以及「不定詞作目的/結果的副詞用法」。

## 1. 不定詞的三種功能

### A. 作名詞 (主詞 / 受詞 / 補語)
*   **作主詞**：To learn English takes time. (但較常用動名詞作主詞)
*   **作受詞**：I decided **to postpone** the meeting.
*   **作補語**：Our goal is **to improve** customer satisfaction.

### B. 作形容詞 (修飾名詞)
放在名詞後方，說明名詞的用途或內容。
*   He is the first employee **to arrive** every day.
*   We need a plan **to address** these issues.

### C. 作副詞 (表目的 / 結果)
*   **表目的** (為了)：She went to the store **to buy** supplies.
*   **in order to / so as to**：更正式的「為了」，多益書信文體常用。
    *   We updated the system **in order to** *improve* performance.

## 2. 必須接不定詞的動詞 🌟
看到這些動詞，後面接的動詞一定是 **to V**：
*   **意志/計畫類**：want, wish, hope, plan, intend, decide, choose
*   **嘗試/承諾類**：agree, promise, offer, refuse, manage
*   **其他**：expect, need, fail, tend, afford

| 動詞 | 範例 |
| :--- | :--- |
| **want** | I want **to attend** the seminar. |
| **plan** | We plan **to expand** into new markets. |
| **manage** | She managed **to finish** the project on time. |
| **fail** | He failed **to submit** the report. |
| **afford** | We cannot afford **to lose** this client. |

## 3. 不定詞 vs. 動名詞：語意改變的動詞 🌟

| 動詞 | to V (未來/刻意) | V-ing (過去/一般) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **remember** | remember *to do* (記得去做) | remember *doing* (記得曾做過) |
| **forget** | forget *to do* (忘了去做) | forget *doing* (忘了曾做過) |
| **stop** | stop *to do* (停下來去做) | stop *doing* (停止做...) |
| **try** | try *to do* (試圖做) | try *doing* (試試看做) |

*   Please **remember to** *submit* the form by Friday. (記得去交)
*   I **remember** *meeting* her at the conference. (記得曾見過她)

## 4. 不定詞的省略 (bare infinitive)
在使役動詞 (make, have, let) 和感官動詞 (see, hear, watch) 後，不定詞的 **to** 要去掉，直接用**原形動詞**。
*   Let me *know* your decision. (不是 to know)
*   I saw him *leave* the office. (不是 to leave)

## 📝 多益秒殺解題技巧
1.  看到 `want, plan, decide, manage, fail` 後面的空格 ➔ 秒選 **to + V**。
2.  看到 `in order to` 後面的空格 ➔ 秒選**原形動詞 V**。
3.  看到 `remember / forget + ___`：要判斷語意是「未來的行動」(to V) 還是「過去的記憶」(V-ing)。
4.  看到 `make / let / have / see / hear + 受詞 + ___` ➔ 秒選**原形動詞 V** (省略 to)。

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **too...to / enough to**：`too + adj/adv + to V` (太...而不能) 與 `adj/adv + enough to V` (足夠...而能) ➔ 空格後方一律接**原形動詞**。
    *   The contract is **too complicated to** *sign* today.
    *   She is **experienced enough to** *lead* the team.
2.  **be + adj + to V** 固定搭配：`be likely/able/willing/eager/reluctant to V` ➔ 看到這些形容詞，後面秒選 **to + 原形動詞**。
3.  **it is + adj + (for/of sb) + to V** 假主詞句型：真正主詞是後面的不定詞片語。
    *   **It** is important **to** *submit* the form on time.
4.  **疑問詞 + to V**：`how/what/when/where + to V` 可作受詞，常考選擇題陷阱（誤選疑問詞 + 子句）。
    *   Please let me know **how to** *access* the shared folder.
5.  **陷阱**：`be supposed to V`（應該）、`be scheduled to V`（預計）是多益書信/公告中高頻搭配，後方也一定接原形動詞，不要被中間的名詞片語混淆。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

### 動詞（後接不定詞）
- **intend** 及物動詞 打算 | We **intend to** expand our operations next year.
- **refuse** 及物動詞 拒絕 | He **refused to** sign the agreement.
- **afford** 及物動詞（常與 can/cannot 連用） 負擔得起 | We cannot **afford to** delay the shipment.
- **hesitate** 不及物動詞 猶豫 | Please do not **hesitate to** contact us.
- **strive** 不及物動詞 努力 | The company **strives to** improve customer service.

### 形容詞（+ to V 固定搭配）
- **eager** 形容詞 渴望的 | She is **eager to** learn new skills.
- **reluctant** 形容詞 不情願的 | He was **reluctant to** accept the offer.
- **eligible** 形容詞 有資格的 | Employees are **eligible to** apply for the program.
- **likely** 形容詞 可能的 | The price is **likely to** increase next month.

### 片語與句型
- **in order to** 片語 為了 | We hired more staff **in order to** meet the deadline.
- **so as to** 片語 為了（較正式） | The manager arrived early **so as to** prepare the meeting room.
- **be supposed to V** 片語 應該 | The report **is supposed to** be finished by Friday.
- **be scheduled to V** 片語 預計 | The flight **is scheduled to** depart at noon.
- **too...to V** 句型 太...以致於不能 | The box is **too heavy to** carry alone.

**題目 1**
The board decided ___ the merger proposal until further review.
(A) postpone
(B) postponing
(C) to postpone
(D) postponed

**題目 2**
The new intern is eager ___ from the senior consultants during the training program.
(A) learn
(B) learning
(C) to learn
(D) learned

**題目 3**
The shipment was too damaged ___ to customers as originally planned.
(A) send
(B) sending
(C) to send
(D) sent

**題目 4**
Please remember ___ the client before the conference call begins tomorrow morning.
(A) to notify
(B) notifying
(C) notify
(D) notified

**題目 5（短文填空）**
To all staff,

We are pleased to announce that the new employee handbook is now available on the intranet. All employees are required (1) ___ the handbook and acknowledge receipt by the end of this week. In order (2) ___ any confusion, please direct all questions to the HR department.

(1)
(A) review
(B) reviewing
(C) to review
(D) reviewed

(2)
(A) avoid
(B) avoiding
(C) to avoid
(D) avoided
