# 07_請假單與內部信件組合

## 📝 答題策略
1.  **表單類文章先看欄位結構**：請假單、申請單常以「欄位：內容」的格式呈現，先掃過所有欄位標題，再回頭找題目對應的欄位。
2.  **回覆信件留意「對照原信」的線索**：主管的回覆信通常會逐項回應申請單中的請求，答題時需比對兩篇文章的對應段落，而非只看單一篇文章。
3.  **主旨題看信件開頭與結尾**：回覆信的第一句通常說明「針對哪件事回覆」，結尾則常帶出後續動作或提醒事項。
4.  **細節題鎖定條件與例外**：核准、部分核准、附帶條件（如「須先完成交接」）是細節題最常考的內容，須逐字核對。
5.  **推論題找「兩篇文章之間」的合理連結**：正確答案往往需要同時參考申請單與回覆信才能推得，單看一篇文章可能不足以判斷。
6.  **字義題代入句子語境**：先定位該字所在句子，再依上下文選出最貼近的意思，避免直接套用字典最常見釋義。

## 文章：請假暨加班申請單

Employee Leave and Overtime Request Form

Name: Daniel Osei
Department: Logistics
Date Submitted: October 6

Type of Request: Leave of Absence
Requested Dates: October 20 – October 22 (3 days)
Reason: I need to travel to my hometown to assist with a family matter that requires my presence in person. I have already arranged for my colleague, Priya Nandan, to cover urgent shipment tracking tasks during my absence, and I have briefed her on the current status of all open orders.

Additional Note: Given the upcoming absence, I would like to request permission to work two extra hours each day from October 16 to October 19 in order to finish reviewing the November shipment contracts before I leave. I understand that overtime must be pre-approved and am submitting this request accordingly.

Signature: Daniel Osei

### 題目 1（主旨題）
What is the main purpose of this form?

(A) To report a workplace accident involving a colleague
(B) To request time off and pre-approval for overtime work
(C) To formally resign from a logistics position
(D) To complain about an unfair overtime policy

### 題目 2（細節題）
What has Daniel Osei already done to prepare for his absence?

(A) He canceled all pending shipment orders.
(B) He briefed a colleague on the status of open orders.
(C) He hired a temporary replacement.
(D) He postponed the November shipment contracts.

### 題目 3（推論題）
Why does Daniel Osei want to work extra hours before October 20?

(A) To make up for hours missed during a previous absence
(B) To train Priya Nandan on a new tracking system
(C) To finish reviewing contracts before he leaves
(D) To qualify for an overtime bonus this month

### 題目 4（字義題）

(A) shortened

## 文章：主管回覆信件

To: Daniel Osei
From: Monica Alvarez, Logistics Supervisor
Subject: Re: Your Leave and Overtime Request

Dear Daniel,

Thank you for submitting your request in advance. I have reviewed your form and I am pleased to approve your leave from October 20 to October 22. It is reassuring to hear that Priya has already been briefed on the open orders; please make sure she also has access to the shared tracking spreadsheet before you leave.

Regarding the overtime request, I can approve two extra hours on October 16, 17, and 19, but October 18 will not be possible, as the office will be closed that evening for scheduled system maintenance. If the contract review cannot be completed within the approved hours, please let me know by October 14 so that we can arrange for someone to assist you.

One more thing: since your leave overlaps with the department's monthly report deadline, please submit your section of the report no later than October 19, before your extra hours end that day.

Thank you again for planning ahead. Enjoy your time with your family.

Best regards,
Monica Alvarez

### 題目 5（主旨題）
What is the main purpose of this email?

(A) To deny Daniel's leave request entirely
(B) To respond to Daniel's request with approval and additional instructions
(C) To reschedule the department's system maintenance
(D) To ask Daniel to postpone his family visit

### 題目 6（細節題）
Why can Daniel not work overtime on October 18?

(A) He will already be on leave that day.
(B) The office will be closed for system maintenance.
(C) Priya Nandan will need the office to herself.
(D) The monthly report deadline falls on that day.

### 題目 7（推論題）
What can be inferred about the department's monthly report?

(A) It has been permanently canceled this month.
(B) It must be submitted before Daniel's approved overtime ends on October 19.
(C) It will be completed entirely by Priya Nandan.
(D) It is unrelated to Daniel's leave schedule.

### 題目 8（字義題）

(A) avoids
