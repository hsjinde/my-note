# 13_商業書信慣用語與正式用語替換

## 核心概念

多益 Part 5、6、7 大量取材自商業書信（email、memo、letter）與正式文件（notice、announcement、policy）。這類文本偏好使用**較為正式（formal）的字彙與片語**，取代日常口語中常見的說法。考生若只認得口語版本（about、before、to、if、buy、use…），遇到正式書信的替換詞（regarding、prior to、in order to、in the event that、purchase、utilize…）就可能誤判詞意或誤選文法功能相近但語域不符的選項。

本章聚焦三件事：
1. **語域（register）替換**：同一個意思，口語 vs. 正式書信的不同說法。
2. **書信慣用語**：開頭致意、結尾請求回覆、附件說明等固定句型。
3. **法律／公告用語**：hereby、pursuant to、notwithstanding 這類較艱澀但常出現在公司政策、合約摘要中的字。

### 為什麼要特別學這個？

Part 6、7 常出現的文本類型（memo to all staff、customer notice、policy update）本身就是正式文件，作者會刻意使用正式用語營造專業感；因此考題選項也常常故意混入一個「意思對但語域不對」的口語選項作為陷阱。雖然語意相近，多益不會單純考「語域」讓口語版本變成錯的，而是會透過**詞性/句構**排除其餘選項，所以本章同時複習每個正式用語的**詞性限制**（例如 prior to 後面只能接名詞/動名詞，不能接子句）。

## 一、正式介系詞片語替換

| 口語／基本用法 | 正式書信用法 | 用法差異 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **about** | **regarding / concerning / with regard to** | 正式用法多為介系詞（片語），後面直接接名詞，不加 be 動詞 |
| **to V（表目的）** | **in order to V / so as to V** | 正式書信偏好完整片語表達「為了要」，尤其用於句首 |
| **if** | **in the event that / should + 主詞 + 原V** | in the event that 後接完整子句；should 倒裝句更正式 |
| **because of** | **owing to / due to** | 皆為正式介系詞片語，後接名詞 |
| **but / however** | **notwithstanding** | notwithstanding 可當介系詞（不受...影響），語氣更正式莊重 |

例句對照：
- 口語：I'm writing **about** the delayed shipment.
- 正式：I am writing **regarding** the delayed shipment.

## 二、法律／公告慣用語（hereby, pursuant to 系列）

這組字幾乎只出現在正式文件（合約摘要、公司公告、法規條文）中，考生只需認得意思：

| 字彙 | 意思 |
| :--- | :--- |
| **hereby** | 特此（宣示效力用），通常緊接主詞後、動詞前 |
| **herein** | 於此（文件）之中 |
| **thereof** | 其（指代前述事物） |
| **aforementioned** | 前述的，須放在名詞前 |
| **henceforth** | 從此以後 |
| **undersigned** | 簽署人（本人） |
| **pursuant to** | 依照、根據（規定、條款），比 according to 更專用於法規合約 |
| **notwithstanding** | 儘管、不受...影響，不可誤加 of 變成 despite of 的錯誤結構 |

## 三、書信開頭、結尾與請求的固定慣用語

正式書信有一套固定的請求／告知句型，多益 Part 7 的信件題常直接引用：

| 慣用語 | 用途 |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Please be advised that...** | 正式告知某項訊息（比 "Please note that" 更正式） |
| **We are pleased to inform you that...** | 正式告知好消息 |
| **Should you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact us.** | 結尾邀請對方提出疑問 |
| **At your earliest convenience** | 請對方儘快處理（比 "as soon as possible" 更委婉） |
| **For your reference / For your information** | 提供資訊供對方參考 |

> [!note]
> `Please be advised that` 與 `We are pleased to inform you that` 差異在於**訊息性質**：前者中性告知（可能是壞消息或規定變更），後者專用於好消息或核准通知。

## 四、動詞語域替換

商業書信偏好使用「較正式、單一動詞」取代日常動詞：

| 口語 | 正式書信用語 |
| :--- | :--- |
| **buy** | **purchase** |
| **use** | **utilize** |
| **start** | **commence** |
| **end** | **terminate** |
| **get** | **obtain** |
| **ask for** | **request** |
| **tell** | **notify** |

> [!note]
> 這些替換多為「意思相同、語域不同」，不是文法陷阱本身；但多益常把它們放進**詞性混淆題**（例如 purchase 名詞/動詞同形、termination 為名詞），所以仍要注意詞性。

## 📝 多益秒殺解題技巧

看到 email / memo / notice 開頭的閱讀題型，優先預期會出現 regarding、pursuant to、herein 等正式用語；`prior to`、`in order to`、`regarding` 這類片語後方**只能接名詞或動名詞**，若選項後面是完整子句，就要改選連接詞而非這些片語。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

### 正式連接詞／介系詞片語

*   **pursuant to** 片語 依照、根據 | The refund was issued *pursuant to* the return policy stated in Section 3.
*   **with regard to** 片語 關於 | We are writing *with regard to* your recent inquiry about our services.
*   **for your reference** 片語 供您參考 | A copy of the updated policy is attached *for your reference*.
*   **at your earliest convenience** 片語 請儘快 | Please review the attached draft *at your earliest convenience*.

### 法律／公告慣用語

*   **inform** (v. 及物) 告知 | We are writing to *inform* you that your subscription will expire soon.
*   **formal** adj. 正式的 | A *formal* letter of apology was issued to all affected customers.
*   **hereby** adv. 特此 | The board *hereby* approves the proposed merger.
*   **herein** adv. 於此文件中 | Any changes to the terms *herein* must be made in writing.
*   **thereof** adv. 其、關於該事物 | The client received the contract along with a summary *thereof*.
*   **whereby** adv. 憑藉、據此 | The company adopted a new policy *whereby* overtime is compensated with paid leave.
*   **undersigned** n. 簽署人（本人） | The *undersigned* confirms acceptance of all terms listed above.

### 正式書信動詞替換

*   **reiterate** (v. 及物) 重申 | The manager wished to *reiterate* the importance of meeting the deadline.
*   **duly** adv. 依規定地、正式地 | The application was *duly* reviewed and approved by the committee.
*   **purchase** (v. 及物) 購買，正式用語，替代 buy | The office will *purchase* new laptops for the design team.
*   **utilize** (v. 及物) 利用，正式用語，替代 use | Managers are encouraged to *utilize* the new scheduling software.
*   **obtain** (v. 及物) 取得，正式用語，替代 get | Employees must *obtain* written consent before sharing client data.

## 📚 補充秒殺公式

1.  **regarding / concerning 是介系詞，本身已含 about 的意思**：後面不可再加 about，也不可加 to，直接接名詞或動名詞。
2.  **prior to 與 subsequent to 只接名詞或動名詞，不接完整子句**：若空格後面是主詞+動詞的子句，應改選 before / after 而非 prior to / subsequent to。
3.  **in the event that 後接子句，in the event of 後接名詞**：兩者意思都是「萬一」，差別在後面接的詞性。
4.  **notwithstanding 可放名詞前或後**：notwithstanding the delay 與 the delay notwithstanding 皆可，是商業合約中少數可倒裝的介系詞，且不可誤寫成 despite of 的結構。
5.  **be advised that 後面接完整子句**：advised 是過去分詞當形容詞用，此處不可誤選為 advise 的原形動詞。
6.  **hesitate to + V 原形**：do not hesitate to contact us 是固定搭配，to 之後一定接原形動詞，不接動名詞。
7.  **pursuant to vs. according to**：兩者都表「依照」，但 pursuant to 專用於依照法規、條款、合約，according to 泛指依照任何資料來源。
8.  **hereby 的位置**：通常緊接在主詞後、動詞前（The company hereby confirms...），作用是加強官方宣示的語氣，可視為插入語，去掉也不影響句子文法完整性。
9.  **正式書信套語中的 that 子句不可省略主詞**：We regret to inform you that + 完整子句，主詞不可省略。
10. **aforementioned 修飾先前提過的名詞，須放名詞前**：the aforementioned contract，不可改為 the contract aforementioned。
11. **purchase / commence / terminate / utilize 詞性須留意**：這些多益愛考「動詞 vs. 名詞」的詞性題，例如 purchase（v. 購買 / n. 購買行為）、termination（n. 終止），作答時先判斷空格要填動詞還是名詞。
12. **正式書信偏好非人稱主詞開頭**：It is recommended that... / It has come to our attention that... 是固定句型，it 為虛主詞，後面接 that 子句。

## 💪 練習題（15 題）

**1.** I am writing ______ the invoice we received last week, which appears to contain an error.
(A) about (B) regarding (C) regard (D) regarded

**2.** ______ the current supply shortage, the factory managed to fulfill all orders on time.
(A) Notwithstanding (B) Although (C) However (D) Despite of

**3.** All applicants must submit their portfolios ______ the interview, not on the day itself.
(A) prior to (B) prior (C) subsequent (D) previously

**4.** ______ the shipment does not arrive by Friday, the client will be entitled to a full refund.
(A) In the event of (B) In the event that (C) In case (D) In spite of

**5.** The management team wishes to ______ that all expense reports are due by the fifth of each month.
(A) reiteration (B) reiterated (C) reiterate (D) reiterating

**6.** The service contract clearly ______ that either party may terminate the agreement with thirty days' notice.
(A) stipulates (B) stipulation (C) stipulated to (D) stipulating

**7.** The revised delivery schedule was ______ reviewed and approved before being sent to all branch offices.
(A) duly (B) dully (C) duty (D) dutiful

**8.** Please be ______ that the parking garage will be closed for repairs next week.
(A) advise (B) advising (C) advised (D) advisable

**9.** ______, all correspondence regarding the merger should be directed to the legal department rather than to individual managers.
(A) Henceforth (B) Hence (C) Henceforward it (D) Hencefrom

**10.** 第一空：The company adopted a new remote-work arrangement ______ employees may choose to work from home up to two days per week.
第二空：In addition, staff are asked to confirm their schedule with their supervisors ______ the beginning of each month.
第一空：(A) whereby (B) wherein (C) herein (D) thereof
第二空：(A) prior to (B) prior (C) subsequent (D) previous

**11.** The undersigned ______ hereby acknowledges receipt of the shipment described above.
(A) representative (B) representing (C) represented (D) representatively

**12.** The new online portal is designed to ______ faster communication between the sales and logistics teams.
(A) facilitate (B) facilitation (C) facilitated (D) facilitating

**13.** 第一空：Customers who notice any discrepancy on their monthly statement should contact the billing department so that the error can be ______ promptly.
第二空：A written notification will then be sent to confirm that the matter has been ______ to the customer's satisfaction.
第一空：(A) rectify (B) rectified (C) rectification (D) rectifying
第二空：(A) resolve (B) resolves (C) resolving (D) resolved

**14.** All departments must ______ written approval from the finance director before purchasing new equipment.
(A) obtain (B) obtaining (C) obtainment (D) obtainable

**15.** The updated safety guidelines apply to all warehouse staff; ______, any exceptions must be approved by the site manager.
(A) therefore (B) notwithstanding (C) henceforth (D) meanwhile
