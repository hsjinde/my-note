## Part 6：短文填空（2 篇，每篇 4 題）

### 短文一：公司內部公告

To: All Staff
From: Facilities Management
Subject: Office Renovation Notice

We would like to inform all employees that the third-floor office space will undergo renovation starting next Monday. During this period, staff members currently ______ (16) on the third floor will be temporarily relocated to the fifth floor.

The renovation is expected to take approximately three weeks. ______ (17), the completion date may be extended if unforeseen issues arise.

**(18)** ______

Please remove all personal belongings from your desks by Friday. Facilities staff will not be responsible for any items left behind. If you have questions regarding the relocation, please contact the facilities office ______ (19) extension 245.

We appreciate your patience and cooperation during this transition.

### 題目 16
(A) work
(B) working
(C) worked
(D) to work

### 題目 17
(A) Therefore
(B) However
(C) In addition
(D) For example

### 題目 18：選出最適合插入的句子

### 題目 19

**Job Posting: Administrative Assistant**

Global Tech Solutions is seeking a detail-oriented Administrative Assistant to join our growing office in downtown Seattle. The successful candidate will support the operations manager with scheduling, correspondence, and general office duties.

**Responsibilities:**
- Answer and direct phone calls
- Maintain filing systems, both electronic and physical
- Schedule appointments and manage the operations manager's calendar
- Prepare reports and presentations as needed

**Requirements:**
- At least two years of administrative experience
- Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite
- Strong written and verbal communication skills

This is a full-time position with a competitive salary and benefits package, including health insurance and paid vacation. Interested candidates should submit a resume and cover letter to careers@globaltechsolutions.com by July 20. Only shortlisted applicants will be contacted for an interview.

### 文章二：客戶通知信

Dear Valued Customer,

We are writing to inform you of an important update to our online banking service. Beginning August 1, our mobile application will introduce a new two-step verification process to enhance the security of your account.

After this date, whenever you log in from a new device, you will be required to enter a verification code sent to your registered mobile number, in addition to your regular password. This additional layer of protection is designed to prevent unauthorized access to your account, especially in cases where your password may have been compromised.

Please ensure that your contact information, including your mobile number, is up to date in our system. You can verify or update this information by visiting the "Account Settings" section of the app or by contacting our customer service center.

We understand that this change may require a brief adjustment period, and we appreciate your understanding as we work to keep your financial information safe.

If you have any questions about this update, please do not hesitate to contact our support team at support@securebank.com or call 1-800-555-0199.

Sincerely,
Customer Security Team
SecureBank
