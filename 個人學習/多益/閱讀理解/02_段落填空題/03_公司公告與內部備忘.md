# 03_公司公告與內部備忘

## 📝 答題策略 🌟
1.  **公告類文章先抓「生效時間」**：effective..., beginning..., starting... 等字眼決定後續動詞該用未來式還是現在完成式。
2.  **被動語態判斷主詞角色**：規定、報告、系統等無生命名詞常是「被規定/被建立」的對象，須用被動語態。
3.  **句子插入題看「功能」而非「主題相近」**：插入句要能承接上一句的具體內容，並自然引出下一句，光是主題相關但功能不對仍是錯的。
4.  **轉折/對比連接詞題**：instead of、despite、because of 意義相近但詞性不同，需依空格後方是名詞、動名詞還是子句判斷。
5.  **介系詞搭配固定用法**：contact/direct/cooperation 等字有固定介系詞搭配，須靠語感與常見搭配記憶，而非逐字翻譯。

## 短文一：資訊安全政策公告

To All Staff
From: IT Security Team

In response to a recent increase in phishing attempts, the company ______(1) new password requirements for all employees, effective next Monday. Under the updated policy, passwords must be changed every ninety days and must include a combination of letters, numbers, and symbols. ______(2). Staff who fail to comply by the deadline may temporarily lose access to internal systems. If you experience any difficulty resetting your password, please contact the IT help desk directly ______(3) submitting a ticket through the portal. We appreciate everyone's cooperation ______(4) keeping our network secure.

### 題目 1
(A) introduces
(B) introduced
(C) will introduce
(D) has introduced

### 題目 2
(A) A short video tutorial demonstrating the new login process will be posted on the intranet.
(B) Our company was founded more than fifteen years ago.
(C) The annual holiday party has been rescheduled to next month.
(D) Employees are encouraged to take a longer lunch break this week.

### 題目 3
(A) instead of
(B) despite
(C) because of
(D) in addition to

### 題目 4
(A) in
(B) for
(C) on
(D) at

## 短文二：會議室預約規則備忘錄

MEMO
To: All Staff
From: Office Administration
Re: New Conference Room Booking System

Beginning next week, all conference room reservations ______(5) through the new online booking system instead of by email. This change is intended to reduce scheduling conflicts and give everyone real-time visibility into room availability. ______(6). Employees who need a room for more than two hours must first obtain approval from their department manager. We understand that adjusting to a new system takes time, and our office administration team ______(7) available to assist with any questions. Please direct all inquiries about the new procedure ______(8) the front desk during business hours.

### 題目 5
(A) must be made
(B) must make
(C) made
(D) making

### 題目 6
(A) The booking system can be accessed from any computer connected to the company network.
(B) Our previous office location had only three meeting rooms.
(C) The annual holiday party will be held in December this year.
(D) All visitors must sign in at the front desk before entering the building.

### 題目 7
(A) remains
(B) remained
(C) will remain
(D) has remained

### 題目 8
(A) to
(B) at
(C) for
(D) with
