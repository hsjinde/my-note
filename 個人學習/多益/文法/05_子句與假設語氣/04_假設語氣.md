# 04_假設語氣

## 核心概念
假設語氣用來表達與事實相反的假設、願望、或是某種條件下可能發生的事。它的特色是「**時態會往後退一格**」。

## 1. 與現在事實相反 (Present Unreal)
*   **條件句公式**：`If + S + 過去式動詞 (be動詞一律用 were), S + would/could/should/might + V (原形)`
*   **例句**：If I **were** you, I **would not accept** the offer. (如果我是你，我就不會接受。 -> 但我不是你)

## 2. 與過去事實相反 (Past Unreal)
*   **條件句公式**：`If + S + had + p.p. (過去完成式), S + would/could/should/might + have + p.p.`
*   **例句**：If he **had studied** harder, he **would have passed** the exam. (如果他當時有努力讀書，他當時就會及格了。 -> 但他當時沒讀書)

## 3. 與未來事實相反 / 未來極小可能性 (Future Unreal)
*   **條件句公式**：`If + S + should + V, S + 助動詞 + V / 祈使句` (萬一...)
*   **例句**：If you **should** have any questions, please **contact** me. (萬一你有任何問題，請聯絡我。)

## 📝 多益必考重點 (秒殺技巧)

### 考點 1：條件句的倒裝 (If 省略)
多益極愛考 If 省略的倒裝句型，請將助動詞移到主詞前面。
1.  **與過去相反倒裝**：`Had + S + p.p., S + would have p.p.`
    *   *Had* I *known* the truth, I would have told you. (原句: If I had known...)
2.  **與未來相反倒裝** (最常考)：`Should + S + V, 祈使句`
    *   *Should* you *need* further assistance, please let us know. (原句: If you should need...)
3.  **與現在相反倒裝** (較少考)：`Were + S ..., S + would V`
    *   *Were* I the manager, I would fire him.

### 考點 2：Without / But for (要不是因為...)
*   與現在相反：`Without / But for + N, S + would + V`
*   與過去相反：`Without / But for + N, S + would have + p.p.`

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **混合時態假設語氣 (Mixed Conditional)**：if 子句與主要子句的時間點不同時，動詞時態可混用。
    *   If he **had studied** medicine, he **would be** a doctor now. (過去沒讀醫學系 → 導致現在不是醫生)
2.  **I wish + 假設語氣**：表達「希望、可惜」，時態同樣往後退一格。
    *   I **wish** I **had more time**. (但現在沒有 → 與現在事實相反)
    *   I **wish** I **had studied** harder. (但當時沒有 → 與過去事實相反)
3.  **as if / as though + 假設語氣**：詳見 [[03_副詞子句]]，常與 seem/look/talk 等動詞搭配。
    *   She looks **as if** she **were** exhausted.
4.  **意志動詞 + that 子句用原形動詞**：suggest, insist, demand, require, recommend 等動詞後的 that 子句要用原形動詞 (不受主詞人稱/時態影響)，這與假設語氣同屬「虛擬語氣」家族，多益常混合考。
    *   The board insisted that the CEO **resign** immediately.
5.  **陷阱：if only**：語氣比 I wish 更強烈的「要是...就好了」，句型與 If 子句相同，但通常單獨成句。
    *   **If only** I **had known** about the sale earlier!

## 🔤 相關單字和片語
*   **unreal** (adj.) 不真實的、假設的 | This is an **unreal** condition contrary to fact.
*   **hypothetical** (adj.) 假設性的 | The scenario is purely **hypothetical**.
*   **insist** (v.) 堅持 | The client **insisted** that the deadline be extended.
*   **assumption** (n.) 假設 | Our budget is based on an **assumption** that costs stay stable.
*   **contrary to** (片語) 與...相反 | **Contrary to** expectations, sales dropped.
*   **regret** (v./n.) 後悔 | I **regret** that I did not accept the offer.
*   **otherwise** (adv.) 否則、不然 | Submit the form by Friday; **otherwise**, it will not be processed.
*   **assistance** (n.) 協助 | **Should** you need further **assistance**, contact support.
*   **inquiry** (n.) 詢問 | **Should** you have an **inquiry**, please call the hotline.
*   **compensate** (v.) 補償 | If the flight had been cancelled, the airline **would have compensated** passengers.
*   **presume** (v.) 推測、假定 | I **presume** that he would have agreed if asked.
*   **wish** (v.) 希望（與事實相反） | I **wish** the meeting **were** shorter.
*   **exhausted** (adj.) 精疲力盡的 | He looked **as if** he **were exhausted**.
*   **had it not been for** (片語) 要不是因為 | **Had it not been for** her help, we would have failed.
*   **on short notice** (片語) 臨時通知 | **Were** we notified **on short notice**, we could still adapt.

## 💪 練習題（5 題）
1.  If the company ______ more research, it would have avoided the product recall.
    (A) conducts
    (B) had conducted
    (C) conducted
    (D) has conducted

2.  ______ you require any further information, please do not hesitate to contact our office.
    (A) Should
    (B) Would
    (C) Were
    (D) Had

3.  I wish I ______ about the schedule change before I booked my flight.
    (A) know
    (B) knew
    (C) had known
    (D) have known

4.  短文填空：If the merger (1)______ approved next month, both companies will begin integrating their systems immediately. Analysts say that (2)______ the deal fallen through, shareholders would have lost significant value.
    (1)
    (A) is
    (B) were
    (C) had been
    (D) will be
    (2)
    (A) if
    (B) had
    (C) unless
    (D) once

5.  ______ for the technical support team, the server outage would have lasted much longer.
    (A) Without
    (B) Unless
    (C) If
    (D) Despite
