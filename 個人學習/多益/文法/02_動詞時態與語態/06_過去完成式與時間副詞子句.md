# 06_過去完成式與時間副詞子句

## 核心概念
過去完成式（had + p.p.）的核心功能是標記「過去的過去」——在過去某個時間點或某個過去動作發生之前，已經先完成的另一個動作。多益考點很少單獨考「had + p.p.」的形式，而是考**它跟哪個時間副詞子句連接詞搭配、以及該連接詞子句本身該用什麼時態**。本章專注於 by the time、before、after、once、not until 等連接詞與過去完成式的固定組合，以及 no sooner...than、hardly...when 這類倒裝句型，是對「過去的過去」這個概念的深化，而非重複前一章「完成式總論」的基礎介紹。

## 1. 過去完成式的判斷原則：兩個過去動作，先發生的用 had + p.p.
當句子描述兩個都發生在過去的動作時，**先發生的動作**用過去完成式，**後發生的動作**用簡單過去式，藉此在沒有時間軸圖示的情況下，用動詞形式本身標出先後順序。
*   The technician **had replaced** the router before the client **noticed** the outage. (先換路由器，才發現斷線)

## 2. by the time 子句：子句用簡單過去式，主句才用過去完成式
by the time 引導的子句本身用**簡單過去式**（描述某個過去時刻已經到達），主句則用**過去完成式**，表示到那個時刻為止，某事已經先完成。這是本章最核心、也最常考的結構。
*   **By the time** the vendor **delivered** the replacement parts, the assembly line **had been idle** for two days.
*   注意：若子句主詞改成未來事件（即整句仍在敘述未來），則子句仍用現在式、主句改用未來完成式（will have + p.p.），這是與「未來式進階」章節的分工邊界，本章只處理**整句都發生在過去**的情況。

## 3. before / after 子句：時態選擇有彈性，但過去完成式強調「完成的間隔」
before、after 本身的邏輯已經標明先後順序，因此嚴格來說主句與子句都可以用簡單過去式；但當作者想**強調兩個動作之間有明顯的時間間隔**，或想避免歧義，會刻意在較早發生的那個動作上使用過去完成式。
*   The auditor signed off on the report only **after** the finance team **had corrected** every discrepancy in the ledger.
*   相反地，若只是單純敘述先後、沒有特別強調「間隔」，直接用簡單過去式即可：She left the office after she finished the call.（兩者皆簡單過去式，語意仍清楚）

## 4. once 子句：一旦...之後，強調條件達成後緊接著發生後續動作
once 作連接詞使用時，語意接近 as soon as，但更強調「一旦某條件被滿足」。描述過去情境時，once 子句常用過去完成式來強調該條件**確實已經先被滿足**，主句再接續簡單過去式描述隨後發生的事。
*   **Once** the shipment **had cleared** customs, the distributor released it to regional warehouses the same afternoon.

## 5. not until 與倒裝句：Not until + 過去子句，主句過去完成式否定形態
not until 開頭的句子強調「直到...才」，若 not until 被移到句首，主句須採**部分倒裝**（助動詞提前），且主句常用過去完成式的否定形態，表示「在那之前一直沒有完成」。
*   **Not until** the compliance officer **reviewed** every contract **had** the department **realized** how many clauses were outdated.

## 6. no sooner...than 與 hardly...when：一...就...的倒裝句型
這兩組句型都表示「A 動作剛完成，B 動作就緊接著發生」，前半句一律用過去完成式，後半句用簡單過去式；當 no sooner／hardly 置於句首時，前半句必須倒裝（had 提前於主詞）。
*   **No sooner had** the merger **been announced than** the stock price surged by 12 percent.
*   **Hardly had** the technician **restarted** the server **when** a new error message appeared on screen.

## 7. 已經、從未、兩次以前：過去完成式常見的伴隨副詞
already、never、twice、once (副詞用法，非連接詞)、just 等副詞常出現在過去完成式句中，強化「在某個過去時間點之前」這個語意，是快速辨識過去完成式的線索詞。
*   By the time the new CFO joined, the company **had already relocated** its headquarters twice.

## 8. 過去完成式的被動語態：had been + p.p.
當「先發生的那個動作」的邏輯主詞是承受者而非執行者時，過去完成式須改為被動：had been + p.p.，考試常故意漏掉 been 或誤用 was/were 混淆時態。
*   By the time the shareholders convened, the revised bylaws **had already been approved** by the legal department.

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **by the time 的固定分工**：全句發生在過去時，by the time 子句永遠用簡單過去式，主句永遠用過去完成式；不會反過來。
2.  **before/after 不強制過去完成式**：兩者本身已標明順序，簡單過去式也合乎邏輯；只有在強調「間隔」或避免歧義時才刻意選過去完成式。
3.  **no sooner...than 與 hardly...when 的固定搭配**：no sooner 只能接 than，hardly 只能接 when（或 before），不可混用；且兩者置於句首時前半句都要倒裝。
4.  **倒裝觸發詞**：Not until、No sooner、Hardly、Rarely、Never 等否定副詞片語置於句首時，緊接的子句要用部分倒裝（助動詞/be動詞提前）。
5.  **already/never/twice + 過去完成式**：這些副詞是過去完成式的強烈語意線索，尤其常與 by the time 主句連用。
6.  **once 作連接詞 vs. once 作副詞**：作連接詞時意近 as soon as，接子句；作副詞時單獨修飾動詞，表「曾經、一次」，兩者不可混淆詞性。
7.  **過去完成式被動態別漏 been**：had been + p.p.，是這個時態最容易漏字或誤用 was/were 的地方。
8.  **過去完成式不能單獨存在**：一律需要另一個簡單過去式的動作或明確的過去時間點作為參照，句中若只有一個過去動作，不可單獨使用過去完成式。
9.  **by the time 與 by 的差異**：by the time 後面接**完整子句**（主詞+動詞）；by 後面直接接**時間點或名詞**，例如 by 2020、by the deadline，不可誤植。
10. **與未來完成式的邊界**：若全句其實是描述「未來的某個過去參照點」（如敘述未來某天回顧過去），子句與主句時態會整組往後推一格，屬於未來式範疇，非本章討論的過去完成式。
11. **強調完成間隔的片語**：long before、well before 等片語常與過去完成式連用，強調「早在...之前很久」就已經完成。
12. **篇章題中的時態一致性**：若前一句已用過去完成式建立「更早發生」的事件，後續描述隨之而來的結果時，通常改回簡單過去式，不會整段都停留在過去完成式。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

### 表示先後順序的動詞與形容詞
*   **precede** v. (及物) 先於、在...之前發生 | A brief safety briefing *precedes* every warehouse tour.
*   **preceding** adj. 先前的、前面的 | Refer to the figures in the *preceding* section of the report.
*   **ensuing** adj. 隨後的、接踵而至的 | The recall triggered widespread criticism in the *ensuing* weeks.
*   **subsequent** adj. 隨後的、後續的 | All *subsequent* orders will be shipped from the new warehouse.
*   **prerequisite** n. 先決條件 | Completing the safety course is a *prerequisite* for operating the machinery.
*   **settle** v. (及物) 敲定、解決 | The two firms finally *settled* the dispute out of court.
*   **conclude** v. 結束、下結論 | The negotiations *concluded* just before midnight.

### 表示時間先後的副詞與片語
*   **thereafter** adv. 此後、其後 | The pilot program launched in March; sales figures rose steadily *thereafter*.
*   **afterward** adv. 之後 | The vendor confirmed the order and sent an invoice shortly *afterward*.
*   **meanwhile** adv. 同時、與此同時 | The design team revised the packaging; *meanwhile*, marketing prepared the launch campaign.
*   **simultaneously** adv. 同時地 | Two production lines were upgraded *simultaneously* to save time.
*   **in the meantime** phr. 在此期間 | The system will be down for maintenance; *in the meantime*, please use the backup server.
*   **in advance** phr. 事先、提前 | The caterer requested full payment *in advance*.
*   **ahead of schedule** phr. 提前於預定進度 | The renovation was finished *ahead of schedule*.
*   **belatedly** adv. 遲來地、事後才 | The supplier *belatedly* admitted that the shipment had been delayed.
*   **promptly** adv. 立即地、準時地 | The technician responded *promptly* once the alarm was triggered.
*   **in a timely manner** phr. 及時地 | Please submit the reimbursement form *in a timely manner*.
*   **up until** phr. 直到...為止 | *Up until* last year, the firm had never missed a delivery deadline.
*   **no earlier than** phr. 不早於 | The shipment cannot be released *no earlier than* Thursday.
*   **long before** phr. 早在...之前很久 | The company had digitized its records *long before* the new regulation took effect.
*   **ever since** phr. 自從...以來一直 | The branch has followed the revised protocol *ever since* the incident.
*   **scarcely** adv. 幾乎不、剛...就（常與 when 連用） | The technician had *scarcely* finished the repair when the machine failed again.

### 描述事件過程與結果的名詞
*   **sequence** n. 順序、次序 | The manual outlines the correct *sequence* of installation steps.
*   **chronological** adj. 按時間順序的 | The report presents the incidents in *chronological* order.
*   **interim** n./adj. 過渡期（的）、臨時的 | An *interim* manager was appointed while the search continued.
*   **aftermath** n. 事件之後的情況、餘波 | In the *aftermath* of the outage, the company revised its backup protocol.
*   **retrospectively** adv. 追溯地、事後回顧地 | The policy was applied *retrospectively* to contracts signed earlier that year.
*   **wrap up** phr.v. 結束、完成收尾 | The committee *wrapped up* the review just before the holiday break.
*   **till** conj./prep. 直到 | The warehouse remained locked *till* the inspection team arrived.

## 💪 練習題（15 題）

**1.** By the time the vendor ______ the replacement parts, the assembly line had already been idle for two days.
(A) delivered (B) delivers (C) will deliver (D) had delivered

**2.** The auditor signed off on the report only after the finance team ______ every discrepancy in the ledger.
(A) corrects (B) had corrected (C) will correct (D) is correcting

**3.** No sooner ______ the merger been announced than the stock price surged by 12 percent.
(A) has (B) was (C) had (D) did

**4.** Once the shipment ______ customs, the distributor released it to regional warehouses the same afternoon.
(A) clears (B) clear (C) will clear (D) had cleared

**5.** Not until the compliance officer reviewed every contract ______ the department realized how many clauses were outdated.
(A) had (B) has (C) was (D) did

**6.** By the time the new CFO joined, the company ______ its headquarters twice already.
(A) relocates (B) had relocated (C) has relocated (D) will relocate

**7.** By the time the shareholders convened, the revised bylaws ______ by the legal department.
(A) had already approved (B) were already approving (C) had already been approved (D) approved already

**8.** Hardly ______ the technician restarted the server when a new error message appeared on screen.
(A) did (B) was (C) has (D) had

**9.** The warehouse manager confirmed that every pallet ______ before the inspector's unannounced visit last Tuesday.
(A) had been scanned (B) is scanned (C) scans (D) will be scanned

**10.** The two firms had negotiated for nearly a year before they ______ the licensing agreement.
(A) sign (B) signed (C) had signed (D) will sign

**11.** The branch manager admitted that the backup server ______ properly tested prior to the outage.
(A) has never been (B) is never (C) had never been (D) will never be

**12.** 第一空：By the time the design team submitted the final mockups, the client ______ the budget twice.（第二空）Fortunately, the last revision ______ the project back within range.
(A) revised / had brought (B) has revised / brings (C) had revised / had brought (D) had revised / brought

**13.** The regional office had already relocated its files to the cloud long before headquarters ______ the same migration.
(A) completed (B) completes (C) had completed (D) will complete

**14.** It was not until the recall notice reached every retailer that the company ______ the full scope of the defect.
(A) understands (B) understood (C) has understood (D) had understood

**15.** 第一空：The maintenance crew had scarcely finished inspecting the elevator when a second complaint ______.（第二空）By then, the building manager ______ two separate technicians to the site that same week.
(A) had arrived / sent (B) arrives / sends (C) arrived / had already sent (D) had arrived / had already sent
