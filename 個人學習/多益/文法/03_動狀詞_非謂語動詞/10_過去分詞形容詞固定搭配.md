# 10_過去分詞形容詞固定搭配

## 核心概念
許多過去分詞（p.p.）在多益中已經「形容詞化」，固定搭配特定介系詞或不定詞，變成整組片語一起背誦、一起選。這一類題目不是考「主動 vs 被動」的分詞構句判斷（那是情緒動詞分詞的範疇），而是考「介系詞是否正確」以及「be + p.p. + prep/to V」的固定用法是否熟練。

## 1. 為什麼過去分詞可以當形容詞
動詞的過去分詞經過長期使用，語意已經固定為一種「狀態」形容詞，例如 be dedicated to（致力於）、be geared toward（針對...而設計）。這些片語中的分詞已經不再強調「被做了什麼動作」，而是描述主詞目前的狀態或傾向，因此後面接的介系詞是固定的，換一個介系詞語意就不通或不合乎慣用法。

## 2. 判斷技巧：先鎖定分詞，再鎖定介系詞
遇到「be ___ to/about/in/with...」的題型時，兩層判斷：
1. 先看空格該填動詞的哪個形式（多半已經是 p.p.，題目通常直接考介系詞或考 p.p. vs 原形/V-ing 的選項）。
2. 再看後面固定介系詞是否對應，介系詞用錯即使分詞正確也算錯。

### 常見固定搭配一覽
*   be **dedicated to** + N/V-ing（致力於）
*   be **devoted to** + N/V-ing（奉獻於）
*   be **geared toward** + N（針對...而設計）
*   be **affiliated with** + N（與...有關聯／隸屬於）
*   be **inclined to** + V（傾向於）
*   be **accustomed to** + N/V-ing（習慣於）
*   be **tailored to** + N（量身打造以符合...）
*   be **rooted in** + N（根源於）
*   be **confined to** + N（僅限於）
*   be **bound to** + V（勢必會）
*   be **associated with** + N（與...有關）
*   be **involved in** + N（涉入、參與）
*   be **preoccupied with** + N（全神貫注於）
*   be **convinced of** + N（確信）
*   be **poised to** + V（準備好要）

## 3. 容易混淆的近義搭配辨析
*   **be dedicated to** vs **be devoted to**：兩者語意接近，皆表「致力/奉獻於」，多益中可視為同義互換，重點在於 to 後面接名詞或動名詞，而非原形動詞。
*   **be confined to** vs **be restricted to** vs **be limited to**：三者都表「僅限於」，介系詞皆為 to，多益常以此三者互為誘答選項，只要記住介系詞固定用 to 即可。
*   **be bound to** vs **be poised to** vs **be destined to**：三者都表「勢必、注定會」，後面直接接原形動詞（不是介系詞+名詞），與前面「be p.p. + 介系詞」的組別不同，要小心分辨。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

### be + p.p. + 介系詞（狀態形容詞）
*   **be concerned about** 片語 對...感到擔憂/關切 | Senior management **is concerned about** the *rising* cost of raw materials.
*   **be dedicated to** 片語 致力於 | The customer support team **is dedicated to** *resolving* complaints within 24 hours.
*   **be devoted to** 片語 奉獻於 | The foundation **is devoted to** improving working conditions in the factory.
*   **be geared toward** 片語 針對...而設計 | This training program **is geared toward** first-time managers.
*   **be affiliated with** 片語 與...有關聯／隸屬於 | The consulting firm **is affiliated with** several major accounting networks.
*   **be inclined to** 片語 傾向於 | The board **is inclined to** approve the expansion plan next quarter.
*   **be accustomed to** 片語 習慣於 | Veteran staff **are accustomed to** working under tight deadlines.
*   **be attached to** 片語 附加於／依戀於 | Please note that a revised invoice **is attached to** this email.
*   **be tailored to** 片語 量身打造以符合... | The proposal **was tailored to** the client's specific budget constraints.
*   **be oriented toward** 片語 以...為導向 | The new curriculum **is oriented toward** practical, hands-on skills.
*   **be rooted in** 片語 根源於 | The company's conservative pricing strategy **is rooted in** its early struggles during the recession.
*   **be grounded in** 片語 基於 | Every recommendation in the report **is grounded in** verifiable market data.
*   **be confined to** 片語 僅限於 | Access to the server room **is confined to** authorized IT personnel.
*   **be restricted to** 片語 僅限於 | Parking on the ground floor **is restricted to** executives and visiting clients.
*   **be limited to** 片語 僅限於 | The warranty **is limited to** manufacturing defects only.
*   **be bound to** 片語 勢必會 | Without additional funding, the project **is bound to** fall behind schedule.
*   **be tied to** 片語 與...掛鉤 | Employee bonuses **are tied to** individual performance reviews.
*   **be linked to** 片語 與...有關聯 | The delivery delay **was linked to** a shortage of shipping containers.
*   **be associated with** 片語 與...有關 | High turnover **is often associated with** poor management practices.
*   **be involved in** 片語 涉入、參與 | Three departments **are directly involved in** the product launch.
*   **be engaged in** 片語 從事於 | The legal team **is currently engaged in** contract negotiations.
*   **be absorbed in** 片語 專注於 | The analyst **was so absorbed in** the spreadsheet that she missed the meeting reminder.
*   **be preoccupied with** 片語 全神貫注於 | Executives **are preoccupied with** the upcoming merger announcement.
*   **be convinced of** 片語 確信 | The investors **are convinced of** the product's long-term profitability.
*   **be poised to** 片語 準備好要 | The startup **is poised to** enter the European market next year.
*   **be positioned to** 片語 處於有利位置以... | The firm **is well positioned to** benefit from the new trade agreement.
*   **be slated to** 片語 預定要 | The renovation **is slated to** begin in early September.
*   **be destined to** 片語 注定會 | Analysts believe the merger **is destined to** reshape the industry.
*   **be plagued by** 片語 為...所困擾 | The rollout **was plagued by** technical glitches during its first week.
*   **be steeped in** 片語 深受...影響／充滿 | The firm's culture **is steeped in** tradition, which sometimes slows decision-making.

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **先分組再背**：把「be p.p. + 介系詞」依語意分成三組——狀態描述（concerned, involved, engaged）、範圍限定（confined, restricted, limited，一律接 to）、因果連結（tied, linked, associated），分組記憶比逐字硬背快得多。
2.  **confined/restricted/limited to 三胞胎**：這三個字幾乎可以互換，多益考試常把其中一個設計成正確答案、另外兩個放進選項當誘餌，只要看到「僅限於」的語意，介系詞一律鎖定 to。
3.  **dedicated/devoted to + V-ing，不接原形動詞**：to 在這裡是介系詞不是不定詞記號，後面若接動詞必須是動名詞形式，例如 be dedicated to *improving*，而非 be dedicated to *improve*。
4.  **bound / poised / destined to + 原形動詞**：這一組雖然長得像固定介系詞搭配，但 to 其實是不定詞記號，後面直接接原形動詞，不能加 Ving 或名詞，是最容易與「to + 介系詞」搞混的一組。
5.  **concerned about vs concerned with**：be concerned about 表「擔憂」，be concerned with 表「與...有關」，語意不同，多益偶爾會在同一篇文章中考出兩種用法對比。
6.  **affiliated with 的主詞多為機構**：這個片語主詞通常是公司、機構、組織，而非單一個人，若題目主詞是人，通常應選 associated with 或 involved in 更為自然。
7.  **preoccupied / absorbed with 前的 be 動詞可換成 seem/become/remain**：狀態動詞（seem, become, remain, stay）都能取代 be 出現在這些固定搭配前面，句意仍然通順，考題常以此變化句型混淆考生。
8.  **plagued/steeped by/in 屬於偏書面用語**：這兩個字常出現在 Part 7 的新聞稿或分析報導中，考生看到 be plagued by + 負面名詞（glitches, delays, complaints）幾乎可以直接判斷語意為「深受...所苦」。
9.  **rooted in / grounded in 常見於報告類文體**：這兩者強調「有事實或歷史依據」，常與 data、evidence、history、tradition 等名詞搭配，Part 6 商業報告中出現機率高。
10. **positioned to 常與 well 連用**：be well positioned to 是固定加強語氣的用法，中間插入 well 不影響片語判讀，考生若只背 be positioned to 容易在句中看到 well 而遲疑。
11. **slated to 與 scheduled to 可互換**，但 slated to 更常見於書面新聞稿，scheduled to 則兩種文體都常見，語意上兩者幾乎相同，皆表「預定」。
12. **選項策略**：若四個選項同時出現「介系詞不同」（about/with/to/in），代表考點在固定介系詞搭配本身，此時應先鎖定句中的過去分詞，直接背誦其固定介系詞，而非重新分析句意。

## 💪 練習題（15 題）

**1.** Senior management ______ about the rising cost of raw materials and has asked each department to cut nonessential spending.
(A) concern (B) concerning (C) concerned (D) to concern

**2.** The customer support team is dedicated ______ resolving every complaint within twenty-four hours.
(A) to (B) for (C) with (D) at

**3.** This new onboarding program is geared ______ employees who have never worked in a corporate office before.
(A) toward (B) of (C) about (D) into

**4.** Because parking on the ground floor is ______ to executives and visiting clients, most staff must use the rear lot.
(A) restrict (B) restricting (C) restricted (D) restriction

**5.** Without additional funding approved this week, the pilot project ______ to fall behind its original schedule.（第一空）Once the delay becomes public, the client's confidence in the vendor ______ to weaken further.（第二空）
第一空：(A) bounds (B) bound (C) is bound (D) binding
第二空：(A) is likely (B) likely (C) likelihood (D) likeliness

**6.** Employee bonuses are directly ______ to individual performance reviews rather than to overall company profit.
(A) tie (B) tying (C) tied (D) ties

**7.** High turnover rates are often ______ with inconsistent management practices across regional branches.
(A) associate (B) associating (C) associated (D) association

**8.** The legal department is currently ______ in a series of contract negotiations with three overseas suppliers.
(A) engage (B) engaging (C) engaged (D) engagement

**9.** The analyst was so ______ in the reconciliation spreadsheet that she completely missed the ten o'clock conference call.
(A) absorb (B) absorbing (C) absorbed (D) absorption

**10.** Investors appeared fully ______ of the product's long-term profitability after reviewing the latest sales figures.
(A) convince (B) convincing (C) convinced (D) conviction

**11.** After two years of steady growth, the startup is ______ to enter the European market by the end of next year.
(A) poise (B) poising (C) poised (D) poises

**12.** Given its strong logistics network, the firm is well ______ to benefit from the newly signed trade agreement.
(A) position (B) positioning (C) positioned (D) positions

**13.** The product rollout was ______ by technical glitches during its first week, leading to a wave of customer complaints.（第一空）As a result, the technical director's quarterly report is entirely ______ in data collected from the help desk logs.（第二空）
第一空：(A) plague (B) plaguing (C) plagued (D) plagues
第二空：(A) ground (B) grounding (C) grounded (D) grounds

**14.** The renovation of the downtown branch is ______ to begin in early September, pending final approval from the city planning office.
(A) slate (B) slating (C) slated (D) slates

**15.** The consulting firm is officially ______ with several major accounting networks, which allows it to serve clients across multiple countries.
(A) affiliate (B) affiliating (C) affiliated (D) affiliation
