# 05_未來式與未來完成式進階

## 核心概念
多益基礎題型只要求辨認「will + V」就是未來式，但中高分題目經常在**同一個未來時間軸上**放入好幾種不同的「未來家族」動詞形式──will、be going to、未來進行式、未來完成式、甚至現在進行式──要求考生依語境（有無證據、有無期限、是否已排定）挑出唯一合乎邏輯的形式。本章聚焦這些「未來家族」內部的辨析，以及它們在時間副詞子句與商業情境中的固定搭配，是第一章「未來簡單式」的進階延伸。

## 1. 未來簡單式 will：當下決定、承諾、無根據的預測
will 用於**說話當下才做的決定**、**當場的承諾或提議**，或是**沒有明顯根據的主觀預測**。
*   *At the moment of speaking*：The phone rings, and someone says, "I **will** get it."
*   **承諾／提議**："Don't worry, I **will** finish the slides tonight."

## 2. be going to：事先計畫、有明顯跡象的預測
be going to 用於**說話之前就已經存在的計畫**，或**根據眼前證據所做的預測**。
*   **已有計畫**：We **are going to** launch the new app next quarter. (計畫已排定)
*   **根據跡象預測**：Look at the order numbers — this product **is going to** sell out. (有具體證據)

> **辨析口訣**：句子若出現 based on / given / considering + 證據，或描述「事先安排」，優先選 be going to；句子若描述「當場才決定」的事，優先選 will。

## 3. 未來進行式（will be + V-ing）
表示**未來某個特定時間點正在進行中**的動作，也常用來讓語氣更**委婉、客觀**，聽起來不像刻意的決定，而像既定行程的自然發展。
*   **特定未來時間點**：This time next week, the negotiation team **will be meeting** with suppliers in Frankfurt.
*   **委婉語氣**：Ms. Alvarez **will be conducting** a workshop next month. (比 will conduct 更像既定行程，語氣更中性)

## 4. 未來完成式（will have + p.p.）
表示**到未來某個時間點（期限）為止，動作將已經完成**，常與 by、by the time、before 等搭配。
*   By the time the auditors arrive on Monday, the accounting team **will have reconciled** all the outstanding invoices.

## 5. 未來完成式 vs. 未來完成進行式
兩者都指向未來某期限，差別在於**強調結果／次數**還是**強調持續的過程**：
*   句中出現「次數」（three times, twice）或明確可數的成果 ➔ 用**未來完成式**：By March, the team **will have revised** the layout three times.
*   句中出現「for + 一段時間」，強調過程持續 ➔ 用**未來完成進行式**：By March, the team **will have been revising** the layout for six months.

## 6. 時間／條件子句中的「現在式代未來」規則（進階）
if, when, before, after, once, as soon as, by the time 等連接詞引導的**子句**，即使語意指向未來，動詞一律用**現在簡單式**；未來式或未來完成式只能出現在**主句**。
*   The finance director will submit the projections **as soon as** the branch offices **send** their final figures. (子句用現在式，主句才用未來式)
*   **By the time** the merger officially **takes effect**, both companies will have finalized their integration plans. (子句 takes effect 為現在式，主句才是未來完成式)

## 7. 商業情境中其他表達未來的正式用法
*   **be to V**：官方行程、正式公告常用，語氣比 will 更正式、更不容更改。
    *   According to the investor relations calendar, the earnings call **is to** take place next Thursday.
*   **be scheduled / set / due / slated to V**：商業英文中極常見的「準未來」片語，皆表「預定要」。
    *   The new plant **is set to** open in the third quarter, pending regulatory approval.
*   **現在進行式表已安排的未來**：強調「個人已確認的行程」，常搭配明確的未來時間點。
    *   Ms. Kim **is visiting** the Tokyo office next Tuesday to finalize the agreement.

## 8. was/were about to + V：過去視角下「原本即將要」發生的動作
表示「當時正要做某事」，動作常被 when 子句中的事件打斷，是 be about to 的過去式延伸用法。
*   The CFO **was about to** present the final figures when the fire alarm went off.

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **will vs. be going to**：will 用於「當下決定、承諾、無根據的預測」；be going to 用於「事先計畫、有明顯跡象的預測」。看到 based on / given / considering + 證據 ➔ 優先選 be going to。
2.  **未來進行式的委婉語氣**：will be + V-ing 除了表「未來某時刻進行中」，也常用來淡化語氣，讓陳述聽起來更客觀、不帶強迫意味，常見於商務書信與行程公告。
3.  **未來完成式的關鍵字**：by、by the time、before + 未來時間點 ➔ 優先考慮 will have + p.p.，強調「到那時已經完成」。
4.  **未來完成式 vs. 未來完成進行式**：句中出現「次數」（three times, twice）或明確結果 ➔ 用未來完成式；句中出現「for + 一段時間」且強調過程 ➔ 用未來完成進行式。
5.  **時間／條件子句一律用現在式代未來**：if, when, before, after, once, as soon as, by the time 等連接詞引導的子句，動詞一律用現在簡單式，未來式或未來完成式只能留給主句。
6.  **be to V 的正式語感**：官方行程、法規、正式公告常用 be to V 表達「已排定的未來」，語氣比 will 更正式。
7.  **be scheduled/set/due/slated to V**：這組「準未來」片語在商業英文中極常見，皆表「預定要」，考試常混在時態選項中誘答。
8.  **現在進行式表已安排的未來**：強調「個人已確認、已安排好」的行程，常搭配明確的未來時間點（next Tuesday, this weekend）。
9.  **was/were about to + V**：表「原本即將要做某事」，常與 when 子句搭配描述動作被打斷。
10. **未來完成式的被動語態**：will have been + p.p.，常考「未來期限前，某事將已被完成」，注意不要漏掉 been。
11. **篇章題中的未來標記詞**：starting/beginning + 未來日期、effective + 日期，常暗示後續句子應選現在簡單式（表既定事實）或未來完成式（表期限前完成）。
12. **否定未來完成式**：will not have + p.p. 常搭配 unless 子句，表「除非...，否則到期限前不會完成」，是商業情境常考的邏輯句型。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

### 表示「即將發生」的形容詞與動詞
*   **upcoming** adj. 即將到來的 | Please review the agenda for the *upcoming* board meeting.
*   **forthcoming** adj. 即將公布／提供的 | Details of the merger will be *forthcoming* within two weeks.
*   **imminent** adj. 迫在眉睫的 | The launch of the new product line is *imminent*.
*   **impending** adj. 即將發生的（常指負面） | Staff were notified of the *impending* office relocation.
*   **anticipate** v. 預期、預料 | The firm *anticipates* a sharp rise in demand next quarter.
*   **foresee** v. 預見 | Few executives *foresaw* how quickly the market would shift.
*   **forecast** v./n. 預測 | Economists *forecast* steady growth for the coming year.
*   **project** v. (及物) 預估、推算 | Analysts *project* that sales will climb 12 percent.
*   **envision** v. 展望、設想 | The CEO *envisions* doubling revenue within five years.
*   **estimate** v. 估計 | The contractor *estimated* that renovations would finish by June.
*   **prospective** adj. 未來的、可能成為...的 | The recruiter contacted several *prospective* candidates.

### 商業情境常用的未來片語
*   **slated to V** phr. 預定要 | The product is *slated to* launch in the third quarter.
*   **poised to V** phr. 準備好要、蓄勢待發 | The startup is *poised to* enter the European market.
*   **contingent upon** phr. 視...而定 | The bonus is *contingent upon* meeting the annual sales target.
*   **in the pipeline** phr. 籌備中、規劃中 | Several new features are currently *in the pipeline*.
*   **on the horizon** phr. 即將發生、在望 | A major policy change is *on the horizon* for the industry.
*   **in due course** phr. 屆時、到時候 | Further instructions will be provided *in due course*.
*   **at a later date** phr. 於稍後日期 | The board decided to announce the results *at a later date*.
*   **well in advance** phr. 提前很久 | Please submit travel requests *well in advance* of the trip.
*   **take effect** phr. （法規、政策）生效 | The new expense policy will *take effect* on August 1.
*   **kick off** phr. 展開、啟動 | The marketing campaign will *kick off* next Monday.
*   **commence** v. 開始 | Construction is expected to *commence* early next year.
*   **reschedule** v. 重新安排時間 | The client asked to *reschedule* the meeting to Friday.

### 描述進度、期限與結果的詞彙
*   **culminate in** phr. 以...達到高潮／告終 | Months of negotiation *culminated in* a signed partnership agreement.
*   **materialize** v. 實現、成真 | Investors are waiting to see whether the merger will *materialize*.
*   **expire** v. 到期、失效 | The current lease will *expire* at the end of the year.
*   **renewal** n. 續約、更新 | The vendor sent a *renewal* notice for the annual contract.
*   **deadline** n. 截止期限 | Employees must submit expense reports before the *deadline*.

## 💪 練習題（15 題）

**1.** When the printer suddenly jammed during the client presentation, the assistant said, "I ______ the technician right away."
(A) will call (B) am going to call (C) called (D) have called

**2.** Given the strong pre-order numbers, the product team is confident that the new tablet ______ well when it launches next month.
(A) sold (B) is going to sell (C) has sold (D) sells

**3.** This time next week, the negotiation team ______ with the overseas suppliers in Frankfurt to finalize contract terms.
(A) meets (B) has met (C) will be meeting (D) met

**4.** As part of the standing training calendar, Ms. Alvarez ______ a workshop on data privacy for new hires throughout next month.
(A) conducted (B) has conducted (C) conducts (D) will be conducting

**5.** By the time the auditors arrive on Monday, the accounting team ______ all the outstanding invoices.
(A) will have reconciled (B) reconciles (C) had reconciled (D) is reconciling

**6.** By the time the new distribution center opens in March, the logistics team ______ its layout three times to optimize workflow.
(A) will have been revising (B) will have revised (C) revises (D) is revising

**7.** The finance director will submit the regional projections as soon as the branch offices ______ their final figures.
(A) will send (B) would send (C) send (D) will have sent

**8.** According to the investor relations calendar, the quarterly earnings call ______ take place next Thursday at 10 a.m. Eastern Time.
(A) was to (B) had been to (C) would have been to (D) is to

**9.** The new manufacturing plant ______ open in the third quarter, pending final regulatory approval.
(A) is set to (B) has set to (C) was setting to (D) sets to

**10.** Ms. Kim ______ the Tokyo office next Tuesday to finalize the licensing agreement in person.
(A) visits (B) is visiting (C) will have visited (D) was visiting

**11.** By the time the shareholders meet next month, the revised bylaws ______ by the legal department.
(A) will review (B) are reviewing (C) will have been reviewed (D) reviewed

**12.** To All Department Heads: Starting next Monday, the new expense-reporting platform (1) ______ live company-wide. By the end of that same week, every department (2) ______ at least one training session on the new system, so please block time on your calendars accordingly.

第一空：(A) went (B) will go (C) is going to go (D) goes
第二空：(A) will have completed (B) completes (C) is completing (D) had completed

**13.** Unless the supplier resolves the shipping delay soon, the flagship product ______ its holiday sales target by December 25.
(A) does not meet (B) will not have met (C) has not met (D) will not meet

**14.** The CFO ______ present the final budget figures when the fire alarm suddenly went off, forcing an evacuation.
(A) is about to (B) has been about to (C) was about to (D) will be about to

**15.** To close the deal today, the sales representative said, "Our company ______ offer you an additional five percent discount if you sign before Friday."
(A) was going to (B) is going to (C) would (D) will
