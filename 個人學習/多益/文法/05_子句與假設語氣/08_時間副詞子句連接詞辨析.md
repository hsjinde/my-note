# 08_時間副詞子句連接詞辨析

## 核心概念
多益文法題常把 `by the time`、`as soon as`、`once`、`until`、`since` 這五個時間副詞子句連接詞放在一起考，因為它們中文翻譯常常都能勉強套用「當...時／一...就...」，但**語意重點與時態搭配完全不同**。本章聚焦連接詞本身的辨析，而非單純的動詞時態變化（動詞時態請參考單獨的時態章節）。

### 1. by the time：強調「到某個時間點為止，動作已完成」
`by the time` 引導的子句描述一個**參考時間點**，主句動詞通常用**完成式**（過去完成式或未來完成式），強調在該時間點之前某事已經／將會完成。
- *By the time* the shipment **clears** customs, the retailer **will have restocked** its shelves through another supplier.
- *By the time* the audit **was announced**, the accounting team **had already reconciled** every account.

### 2. as soon as：強調「幾乎沒有間隔的立即接續」
`as soon as` 著重「A 一發生，B 馬上跟著發生」，兩個動作之間**幾乎沒有時間差**，子句用現在式代替未來式（若主句是未來時態）。
- *As soon as* the vendor **confirms** the shipping date, procurement **will notify** the warehouse team.

### 3. once：強調「條件一旦成立，後續就會發生／持續生效」
`once` 語氣介於「時間」與「條件」之間，重點不在速度，而在「這件事一旦成立，就是一個新狀態的起點」，常見於核准、簽署、生效類句子，且很自然接**被動語態**。
- *Once* the contract **is ratified** by both parties, the terms become legally binding.

### 4. until：強調「持續到某個終點為止（之前不會結束/不會發生）」
`until` 描述一個**動作或狀態持續的終點**，常搭配延續性動詞（remain, continue, stay, not + V）。「not... until」是常考句型，意為「直到...才」。
- The negotiation **will continue** *until* both sides **reach** a mutual agreement.
- *Not until* the inspection **is complete** **will** the factory **resume** production.（倒裝句型，見下方公式）

### 5. since：強調「從某個過去時間點到現在」，主句幾乎必為現在完成式
`since` 作連接詞時，子句用**過去簡單式**標示起點，主句用**現在完成式**（或現在完成進行式）表示動作持續到現在。`since` 也可作介系詞直接接時間名詞（*since 2020*），此時後面不能接完整子句。
- Operating costs **have declined** steadily *since* the company **switched** to renewable energy.

### 6. 五個連接詞的核心對照表
| 連接詞 | 語意核心 | 常見主句時態 | 子句時態 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| by the time | 到某時間點為止已完成 | 未來完成式／過去完成式 | 現在式／過去式 |
| as soon as | 幾乎零時間差的立即接續 | 未來式／現在式 | 現在式（不用 will） |
| once | 條件一旦成立即生效 | 未來式／現在式 | 現在式／現在完成式 |
| until | 持續到某終點為止 | 未來式／現在式／過去式 | 現在式／過去式 |
| since | 從過去某點延續至今 | 現在完成式 | 過去簡單式 |

## 📝 多益秒殺解題技巧
1. 看到主句是 **未來完成式 (will have p.p.)** 或 **過去完成式 (had p.p.)** ➔ 優先選 **by the time**。
2. 看到主句是 **現在完成式 (has/have p.p.)** 且子句是過去簡單式的一個明確起點 ➔ 選 **since**。
3. 句子強調「馬上、立刻」且沒有持續或完成的語感 ➔ 選 **as soon as**，不要跟 once 搞混。
4. 句子在講「核准、簽署、生效」之後帶來一個新狀態 ➔ 通常是 **once**。
5. 句子強調「一直到...才結束／才發生」，尤其搭配 not、remain、continue ➔ 選 **until**。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語
### 時序與延續
*   **elapse** (v. 不及物) （時間）消逝、過去 | Several weeks **elapsed** before the supplier responded to the complaint.
*   **lapse** (n.) （時間的）間隔、流逝；(v.) 失效 | There was a two-week **lapse** between the proposal and its approval.
*   **interim** (adj./n.) 過渡期間的；期中 | An **interim** manager was appointed until a permanent director was hired.
*   **meanwhile** (adv.) 在此同時 | The lead engineer is reviewing the design; **meanwhile**, the team is preparing the prototype.
*   **thereafter** (adv.) 此後 | The policy takes effect in March; **thereafter**, all invoices must follow the new format.
*   **henceforth** (adv.) 從今以後 | **Henceforth**, all expense claims must be submitted through the online portal.
*   **hitherto** (adv.) 迄今為止（尚未） | The department had **hitherto** relied on manual inventory checks.
*   **precede** (v. 及物) 先於、在...之前發生 | A brief safety briefing **precedes** every factory tour.
*   **subsequent** (adj.) 隨後的、接下來的 | The **subsequent** quarter saw a sharp rise in overseas orders.
*   **concurrently** (adv.) 同時地 | The two product lines were developed **concurrently** to save launch time.
*   **simultaneously** (adv.) 同時地 | Both branches upgraded their systems **simultaneously** to avoid downtime.
*   **intermittent** (adj.) 間歇性的 | **Intermittent** delays in the supply chain frustrated the logistics team.

### 期限與時效
*   **deadline** (n.) 截止期限 | Missing the submission **deadline** could delay the entire launch.
*   **punctual** (adj.) 準時的 | Suppliers are expected to be **punctual** with every delivery.
*   **timely** (adj.) 及時的 | A **timely** response from the vendor prevented a production halt.
*   **overdue** (adj.) 逾期的、過期未付的 | Several invoices remain **overdue** despite repeated reminders.
*   **pending** (adj.) 待定的、未決的 | The contract renewal is still **pending** legal review.
*   **expire** (v. 不及物) 到期、失效 | The current lease will **expire** at the end of the fiscal year.
*   **expiration** (n.) 到期、期滿 | Staff must renew their access badges before **expiration**.
*   **retroactive** (adj.) 追溯既往的 | The salary adjustment is **retroactive** to the start of the quarter.
*   **timeframe** (n.) 時間框架、期限範圍 | The consultant proposed a realistic **timeframe** for the migration project.
*   **protracted** (adj.) 拖延已久的 | A **protracted** dispute with the supplier delayed the shipment for months.

### 進度與轉折
*   **milestone** (n.) 里程碑、重大進展 | Reaching one million users was a major **milestone** for the platform.
*   **backlog** (n.) 積壓的工作、未處理事項 | The support team is working through a large **backlog** of tickets.
*   **contingent** (adj.) 視情況而定的、有條件的 | The bonus payment is **contingent** on meeting the quarterly target.
*   **expedite** (v. 及物) 加速、促進 | The manager asked the courier to **expedite** the delivery.
*   **culminate** (v. 不及物) 最終形成、達到高潮 | Months of negotiation **culminated** in a signed partnership agreement.
*   **imminent** (adj.) 即將發生的、迫近的 | With the merger **imminent**, employees braced for organizational changes.
*   **up until** (片語) 直到...之前（一直） | **Up until** last year, the firm outsourced all of its IT support.
*   **in the meantime** (片語) 在此期間 | The new system will launch next month; **in the meantime**, please use the old portal.

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **since 子句用過去式，主句用現在完成式**：這是本章最常考的固定搭配，看到 since 引導完整子句，主句幾乎不會是簡單過去式。
    *   Revenue **has grown** by 20 percent *since* the firm **launched** its online store.
2.  **since 作介系詞後面只能接名詞/時間點，不能接完整子句**：`since 2019`、`since last spring` 是介系詞用法，`since the company was founded` 才是連接詞用法。
3.  **not until + 倒裝句**：`Not until + 子句` 放句首時，主句須主詞助動詞倒裝。
    *   **Not until** the final report is submitted **will** the project be considered closed.
4.  **by the time 常與未來完成式連用**：主句描述「到那時已經完成」，常見 will have p.p.。
    *   *By the time* the new hires finish orientation, the manager **will have prepared** their workstations.
5.  **once 之後常接被動語態，表示「一旦被...」**：once approved / once signed / once confirmed 是高頻搭配。
    *   **Once** the budget **is finalized**, department heads may begin allocating funds.
6.  **as soon as 不能拿來表示長期延續**：如果句子講的是「持續到...」而非「立刻」，就不能選 as soon as，該選 until。
7.  **until 後接時間點名詞時可當介系詞**：`until Friday`、`until the end of the month`，此時後面不接完整子句。
8.  **by 與 by the time 的差別**：`by + 時間點`（介系詞片語）表示「不遲於某時間」；`by the time + 子句`才是連接詞用法，兩者常混選。
9.  **once 也可作副詞，意思是「曾經、一度」**：與連接詞用法（一旦）需依句意判斷，此處只考連接詞用法。
10. **since 表原因時不接完成式規則**：注意 since 也可以表示「因為」，此時不受「主句須為完成式」限制；判斷時要看句意是「時間」還是「原因」。
11. **elapse / lapse 常搭配時間副詞子句作為主詞**：`Several months elapsed before...`，是 by the time 家族的變形考法。
12. **milestone、backlog、contingent 常出現在時間子句句尾，測驗字彙+子句雙重理解**：讀懂句意才能選對連接詞。

## 💪 練習題（15 題）
**1.** ______ the shipment clears customs next week, the retailer will have already restocked its shelves through a backup supplier.
(A) Since (B) By the time (C) As soon as (D) Until

**2.** Procurement will notify the warehouse team ______ the vendor confirms the shipping date.
(A) since (B) until (C) as soon as (D) by the time

**3.** Operating costs have declined steadily ______ the company switched to a renewable energy provider.
(A) since (B) once (C) until (D) by the time

**4.** ______ the merger agreement is ratified by both boards, the two firms will begin integrating their payroll systems.
(A) Since (B) Once (C) As soon as (D) Until

**5.** The customer support hotline will remain open ______ every backlog ticket from the outage has been resolved.
(A) since (B) once (C) until (D) by the time

**6.** ______ 2019, the firm has outsourced its overseas logistics to a third-party carrier.
(A) Since (B) Once (C) Until (D) As soon as

**7.** The negotiation team had already drafted three revisions ______ the client finally approved the final wording.
(A) since (B) by the time (C) until (D) once

**8.** **第一空：** The finance director insists that no reimbursement be processed ______ the receipts are verified. **第二空：** ______ verification is complete, the payment will be released within two business days.
(A) until (B) once (C) since (D) as soon as

**9.** A brief safety briefing precedes the factory tour, and ______ it concludes, visitors may proceed to the assembly line.
(A) since (B) until (C) once (D) by the time

**10.** It has been nearly three years ______ the branch first opened its doors to overseas clients.
(A) since (B) until (C) once (D) by the time

**11.** ______ the quarterly report is finalized, department heads should refrain from releasing preliminary figures to the press.
(A) Since (B) Until (C) As soon as (D) By the time

**12.** The design team worked through the night, and ______ the prototype was ready, the marketing materials had already been printed.
(A) since (B) until (C) by the time (D) once

**13.** ______ the supplier notifies us of a delay, we will immediately inform the affected retail partners.
(A) Until (B) Since (C) As soon as (D) By the time

**14.** Employees are asked to keep their badges active ______ their contract officially expires.
(A) since (B) until (C) once (D) as soon as

**15.** **第一空：** The onboarding process has changed considerably ______ the company adopted the new HR platform last spring. **第二空：** ______ new hires complete the digital orientation, their access credentials are activated automatically.
(A) since (B) until (C) once (D) by the time
