# 03_形容詞與副詞

## 核心概念
形容詞與副詞是多益詞性題的核心。**形容詞**修飾名詞；**副詞**修飾動詞、形容詞或其他副詞。辨別兩者的關鍵在於「它修飾的對象是什麼」。

## 1. 必背形容詞字尾 (字尾判斷法) 🌟

| 字尾 | 例字 |
| :--- | :--- |
| **-ful** | successful, helpful, useful |
| **-less** | careless, hopeless, effortless |
| **-ous / -ious** | numerous, serious, previous |
| **-al / -ial** | annual, financial, additional |
| **-ive / -ative** | effective, productive, creative |
| **-able / -ible** | available, responsible, flexible |
| **-ent / -ant** | efficient, significant, relevant |
| **-ing / -ed** | (情緒動詞) interesting / interested |

## 2. 必背副詞字尾

*   最常見：**形容詞 + -ly** ➔ quickly, efficiently, carefully
*   注意：不是所有 -ly 結尾的字都是副詞！(friendly, costly, timely 是**形容詞**)
*   常見不加 -ly 的副詞：`hard`, `fast`, `well`, `late`, `early`, `high`
    *   He worked **hard**. (副詞) / a **hard** worker. (形容詞)
    *   ⚠️ `hardly` (幾乎不) ≠ `hard` (努力地)；`lately` (最近) ≠ `late` (晚)

## 3. 形容詞 vs. 副詞的位置 (多益考點) 🌟

| 詞性 | 修飾對象 | 常見位置 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **形容詞** | 名詞 | **名詞前**、連綴動詞 (be/seem/remain) **後** |
| **副詞** | 動詞、形容詞、副詞 | 動詞前後、形容詞/副詞**前** |

*   **形容詞位置**：
    *   a **successful** *launch* (名詞前)
    *   The plan seems **effective**. (連綴動詞後，補語)
*   **副詞位置**：
    *   The project was completed **efficiently**. (修飾動詞)
    *   She did an **extremely** *good* job. (修飾形容詞)

## 4. 多益常混淆組合

| 形容詞 | 副詞 | 辨別要點 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| high (高的) | highly (非常、高度地) | *highly* qualified (非常有資格的) |
| near (附近的) | nearly (幾乎) | *nearly* finished (幾乎完成) |
| hard (努力的、困難的) | hardly (幾乎不) | *hard*ly *any* rain (幾乎沒有下雨) |
| late (晚的) | lately (最近) | I've been busy *lately*. |

## 📝 多益秒殺解題技巧
1.  **空格前有冠詞 (a/the) 或後有名詞** ➔ 秒選**形容詞**。
2.  **空格後修飾動詞 (如 work, complete, run)** ➔ 秒選**副詞** (-ly)。
3.  **空格修飾空格後的形容詞** (如 ___ effective) ➔ 秒選**副詞**。
4.  **看到連綴動詞 (seem, remain, become) 後面的空格** ➔ 秒選**形容詞**，不選副詞。

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **數量形容詞陷阱**：`much/little` 修飾不可數名詞、`many/few` 修飾可數名詞，`a lot of/lots of` 兩者皆可，注意可數與不可數的搭配。
2.  **分詞形容詞方向判斷**：`-ing`（主動、令人感到）用於「事物」，`-ed`（被動、感受到）用於「人」。如 `an interesting book`（書令人感興趣）vs. `an interested reader`（讀者感到興趣）。
3.  **易混淆副詞位置**：`enough` 當副詞須放在形容詞**之後**（如 `good enough`），但當形容詞則放在名詞**之前**（如 `enough time`）。
4.  **頻率副詞位置**：`always, usually, often, rarely` 等頻率副詞一般放在**一般動詞前、be動詞/助動詞後**，如 `He usually arrives early.` / `She is always punctual.`
5.  **only / even 等限定副詞**：修飾的對象通常是**緊接在後面**的字詞，位置移動會改變句意，考題常用來測試考生對修飾對象的判斷。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語
### 常考形容詞
*   **considerable** 形容詞 相當大的 | The project required a *considerable* amount of time.
*   **reliable** 形容詞 可靠的 | We need a *reliable* supplier for this material.
*   **comprehensive** 形容詞 全面的 | The manual provides a *comprehensive* guide to the software.
*   **noticeable** 形容詞 顯著的 | There has been a *noticeable* increase in sales.
*   **considerate** 形容詞 體貼的（易與 considerable 混淆） | He is always *considerate* toward his colleagues.

### 常考副詞
*   **approximately** 副詞 大約地 | The meeting will last *approximately* two hours.
*   **significantly** 副詞 顯著地 | Revenue increased *significantly* this quarter.
*   **accordingly** 副詞 相應地 | Please review the schedule and plan *accordingly*.
*   **currently** 副詞 目前 | The position is *currently* unavailable.
*   **relatively** 副詞 相對地 | The cost is *relatively* low compared to competitors.

### 常混淆組
*   **economic / economical** 形容詞 經濟(學)的 / 節省的 | *economic* growth / an *economical* car
*   **respective / respectively** 形容詞/副詞 個別的 / 分別地 | They returned to their *respective* offices. / They arrived at 9 and 10, *respectively*.

## 💪 練習題（5 題）

**1.** The training session was ___ helpful for new employees.
(A) extreme (B) extremely (C) extremity (D) extremist

**2.** Customers found the new interface ___ confusing at first.
(A) somewhat (B) some (C) something (D) somewhere

**3.** The report showed a ___ decline in customer complaints after the policy change.
(A) notice (B) noticing (C) noticeable (D) noticeably

**4.** Employees who arrive ___ are asked to inform their supervisors in advance.
(A) late (B) lately (C) latest (D) later

**5.** The marketing team conducted a survey to gather ___ feedback from customers.（第一空）The results were ___ positive, prompting the company to expand the product line.（第二空）
第一空：(A) detail (B) detailed (C) details (D) detailing
第二空：(A) overwhelm (B) overwhelmed (C) overwhelming (D) overwhelmingly
