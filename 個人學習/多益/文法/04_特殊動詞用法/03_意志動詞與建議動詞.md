# 03_意志動詞與建議動詞

## 核心概念
意志動詞 (Verbs of Volition) 與建議動詞 (Verbs of Suggestion) 後面接的 that 子句中，動詞必須用**原形動詞 (should + V 或直接省略 should，稱為「假設現在式」/「虛擬語氣現在式」)**。這是多益必考的重要文法點！

## 1. 常見的意志動詞與建議動詞 🌟

這些動詞後面的 `that 子句`，動詞要用**原形動詞**：

| 類別 | 動詞 |
| :--- | :--- |
| **意志/命令** | insist, demand, require, order, command |
| **建議/請求** | suggest, recommend, propose, request, urge, ask |
| **決定** | decide (有時適用) |

## 2. 句型結構 🌟

**公式**：`S + 意志/建議動詞 + that + S + (should) + 原形動詞`

注意：`should` **可以省略**，但原形動詞必須保留，即使主詞是第三人稱單數也**不加 -s**。

| 動詞 | 正確範例 | 說明 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **suggest** | The manager suggested that he **submit** the report. | 不是 submits 或 submitted |
| **recommend** | I recommend that she **attend** the training. | 不是 attends |
| **require** | The policy requires that all employees **wear** ID badges. | 不是 wears |
| **insist** | He insisted that the meeting **be** held immediately. | be，不是 is |
| **demand** | The client demanded that we **revise** the proposal. | 不是 revises |
| **request** | She requested that the deadline **be** extended. | be，被動也用原形 |

## 3. 名詞形式也適用
相同的動詞若變成名詞，之後的 that 子句同樣使用原形動詞。
*   *His* **suggestion** *that the meeting be postponed* was accepted.
*   *The* **requirement** *that all applicants submit a portfolio* is strict.

## 4. 形容詞形式 (It is + adj. + that ...)
以下形容詞後面的 that 子句，同樣需要原形動詞：
*   `important, necessary, essential, vital, crucial, advisable, imperative, urgent`
    *   It is **essential** that everyone **be** present at the meeting.
    *   It is **important** that she **submit** the form before the deadline.

## 📝 多益秒殺解題技巧
1.  看到 `suggest, recommend, require, insist, demand, request` 後面有 that 子句 ➔ that 子句的動詞秒選**原形動詞**，不管主詞是誰。
2.  看到 `It is important/necessary/essential that...` ➔ 子句動詞秒選**原形動詞**。
3.  **陷阱**：`suggest` 和 `recommend` 還可以接 `V-ing` (不接 that 子句時)。
    *   I suggest **reviewing** the report. (V-ing，沒有 that 子句) ✓
    *   I suggest that she **review** the report. (that 子句，原形動詞) ✓

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **insist 的雙重用法**：insist 除了表示「堅持要求」(接原形動詞) 外，也可表示「堅稱、堅持主張某事實」，此時 that 子句要用**正常時態**，不用原形動詞。例如：He insisted that he **was** innocent. (堅稱事實，非要求)
2.  **否定句陷阱**：原形動詞的否定形式是 `not + 原形動詞`，不要誤加 don't/doesn't。例如：The policy requires that employees **not use** personal devices at work.
3.  **be 動詞的原形是 be**：無論主詞單複數，一律用 **be**，這是最容易被考的細節，例如 It is essential that the report **be** accurate.
4.  **advise、propose 也適用**：除了常見清單外，`advise, propose, move (提議)` 後接 that 子句時同樣使用原形動詞，容易被忽略。
5.  **陷阱**：`recommend/suggest + that + S + V (原形)` 中，若選項出現第三人稱單數 -s 或過去式，都是錯誤選項，只要看到觸發詞就直接刪除這些選項。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

### 意志/命令類動詞
- **insist** (v.) 堅持 | The client **insisted** that the order be delivered by Friday.
- **demand** (v.) 要求 | The union **demanded** that management raise wages.
- **require** (v.) 要求、規定 | The law **requires** that all drivers carry insurance.
- **command** (v.) 命令 | The general **commanded** that the troops withdraw immediately.

### 建議/請求類動詞
- **suggest** (v.) 建議 | The doctor **suggested** that she rest for a week.
- **recommend** (v.) 推薦、建議 | We **recommend** that applicants submit their forms early.
- **propose** (v.) 提議 | He **proposed** that the meeting be rescheduled.
- **urge** (v.) 敦促 | The manager **urged** that the team finish the project on time.
- **advise** (v.) 建議、勸告 | The consultant **advised** that the company invest in training.

### 形容詞 (It is + adj. + that...)
- **imperative** (adj.) 必要的、緊急的 | It is **imperative** that the issue be resolved today.
- **advisable** (adj.) 明智的、可取的 | It is **advisable** that new staff attend orientation.

### 名詞形式
- **requirement** (n.) 要求、規定 | The **requirement** that all staff wear badges is strictly enforced.
- **recommendation** (n.) 建議 | Her **recommendation** that the budget be increased was approved.
