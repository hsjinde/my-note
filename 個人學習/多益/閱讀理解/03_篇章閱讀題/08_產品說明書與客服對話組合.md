# 08_產品說明書與客服對話組合

## 📝 答題策略
1.  **說明書類文章先看標題與分節**：產品說明書常以「Setup」「Maintenance」「Troubleshooting」等小標分節，先掃過小標可快速定位細節題答案所在段落。
2.  **步驟型內容注意順序詞**：說明書中的 first、before、once、then 等順序詞是細節題與推論題的常見陷阱，須確認事件先後關係。
3.  **客服信件看信件走向**：一來一往的客服對話要先分清楚「顧客提出的問題」與「客服的回覆／處理方式」，避免張冠李戴。
4.  **主旨題留意文件目的而非單一步驟**：說明書或客服信的主旨通常是整份文件的功能（如「說明如何組裝」「回覆客訴並提供補償」），不是其中一個小細節。
5.  **推論題找「言外之意」**：正確答案須是文章合理帶出的結論，而非文章逐字寫出的內容，也不能超出文章邏輯範圍。
6.  **字義題務必代入原句情境**：同一單字在說明書與客服信中的用法可能不同，須依上下文判斷最貼切的意思。

## 文章：產品說明書節錄

**Model AX-220 Space Heater — User Guide (Excerpt)**

**Initial Setup**

Before turning on the heater for the first time, remove all packaging materials from the unit, including the plastic film covering the control panel. Place the heater on a flat, level surface at least 60 centimeters away from curtains, furniture, or any flammable materials. Do not operate the heater on carpeted surfaces unless the included stability base is attached, as this could cause the unit to tip over.

**Operation**

Press and hold the power button for two seconds to turn on the heater. The display will show the current room temperature; use the up and down arrows to set your desired temperature between 15°C and 30°C. The heater will automatically shut off once the room reaches the set temperature and will restart if the temperature drops more than 2°C below that setting. For safety, the unit is also equipped with a tip-over switch that immediately cuts power if the heater is knocked over.

**Maintenance**

Unplug the heater and allow it to cool completely before cleaning. Wipe the exterior with a dry cloth only; never use water or liquid cleaners, as moisture entering the vents may damage internal components. The air filter behind the rear grille should be removed and rinsed with water every three months; reinstall it only after it is fully dry. Using the heater with a wet or clogged filter may reduce heating efficiency and trigger the overheat protection feature.

**Troubleshooting**

If the unit shuts off unexpectedly and the display shows an "E1" error, this generally indicates the overheat protection has activated. Allow the heater to cool for at least 30 minutes before restarting. If the error persists, check that the air filter is clean and that nothing is blocking the front or rear vents.

## 文章：客服信件往來

From: t.alvarez@mailhub.com
To: support@brightwave-appliances.com
Subject: Heater Making Rattling Noise

Hello,

I purchased a Model AX-220 space heater about six weeks ago, and for the past few days it has been making a rattling noise whenever the fan turns on. The noise seems to come from inside the unit, and it happens even when the heater is sitting on a flat, hard floor. I have already checked that nothing is blocking the vents. Could you let me know what might be causing this and whether it needs to be repaired or replaced?

Thank you,
Teresa Alvarez

From: support@brightwave-appliances.com
To: t.alvarez@mailhub.com
Subject: RE: Heater Making Rattling Noise

Dear Ms. Alvarez,

Thank you for reaching out, and we're sorry to hear about the noise you've been experiencing. Based on your description, this is most likely caused by a loose internal fan blade, which is a known issue affecting a small number of units from this production batch. This is not something that can be fixed by the customer, so we recommend against attempting to open the unit yourself, as doing so would void the remaining warranty.

Since your heater is still within the one-year warranty period, we would like to arrange a free replacement rather than a repair, as replacement units from a later production batch do not have this issue. To proceed, please reply with your order number and shipping address, and we will send a prepaid return label along with the new unit. You may continue using your current heater until the replacement arrives, but please turn it off immediately if the rattling becomes louder or is accompanied by any burning smell.

We appreciate your patience, and please let us know if you have any further questions.

Best regards,
Customer Support Team
Brightwave Appliances
