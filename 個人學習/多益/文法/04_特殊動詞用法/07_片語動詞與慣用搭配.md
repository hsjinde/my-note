# 07_片語動詞與慣用搭配

## 核心概念
片語動詞（phrasal verb）是「動詞 + 介係詞/副詞」組合出全新意義的用法，意思往往無法從單字字面直接猜出，是多益 Part 5、Part 6 商業文章中極高頻的考點。這一章聚焦在辦公室、會議、人資、行銷、財務、物流情境中最常見的片語動詞，並整理其及物/不及物特性與常見搭配介系詞。

## 1. 片語動詞的兩大類型
- **可分離型（動詞+副詞，受詞可插入中間）**：如 carry out, set up, look into 常以「動詞+受詞+副詞」或「動詞+副詞+受詞」皆可，但受詞若是代名詞則**必須插入中間**。
  *例*：The manager **carried** the plan **out** successfully. / The manager **carried out** the plan successfully. / The manager **carried it out**.（不可說 carried out it）
- **不可分離型（動詞+介係詞，受詞只能放最後）**：如 look into, deal with, come up with，受詞一律接在片語動詞之後，不可插入中間。
  *例*：The committee will **look into** the complaint.（不可說 look the complaint into）

## 2. 常見商業片語動詞分類
- **調查/處理類**：look into（調查）、deal with（處理）、cope with（應付）、attend to（處理、照料）
- **建立/啟動類**：set up（建立、設立）、carry out（執行）、bring about（促成）、roll out（推出）
- **提出/發想類**：come up with（想出）、put forward（提出）、draw up（草擬）
- **取消/延後類**：call off（取消）、put off（延後）、hold off on（暫緩）
- **淘汰/縮減類**：phase out（逐步淘汰）、cut back on（削減）、scale back（縮減規模）
- **接手/移交類**：take over（接管）、hand over（移交）、pass on（轉交）

## 3. 常考的固定搭配介系詞
許多動詞後方固定搭配特定介系詞，介系詞一旦用錯，整句語意會偏移，是多益選項陷阱的最愛。
- **account for**（說明、佔比）vs. **account to**（向…報告，較少用）
- **comply with**（遵守）— 只能接 with，不能接 to
- **result in**（導致）vs. **result from**（源自於）— 方向相反，考試常互換選項誤導
- **apply for**（申請職位/項目）vs. **apply to**（適用於）

## 4. 動詞 + 介系詞 + V-ing
片語動詞之後若接介系詞，後面若再接動詞，必須用 **V-ing**，不可用不定詞 to V。
*例*：The team **succeeded in launching** the product ahead of schedule.
*例*：Employees should **refrain from using** company devices for personal matters.（refrain from 已在既有單字庫，此處僅作句型示範）

## 5. 名詞化陷阱
片語動詞常有對應的名詞形式（多為複合名詞），選擇題常同時出現動詞片語與其名詞化選項讓考生混淆，需依空格前後的詞性（是否需要主詞/受詞位置的名詞）判斷。
*例*：The **rollout** of the new system was delayed.（名詞，作主詞）vs. The company will **roll out** the new system next month.（動詞片語）

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

### 調查/處理類
*   **deal with** (phr. v.) 處理 | The support team is trained to **deal with** difficult customers.
*   **cope with** (phr. v.) 應付、承受 | The warehouse struggled to **cope with** the surge in holiday orders.
*   **attend to** (phr. v.) 處理、照料 | Please **attend to** the client's request as soon as possible.
*   **sort out** (phr. v.) 解決、釐清 | The IT staff finally **sorted out** the login issue.
*   **iron out** (phr. v.) 解決（細節上的問題）| The two firms met to **iron out** the remaining contract details.

### 建立/啟動/執行類
*   **set up** (phr. v.) 建立、設立 | The founder **set up** the company in 2015.
*   **carry out** (phr. v.) 執行、實施 | The consultant was hired to **carry out** the market survey.
*   **bring about** (phr. v.) 促成、導致 | The new policy **brought about** a sharp rise in productivity.
*   **roll out** (phr. v.) 推出、發布 | The firm plans to **roll out** the update next quarter.
*   **carry on** (phr. v.) 繼續進行 | Despite the outage, the staff **carried on** with the meeting.

### 提出/發想類
*   **come up with** (phr. v.) 想出 | The design team **came up with** a creative solution.
*   **put forward** (phr. v.) 提出（建議、方案）| The board **put forward** a proposal to cut costs.
*   **draw up** (phr. v.) 草擬 | The lawyer was asked to **draw up** the contract.
*   **work out** (phr. v.) 想出、算出、順利進行 | The two sides finally **worked out** an agreement.

### 取消/延後/淘汰類
*   **call off** (phr. v.) 取消 | Organizers had to **call off** the outdoor event due to rain.
*   **put off** (phr. v.) 延後 | The launch was **put off** until the supply issue was resolved.
*   **hold off on** (phr. v.) 暫緩 | Management decided to **hold off on** hiring until next quarter.
*   **phase out** (phr. v.) 逐步淘汰 | The manufacturer will **phase out** the older model by year-end.
*   **cut back on** (phr. v.) 削減 | The department was told to **cut back on** travel expenses.
*   **scale back** (phr. v.) 縮減規模 | The firm decided to **scale back** its overseas operations.

### 接手/移交/合作類
*   **take over** (phr. v.) 接管 | A larger corporation **took over** the struggling startup.
*   **pass on** (phr. v.) 轉交、傳遞 | Please **pass on** this message to the finance team.
*   **team up with** (phr. v.) 與…合作 | The agency **teamed up with** a local supplier for the project.
*   **team up** (phr. v.) 組隊合作 | The two departments **teamed up** to launch the initiative.

### 固定搭配介系詞
*   **account for** (v.) 說明、佔（比例）| Shipping costs **account for** nearly 20 percent of total expenses.
*   **comply with** (v.) 遵守 | All vendors must **comply with** the safety regulations.
*   **apply for** (v.) 申請 | Candidates should **apply for** the position online.
*   **apply to** (v.) 適用於 | This discount **applies to** first-time customers only.

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **可分離 vs. 不可分離判斷法**：若空格後方直接接代名詞（it, them），可分離片語動詞（carry out, set up）的代名詞必須夾在中間；不可分離片語動詞（look into, deal with）則代名詞直接接在後方，不能拆開。
2.  **result in / result from 方向鎖定**：result **in** 後接「結果」，result **from** 後接「原因」，看空格前後何者是原因、何者是結果即可秒選。
3.  **account for 兩種意思**：接數字/比例時譯為「佔」，接原因時譯為「說明、解釋」，兩種用法都常出現在財務報告類文章。
4.  **comply with 不可用 comply to**：多益陷阱選項常放 comply to 或 comply of，只有 with 是正確搭配。
5.  **片語動詞 + V-ing**：succeed in, refrain from, insist on 等後方若再接動詞，一律用 V-ing，不可用原形或 to V。
6.  **名詞化選項排除法**：若空格前有助動詞（will, should, must）或空格本身要作謂語動詞，優先選片語動詞本身（如 roll out），若空格是主詞或受詞位置，才考慮名詞化形式（rollout）。
7.  **call off vs. put off 語意差異**：call off 語氣偏向「完全取消」，put off 則是「延後、擇期再辦」，文意題常以此區分正確選項。
8.  **cut back on / scale back 後接名詞**：這兩個片語表示「縮減」，後方通常接支出、規模等名詞，常與 reduce, decrease 互為同義詞選項。
9.  **team up with 的固定搭配**：team up 之後如果要接合作對象，必須加 with，不能直接接受詞。
10. **phase out 的被動用法**：常以被動語態出現，如 "The old machines will be phased out by next year."，考生需認出被動片語動詞整體不可拆散意義。
11. **draw up 與 draft 的替換**：draw up（草擬合約、計畫）常與 draft 同義互換出現在選項中，需依詞性（draw up 為動詞片語，draft 亦可作動詞）判斷句子結構是否成立。
12. **iron out 限定用於細節問題**：iron out 通常搭配 details, differences, issues 等字，而非用於重大危機，语意選擇題常以此區分 iron out 與 deal with。

## 💪 練習題（15 題）

**1.** The compliance team will ______ the anonymous complaint before the end of the week.
(A) look into (B) look for (C) look up (D) look after

**2.** Because the client was a longtime partner, the account manager handled the negotiation and worked it ______ personally rather than delegating it.
(A) up (B) out (C) off (D) over

**3.** The two logistics companies decided to ______ in order to reduce shipping costs across the region.
(A) team up (B) hand over (C) call off (D) look into

**4.** Rising fuel prices largely ______ the increase in the company's shipping expenses last quarter.
(A) accounted for (B) accounted to (C) resulted from (D) complied with

**5.** All contractors working on the site must ______ the updated safety regulations issued last month.
(A) comply to (B) comply with (C) comply of (D) comply for

**6.** Due to the sudden staff shortage, the marketing director had to ______ the product launch until next month.
(A) call off (B) put off (C) carry out (D) phase out

**7.** After months of research, the engineering team finally ______ a design that met all client requirements.
(A) came up with (B) put forward (C) took over (D) drew up

**8.** The finance department plans to ______ several outdated reporting procedures once the new software is fully installed. Employees who rely on the old forms will need to switch to the digital system before it is ______ completely by year-end.
第一空：(A) phase out (B) call off (C) hand over (D) team up with
第二空：(A) phased out (B) called off (C) handed over (D) teamed up with

**9.** The outgoing regional manager will ______ all client files to her successor before her last day.
(A) hand over (B) look into (C) draw up (D) cut back on

**10.** Because of falling demand, headquarters instructed each branch to ______ non-essential spending immediately.
(A) cut back on (B) succeed in (C) apply for (D) account for

**11.** The legal team was asked to ______ the merger agreement before the shareholders' meeting.
(A) draw up (B) call off (C) rely on (D) team up

**12.** The support staff often struggle to ______ the volume of complaints during the holiday shopping season.
(A) cope with (B) apply to (C) result in (D) put forward

**13.** Only after the two remaining issues were ______ did the vendor agree to sign the final contract.
(A) ironed out (B) called off (C) accounted for (D) applied to

**14.** The startup ______ succeeded in securing a major investor after presenting its revised business plan to the board.
(A) eventually (B) accordingly (C) presumably (D) considerably

**15.** The new onboarding process will officially ______ next Monday, replacing the outdated paper-based system.
(A) kick off (B) call off (C) cut back on (D) hand over
