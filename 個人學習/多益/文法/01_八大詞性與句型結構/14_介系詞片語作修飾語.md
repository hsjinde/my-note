# 14_介系詞片語作修飾語

## 核心概念

### 1. 介系詞片語的兩種修飾功能
介系詞片語（介系詞 + 名詞/動名詞）在句子中主要扮演兩種修飾角色：
*   **形容詞用法（後位修飾名詞）**：緊接在名詞之後，說明該名詞的性質或範圍，功能等同形容詞子句或分詞片語。
    *   the employee **in the accounting department**（那位在會計部門的員工）
*   **副詞用法（修飾動詞或整句）**：說明動作發生的時間、原因、方式、條件等，位置較自由，可放在句首、句中或句尾。
    *   **In light of the delay**, the client requested a refund. = The client requested a refund **in light of the delay**.

### 2. 如何判斷是形容詞用法還是副詞用法
判斷關鍵在於：把介系詞片語拿掉之後，句子的「主要語意」是否還完整。
*   拿掉後句子仍是完整句（S+V+O 齊全），只是少了細節 → **副詞用法**，可自由移動位置。
*   拿掉後緊鄰的名詞語意變得不完整或所指對象改變 → **形容詞用法**，須緊貼在名詞之後。

### 3. 後位修飾的位置規則
形容詞用法的介系詞片語必須緊鄰被修飾的名詞。若句中有多個名詞，介系詞片語預設修飾「離它最近的那個名詞」，因此句子安排不當容易讓修飾對象變得模糊，例如 the report on the budget from the finance team，on the budget 修飾最近的 the report，而不是句子中其他名詞。

### 4. 副詞用法的位置彈性與逗號
副詞用法的介系詞片語可以：
1.  放在**句首**：常用來強調背景、原因或條件，後方通常加逗號。
2.  放在**句中**：緊跟在被修飾的動詞之後。
3.  放在**句尾**：最常見、最自然的位置。

三種位置語意基本相同，但 Part 5、6 常利用「句首片語 + 逗號」來測試考生是否能快速辨認片語在哪裡結束、主要子句從哪裡開始。

### 5. 介系詞片語 vs. 連接詞的替換關係
許多表達「儘管、因為、除非」等語意的功能詞都有「介系詞版」與「連接詞版」：介系詞版後面只能接**名詞或動名詞**，連接詞版後面才能接**完整子句（S+V）**。這個判斷原則在 04_連接詞 已介紹過 despite / because of 等基本款，本章聚焦於更進階、多益中高分常考的介系詞片語，例如 notwithstanding、barring、irrespective of、pending 等。

## 📝 多益秒殺解題技巧
1.  **看空格後面接什麼**：接名詞/動名詞 → 選介系詞片語；接完整子句（S+V）→ 選連接詞，不能混用。
2.  **句首出現逗號前的片語**：先看它是不是介系詞片語的「候選字」，若後面直接接名詞（無主詞動詞），八九不離十是介系詞用法。
3.  **修飾語刪除測試法**：把可疑片語從句子中刪掉，句子仍完整 → 副詞用法可移動；名詞語意變得不完整 → 形容詞用法須緊貼名詞。

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **判斷修飾對象是名詞還是動詞**：介系詞片語緊接在名詞後面時是「形容詞用法」（後位修飾名詞，如 employees with five years of experience）；若整句移除該片語後句意仍完整，則多半是「副詞用法」，可自由移動於句首、句中、句尾。
2.  **副詞用法的位置彈性**：修飾動詞或全句的介系詞片語可放句首（加逗號）、句尾（通常不加逗號），語意不變，是考生最容易誤判「位置錯了就是錯字」的地方——其實只是位置不同。
3.  **後位修飾應緊鄰名詞**：形容詞用法的介系詞片語必須緊接在被修飾名詞之後，中間插入其他成分容易造成修飾對象混淆。
4.  **多個介系詞片語堆疊時的語序**：一般遵循「方式 → 地點 → 時間」的順序（如 arrived by train at the station on Monday），時間片語通常放在最後。
5.  **介系詞 vs 連接詞的相同語意配對**：despite / because of（介系詞+名詞）對應 although / because（連接詞+子句），本章的 notwithstanding、barring、irrespective of 都屬於介系詞家族，後面只能接名詞或動名詞，不能接完整子句。
6.  **pending 與 following 的雙重詞性**：兩者除了當介系詞，也可當形容詞（pending approval 待批准的、the following day 接下來的一天），須依上下文判斷詞性。
7.  **複合介系詞片語中不可省略冠詞**：with the exception of、in the event of 等片語中的 the 為固定用法，若寫成 with exception of 或 in event of 都是常見錯誤，是文法題常設的陷阱。
8.  **as per / per 的正式文書用法**：常出現在合約、公告等正式商業文書中，表示「依照」，比 according to 更精簡，是商業書信中常考詞彙。
9.  **upon + V-ing 表示「一...就」**：upon（或 on）後面接動名詞，構成表示動作先後緊接發生的固定用法，等同於 as soon as + 子句。
10. **courtesy of 的特殊語意**：字面上是「基於...的禮貌」，實際考題中常表示「由...提供、承蒙...贊助」，注意不要與 because of 的因果語意混淆。
11. **修飾語省略測試法**：判斷一個介系詞片語是否為可移動的副詞用法，可先將它從句子中刪除；若刪除後句子文法仍完整（S+V+O 齊全），就是副詞用法；若刪除後名詞語意變得不完整或所指對象改變，則為形容詞後位修飾用法。
12. **句首介系詞片語與逗號**：長度較長或為固定片語時，句首介系詞片語後方習慣加逗號分隔，是閱讀斷句的重要視覺線索，能幫助快速判斷子句起訖。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

### 狀語功能：表達情況、原因、對比的介系詞片語
*   **following** 介系詞 在...之後 | *Following* the merger, several departments were consolidated.
*   **pending** 介系詞 在...尚未確定／等待...期間 | The contract remains inactive, *pending* final approval.
*   **notwithstanding** 介系詞 儘管、雖然 | *Notwithstanding* the budget cuts, the project proceeded on schedule.
*   **amid** 介系詞 在...之中、伴隨著 | Sales figures fluctuated *amid* ongoing supply shortages.
*   **barring** 介系詞 除非發生、若非 | *Barring* any last-minute changes, the launch will proceed as planned.
*   **in light of** 片語 鑑於 | *In light of* the positive feedback, the firm expanded the product line.
*   **in response to** 片語 回應 | The policy was revised *in response to* employee complaints.
*   **subsequent to** 片語 在...之後 | *Subsequent to* the recall, the company issued a public apology.
*   **in the wake of** 片語 在...之後（隨之而來） | *In the wake of* the scandal, several executives resigned.
*   **irrespective of** 片語 不論、不管 | The vote will proceed *irrespective of* the objections raised.

### 補充/例外/共同的複合介系詞片語
*   **apart from** 片語 除了...之外 | *Apart from* the standard insurance, the firm offers a wellness stipend.
*   **aside from** 片語 除了...之外 | *Aside from* minor delays, the shipment arrived intact.
*   **with the exception of** 片語 除了...之外 | All budgets were cut, *with the exception of* R&D.
*   **inclusive of** 片語 包含 | The quoted price is *inclusive of* shipping and handling.
*   **on top of** 片語 除了...之外（還） | *On top of* her regular duties, she now manages vendor contracts.
*   **in conjunction with** 片語 與...共同 | The workshop was held *in conjunction with* the annual sales conference.
*   **for the sake of** 片語 為了...的緣故 | The team worked overtime *for the sake of* meeting the deadline.
*   **in recognition of** 片語 為表彰 | The bonus was awarded *in recognition of* her outstanding performance.
*   **courtesy of** 片語 承蒙、由...提供 | The upgrade was offered *courtesy of* a partnership with the hotel chain.
*   **for the purpose of** 片語 為了...目的 | The survey was conducted *for the purpose of* improving customer service.

### 比較/依據/關聯類介系詞片語（常修飾動詞或整句）
*   **in terms of** 片語 就...而言 | The two suppliers are similar *in terms of* price.
*   **with respect to** 片語 關於 | The manager has no further comment *with respect to* the layoffs.
*   **in relation to** 片語 與...有關 | Questions *in relation to* the merger should be directed to legal.
*   **as regards** 片語 關於 | *As regards* the budget proposal, the board requested more detail.
*   **as per** 片語 依照 | The shipment was processed *as per* the client's instructions.
*   **per** 介系詞 依照、每 | Employees are reimbursed *per* the company's expense policy.
*   **with a view to** 片語 以...為目的 | The firm hired two analysts *with a view to* expanding overseas.
*   **in the event of** 片語 萬一發生...時 | *In the event of* a system failure, contact IT immediately.
*   **in case of** 片語 如果發生 | *In case of* an emergency, use the stairs, not the elevator.
*   **upon** 介系詞 一...就 | *Upon* arrival, employees must sign in at the front desk.

## 💪 練習題（15 題）

**1.** ______ the company's merger with a regional competitor, several overlapping departments were consolidated.
(A) Because (B) Following (C) Although (D) Unless

**2.** Employees ______ at least five years of tenure are eligible for the enhanced retirement plan.
(A) at (B) about (C) since (D) with

**3.** The contract, ______ final sign-off from the CFO, cannot be executed yet.
(A) pending (B) despite (C) unless (D) considering

**4.** The two suppliers are almost identical ______ price, but the second offers faster delivery.
(A) in spite of (B) because of (C) in terms of (D) instead of

**5.** The committee will proceed with the vote as scheduled, ______ the objections raised by several members.
(A) unless (B) irrespective of (C) so that (D) provided that

**6.** The accountant could not locate the invoice ______ last month's office supplies.
(A) about (B) on (C) at (D) for

**7.** ______ the sharp rise in raw material costs, the manufacturer raised its wholesale prices by 8 percent.
(A) In light of (B) Even though (C) So that (D) Provided that

**8.** Sales figures fluctuated ______ ongoing supply shortages last quarter.
(A) among (B) between (C) amid (D) despite

**9.** ______ any last-minute changes, the product launch will take place on schedule next Monday.
(A) Unless (B) Barring (C) Provided (D) Given that

**10.** ______ her regular administrative duties, the office manager now oversees vendor contracts.
(A) Instead of (B) Because of (C) Regardless of (D) On top of

**11.** ______ the recent audit findings, the finance department revised its expense-reporting procedures.（第一空）______ the new procedures, every reimbursement request must include a scanned receipt.（第二空）
第一空：(A) In response to (B) Because (C) Although (D) Whereas
第二空：(A) Despite (B) Whereas (C) Under (D) Because

**12.** The department ______ the highest turnover rate this year was Customer Support.
(A) among (B) with (C) despite (D) besides

**13.** ______ the initial product recall, the company issued a public apology and offered full refunds.
(A) Because (B) Although (C) So that (D) Subsequent to

**14.** All departmental budgets were reduced by 10 percent this year, ______ the R&D division, which received a slight increase.
(A) with the exception of (B) with exception of (C) except of (D) aside of

**15.** The venue for this year's annual conference was upgraded at no extra charge, ______ a partnership with a local hotel chain.
(A) instead of (B) regardless of (C) courtesy of (D) contrary to
