# 13_商業書信慣用語與正式用語替換

## 核心概念

多益 Part 5、6、7 大量取材自商業書信（email、memo、letter）與正式文件（notice、announcement、policy）。這類文本偏好使用**較為正式（formal）的字彙與片語**，取代日常口語中常見的說法。考生若只認得口語版本（about、before、to、if、buy、use…），遇到正式書信的替換詞（regarding、prior to、in order to、in the event that、purchase、utilize…）就可能誤判詞意或誤選文法功能相近但語域不符的選項。

本章聚焦三件事：
1. **語域（register）替換**：同一個意思，口語 vs. 正式書信的不同說法。
2. **書信慣用語**：開頭致意、結尾請求回覆、附件說明等固定句型。
3. **法律／公告用語**：hereby、pursuant to、notwithstanding 這類較艱澀但常出現在公司政策、合約摘要中的字。

### 為什麼要特別學這個？

Part 6、7 常出現的文本類型（memo to all staff、customer notice、policy update）本身就是正式文件，作者會刻意使用正式用語營造專業感；因此考題選項也常常故意混入一個「意思對但語域不對」的口語選項作為陷阱。例如：

> All employees are required to submit the report ______ Friday.
> (A) before (B) prior to (C) previous (D) former

雖然 (A) before 語意正確，但若原文語境是制式化的公司公告，正式書信通常偏好 (B) prior to；但這只是語域偏好，並非文法錯誤——多益不會單純考「語域」讓 before 變成錯的，而是會透過**詞性/句構**排除其餘選項，所以本章也會同時複習每個正式用語的**詞性限制**（例如 prior to 後面只能接名詞/動名詞，不能接子句）。

## 一、及物連接詞 vs. 正式介系詞片語替換

| 口語／基本用法 | 正式書信用法 | 用法差異 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **about** | **regarding / concerning / with regard to / with reference to** | 正式用法多為介系詞（片語），後面直接接名詞，不加 be 動詞 |
| **to V（表目的）** | **in order to V / so as to V** | 正式書信偏好完整片語表達「為了要」，尤其用於句首 |
| **if** | **in the event that / should + 主詞 + 原V** | in the event that 後接完整子句；should 倒裝句更正式 |
| **because of** | **owing to / due to** | 皆為正式介系詞片語，後接名詞 |
| **but / however** | **notwithstanding** | notwithstanding 可當介系詞（不受...影響）或連接詞，語氣更正式莊重 |

### 例句對照

- 口語：I'm writing **about** the delayed shipment.
- 正式：I am writing **regarding** the delayed shipment.

- 口語：We called a meeting **to** discuss the budget.
- 正式：We called a meeting **in order to** discuss the budget.

## 二、法律／公告慣用語（hereby, pursuant to 系列）

這組字幾乎只出現在正式文件（合約摘要、公司公告、法規條文）中，考生只需認得意思，不必自己在口語中使用：

| 字彙 | 意思 | 例句 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **hereby** | 特此（宣示效力用） | The company **hereby** confirms that the contract is terminated. |
| **herein** | 於此（文件）之中 | The terms **herein** shall apply to all future orders. |
| **thereof** | 其（指代前述事物） | The client requested a copy of the invoice and a summary **thereof**. |
| **thereafter** | 此後 | The trial period ends in June; **thereafter**, normal pricing applies. |
| **aforementioned** | 前述的 | The **aforementioned** policy takes effect on July 1. |
| **henceforth** | 從此以後 | **Henceforth**, all requests must be submitted in writing. |
| **hereinafter** | （合約中）以下簡稱 | ABC Corporation, **hereinafter** referred to as "the Vendor," agrees to the following terms. |
| **undersigned** | 簽署人（本人） | The **undersigned** hereby agrees to the terms outlined above. |
| **pursuant to** | 依照、根據（規定、條款） | The refund was processed **pursuant to** Section 4 of the agreement. |
| **notwithstanding** | 儘管、不受...影響 | **Notwithstanding** the delay, the project was completed on time. |
| **in lieu of** | 代替 | Staff may take a day off **in lieu of** overtime pay. |

## 三、書信開頭與結尾的固定慣用語

正式書信有一套固定的請求／告知句型，多益 Part 7 的信件題常直接引用，Part 6 填空題也常要考生選出符合語域與句意的固定用語：

| 慣用語 | 用途 |
| :--- | :--- |
| **We would appreciate it if you could...** | 禮貌請求對方做某事 |
| **Please be advised that...** | 正式告知某項訊息（比 "Please note that" 更正式） |
| **We are pleased to inform you that...** | 正式告知好消息 |
| **Should you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact us.** | 結尾邀請對方提出疑問 |
| **Kindly + V...** | 正式請求（比 please 更客氣、書面） |
| **At your earliest convenience** | 請對方儘快處理（比 "as soon as possible" 更委婉） |
| **For your reference / For your information** | 提供資訊供對方參考（常縮寫 FYR / FYI） |
| **Feel free to contact us** | 邀請對方主動聯繫，語氣較 kindly 系列略輕鬆 |

> [!note]
> `Please be advised that` 與 `We are pleased to inform you that` 差異在於**訊息性質**：前者中性告知（可能是壞消息或規定變更），後者專用於好消息或核准通知。

## 四、動詞語域替換

商業書信偏好使用「較正式、單一動詞」取代「動詞片語」或「日常動詞」：

| 口語 | 正式書信用語 | 例句 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **buy** | **purchase** | The department will **purchase** new equipment next month. |
| **use** | **utilize** | The team should **utilize** the available resources more efficiently. |
| **start** | **commence** | The training program will **commence** on Monday. |
| **end** | **terminate** | Either party may **terminate** the contract with 30 days' notice. |
| **get** | **obtain** | Employees must **obtain** approval before booking travel. |
| **ask** | **request** | Please **request** a replacement badge from security. |
| **tell** | **inform / notify** | The supervisor will **inform** the team of the new schedule. |

> [!note]
> 這些替換多為「意思相同、語域不同」，不是文法陷阱本身；但多益常把它們放進**詞性混淆題**（例如 purchase 名詞/動詞同形、terminate/termination），所以仍要注意詞性。

## 📝 多益秒殺解題技巧

1. 看到 email / memo / notice 開頭的閱讀題型，優先預期會出現 regarding、pursuant to、herein 等正式用語，不要被「沒看過的字」嚇到，先判斷詞性再判斷語意。
2. `prior to`、`in order to`、`regarding` 這類片語後方**只能接名詞或動名詞**，不能直接接完整子句；若選項後面是完整子句（有主詞+動詞），就要選連接詞（before、because、about 不適用時的替代方案）而非這些片語。
3. `hereby`、`herein`、`hereinafter` 開頭都是 here-，`thereof`、`thereafter` 開頭都是 there-，可用字首快速分類：here- 系列多指「本文件」，there- 系列多指「前述提到的事物」。
4. Part 6 空格若在書信的**開頭或結尾**，優先考慮固定慣用語（Please be advised that、We would appreciate it if、Should you have any questions），而非單純代換單字。
5. `notwithstanding` 當介系詞用時，後面直接接名詞，語意等同 despite / in spite of，不要誤判為連接詞而在後面找子句。

## 📚 補充秒殺公式

1.  **regarding vs. about**：兩者意思相同，但 regarding 是正式書信的介系詞，不需要 be 動詞連接，可直接放在信件開頭主詞後方（I am writing regarding...）。
2.  **in order to vs. to**：句首若要強調「目的」，正式書信偏好完整片語 in order to；純粹考文法功能兩者可互換，選項若同時出現則優先選符合語域的 in order to。
3.  **prior to 後面接名詞/動名詞，不接子句**：若空格後面是完整子句（有主詞動詞），prior to 不能選，要改選 before 當連接詞。
4.  **in the event that = should + 主詞 + 原V（倒裝)**：兩者皆表「萬一」，in the event that 後接直述句，should 倒裝句省略 if，語氣同樣正式。
5.  **hereby 的位置**：通常緊接在主詞後、動詞前（The company hereby confirms...），作用是加強官方宣示的語氣，可視為插入語，去掉也不影響句子文法完整性。
6.  **pursuant to vs. according to**：兩者都表「依照」，但 pursuant to 專用於依照**法規、條款、合約**，according to 泛指依照任何資料來源（報告、統計、某人所說）。
7.  **in lieu of vs. instead of**：意思相同（代替），in lieu of 為較正式書面用語，常見於人資／薪資相關文件（in lieu of overtime pay）。
8.  **notwithstanding 當介系詞**：Notwithstanding + N，語意等同 despite N，注意不要跟 despite 混用成 despite of。
9.  **purchase / commence / terminate / utilize 詞性**：這些多益愛考「動詞 vs. 名詞」的詞性題，例如 purchase（v. 購買 / n. 購買行為）、termination（n. 終止），作答時先判斷空格要填動詞還是名詞。
10. **please be advised that vs. please note that**：兩者都表「請注意」，please be advised that 更常見於正式公告或法律通知，語氣更莊重。
11. **should you have any questions 的倒裝**：這是 if you should have any questions 省略 if 後的倒裝形式，句首用助動詞 should，不能誤加 if。
12. **for your reference (FYR) vs. for your information (FYI)**：FYR 強調「供查閱、留存」，FYI 強調「單純告知」，兩者皆放在句尾或另起一行，非完整句子的一部分。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

### 正式連接詞／介系詞片語
*   **pursuant to** (phr. 依照、根據) | The refund was issued **pursuant to** the return policy stated in Section 3.
*   **notwithstanding** (prep. 儘管、不受...影響) | **Notwithstanding** the budget cuts, the project proceeded as scheduled.
*   **with regard to** (phr. 關於) | We are writing **with regard to** your recent inquiry about our services.
*   **with reference to** (phr. 關於、參照) | **With reference to** our phone conversation, please find the attached quotation.
*   **as per** (phr. 依照) | The goods were delivered **as per** the agreed schedule.
*   **in lieu of** (phr. 代替) | The manager offered additional vacation days **in lieu of** a salary increase.

### 法律／公告慣用語
*   **hereby** (adv. 特此) | The board **hereby** approves the proposed merger.
*   **herein** (adv. 於此文件中) | Any changes to the terms **herein** must be made in writing.
*   **thereof** (adv. 其、關於該事物) | The client received the contract along with a summary **thereof**.
*   **thereafter** (adv. 此後) | The warranty covers the first year; **thereafter**, repairs are charged separately.
*   **aforementioned** (adj. 前述的) | The **aforementioned** deadline may not be extended under any circumstances.
*   **henceforth** (adv. 從此以後) | **Henceforth**, all purchase orders must be approved by the finance director.
*   **hereinafter** (adv. 以下簡稱) | Global Freight Ltd., **hereinafter** referred to as "the Carrier," shall deliver the goods within ten days.
*   **undersigned** (n. 簽署人) | The **undersigned** confirms acceptance of all terms listed above.

### 書信禮貌慣用語
*   **at your earliest convenience** (phr. 請儘快) | Please review the attached draft **at your earliest convenience**.
*   **kindly** (adv. 敬請、勞駕) | Guests are **kindly** requested to check out by 11 a.m.
*   **please be advised that** (phr. 特此告知) | **Please be advised that** the office will be closed on Monday for maintenance.
*   **we are pleased to inform you that** (phr. 很高興通知您) | **We are pleased to inform you that** your application has been approved.
*   **should you have any questions** (phr. 若您有任何疑問) | **Should you have any questions**, please contact our support team.
*   **feel free to** (phr. 歡迎、請隨時) | **Feel free to** reach out if you need further clarification.
*   **we would appreciate it if** (phr. 若能...我們將不勝感激) | **We would appreciate it if** you could confirm your attendance by Friday.
*   **for your reference** (phr. 供您參考) | A copy of the updated policy is attached **for your reference**.
*   **for your information** (phr. 謹供告知) | The revised schedule is attached **for your information**.

### 正式書信動詞替換
*   **purchase** (v. 購買，正式用語，替代 buy) | The office will **purchase** new laptops for the design team.
*   **utilize** (v. 利用，正式用語，替代 use) | Managers are encouraged to **utilize** the new scheduling software.
*   **commence** (v. 開始，正式用語，替代 start) | Construction will **commence** once the permit is approved.
*   **terminate** (v. 終止，正式用語，替代 end) | The landlord may **terminate** the lease with 60 days' notice.
*   **obtain** (v. 取得，正式用語，替代 get) | Employees must **obtain** written consent before sharing client data.
*   **request** (v. 要求、請求，正式用語，替代 ask) | Vendors may **request** an extension by contacting the procurement office.
*   **correspondence** (n. 書信往來、通訊) | All **correspondence** regarding the contract should be directed to the legal department.
