# 09_獨立分詞構句

## 核心概念
一般分詞構句的隱含主詞必須與主要子句的主詞相同（例如 *Finishing the report, she went home.* 隱含主詞是 she）。但當**分詞構句的動作執行者跟主要子句的主詞不一樣**時，就不能省略分詞自己的主詞，必須把它保留在分詞前面——這就是「獨立分詞構句 (Nominative Absolute / Independent Participial Phrase)」。

結構固定為：

> **〔獨立主詞〕+〔分詞 (V-ing / p.p. / being + p.p.）〕, 主要子句.**

*   **The manager being on vacation**, the team continued to meet the deadline.（manager 是分詞的主詞，不是主要子句的 the team，兩者不同，所以 manager 必須留下）
*   比較：*Being on vacation, the manager missed the meeting.*（省略主詞的一般分詞構句，隱含主詞 = manager = 主要子句主詞，兩者相同才能省略）

## 1. 為什麼要保留主詞：懸垂分詞 vs. 獨立分詞構句
若分詞構句省略主詞、又跟主要子句主詞對不上，就會造成邏輯錯誤的「懸垂分詞 (dangling participle)」。獨立分詞構句就是刻意保留專屬主詞來避免這個錯誤：

*   ❌ *Arriving late, the meeting had already started.*（會議自己不會「抵達」，邏輯錯誤）
*   ✅ **The client arriving late**, the meeting had already started without her.（保留 the client 作為分詞的主詞，邏輯正確）

## 2. 主動 vs. 被動：V-ing 與 p.p.
獨立分詞構句的動詞形式判斷邏輯跟一般分詞構句一樣，只是多了一個獨立主詞要先確認主動或被動：

*   獨立主詞「主動做」動作 ➔ **V-ing**
    *   **The technician finishing** the repairs, the office reopened for business.
*   獨立主詞「被動接受」動作 ➔ **p.p.**（常省略 being，直接用 p.p.）
    *   **The contract (being) signed** by both parties, the deal was officially closed.
*   動作發生在主要子句之前，強調時間先後 ➔ **having (been) + p.p.**
    *   **All invoices having been reviewed**, the accountant closed the monthly ledger.

## 3. 固定化的獨立分詞構句慣用語
有一批獨立分詞構句已經固定成片語，直接背誦即可，不需要每次重新分析主詞邏輯：

| 慣用語 | 中文意思 |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Weather permitting** | 天氣許可的話 |
| **Time permitting** | 時間允許的話 |
| **Budget permitting** | 預算允許的話 |
| **All things considered** | 綜合考量之下 |
| **Everything considered** | 一切都考慮進去的話 |
| **That being said** | 話雖如此 |
| **Other things being equal** | 其他條件相同的情況下 |
| **All being well** | 一切順利的話 |

*   **Weather permitting**, the outdoor launch event will proceed as scheduled.
*   **All things considered**, the proposal still seems like the safer option.

## 4. 與「with + O + 分詞」的關係
獨立分詞構句常常搭配介系詞 `with` 出現在句尾，語感上等同於「伴隨狀況」的獨立分詞構句，差別只在 `with` 讓整個結構可以放在句尾而不必放句首：

*   句首獨立分詞構句：**The budget being tight**, the manager postponed the office renovation.
*   句尾 with 版本：The manager postponed the office renovation, **with the budget being tight**.

## 📖 秒殺解題技巧
1.  看到句首「名詞 + V-ing/p.p.」後面接逗點跟一個完整句子，且這個名詞**不等於**主要子句主詞 ➔ 直接判斷是獨立分詞構句，先找獨立主詞的動作是主動還是被動。
2.  獨立主詞若在句中做動作的「發出者」（做事、決定、完成…）➔ 選 V-ing；若是動作的「承受者」（被完成、被核准、被指派…）➔ 選 p.p.。
3.  看到 `weather/time/budget/circumstances + permitting` 這種「名詞 + permitting」組合，直接視為固定片語，`permitting` 不需再分析主被動。
4.  `All things considered` / `That being said` / `Other things being equal` 這類轉折語固定放句首，選項若把字序或單複數打亂（如 All thing considered）就是陷阱。

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **獨立分詞構句公式**：名詞（≠主要子句主詞）+ 現在分詞/過去分詞, 主要子句. 保留主詞是判斷「這不是懸垂分詞」的關鍵訊號。
2.  **主詞不同判斷法**：先找主要子句的主詞是誰，再看句首逗點前的名詞是否跟它相同；不同 ➔ 獨立分詞構句，該名詞不能刪。
3.  **being 可省略但不影響對錯**：p.p. 前的 being 常被省略，`The report (being) approved, the team began printing copies.` 兩種寫法都對，考題常直接省略考 p.p.。
4.  **having + p.p. 表示時間先後**：獨立分詞構句若強調「先發生」的動作，用 `Having + p.p.`，例如 `The invoice having been paid, the vendor released the shipment.`
5.  **「名詞 + permitting」固定搭配**：`weather / time / budget / schedule / resources + permitting` 全部視為同一組合背下來，`permitting` 不隨主詞改變形式。
6.  **轉折類獨立分詞構句已成慣用語**：`that being said`、`all things considered`、`everything considered` 不需再分析文法，直接當固定句首插入語處理。
7.  **句首名詞 + 分詞，逗點後接完整句子** ➔ 一句話裡有兩個動詞卻沒有連接詞，代表句首那個一定不是限定動詞，必須用分詞。
8.  **與分詞構句的差異**：一般分詞構句省略主詞的前提是「兩個主詞相同」；獨立分詞構句正是「兩個主詞不同」時的處理方式，兩者互為對照。
9.  **與 with + O + 分詞 的呼應**：句尾出現 `with + 名詞 + V-ing/p.p.` 時，功能等同於獨立分詞構句放在句尾，判斷邏輯完全一樣（該名詞主動用 V-ing，被動用 p.p.）。
10. **陷阱：獨立主詞後面接限定動詞**：若選項給出 `is/are/was/were` 等限定動詞，會讓句子出現兩個主要動詞卻沒有連接詞，文法錯誤，應排除。
11. **陷阱：誤刪獨立主詞**：若把獨立分詞構句的主詞刪掉，改成一般分詞構句，會產生邏輯不通的懸垂分詞，是常見的錯誤選項設計方向。
12. **雙格題判斷法**：短文填空中若第一格考獨立分詞構句的動詞形式，第二格常常換考固定慣用語（如 permitting 系列），兩者分開判斷即可，不需互相牽動。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

### 形容詞
*   **absent** 形容詞 缺席的 | The manager was **absent** from the meeting due to a scheduling conflict.

### 介系詞 / 連接詞 / 副詞
*   **henceforth** 副詞 從此以後 | **Henceforth**, all expense reports must be submitted electronically.
*   **albeit** 連接詞 雖然 | The plan succeeded, **albeit** with a few unexpected setbacks.

### 名詞
*   **postponement** 名詞 延期 | The client requested a **postponement** of the contract signing.
*   **absence** 名詞 缺席 | The supervisor's **absence** delayed the approval process.
*   **shortage** 名詞 短缺 | A staff **shortage** forced the warehouse to reduce operating hours.
*   **constraint** 名詞 限制 | Budget **constraints** prevented the department from hiring additional staff.
*   **workload** 名詞 工作量 | The new system was designed to reduce the team's **workload**.
*   **resource** 名詞 資源 | The project requires more financial **resources** than originally planned.
*   **approval** 名詞 核准 | The proposal is still awaiting final **approval** from the board.

### 獨立分詞構句慣用語（名詞 + permitting／considered 型）
*   **weather permitting** 片語 天氣許可的話 | **Weather permitting**, the site inspection will take place outdoors.
*   **time permitting** 片語 時間允許的話 | **Time permitting**, we will also cover next quarter's targets.
*   **budget permitting** 片語 預算允許的話 | **Budget permitting**, the firm plans to open a second branch office.
*   **schedule permitting** 片語 行程允許的話 | **Schedule permitting**, the director will join the site visit in person.
*   **all things considered** 片語 綜合考量之下 | **All things considered**, the merger still appears financially sound.
*   **everything considered** 片語 一切都考慮進去的話 | **Everything considered**, the vendor's proposal offers the best value.
*   **that being said** 片語 話雖如此 | The results were disappointing; **that being said**, the team learned valuable lessons.
*   **having said that** 片語 話雖如此 | **Having said that**, management still supports the expansion plan.
*   **all being well** 片語 一切順利的話 | **All being well**, the new branch will open by early next year.
*   **other things being equal** 片語 在其他條件相同的情況下 | **Other things being equal**, the committee prefers the local supplier.
*   **generally speaking** 片語 一般來說 | **Generally speaking**, quarterly reports are due within five business days.
*   **strictly speaking** 片語 嚴格來說 | **Strictly speaking**, the contract does not cover overtime compensation.

## 💪 練習題（15 題）

**1.** The keynote speaker ______ ill at the last minute, the organizers quickly rearranged the schedule.
(A) being (B) is (C) was (D) to be

**2.** Weather ______, the outdoor product launch will proceed as scheduled.
(A) permitted (B) permitting (C) permit (D) to permit

**3.** The budget review ______, the finance team submitted its report a day early.
(A) completing (B) complete (C) completed (D) to complete

**4.** ______, the board decided to postpone the merger until next quarter.
(A) Considering all things (B) All things considering (C) All thing considered (D) All things considered

**5.** With the new system ______ in place, employees can now submit reports online.
(A) being (B) is (C) was (D) been

**6.** The contract ______ still pending approval, the vendor has not yet shipped the equipment.
(A) is (B) being (C) was (D) be

**7.** The deadline ______, the marketing team worked overtime to finish the campaign materials.
(A) approach (B) approaches (C) approaching (D) approached

**8.** Two employees ______ absent due to illness, the supervisor reassigned their tasks to the rest of the team.
(A) are (B) were (C) be (D) being

**9.** ______, the proposal still requires additional funding before it can move forward.
(A) That being said (B) That is said (C) Saying that (D) To say that

**10.** Notice to All Staff: Due to the ongoing office renovation, the project (1)______ expected to finish by the end of next month, employees on the third floor will temporarily relocate to the annex building. (2)______, the relocated staff may return to their original desks two weeks ahead of schedule.
第一空：(A) which is (B) being (C) is (D) it is
第二空：(A) Scheduled (B) Scheduling (C) Schedule (D) To schedule

**11.** ______ speaking, quarterly reports submitted after the fifth business day are considered late.
(A) General (B) Generalize (C) Generalized (D) Generally

**12.** All resources ______ allocated, the project manager assigned the remaining budget to marketing.
(A) having already been (B) already (C) already been (D) have already been

**13.** ______, the committee would prefer the vendor with faster delivery times.
(A) Others things being equal (B) Other things being equal (C) Other thing being equal (D) Other things being equally

**14.** The two departments ______ merged next year, employees are already discussing how responsibilities will be divided.
(A) is expected to be (B) being expected to be (C) expected to be (D) expects to be

**15.** Staffing levels ______ low this quarter, the HR director has approved three additional hires.
(A) remain (B) remained (C) to remain (D) remaining
