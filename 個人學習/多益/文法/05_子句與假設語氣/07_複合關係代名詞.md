# 07_複合關係代名詞

## 核心概念
複合關係代名詞（Compound Relative Pronoun）指 **whoever, whomever, whatever, whichever**，以及功能相近的複合關係副詞 **whenever, wherever**。這些字本身「已經包含先行詞」，等於「先行詞 + 關係代名詞」的合體，所以：
1. 前面**絕對不能再加名詞**（這點跟 02 章節已經提過的規則一致，本章著重在「子句功能」與「格位」的進階辨識）。
2. 引導出的子句可能是**名詞子句**（當主詞、受詞、補語），也可能是**副詞子句**（表示「無論...」的讓步語氣）。判斷方式要看這個子句在整句中扮演的角色，而不是死背單字本身。

## 1. 名詞子句用法：whoever / whomever / whatever / whichever
當複合關係代名詞子句在句中**當主詞或受詞**時，等於一個名詞。
*   **Whoever** submits the highest bid will win the contract. (Whoever 子句當整句主詞 = Anyone who...)
*   The manager will approve **whatever** the committee decides. (whatever 子句當 approve 的受詞)
*   You may select **whichever** supplier offers the best price. (whichever + 名詞，當受詞)

## 2. whoever 與 whomever 的格位辨識
和 who/whom 一樣，whoever 用來取代「子句中的主詞」，whomever 取代「子句中的受詞」。多益考題常故意把及物動詞的受詞位置留空，此時要選 whomever。
*   **Whoever** *arrives* first should notify the front desk. (whoever 在子句中當主詞，後面直接接動詞)
*   The board will hire **whomever** the panel *recommends*. (whomever 在子句中當 recommends 的受詞，後面接主詞+動詞)
*   小技巧：把子句單獨抽出來看，若「後面缺主詞」用 whoever；若「後面主詞、動詞都齊全，缺受詞」用 whomever。實務上正式書面才嚴格區分，口語與多益選項中 whoever 也常通用，但只要選項同時出現兩者，就要照格位判斷。

## 3. 副詞子句用法：no matter who/what/which 的同義替換
whoever, whatever, whichever 也可以引導**讓步副詞子句**，這時候可以跟 "no matter who/what/which" 互換，意思是「不論是誰／不論什麼／不論哪一個」，主要子句與從屬子句都各自要有完整的主詞動詞。
*   **Whatever** the outcome may be, the team will present the results honestly. (= No matter what the outcome may be)
*   **Whoever** is responsible for the delay, the client still expects an apology. (= No matter who is responsible)
*   判斷技巧：如果整句話拿掉 whoever/whatever 子句，剩下仍是一句完整句子，代表該子句是「多出來的」副詞子句，而非句子必要的主詞或受詞。

## 4. whenever 與 wherever：複合關係副詞
whenever（無論何時／每當）與 wherever（無論何處／到處）同樣自帶先行詞，後面接的子句主詞動詞要完整，且**不可再加時間/地點名詞**（不可寫成 whenever the time...）。
*   **Whenever** the inventory falls below the threshold, the system automatically reorders supplies. (每當...)
*   Employees may work **wherever** they have a stable internet connection. (無論在哪裡)
*   whenever/wherever 幾乎都是副詞子句用法，較少當名詞子句，這點跟 whoever/whatever/whichever 可以身兼兩種功能不同，是本章與 02 章節區隔的重點。

## 5. 常見誤用與混淆點
*   **whatever + 名詞 vs. whatever 單獨用**：whatever 可以像 whichever 一樣直接加名詞（whatever budget is approved...），也可以獨立當代名詞（accept whatever is offered）。
*   **whoever 不可與 anyone who 疊用**：whoever 本身已含「任何人」之意，若前面又出現 anyone、everyone 等先行詞，就必須改回一般關係代名詞 who，而非 whoever。
*   **whichever 隱含「有限選項」**：whichever 通常用在「幾個選項中選一個」，whatever 則沒有此限制，常考在有明確列舉選項（如 A 或 B 兩案）的情境。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語
*   **arbitration** (n.) 仲裁 | Whichever method the two firms choose, **arbitration** remains the final option if negotiations fail.
*   **arbitrarily** (adv.) 隨意地 | The auditor may select whichever files she deems necessary, but she should not choose samples **arbitrarily**.
*   **authorize** (v. 及物) 授權 | Whoever is **authorized** to sign contracts must attend the closing ceremony in person.
*   **bidder** (n.) 投標人 | Whichever **bidder** submits the lowest quote will be awarded the logistics contract.
*   **circulate** (v. 及物) 傳閱、流通 | Whatever draft the legal team finalizes will be **circulated** to every department head.
*   **contingent** (adj.) 視情況而定的 | Whichever plan is chosen, the launch date remains **contingent** on regulatory approval.
*   **disqualify** (v. 及物) 取消資格 | Whichever entry violates the submission guidelines will be **disqualified** immediately.
*   **facilitate** (v. 及物) 促進、使便利 | Whichever software the IT team installs should **facilitate** faster data sharing among branches.
*   **grievance** (n.) 申訴、不滿 | Whoever files a formal **grievance** will receive a written response within ten business days.
*   **impartial** (adj.) 公正的、不偏袒的 | Whichever candidate is selected, the interview panel must remain **impartial** throughout the process.
*   **incur** (v. 及物) 招致（費用、損失） | Whatever additional costs the delay may **incur** will be covered by the contractor.
*   **jurisdiction** (n.) 管轄權 | Whoever handles the overseas contract must understand the local **jurisdiction** before proceeding.
*   **mediator** (n.) 調解人 | Whoever is chosen as the **mediator** must remain neutral throughout the dispute.
*   **negligence** (n.) 疏失 | Whatever caused the shipment error, the warehouse must investigate any sign of **negligence**.
*   **precedent** (n.) 先例 | Whichever ruling the tribunal issues will set a **precedent** for similar disputes in the industry.
*   **prevail** (v.) 勝出、佔優勢 | Whatever argument **prevails** in the meeting, the minutes must record every viewpoint discussed.
*   **reallocate** (v. 及物) 重新分配 | The board plans to **reallocate** whatever funds remain after the renovation project concludes.
*   **subordinate** (n.) 部屬、下屬 | Whoever manages the project should assign clear duties to each **subordinate**.

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **先行詞歸零原則**：whoever/whatever/whichever/whenever/wherever 前面看到多餘的名詞（the person, the thing, the time, the place）就是錯誤選項，因為複合關係代名詞已內建先行詞。
2.  **名詞子句 vs. 副詞子句判斷法**：把整個複合關係代名詞子句拿掉，若剩餘部分仍是完整句子，代表該子句是「額外的」副詞子句（讓步用法）；若拿掉後主詞或受詞不見了，代表該子句是句子必要的名詞子句。
3.  **格位判斷口訣**：子句「後面缺主詞」→ whoever；子句「後面主詞動詞完整、缺受詞」→ whomever。
4.  **whatever ≈ no matter what**：兩者可直接互換，常出現在「無論結果、無論決定」等讓步語氣的商業情境句。
5.  **whichever 常搭配明確選項**：句子中若出現「A 或 B 兩者擇一」的情境（如 two proposals, either plan），優先考慮 whichever 而非 whatever。
6.  **whenever/wherever 不可再加時間地點名詞**：whenever the time、wherever the place 都是常見誤用陷阱，一律要刪去多餘名詞。
7.  **whoever 與 anyone who 不可疊用**：句中若已出現 anyone/everyone 等先行詞，空格應選一般關係代名詞 who，而非 whoever。
8.  **複合關係代名詞子句可當主詞，動詞用單數**：Whoever 引導的名詞子句當主詞時，主要動詞通常視為單數，例如 Whoever wins... receives（不是 receive）。
9.  **whatever + 名詞 = 形容詞用法，不可誤用其他複合詞**：只有 whatever 和 whichever 可以直接加名詞（whatever budget, whichever supplier），whoever/whomever 後面不可直接加名詞。
10. **副詞子句可以放句首或句尾**：whoever/whatever 引導的讓步副詞子句可自由移到句首（逗號隔開）或句尾，位置不影響語意判斷，但要留意逗號有無。
11. **與關係副詞 where/when 的差異**：where/when 需要先行詞（the place where..., the time when...），而 wherever/whenever 本身已包含先行詞，兩者不可混用在同一結構中。
12. **時態呼應**：whenever 引導的子句常表示「每當、只要」的習慣性動作，主要子句與從屬子句多半用相同時態（現在簡單式對現在簡單式）。

## 💪 練習題（15 題）

**1.** ______ is selected to lead the overseas expansion will report directly to the CEO.
(A) Whoever (B) Whomever (C) Whichever (D) Whenever

**2.** The purchasing department will approve ______ the supplier proposes, as long as the price stays within budget.
(A) whom (B) whatever (C) whoever (D) whose

**3.** The committee will hire ______ the search firm recommends most strongly for the vice president position.
(A) whoever (B) whomever (C) whichever (D) whatever

**4.** ______ the merger negotiations conclude, both companies must notify their shareholders within thirty days.
(A) Whenever (B) Wherever (C) Whoever (D) Whose

**5.** ______ plan the board eventually approves, the two proposals under consideration both require significant capital investment.
(A) Whoever (B) Whatever (C) Whichever (D) Whomever

**6.** The regional director insisted that staff may be transferred ______ the company decides to open a new branch.
(A) wherever (B) whoever (C) whichever (D) whom

**7.** ______ the outcome of the audit turns out to be, the finance team must submit a full report by Friday.
(A) Whoever (B) Whatever (C) Whichever (D) Whomever

**8.** ______ submits the reimbursement form after the deadline will not be compensated this quarter. The accounting office will still process ______ requests remain, regardless of how minor they are.
第一空：(A) Who (B) Whoever (C) Whomever (D) Whichever
第二空：(A) whichever (B) whatever (C) whoever (D) whomever

**9.** The logistics manager will contact ______ carrier offers the fastest delivery route between the two warehouses.
(A) whoever (B) whom (C) whichever (D) whomever

**10.** ______ two candidates the panel narrows the shortlist down to, both will be invited for a final interview.
(A) Whatever (B) Whichever (C) Whoever (D) Whomever

**11.** The warranty policy applies ______ the customer purchased the product, whether online or in a physical store.
(A) whenever (B) wherever (C) whoever (D) whose

**12.** The consultant advised the executives to consult ______ they believed had the most relevant industry experience.
(A) whoever (B) whomever (C) whichever (D) whatever

**13.** ______ objections the shareholders raise during the meeting, the merger vote will still proceed as scheduled.
(A) Whoever (B) Whichever (C) Whatever (D) Whomever

**14.** The IT department must grant system access to ______ is authorized by the security office, regardless of seniority. Employees are reminded that access will be revoked ______ the authorization expires.
第一空：(A) whoever (B) whomever (C) whichever (D) whatever
第二空：(A) whenever (B) wherever (C) however (D) whatever

**15.** ______ the acquisition ultimately benefits both companies remains uncertain, though analysts largely agree it will reduce costs.
(A) Whoever (B) Whatever (C) Whichever (D) Whether
