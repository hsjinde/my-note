# 02_動詞分類

## 核心概念
動詞是句子的核心，描述主詞的動作或狀態。多益常考「及物/不及物」的區別，以及「連綴動詞」後應接形容詞而非副詞的陷阱。

## 1. 及物動詞 (Transitive Verbs) vs. 不及物動詞 (Intransitive Verbs)

| 分類 | 說明 | 特徵 | 範例 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **及物動詞** | 動作有對象，後面需要接受詞 | 動詞後方有**名詞 (受詞)** | *discuss* the plan, *submit* a report |
| **不及物動詞** | 動作無對象，不能直接接受詞 | 動詞後方**沒有受詞**，常接介系詞 | *arrive* at, *consist* of, *rise* |

*   **多益陷阱**：有些常見動詞永遠不及物，不能有被動式！
    *   `rise` (上升，不及物) / `raise` (提高，及物)
    *   `arrive`, `occur`, `consist` ➔ 絕對不能有被動語態

## 2. 完全動詞 vs. 不完全動詞

*   **完全動詞 (Complete Verb)**：本身語意完整，不需要補語。
    *   He *works* diligently. / The meeting *ended* on time.
*   **不完全動詞 (Incomplete Verb)**：語意不完整，需要受詞或補語才能成句。
    *   She *became* **a manager**. (需要補語)
    *   We *elected* **him** **chairman**. (需要受詞 + 補語)

## 3. 連綴動詞 (Linking Verbs) 🌟
連綴動詞後面必須接**形容詞**，而不是副詞。這是多益詞性題的高頻考點！

*   **感官動詞**：look, sound, smell, taste, feel
*   **狀態/變化動詞**：be, seem, appear, become, remain, stay, keep, turn, get

**正確範例**：
*   The music sounds **beautiful**. (✓ 形容詞，修飾主詞 music)
*   ~~The music sounds **beautifully**.~~ (✗ 副詞，錯誤)
*   He *remained* **calm** during the meeting. (✓)

## 📝 多益秒殺解題技巧
1.  **詞性陷阱**：看到 look, sound, feel, remain 等連綴動詞後的空格 ➔ 秒選**形容詞**，而不是副詞。
2.  **不及物動詞辨認**：選項包含「被動式」，但主詞是做動作的人/物 (而非接受動作) ➔ 選**主動式**。
3.  **不及物動詞清單**：多益常見 `occur`, `rise`, `consist of`, `agree with` ➔ 不接直接受詞。

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **形似陷阱動詞組**：`rise`(不及物,上升) / `raise`(及物,提高) / `arise`(不及物,發生、產生)，三字外型相似，考題常用來混淆，要看後面有沒有受詞。
2.  **lie / lay 陷阱**：`lie`(躺、位於，不及物，過去式 lay) / `lay`(放置，及物，過去式 laid)，考時態時特別容易混淆。
3.  **remain 的雙重用法**：`remain` 可作連綴動詞(+adj.)，也可作不及物動詞單獨使用(=stay, continue to exist)，如 `Many questions remain.`（許多問題仍然存在）。
4.  **被動語態陷阱**：不及物動詞（如 occur, happen, arrive, exist）**沒有被動式**，看到選項出現 `is occurred` 這類形式直接刪除。
5.  **感官動詞 + 受詞 + 原形V/V-ing**：`see/hear/watch + O + V/V-ing` 屬於句型五的變化，並非連綴動詞，注意與句型二區分。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語
### 及物動詞
*   **discuss** 動詞 討論（後不加 about） | The team will *discuss* the proposal tomorrow.
*   **address** 動詞 處理、解決 | The manager will *address* the customer's concerns.
*   **submit** 動詞 提交 | Employees must *submit* the form by Friday.
*   **announce** 動詞 宣布 | The company will *announce* the merger next week.

### 不及物動詞
*   **arrive** 動詞 到達（後接 at/in，不可直接加受詞） | The shipment will *arrive* at the warehouse tomorrow.
*   **occur** 動詞 發生 | The error *occurred* during the system update.
*   **consist of** 片語 由...組成 | The committee *consists of* five members.
*   **result in / result from** 片語 導致 / 起因於 | The delay *resulted from* a technical issue.

### 連綴動詞
*   **appear** 動詞 看起來（連綴） | The results *appear* promising.
*   **remain** 動詞 保持、仍然是 | The office *remains* closed on holidays.
*   **turn** 動詞 變得 | The weather *turned* cold suddenly.

### 常混淆組
*   **rise / raise** 動詞 上升(不及物) / 提高(及物) | Prices *rise* every year. / The firm *raised* its prices.
*   **lie / lay** 動詞 躺、位於(不及物) / 放置(及物) | The documents *lie* on the desk. / She *laid* the documents on the desk.

## 💪 練習題（15 題）

**1.** The number of applicants ___ significantly over the past three years.
(A) raised (B) has risen (C) rose it (D) has been raised

**2.** All visitors must ___ their ID cards at the front desk before entering the building.
(A) show (B) arrive (C) occur (D) consist

**3.** The new policy ___ reasonable to most employees after the explanation.
(A) sounded (B) sounds it (C) was sounded (D) has been sounded

**4.** The annual conference ___ of three main sessions and a networking dinner.
(A) contains (B) consists (C) includes (D) composes

**5.** According to the report, customer complaints ___ mainly because of shipping delays.（第一空）Managers were asked to ___ the issue immediately by contacting the logistics team.（第二空）
第一空：(A) occurred (B) were occurred (C) raised (D) were raised
第二空：(A) address (B) address to (C) respond (D) respond to

**6.** The board voted to ___ the annual membership fee by five percent starting in January.
(A) rise (B) raise (C) arise (D) risen

**7.** After a six-hour session, the negotiating teams finally ___ at a compromise acceptable to both sides.
(A) reached (B) arrived (C) achieved (D) obtained

**8.** The instructions printed on the packaging must remain ___ even after prolonged refrigeration.
(A) legible (B) legibly (C) legibility (D) legibleness

**9.** The shareholders ___ Mr. Ibarra treasurer for a second consecutive term at the annual meeting.
(A) appointed (B) appeared (C) arrived (D) responded

**10.** Several minor issues ___ during the pilot test, and all of them were documented by the engineering team.
(A) were arisen (B) arose (C) were arising to (D) has arisen

**11.** The relocation plan seemed ___ until the finance team presented the projected moving costs.
(A) reason (B) reasonably (C) reasonable (D) reasoning

**12.** The department heads will ___ the proposed budget revisions at Monday morning's planning meeting.
(A) discuss (B) comply (C) consist (D) participate

**13.** Sales of the flagship model ___ steadily since the packaging was redesigned two quarters ago.
(A) have grown (B) have been grown (C) have grown up (D) are grown

**14.** The negotiations turned ___ once the two sides agreed on a phased payment schedule.
(A) productive (B) productively (C) production (D) productivity

**15.** Please ___ the attached reimbursement form and return it to the human resources office by Friday.
(A) complete (B) occur (C) arrive (D) consist
