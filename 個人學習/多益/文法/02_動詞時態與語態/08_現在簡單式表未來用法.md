# 08_現在簡單式表未來用法

## 核心概念
多益商業英文中，有三種情境即使語意指向未來，動詞仍必須用**現在簡單式**，而不是 will／be going to：(1) 交通、播映、營業等**固定時刻表**；(2) 正式**行程表／議程**上已印出的排定事項；(3) **時間或條件副詞子句**。這三種用法的共通點是：說話者陳述的不是「意志」或「預測」，而是一個**已經被制度、時刻表或邏輯條件決定好的事實**。本章聚焦這三種現在簡單式代未來的細節與易混淆的連接詞，與第五章「未來式與未來完成式進階」互補，不重複探討 will／be going to／未來完成式本身的辨析。

## 1. 固定時刻表、班表、營業時間 → 現在簡單式
交通工具的發車／起降時間、戲院播映場次、店家營業時間變更等，只要是**印在時刻表或公告上的既定事實**，一律用現在簡單式，即使描述的是明天甚至下個月的事。
*   The 9:15 train **departs** from Platform 3 tomorrow morning.
*   The renovated branch **reopens** for business next Monday.
*   The documentary **screens** twice daily starting this Friday.

## 2. 正式議程／行程表上已排定的項目 → 現在簡單式
會議議程、出差行程表、活動流程表上逐項列出的內容，即使是描述未來某天的活動，也用現在簡單式陳述，語氣客觀、像在唸出一份文件。
*   According to the agenda, the keynote address **precedes** the panel discussion.
*   Per the itinerary, the delegation **arrives** in Taipei on the 3rd and **departs** on the 6th.
*   The updated policy **takes effect** the moment the board **signs** the resolution.

## 3. 時間／條件副詞子句：現在簡單式代未來（進階連接詞）
除了 if、when、before、after、once、as soon as、by the time 之外，多益中高分題目常換用下列連接詞，規則完全相同——子句一律用現在簡單式，未來式只留給主句：
*   **whenever**：Whenever the inventory system **flags** a shortage, the warehouse manager will place a reorder automatically.
*   **so long as / as long as**：So long as the client **confirms** the order by Friday, production will begin on schedule.
*   **in case**：In case the shipment **arrives** late, the logistics team will notify all retail partners in advance.
*   **provided that / on condition that**：Provided that the auditor **approves** the figures, the report will be released next week.
*   **unless**：The merger will not proceed unless every shareholder **signs** the agreement.
*   **lest**：The technician double-checks every cable lest the demo **fails** during the live presentation.

## 4. 常見誘答：主句用未來，考生誤把子句也寫成未來式
子句加上 will 是最常見的錯誤。判斷方法：先找出句子的**主要子句**（通常帶有 will、be going to 或祈使語氣），其餘由連接詞領出的部分即為副詞子句，一律改回現在簡單式。
*   ❌ Once the contract *will be* signed, the vendor will begin shipping supplies.
    → ✅ Once the contract **is signed**, the vendor will begin shipping supplies.

## 5. 辨析：現在簡單式代未來 vs. 現在進行式代未來
現在進行式（第五章已介紹）強調「某個人已確認的個人行程」；現在簡單式代未來則強調「制度性、非個人的既定事實」，例如時刻表、法規生效日、議程項目。兩者常同時出現在同一則篇章中，分別描述「公司排定的事」與「個人已安排的行程」。
*   時刻表用法：The shuttle bus **leaves** the lobby every hour on the hour.
*   個人行程用法：Ms. Tanaka **is meeting** the auditors at 10 a.m. tomorrow.

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **時刻表 / 播映 / 營業時間題**：出現 train, flight, ferry, screening, showtime, business hours 等字眼並描述未來事件 ➔ 優先選現在簡單式，而非 will。
2.  **議程 / 行程表題**：出現 according to the agenda、per the itinerary、on the schedule 等提示語 ➔ 該子句或主要動詞多半用現在簡單式。
3.  **子句一律現在式**：if, when, before, after, once, as soon as, by the time, whenever, unless, provided that, so long as, in case, lest 引導的副詞子句，動詞一律用現在簡單式，未來式留給主句。
4.  **先抓主句再判斷子句**：句中若已有一個帶 will 的子句，另一個由連接詞引導的子句幾乎必為現在簡單式的陷阱設計。
5.  **effective + 日期**：句尾或句首出現 effective + 未來日期，通常搭配現在簡單式描述「自該日起生效的事實」，而非未來式。
6.  **whenever vs. when 的差異**：whenever 強調「每次、任何時候」的重複條件，when 強調「特定時間點」，但在代未來的現在式規則上兩者相同。
7.  **so long as / provided that / on condition that 同義互換**：三者皆表「只要」，引導的子句一律用現在簡單式，是段落填空常考的同義詞替換陷阱。
8.  **in case 不是 if**：in case 表「以防萬一」，其子句同樣用現在簡單式，但語意是「預先防範」而非「條件成立才發生」，翻譯時要區分。
9.  **lest 後面接原形動詞或 should + V**：正式英文中 lest 子句可用現在式、原形動詞或 should + 原形，多益考題多考現在簡單式版本。
10. **議程類篇章題找關鍵動詞**：precede, follow, conclude, commence 等描述「議程順序」的動詞，即使描述未來活動流程，也常用現在簡單式陳述。
11. **不要被主句時態誤導**：即使整段文章其餘句子都用未來式或未來完成式，只要出現時間／條件連接詞引導的子句，仍要切回現在簡單式判斷。
12. **現在簡單式代未來 vs. 現在進行式代未來**：制度性、非個人的既定事實（時刻表、法規生效）用現在簡單式；個人已確認的行程安排用現在進行式，兩者常在同一篇章中對比出現。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

### 時刻表與行程相關名詞
*   **itinerary** n. 行程表 | Please review the *itinerary* before the delegation departs for Tokyo.
*   **agenda** n. 議程 | The updated *agenda* lists three items for tomorrow's board meeting.
*   **timetable** n. 時刻表 | The revised *timetable* takes effect starting next Monday.
*   **schedule** v./n. 排定；時程表 | The renovation team *schedules* the inspection for early next week.
*   **slot** n. 時段 | Each presenter is given a fifteen-minute *slot* during the conference.

### 出發、召開、恢復相關動詞
*   **depart** v. 出發、離開 | The overnight ferry *departs* from Pier 5 at 11 p.m.
*   **departure** n. 出發、離境 | Passengers should check in two hours before *departure*.
*   **convene** v. 召開（會議） | The committee *convenes* every first Tuesday of the month.
*   **reconvene** v. 重新召開 | The panel *reconvenes* immediately after the lunch break.
*   **adjourn** v. 休會、延期 | The meeting *adjourns* at noon and resumes at two.
*   **resume** v. 恢復、重新開始 | Production *resumes* the moment the new parts arrive.
*   **suspend** v. 暫停 | Ferry service *suspends* whenever wind speeds exceed the safety limit.
*   **dispatch** v. 派遣、發送 | The courier *dispatches* every parcel within one hour of pickup.
*   **proceed** v. 進行、繼續進行 | The launch event *proceeds* as planned unless the weather worsens.

### 指定、規定與分配相關動詞
*   **designate** v. 指定 | The floor plan *designates* Room 3B as the interview venue.
*   **stipulate** v. 明文規定 | The contract *stipulates* that payment is due within thirty days.
*   **allocate** v. 分配 | The finance office *allocates* travel budgets at the start of each quarter.

### 準時與進度相關形容詞、片語
*   **punctual** adj. 準時的 | Delegates are expected to remain *punctual* throughout the summit.
*   **punctuality** n. 準時 | The airline is known for its *punctuality* on domestic routes.
*   **on time** phr. 準時 | The shuttle bus arrives *on time* every single morning.
*   **ahead of schedule** phr. 提前於進度 | The construction crew is running *ahead of schedule* this month.
*   **behind schedule** phr. 落後於進度 | If the shipment is *behind schedule*, notify the client immediately.
*   **run late** phr. 誤點、延誤 | The connecting flight *runs late* whenever the first leg is delayed.
*   **take place** phr. 舉行 | The annual shareholders' meeting *takes place* in the main auditorium.
*   **effective** adj. 生效的 | The new dress code is *effective* the first day of next month.

### 時間／條件副詞子句常用連接詞
*   **whenever** conj. 無論何時 | The system sends an alert *whenever* stock levels fall below the threshold.
*   **so long as** conj. 只要 | So long as the invoice *matches* the purchase order, payment proceeds automatically.
*   **in case** conj. 以防萬一 | Keep a backup generator ready *in case* the main power supply fails.
*   **lest** conj. 以免 | The auditor reviews every entry twice *lest* an error slip through.
*   **insofar as** conj. 就...而言 | The policy applies *insofar as* the employee remains on the payroll.

## 💪 練習題（15 題）

**1.** According to the printed timetable, the express train to the port city ______ Platform 6 at exactly 7:40 tomorrow morning.
(A) will leave (B) leaves (C) left (D) has left

**2.** Per the conference agenda, the opening remarks ______ the keynote speech, so attendees should be seated by nine.
(A) precede (B) will precede (C) preceded (D) have preceded

**3.** The renovated flagship store ______ for business next Saturday with a special discount for early customers.
(A) will reopen (B) reopens (C) will have reopened (D) reopened

**4.** So long as the supplier ______ the revised specifications, the assembly line will resume full production by Thursday.
(A) will confirm (B) confirms (C) confirmed (D) is confirming

**5.** Whenever the monitoring system ______ an unusual spike in traffic, the security team automatically receives an alert.
(A) will detect (B) detects (C) detected (D) had detected

**6.** In case the keynote speaker's flight ______, the organizers have already arranged a backup presenter for the opening session.
(A) will be delayed (B) is delayed (C) was delayed (D) had been delayed

**7.** Unless every regional office ______ its year-end figures by Friday, the consolidated report will not be finalized on time.
(A) submits (B) will submit (C) submitted (D) has submitted

**8.** The updated remote-work policy ______ effective the moment the CEO ______ the final memo.（第一空）（第二空）
第一空：(A) becomes (B) will become (C) became (D) is becoming
第二空：(A) signs (B) will sign (C) signed (D) has signed

**9.** The technician double-checks every connection lest the live demonstration ______ in front of the visiting investors.
(A) will fail (B) fails (C) failed (D) had failed

**10.** Once the shipment ______ customs clearance, the warehouse team will begin unloading it immediately.
(A) will pass (B) passes (C) passed (D) has been passing

**11.** The committee reconvenes at two o'clock, and the revised budget proposal ______ the first item on the agenda for that session.
(A) will be (B) is (C) was (D) has been

**12.** Provided that the landlord ______ the lease amendment by Monday, the branch office can move in as originally planned.
(A) will approve (B) approves (C) approved (D) has approved

**13.** According to the itinerary, the delegation (1) ______ in Osaka on the 3rd and (2) ______ for Seoul two days later.

(1) (A) arrives (B) will arrive (C) arrived (D) has arrived
(2) (A) departs (B) will depart (C) departed (D) had departed

**14.** As long as the vendor ______ every unit before the final inspection, the retailer will not accept the delivery.
(A) does not inspect (B) will not inspect (C) did not inspect (D) had not inspected

**15.** The ferry service suspends automatically whenever the wind speed ______ the posted safety limit, regardless of the day's schedule.
(A) will exceed (B) exceeds (C) exceeded (D) has exceeded
