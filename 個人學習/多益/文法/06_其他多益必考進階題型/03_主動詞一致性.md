# 03_主動詞一致性

## 核心概念
主動詞一致性 (Subject-Verb Agreement) 指的是動詞的單複數必須與主詞保持一致。多益常考「找出真正的主詞」，因為長句中容易被插入語或修飾語干擾，導致誤判主詞的單複數。

## 1. 基本規則

*   **單數主詞** ➔ 動詞加 **-s / -es** (第三人稱單數現在式)
*   **複數主詞** ➔ 動詞**不加 -s**

## 2. 多益陷阱：找出真正的主詞 🌟

### A. 中間修飾語的干擾
介系詞片語 (of/with/in...) 或關係子句插入主詞和動詞之間，**不影響主詞的單複數**。

*   The report *on sales figures* **is** ready. (主詞是 report，不是 figures)
*   The employees *who work in the R&D department* **are** highly qualified.
*   Each of the managers **is** responsible for a different region. (主詞是 Each，單數)

### B. 集合名詞 (Collection Nouns)

| 用法 | 說明 | 範例 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 視為單數整體 | 美式英語常見用法 | The **committee** **has** made a decision. |
| 指個別成員 | 英式英語較常見 | The **team are** divided on this issue. |

多益通常以**單數**處理集合名詞：`company, team, committee, board, jury, family, audience`
*   The **company has** announced a new policy.

> [!note]
> `staff` 較特殊：指「全體員工」時常視為**複數** (The staff **are** well trained)，指「這批人力／編制」整體概念時才視為單數。多益考題中若不確定，優先觀察句意與前後動詞是否已定調。

### C. 不可數名詞 (永遠單數)
`information, advice, equipment, furniture, progress, research, news, evidence`
*   The **information** **is** confidential.
*   **News** of the merger **was** announced this morning.

## 3. 特殊主詞的一致性 🌟

| 主詞形式 | 一致性 | 範例 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **each / every + N** | 單數 | **Each** employee **is** required to sign. |
| **either / neither** (單獨) | 單數 | **Neither** of the proposals **was** accepted. |
| **A or B / Either A or B / Neither A nor B** | 動詞配合**靠近的主詞 (B)** | Neither the manager nor the employees **were** informed. |
| **not only A but also B** | 動詞配合**靠近的主詞 (B)** | Not only the staff but also the CEO **is** attending. |
| **A and B** | 複數 (兩人/物) | The CEO and the CFO **are** both present. |
| **There is/are** | 配合後方的**真正主詞** | **There are** several issues to discuss. |
| **The number of + N** | 單數 ("數量"本身) | **The number of** applicants **has** increased. |
| **A number of + N** | 複數 ("許多") | **A number of** applicants **have** applied. |

## 4. 分數與百分比
分數或百分比 + of + 名詞：動詞配合 **of 後方的名詞**。
*   **Fifty percent** of the workforce **is** working remotely.
*   **Three-fourths** of the documents **have** been reviewed.

## 📝 多益秒殺解題技巧
1.  **找真正的主詞**：先刪去所有 `of...`, `with...`, `in...` 等介系詞片語，剩下的才是真正的主詞。
2.  **each / every / one of** ➔ 動詞一律**單數**。
3.  **The number of** ➔ 單數；**A number of** ➔ 複數。
4.  **A or B / Neither A nor B** ➔ 動詞配合**最靠近的主詞 (B)**。
5.  **不可數名詞**（news, information, advice...）➔ 動詞一律**單數**。

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **動名詞/不定詞當主詞** ➔ 視為單數。
    *   **Completing** the certification **takes** three months.
    *   **To finish** the project on time **requires** extra staff.
2.  **子句 (that/what 子句) 當主詞** ➔ 視為單數。
    *   **What the client wants is** a faster turnaround.
3.  **由 and 連接但指同一人/物時** ➔ 視為單數 (常見於職稱或固定搭配)。
    *   **The CEO and founder** of the company **is** attending the ceremony. (同一人身兼兩職)
4.  **時間、金額、距離當主詞時通常視為單數整體**，而非逐一計算的複數。
    *   **Ten years is** a long time to wait.
    *   **Five hundred dollars is** too much for this item.
5.  **陷阱：Here/There 開頭的倒裝句仍要找真正主詞**，動詞需配合倒裝後方的主詞單複數。
    *   **Here is** the report you requested. (主詞 report，單數)
    *   **Here are** the documents you requested. (主詞 documents，複數)

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

### 集合/不可數名詞
*   **committee** (n. 委員會) 集合名詞，多益視為單數 | The **committee has** approved the budget.
*   **equipment** (n. 設備) 不可數 | The **equipment is** stored in the warehouse.
*   **furniture** (n. 家具) 不可數 | New **furniture has** arrived at the office.
*   **research** (n. 研究) 不可數 | **Research shows** that remote work boosts productivity.
*   **personnel** (n. 全體人員) 恆為複數 | **Personnel are** required to wear ID badges.

### 不定代名詞 / 限定詞
*   **each** (pron./adj. 每一個) 恆單數 | **Each** applicant **is** interviewed individually.
*   **everyone / everybody** (pron. 每個人) 恆單數 | **Everyone is** invited to the event.
*   **several** (adj. 幾個的) 恆複數 | **Several** proposals **have** been submitted.
*   **the majority of** (phr. 大部分的) 依後面名詞決定單複數 | **The majority of** staff **are** satisfied.

### 動詞
*   **consist of** (v. 由...組成) | The team **consists of** ten engineers.
*   **vary** (v. 因人/物而異) | Prices **vary** depending on the region.
