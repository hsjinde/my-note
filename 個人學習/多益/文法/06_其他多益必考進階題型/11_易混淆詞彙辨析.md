# 11_易混淆詞彙辨析

## 核心概念
多益 Part 5 有一類題目不考句型結構，而是考「兩個字形相近、詞性/詞義卻不同」的單字辨析（Word Form / Word Meaning Confusion）。這類題目往往四個選項是同一詞根的不同衍生詞，或是拼字相似但意思完全不同的字，必須靠「精確理解字義＋觀察句中線索（介系詞、名詞單複數、上下文邏輯）」才能選對。

## 1. 字尾相近但意義不同（-ic / -ical）
`economic`（經濟學上的、與經濟有關）vs `economical`（節省的、划算的）是最經典的一組。這類字尾為 -ic/-ical 的形容詞，往往一個偏「學術/領域相關」、一個偏「性質描述」。
- The government released new **economic** indicators. (與「經濟」這個領域有關)
- Buying in bulk is a more **economical** choice. (划算、省錢)

## 2. 字根相同但詞性走向不同（-ful / -ive）
`respectful`（表現出尊重的，形容態度）vs `respective`（各自的，用於修飾名詞表示對應關係）。兩字字根都來自 respect，但語意方向完全不同，考生常靠「感覺很像」誤選。
- Employees should remain **respectful** toward clients at all times.
- The three managers returned to their **respective** offices after the meeting.

## 3. 字尾相近、語意方向相反（-able / -ate）
`considerable`（相當大的，客觀描述數量/程度）vs `considerate`（體貼的，描述人對他人的態度）。前者修飾事物的規模，後者修飾人的性格。
- The company reported a **considerable** increase in revenue.
- It was **considerate** of her to reschedule the call for everyone's convenience.

## 4. 動詞與名詞的拼字陷阱
`affect`（動詞，影響）vs `effect`（名詞，影響/效果，也可作動詞「實現」）。判斷方法：看空格前後是否需要動詞，或前面是否有 the / an 等冠詞。
- Rising fuel costs will **affect** the company's shipping budget.
- The new policy will take **effect** next month.

## 5. 語意相近但語域/情境不同的近義詞
`principal`（首要的、主要的）vs `principle`（原則），`compliment`（稱讚）vs `complement`（互補、搭配），這類同音或形近詞在商業文件中極常被誤用，多益偏好用上下文邏輯來測試考生是否真的理解字義。
- Punctuality is a fundamental **principle** of our workplace culture.
- The new software **complements** the existing accounting system well.

## 6. 解題心法
遇到這類題目，不要只看單字「面熟」，先判斷：(1) 句子需要的詞性是什麼；(2) 空格修飾的對象是「人」還是「事物」；(3) 是否有固定搭配的介系詞或名詞單複數線索。三個線索綜合判斷，才能避開多益刻意設計的形似陷阱。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

### 形容詞字義辨析組
*   **economic** 形容詞 與經濟（學）有關的 | The minister discussed the *economic* impact of the new trade agreement.
*   **economical** 形容詞 節省的、划算的 | Taking the train is a more *economical* option than flying.
*   **respectful** 形容詞 恭敬的、表現尊重的 | Staff are expected to be *respectful* when handling customer complaints.
*   **respective** 形容詞 各自的 | All department heads submitted their *respective* budget proposals.
*   **considerable** 形容詞 相當大的（數量/程度） | The merger resulted in a *considerable* reduction in operating costs.
*   **comprehensive** 形容詞 全面的、詳盡的 | The consultant provided a *comprehensive* review of our supply chain.
*   **comprehensible** 形容詞 可理解的 | The revised contract is far more *comprehensible* to non-lawyers.
*   **sensible** 形容詞 明智的、合理的 | Reducing overtime was a *sensible* decision given the budget cuts.
*   **sensitive** 形容詞 敏感的（資訊/情緒） | Employee salary data is highly *sensitive* information.
*   **imaginative** 形容詞 富有創意的 | The design team came up with an *imaginative* solution to the packaging problem.
*   **imaginary** 形容詞 虛構的、想像中的 | The case study uses an *imaginary* company as an example.
*   **beneficial** 形容詞 有益的 | The training program proved *beneficial* to all new employees.

### 動詞/名詞拼字辨析組
*   **affect** (v. 及物) 影響 | Supply chain delays could *affect* our quarterly earnings.
*   **effect** 名詞 影響、效果 | The policy change had an immediate *effect* on employee morale.
*   **principal** 形容詞/名詞 主要的；本金 | The *principal* concern of the board is data security.
*   **principle** 名詞 原則 | Transparency is a core *principle* of our corporate governance.
*   **compliment** (v. 及物) 稱讚 | The client *complimented* the design team on their creative proposal.
*   **complement** (v. 及物) 使互補、搭配 | The new software *complements* our existing inventory system.
*   **advice** 名詞 建議（不可數） | She offered valuable *advice* on negotiating the contract.
*   **advise** (v. 及物) 建議 | The consultant *advised* management to delay the launch.
*   **council** 名詞 委員會、議會 | The city *council* approved the zoning request.
*   **counsel** (v. 及物) 提供諮詢建議 | The lawyer *counseled* the firm on the merger terms.
*   **stationary** 形容詞 靜止的、不動的 | The delivery truck remained *stationary* outside the warehouse for hours.

### 名詞辨析組
*   **personnel** 名詞 人事、員工（集合名詞） | *Personnel* costs account for the largest share of the budget.
*   **personal** 形容詞 個人的 | Please remove all *personal* items from your desk before the renovation.
*   **eminent** 形容詞 卓越的、著名的 | An *eminent* economist was invited to speak at the conference.
*   **credulous** 形容詞 輕信的 | Investors should not be *credulous* about unrealistic profit claims.
*   **exhaustive** 形容詞 詳盡無遺的 | The team conducted an *exhaustive* review of every vendor contract.
*   **exhausting** 形容詞 令人疲憊的 | The quarter-end audit was an *exhausting* process for the accounting staff.
*   **loyal** 形容詞 忠誠的（對人/公司） | *Loyal* customers receive priority access to new products.
*   **royal** 形容詞 皇室的 | The hotel chain markets itself with a *royal* theme.

## 📚 補充秒殺公式

1.  **economic vs economical**：economic 修飾與「經濟領域」相關的名詞（policy, growth, indicator），economical 修飾「省錢與否」的行為或方案（choice, option, way）。
2.  **respectful vs respective**：respectful 描述「人對他人的態度」，常見 be respectful of/toward；respective 修飾複數名詞表示「各自對應」，常見 their respective + 複數名詞。
3.  **considerable vs considerate**：considerable 描述「數量、程度之大」，常修飾 amount, increase, decline；considerate 描述「為他人著想的性格」，常見 It is considerate of 某人 to V。
4.  **comprehensive vs comprehensible**：comprehensive 表「涵蓋範圍廣、全面的」，修飾 report, review, plan；comprehensible 表「易於理解的」，修飾 language, instruction, explanation。
5.  **affect vs effect**：affect 幾乎都是及物動詞，直接接受詞；effect 幾乎都是名詞，固定搭配 take effect（生效）、have an effect on（對…造成影響）。
6.  **principal vs principle**：principal 作形容詞表「主要的」或作名詞表「本金、校長」；principle 作名詞表「原則」，兩者發音相同但拼字與詞性完全不同，須靠句意與詞性判斷。
7.  **compliment vs complement**：compliment 表「稱讚」，動詞常接人為受詞；complement 表「使互補、搭配得宜」，動詞常接事物為受詞。
8.  **advice vs advise**：advice 是不可數名詞（give/offer advice），advise 是動詞（advise someone to V），拼字只差一個字母卻是完全不同詞性。
9.  **stationary vs stationery**：stationary 表「靜止不動的」，形容詞；stationery 表「文具、信紙」，名詞，兩字發音相同，考題常利用此陷阱測試拼字辨識。
10. **personnel vs personal**：personnel 為集合名詞，指「全體員工、人事」，常搭配 personnel costs/department；personal 為形容詞，表「個人的」，修飾 belongings, information 等。
11. **eminent vs imminent**：eminent 表「卓越、著名的」，修飾人（scholar, expert）；imminent 表「即將發生的」，修飾事件或危機（shutdown, danger, deadline）。
12. **exhaustive vs exhausting**：exhaustive 表「詳盡無遺的」，修飾 review, research, list，與「疲累」無關；exhausting 才是「令人疲憊的」，修飾造成疲勞的事物或過程。

## 💪 練習題（15 題）

**1.** The finance director explained that leasing the delivery vans is a far more ______ option than purchasing them outright.
(A) economic (B) economical (C) economically (D) economics

**2.** Before releasing the new tariff schedule, the ministry consulted several ______ advisors on its likely impact on exports.
(A) economical (B) economic (C) economics (D) economize

**3.** Each regional manager was asked to submit a forecast for his or her ______ territory by Friday.
(A) respectful (B) respective (C) respectfully (D) respect

**4.** Interns are reminded to remain ______ even when handling frustrated customers on the support line.
(A) respective (B) respectful (C) respectfully (D) respects

**5.** The board noted a ______ drop in shipping costs after the new logistics contract was signed.
(A) considerate (B) considerable (C) considerably (D) consider

**6.** It was rather ______ of the supervisor to reschedule the meeting once she learned about the flight delay.
(A) considerable (B) considerate (C) consideration (D) considering

**7.** The consultant's ______ audit covered every branch office, from procurement records to payroll files.
(A) comprehensible (B) comprehensive (C) comprehension (D) comprehend

**8.** After the revision, the loan agreement is now written in far more ______ language for first-time borrowers.
(A) comprehensive (B) comprehensible (C) comprehend (D) comprehension

**9.** A sudden spike in raw material prices could seriously ______ next quarter's profit margin.
(A) effect (B) affect (C) affection (D) effective

**10.** The revised expense policy will take ______ as soon as the new fiscal year begins. The finance office confirmed that the change follows the same ______ used during last year's budget review.
第一空：(A) effect (B) affect (C) effects (D) affects
第二空：(A) principal (B) principals (C) principle (D) principled

**11.** During the closing dinner, the client openly ______ the design team on the originality of the packaging concept.
(A) complemented (B) complimented (C) compliment (D) complementary

**12.** The new scheduling software ______ the existing inventory system rather than replacing it entirely.
(A) compliments (B) complements (C) compliment (D) complementary

**13.** The vendor kept the delivery truck ______ outside the loading dock for nearly an hour while waiting for clearance.
(A) stationery (B) stationary (C) station (D) stationed

**14.** Before the office renovation begins, all ______ files and personal photographs must be cleared from every desk.
(A) personnel (B) personal (C) person (D) personally

**15.** Given the tight production schedule, the quality team was asked to run an ______ inspection of every unit rather than a partial sample. Staff described the two-day process as thoroughly ______, though management deemed it necessary before shipment.
第一空：(A) exhaustive (B) exhausting (C) exhausted (D) exhaustively
第二空：(A) exhaustive (B) exhausting (C) exhausted (D) exhaustively
