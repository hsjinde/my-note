# 01_名詞子句

## 核心概念
名詞子句 (Noun Clause) 是一個完整的子句 (有主詞和動詞)，在句子中扮演「名詞」的角色，可以作為主詞、受詞或補語。多益常考引導名詞子句的連接詞選擇。

## 1. 引導名詞子句的連接詞 🌟

| 連接詞 | 用法 | 範例 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **that** | 引導確定的事實或陳述 (子句完整，that 可省略) | I believe **(that)** the project **will succeed**. |
| **whether / if** | 引導「是否」的疑問 (yes/no question) | Please let me know **whether** you **can attend**. |
| **what** | 引導「什麼」的疑問 (缺受詞或主詞) | I don't understand **what** he **meant**. |
| **who / whom** | 引導「誰」的疑問 | We don't know **who** **will be** in charge. |
| **when / where** | 引導時間/地點的疑問 | Please confirm **when** the meeting **starts**. |
| **how** | 引導「如何/多少」的疑問 | She knows **how** the system **operates**. |
| **why** | 引導「為什麼」的疑問 | No one knows **why** the system **crashed**. |

> [!important] 名詞子句的語序
> 名詞子句必須使用**陳述語序 (S + V)**，不能使用疑問句語序！
> - ❌ I don't know **where is** he.
> - ✅ I don't know **where he is**.

## 2. 名詞子句的三種位置

### A. 作主詞
*   **That** the plan **failed** was unexpected. (名詞子句作主詞)
*   **That** customer satisfaction **has improved** is evident. (常用於正式文書)

### B. 作受詞 (最常見)
*   I know **(that)** the report **is** due tomorrow.
*   They agreed **(that)** changes **were necessary**.
*   Please confirm **whether** you **will attend** the event.

### C. 作補語
*   The problem is **(that)** we lack the budget.
*   My suggestion is **(that)** we **postpone** the meeting. ← (意志動詞規則：原形動詞)

## 3. whether vs. if 的差異

| | **whether** | **if** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 作主詞 | ✓ | ✗ |
| 介系詞後 | ✓ | ✗ |
| 後接 or not | ✓ (whether or not) | ✗ (if or not ✗) |
| 作受詞 | ✓ | ✓ |

*   **Whether** he attends **or not** doesn't matter. (✓ 作主詞，可接 or not)
*   Let me know **whether** / **if** you are available.

## 📝 多益秒殺解題技巧
1.  空格引導一個完整的子句，且在動詞後面 ➔ 先看子句是否缺主詞或受詞：
    *   **子句完整** (有 S + V) ➔ 選 `that` 或 `whether`。
    *   **子句缺主詞/受詞** ➔ 選 `what`, `who`, `which`。
2.  看到「是否」的語意 ➔ 選 `whether` (作主詞或介系詞後) 或 `if` (只作受詞)。
3.  名詞子句的動詞語序：確認是否為**陳述語序 (S + V)**，不是疑問句倒裝語序。

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **the fact that + 完整子句**：`fact` 後面接 `that` 引導的同位語子句 (說明 fact 的內容)，`that` 不可省略也不可換成 `which`。
    *   The fact **that** he **resigned** surprised everyone.
2.  **whatever / whichever / whoever** 也可引導名詞子句：意思是「無論什麼/任一個/無論是誰」，句意通常是「不論...都」。
    *   You may choose **whatever** color **you like**.
3.  **it is + 形容詞/名詞 + that 子句** (虛主詞句型)：句首的 it 是虛主詞，代替後面真正的 that 子句主詞，多益常考此句型的動詞是否為原形（意志動詞：suggest, recommend, require, demand 等）。
    *   It is essential **that** the report **be** submitted on time. (原形動詞)
4.  **名詞子句當介系詞受詞**：介系詞後面只能接 `whether`、`what`、`who` 等疑問詞子句，**不能接 that**。
    *   ❌ I am not sure of **that** he is right.
    *   ✅ I am not sure **of what** he said.
5.  **whether to V / what to V**：疑問詞 + to 不定詞，也是名詞子句的簡化形式，常作受詞。
    *   Please decide **whether to accept** the offer.

## 🔤 相關單字和片語
*   **assume** (v.) 假設、認為 | I **assume** that the shipment will arrive on time.
*   **confirm** (v.) 確認 | Please **confirm** whether you will attend the seminar.
*   **determine** (v.) 決定、判斷 | We need to **determine** what caused the delay.
*   **doubt** (v./n.) 懷疑 | I **doubt** that the plan will work.
*   **essential** (adj.) 必要的、必需的 | It is **essential** that all employees attend the training.
*   **evident** (adj.) 明顯的 | It is **evident** that sales have increased.
*   **inquire** (v.) 詢問 | She **inquired** whether the position was still open.
*   **mandatory** (adj.) 強制的、必須的 | It is **mandatory** that staff wear badges.
*   **recommend** (v.) 建議 | The manager **recommends** that we revise the budget.
*   **require** (v.) 要求 | Policy **requires** that visitors sign in.
*   **suggestion** (n.) 建議 | My **suggestion** is that we postpone the launch.
*   **uncertain** (adj.) 不確定的 | I am **uncertain** whether the data is accurate.
*   **whatever** (pron.) 無論什麼 | Choose **whatever** option suits you best.
*   **in charge of** (片語) 負責 | We don't know who is **in charge of** the project.
*   **as to** (片語) 關於 | There is disagreement **as to** whether the plan is feasible.

## 💪 練習題（15 題）
1.  ______ the merger will proceed as planned remains uncertain among shareholders.
    (A) What
    (B) Whether
    (C) If
    (D) That which

2.  The board recommended that the proposal ______ reviewed by legal counsel before approval.
    (A) is
    (B) was
    (C) be
    (D) being

3.  Mr. Chen was not sure ______ the client had received the revised contract.
    (A) that
    (B) what
    (C) whether
    (D) about that

4.  短文填空：The committee has not yet decided (1)______ the annual conference will be held in Taipei or Kaohsiung. A final announcement is expected once (2)______ has completed its review of venue costs.
    (1)
    (A) what
    (B) whether
    (C) which
    (D) that
    (2)
    (A) it
    (B) whoever
    (C) the committee that
    (D) whatever committee

5.  It is essential that every applicant ______ all required documents before the deadline.
    (A) submits
    (B) submitted
    (C) submit
    (D) submitting

**6.** The dispatcher will check ___ the replacement driver has already left the depot.
(A) that (B) if (C) what (D) which

**7.** ___ the two departments will share a single budget line remains under discussion by senior management.
(A) If (B) Whether (C) That which (D) What

**8.** The technician could not explain ___ had triggered the false alarm on the third floor.
(A) that (B) what (C) whether (D) if

**9.** The interoffice memo did not make clear ___ would be chairing the transition committee.
(A) that (B) whom (C) who (D) it

**10.** Everyone at the briefing agreed ___ the current inspection interval is too long for high-traffic equipment.
(A) that (B) what (C) which (D) whose

**11.** The panel will determine ___ of the three prototypes best meets the durability requirement.
(A) that (B) what (C) which (D) whether

**12.** The shipping manifest does not indicate ___ the damaged container was offloaded.
(A) that (B) what (C) where (D) whom

**13.** ___ the pilot line exceeded its output target surprised even the most optimistic analysts.
(A) That (B) What (C) Which (D) Whether or

**14.** Much depends on ___ the regulator approves the revised emission threshold before the fiscal year ends.
(A) if (B) that (C) whether (D) what

**15.** The onboarding guide clearly states ___ new hires should contact when their access card fails.
(A) that (B) whom (C) whether (D) which
