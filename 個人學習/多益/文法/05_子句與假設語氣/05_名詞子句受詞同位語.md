# 05_名詞子句受詞同位語

## 核心概念
名詞子句除了當主詞、補語之外，最常見的兩種角色是**動詞的受詞**、**介系詞的受詞**，以及本課的重點——**同位語子句 (Appositive Clause)**：緊接在抽象名詞（如 fact, news, belief, rumor）後面，用來「說明這個名詞的具體內容」。同位語子句與形容詞子句（關係子句）最大的差異在於：同位語子句的 that 不能省略、也不能換成 which，且子句本身文法完整，不缺任何成分。

## 1. 名詞子句作動詞受詞
及物動詞（如 believe, announce, confirm, deny, reveal, imply）後面可直接接 that 子句，作為動詞的受詞。口語與非正式書面中 that 常省略，但正式商業書信中通常保留。

*   The manager **announced that** the branch would open in March.
*   Investors **deny that** the merger talks have collapsed.
*   The audit **revealed that** several invoices had been duplicated.

## 2. 名詞子句作介系詞受詞
介系詞後面**不能直接接 that 子句**，只能接疑問詞（what, whether, who, how...）引導的名詞子句，或先加上一個名詞再用同位語子句說明。

*   ❌ The manager is confident **of that** the plan will succeed.
*   ✅ The manager is confident **of what** the new plan can achieve.
*   ✅ The manager is confident **that** the plan will succeed. (confident 後面直接接 that，不透過介系詞)

> [!important] be + 形容詞 + that 子句
> 許多「be + 形容詞」片語（be confident, be aware, be concerned, be convinced）習慣上省略介系詞，直接接 that 子句，這是多益常見的陷阱：看到 be aware **of** 時，後面若是完整子句就不能再保留 of。
> - ❌ We are aware **of that** the deadline has changed.
> - ✅ We are aware **that** the deadline has changed.

## 3. 同位語子句 (Appositive Noun Clause)
特定的抽象名詞（多與「訊息、想法、事實」相關）後面可以直接接 that 子句，說明該名詞的具體內容，這個 that 子句稱為「同位語子句」，功能等同於「= 名詞」。

常見可接同位語子句的名詞：fact, news, belief, rumor, possibility, likelihood, notion, assumption 之外，本課聚焦 allegation, assertion, conviction, speculation, hypothesis, contention, indication, implication, presumption, disclosure, revelation, verdict, confirmation, admission, statement, proof, concern, impression, objection。

*   The **news that** the factory would close shocked every employee.
*   There is a growing **concern that** shipping costs will rise again next quarter.
*   The **allegation that** the supplier falsified the inspection report is under investigation.

## 4. 同位語子句 vs. 關係子句的辨識
| 判斷依據 | 同位語子句 | 關係子句（形容詞子句） |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| that 可否省略 | 不可省略 | 視情況可省略（受格關代） |
| that 可否換成 which | 不可 | 可以（非人稱先行詞） |
| 子句是否缺成分 | 不缺（文法完整） | 缺主詞或受詞 |
| 語意關係 | 子句＝先行名詞的內容 | 子句修飾、限定先行名詞 |

*   同位語：The **claim that the shipment was delayed** turned out to be false. (claim 的內容就是 the shipment was delayed，子句完整)
*   關係子句：The report **that the manager submitted** was rejected. (submitted 後面缺受詞，that 修飾 report)

## 5. 動詞 + 受詞 + that 子句的雙受詞結構
部分動詞（inform, notify, remind, assure, convince）需要「人稱受詞 + that 子句」的結構，that 子句本身不是直接受詞，而是說明內容的補充子句。

*   Please **notify** the finance team **that** the budget has been approved.
*   The supervisor **assured** the client **that** the delay would not affect quality.

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **抽象名詞 + that 子句＝同位語**：只要名詞語意是「事實／消息／看法／指控」類，且後面接的子句文法完整，就是同位語子句，that 不可換成 which。
2.  **介系詞後不接 that**：介系詞（of, in, on, about）之後只能接疑問詞子句（what/whether/how），看到「介系詞 + that + 完整子句」幾乎必是錯誤選項。
3.  **be aware/confident/certain/convinced + that**：這類「be + 形容詞」後面直接加 that 子句，中間不需要介系詞，即使字典上該形容詞常搭配介系詞。
4.  **notify/inform/remind/assure + 人 + that 子句**：人稱受詞在前，that 子句在後，說明告知的內容；不可寫成 notify that 人。
5.  **同位語子句 vs. 關係子句判斷口訣**：子句「不缺東西」就是同位語，子句「缺主詞或受詞」就是形容詞子句。
6.  **the possibility/likelihood that**：兩者皆表「...的可能性」，後面直接接完整子句，不可寫成 the possibility of that。
7.  **concern that vs. concern about + N**：concern 後接子句用 that，接名詞則用 about/over，兩者不可混用（❌ concern about that...）。
8.  **虛主詞 It + be + 過去分詞 + that**：It is confirmed/revealed/reported that... 為被動虛主詞句型，常見於商業新聞與公告，that 子句是真正主詞的內容。
9.  **同位語子句可接在句首主詞位置**：The fact/rumor/belief that S+V + 主要動詞，此時主詞的核心是抽象名詞，動詞須與該抽象名詞（單數）一致，不要被子句內的動詞誤導。
10. **deny/admit + that 子句**：deny（否認）、admit（承認）為及物動詞，直接接 that 子句作受詞，語意相反但句型相同。
11. **statement/proof/testimony + that**：法律與稽核情境常見，statement that..., proof that..., testimony that... 皆為同位語結構，強調「聲明/證明/證詞的內容」。
12. **imply/indication + that 的差異**：imply 是動詞直接接 that 子句作受詞；indication 是名詞，須先接 an indication，再接 that 子句作同位語。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

### 動詞（可直接接 that 子句作受詞）
*   **notify** (v. 及物) 通知 | The office will **notify** employees that the parking lot is closed.
*   **deny** (v. 及物) 否認 | The spokesperson **denied** that the company had violated any regulations.
*   **clarify** (v. 及物) 澄清 | The manager **clarified** that the new policy applies only to full-time staff.
*   **imply** (v. 及物) 暗示 | Her tone **implied** that the negotiation was not going well.
*   **assure** (v. 及物) 向...保證 | The technician **assured** the client that the system was secure.
*   **reveal** (v. 及物) 揭露 | The audit **revealed** that several accounts had been mismanaged.

### 抽象名詞（可接同位語子句：N + that + 完整子句）
*   **notification** (n.) 通知 | We received a **notification** that the shipment had cleared customs.
*   **rumor** (n.) 謠言 | The **rumor** that the CEO would resign turned out to be untrue.
*   **allegation** (n.) 指控 | The **allegation** that the firm overcharged clients is being reviewed.
*   **belief** (n.) 信念 | There is a common **belief** that remote work increases productivity.
*   **possibility** (n.) 可能性 | Management raised the **possibility** that the plant might relocate.
*   **likelihood** (n.) 可能性 | The report noted the **likelihood** that demand would decline next quarter.
*   **assertion** (n.) 主張、斷言 | The lawyer challenged the **assertion** that the contract was invalid.
*   **notion** (n.) 觀念、想法 | Some staff still hold the **notion** that overtime pay is guaranteed.
*   **conviction** (n.) 堅信 | The director spoke with the **conviction** that the plan would succeed.
*   **speculation** (n.) 揣測 | There has been **speculation** that the two firms will merge.
*   **hypothesis** (n.) 假說 | The team tested the **hypothesis** that faster shipping would raise sales.
*   **contention** (n.) 論點、主張 | Her main **contention** is that the budget cuts were unnecessary.
*   **indication** (n.) 跡象 | There was no **indication** that the vendor had missed the deadline.
*   **implication** (n.) 暗示、含意 | The email carried the **implication** that layoffs might follow.
*   **presumption** (n.) 推測、假定 | The contract is based on the **presumption** that both parties act in good faith.
*   **testimony** (n.) 證詞 | The witness gave **testimony** that the invoice had been altered.
*   **disclosure** (n.) 揭露 | The firm made a **disclosure** that its earnings would fall short of forecasts.
*   **revelation** (n.) 驚人的揭露 | The **revelation** that the data had been leaked alarmed customers.
*   **verdict** (n.) 判斷、結論 | The committee's **verdict** was that the proposal needed more research.
*   **confirmation** (n.) 確認 | We are still waiting for **confirmation** that the payment has gone through.
*   **admission** (n.) 承認 | The manager's **admission** that the report was inaccurate surprised the board.
*   **statement** (n.) 聲明 | The bank issued a **statement** that all accounts remained secure.
*   **proof** (n.) 證明 | The auditor found **proof** that the figures had been falsified.
*   **concern** (n.) 憂慮 | There is growing **concern** that the supplier cannot meet the order.
*   **impression** (n.) 印象 | The client left with the **impression** that the deal was final.
*   **objection** (n.) 反對意見 | The board raised the **objection** that the timeline was too tight.

### 補充片語
*   **be convinced that** 深信 | The panel **is convinced that** the candidate is the best fit.
*   **be aware that** 知悉 | Staff **should be aware that** the office will close early on Friday.
*   **there is no doubt that** 毫無疑問 | **There is no doubt that** the campaign boosted sales.

## 💪 練習題（15 題）
**1.** The finance director **______** that the quarterly figures had been miscalculated.
(A) admitted (B) admission (C) admitting (D) admits to

**2.** There is a widespread **______** among staff that the merger will lead to layoffs.
(A) believe (B) belief (C) believing (D) believed

**3.** The technician assured the client **______** the server outage would not happen again.
(A) of (B) about (C) that (D) which

**4.** The auditor found **______** that several receipts had been submitted twice.
(A) proved (B) proving (C) to prove (D) proof

**5.** Employees **______** be aware that the parking garage will be closed for repairs next week.
(A) should (B) being (C) to (D) had

**6.** The **______** that the supplier missed three consecutive deadlines led the committee to seek a new vendor.
(A) allege (B) allegation (C) alleged (D) allegedly

**7.** Analysts have raised the **______** that shipping costs could rise sharply this winter.
(A) possible (B) possibly (C) possibility (D) possibilities

**8.** The spokesperson denied **______** the company had misled investors about its earnings.
(A) of (B) about (C) which (D) that

**9.** Despite the delay, the manager remains **______** that the project will finish on time.
第一空：(A) confident that (B) confident of (C) confidence that (D) confident about
Please notify the warehouse team **______** the delivery schedule has changed.
第二空：(A) of that (B) that (C) about that (D) which

**10.** There was no clear **______** that the machine had been serviced before the accident.
(A) indicate (B) indicating (C) indication (D) indicative

**11.** The board's **______** was that the proposal required further review before approval.
(A) verdicts (B) verdicting (C) to verdict (D) verdict

**12.** The client left the meeting with the **______** that the contract terms were final.
(A) impression (B) impressive (C) impress (D) impressed

**13.** The witness's **______** confirmed that the invoice had been altered after it was signed.
(A) testify (B) testimony (C) testifying (D) testified

**14.** Management is concerned **______** the new regulations will increase production costs.
(A) of (B) about (C) that (D) for

**15.** The report's central **______** is that automation will reduce processing errors by half.
(A) contend (B) contended (C) contentious (D) contention
