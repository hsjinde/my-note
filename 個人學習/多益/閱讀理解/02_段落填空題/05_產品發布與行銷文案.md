# 05_產品發布與行銷文案

## 📝 答題策略 🌟
1.  **公告類文章先抓時間線索**：留意 starting、effective、beginning 等未來時間副詞，決定空格動詞應使用未來式或未來被動。
2.  **語態判斷**：確認空格動詞的主詞是「動作執行者」還是「動作承受者」，產品、方案等無生命名詞常搭配被動語態。
3.  **句子插入題看主題延續**：插入句必須與前一句話題直接相關，且能自然銜接下一句的內容，與全文主題無關的選項即使文法正確也要排除。
4.  **詞性題看空格前後結構**：注意空格前是否有情態助動詞（must、should）或介系詞，決定要填動詞原形、名詞還是分詞。
5.  **轉折/邏輯詞題**：先判斷前後兩句是「條件對比」「補充說明」還是「舉例」關係，再選出對應的轉折副詞（otherwise、moreover、similarly、meanwhile）。
6.  **主詞動詞一致題**：找出真正的文法主詞（尤其是 both A and B、the potential + N 這類看似複雜的主詞），再決定動詞單複數與時態。

## 短文一：新品發布公告

Product Launch Announcement

TechNova Inc. is proud to unveil the SmartHome Hub X2, our latest innovation in home automation. The device ______(1) to the public starting next Tuesday, both online and in select retail stores. It connects wirelessly with more than two hundred compatible appliances, giving users centralized control through a single mobile application. ______(2). Customers who wish to activate the free two-year warranty must ______(3) their device online within 30 days of purchase. ______(4), the standard one-year warranty will still apply automatically without registration.

### 題目 1
(A) release
(B) released
(C) will be released
(D) releasing

### 題目 2
(A) The hub also supports voice commands through most popular virtual assistants.
(B) The company's quarterly earnings exceeded analysts' expectations.
(C) Our headquarters relocated to a larger building last year.
(D) The marketing department will attend an industry trade show in the fall.

### 題目 3
(A) register
(B) registration
(C) registering
(D) registered

### 題目 4
(A) Otherwise
(B) Moreover
(C) Similarly
(D) Meanwhile

## 短文二：會員推薦獎勵活動文案

Introducing Our Refer-a-Friend Rewards Program

Starting this month, GreenLeaf Wellness ______(5) a new referral rewards program to thank loyal customers for spreading the word. Each time a current member refers a friend who signs up for an annual membership, both the member and the friend ______(6) a $20 store credit. ______(7). To participate, simply share your unique referral code, found in your account settings, with anyone interested in joining. There is no limit to how many friends you can refer, so the potential rewards ______(8) endless for our most enthusiastic members.

### 題目 5
(A) launches
(B) launched
(C) is launching
(D) will have launched

### 題目 6
(A) receive
(B) receives
(C) will receive
(D) received

### 題目 7
(A) The credit can be applied toward any purchase and does not expire.
(B) Our stores will be closed for inventory next week.
(C) The company was founded by two college friends in 2010.
(D) Employees receive additional benefits after one year of service.

### 題目 8
(A) is
(B) are
(C) was
(D) has been
