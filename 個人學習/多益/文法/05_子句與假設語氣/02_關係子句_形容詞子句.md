# 02_關係子句_形容詞子句

## 核心概念
關係子句 (Relative Clause) 就是「**用一個句子來當作形容詞**」，用來修飾前面的名詞 (先行詞)。
用來連接這句話的字稱為「關係代名詞」(關代)。

## 1. 關係代名詞的選擇 🌟
關代的選擇取決於：1. 前面的先行詞 (人/物)；2. 關代在子句中扮演的角色 (主詞/受詞/所有格)。

| 先行詞 | 主詞 | 受詞 | 所有格 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **人** | who / that | whom / who / that | whose |
| **事物** | which / that | which / that | whose / of which |

*   **主詞用法**：關代後面直接接動詞。
    *   The man **who** *called* you is my boss.
*   **受詞用法**：關代後面接主詞+動詞。(受詞關代通常可以省略)
    *   The laptop **(which/that)** I *bought* is broken.
*   **所有格用法**：關代後面接名詞 (沒有冠詞)。
    *   The employee **whose** *performance* is the best will get a bonus. (那位表現最好的員工會拿到獎金)

## 2. 關係副詞 (When, Where, Why)
用來代替「介系詞 + 關係代名詞」，修飾時間、地點、原因。
*   **時間**：the day/time + **when** (= on/at which)
*   **地點**：the place/office + **where** (= in/at which)
*   **原因**：the reason + **why** (= for which)
    *   This is the office **where** we work. (= in which we work)

## 3. 限定用法與非限定用法 (逗號的差別)
*   **限定用法 (無逗號)**：用來限縮範圍，指出是「哪一個」。（可以使用 that）
    *   The employees *who work hard* will be promoted. (只有努力的員工會升職)
*   **非限定用法 (有逗號)**：單純補充說明，先行詞通常已經是獨一無二的特定對象。（**絕對不可使用 that**）
    *   Mr. Smith**, who is our CEO,** will visit Tokyo. (史密斯先生，他是我們的CEO，即將訪問東京)

## 📝 多益秒殺解題技巧
1.  **缺主詞 ➔ who/which/that**：空格後面直接接動詞 (如 is, has, are)。
2.  **缺所有格 ➔ whose**：空格前後都是名詞，且有「前面名詞的後面名詞」的關係 (The manager *whose* car...)。
3.  **看到逗號 ➔ 刪去 that**：空格前有逗號 ( , )，選項中有 that 絕對不選。
4.  **修飾地點/時間且後面句子完整 ➔ where/when**：如果空格後面的句子已經不缺主詞和受詞，通常選關係副詞。

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **複合關係代名詞 (what, whoever, whatever, whichever)**：本身已包含先行詞，前面**不可再加名詞**，且整個詞組通常作名詞子句（主詞/受詞），而非單純形容詞子句。
    *   **Whoever** finishes first will receive a bonus. (= Anyone who...)
    *   Please choose **whatever** item you prefer.
2.  **介系詞 + 關係代名詞**：正式書面英文中，介系詞可移到關代前面，此時關代**只能用 which/whom，不能用 that**。
    *   The project **in which** we invested has succeeded。(= The project **that** we invested **in**...)
3.  **省略關代 + 現在分詞/過去分詞** (減化子句)：關代 + be 動詞常被省略，只留下分詞修飾先行詞。
    *   The employees **(who are) working** overtime will be compensated.
    *   The report **(that was) submitted** yesterday contains errors.
4.  **all/some/most/none + of which/whom**：非限定用法中，先行詞為前面提到的全體，常考「數量詞 + of which/whom」。
    *   The company hired ten interns, **most of whom** graduated this year.
5.  **陷阱：先行詞是「整句話」時用 which**：若逗號後的 which 指的是前面「整個子句」而非單一名詞，也是非限定用法的一種。
    *   He arrived late, **which** annoyed his manager. (which 指「他遲到」這件事)

## 🔤 相關單字和片語
*   **whoever** (pron.) 無論是誰 | **Whoever** submits the form first will be considered.
*   **whatever** (pron.) 無論什麼 | You can use **whatever** tools you need.
*   **whichever** (pron.) 無論哪一個 | Choose **whichever** plan fits your budget.
*   **candidate** (n.) 候選人 | The candidate **whom** we interviewed impressed us.
*   **qualify** (v.) 符合資格 | Employees **who qualify** may apply for the grant.
*   **eligible** (adj.) 有資格的 | Staff **who are eligible** will receive benefits.
*   **supervisor** (n.) 主管 | The supervisor **whose** team performed best was promoted.
*   **branch** (n.) 分公司、分行 | This is the branch **where** the incident occurred.
*   **occasion** (n.) 場合、時機 | There was an occasion **when** the system failed.
*   **invest in** (片語) 投資於 | The fund **in which** we invested grew steadily.
*   **be responsible for** (片語) 負責 | The manager **who is responsible for** logistics resigned.
*   **credential** (n.) 資格證明 | Applicants **whose credentials** meet the standard will be shortlisted.
*   **comply with** (片語) 遵守 | Employees **who comply with** the policy avoid penalties.
*   **omit** (v.) 省略、遺漏 | The clause **(which was) omitted** changed the contract's meaning.
*   **majority** (n.) 多數 | The firm has 50 clients, **most of whom** renewed their contracts.

    (A) Whoever
    (B) Whatever
    (C) Whom
    (D) Whichever

    (A) they
    (B) whom
