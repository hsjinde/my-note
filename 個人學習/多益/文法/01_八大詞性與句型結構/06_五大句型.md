# 06_五大句型

## 核心概念
英文所有句子都可以歸納為五大基本句型。理解句型結構有助於判斷空格的詞性，也是閱讀測驗分析長句的基礎。

**縮寫說明**：S = 主詞、V = 動詞、O = 受詞、C = 補語、IO = 間接受詞、DO = 直接受詞

## 1. 五大句型總覽 🌟

| 句型 | 結構 | 範例 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **句型一** | S + Vi (不及物動詞) | The market **grows** rapidly. |
| **句型二** | S + V (連綴) + SC (主詞補語) | The manager **seems** *confident*. |
| **句型三** | S + Vt + O | She **submitted** *the report*. |
| **句型四** | S + Vt + IO + DO | He **gave** *me* *a raise*. |
| **句型五** | S + Vt + O + OC (受詞補語) | They **elected** *him* *president*. |

## 2. 各句型詳解

### 句型一：S + Vi
動詞本身語意完整，不需受詞。常見不及物動詞：appear, arrive, occur, rise。
*   The accident **occurred** at noon.

### 句型二：S + V(連綴) + SC
連綴動詞後面接補語說明主詞的狀態，補語必須是**名詞或形容詞**，不能是副詞！
*   常見連綴動詞：be, seem, appear, become, remain, stay, look, sound
*   The new plan sounds **reasonable**. (reasonable 是形容詞，修飾主詞 plan)

### 句型三：S + Vt + O
最常見的句型。及物動詞後方必須有受詞。
*   The company **launched** *a new product* last week.

### 句型四：S + Vt + IO + DO
「授與動詞」(give, send, offer, show, tell) 可接兩個受詞：先接「人 (間接受詞)」，再接「物 (直接受詞)」。
*   可改寫為：S + Vt + DO + **to/for** + IO
    *   He **gave** *her* *a present*. = He **gave** *a present* **to** *her*.

### 句型五：S + Vt + O + OC
受詞補語 (OC) 說明的是「受詞」的狀態，OC 可以是名詞或形容詞。
*   常見動詞：make, find, consider, keep, call, elect, appoint, name
    *   They **appointed** *her* *director*. (director 說明受詞 her)
    *   We **found** *the meeting* *useful*. (useful 說明受詞 the meeting)

## 📝 多益秒殺解題技巧
1.  **分辨句型二與句型三**：句型二的動詞是連綴動詞 (remain, become)，後面接的是主詞**補語 (形容詞/名詞)**；句型三的動詞是及物動詞，後面接的是受詞。
2.  **句型五陷阱**：`make + O + adj.` → 空格填形容詞。(e.g., The news *made* us **happy**.)
3.  **找主要動詞**：分析長句時，先找出主詞 (S)，再找主要動詞 (V)，剩下的就是受詞或補語。

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **句型四的兩種語序**：授與動詞 `S+V+IO+DO` 可改寫成 `S+V+DO+to/for+IO`，注意 `give/send/tell` 用 `to`，`buy/make/cook` 用 `for`。
2.  **句型五的省略 to be**：`consider, find, make, keep` 等動詞後接受詞補語時，補語前不需要 `to be`，如 `We consider him (to be) qualified.`（口語可省略 to be）。
3.  **修飾語不影響句型判斷**：長句中若有介系詞片語、分詞片語等修飾語，先刪除這些「插入語」，再判斷主要句型結構。
4.  **被動語態改寫句型**：句型三、四、五的主動句改為被動時，原受詞變主詞，句型五的受詞補語會直接留在動詞後，如 `He was elected president.`
5.  **there be 句型**：`There + be + S` 為特殊倒裝句型，此時真正的主詞在 be 動詞之後，動詞單複數需與其一致。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語
### 授與動詞（句型四）
*   **offer** 動詞 提供 | The company *offered* her a promotion.
*   **send** 動詞 寄送 | Please *send* the client a confirmation email.
*   **show** 動詞 展示 | He *showed* the manager his portfolio.
*   **grant** 動詞 授予 | The university *granted* him a scholarship.

### 受詞補語動詞（句型五）
*   **consider** 動詞 認為 | We *consider* the plan feasible.
*   **appoint** 動詞 任命 | The board *appointed* her CEO.
*   **keep** 動詞 保持（使處於某狀態） | Please *keep* the door open.
*   **name** 動詞 任命、命名 | They *named* him team leader.

### 連綴動詞（句型二）
*   **remain** 動詞 保持 | The market *remains* stable.
*   **become** 動詞 變成 | The company *became* profitable within a year.

### 常考結構
*   **there be** 句型 有... | *There are* several options available.
*   **it is...that** 片語 強調句型 | *It was* the manager *that* approved the budget.

## 💪 練習題（5 題）

**1.** The board of directors ___ Mr. Lin the new regional manager last week.
(A) named (B) said (C) told (D) spoke

**2.** The consultant's suggestions ___ very practical to the management team.
(A) seemed (B) made (C) considered (D) kept

**3.** The HR department will ___ all new employees a copy of the handbook.
(A) explain (B) provide (C) give (D) describe

**4.** ___ several qualified candidates for the position, the committee decided to extend the deadline.
(A) There is (B) There are (C) There being (D) It is

**5.** The survey results ___ the marketing team valuable insight into customer preferences.（第一空）Based on these findings, the director ___ the new advertising strategy effective immediately.（第二空）
第一空：(A) gave (B) said (C) explained (D) suggested
第二空：(A) considered (B) said (C) told (D) explained
