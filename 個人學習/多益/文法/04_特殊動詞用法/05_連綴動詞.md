# 05_連綴動詞

## 核心概念
連綴動詞（linking verbs）本身沒有明確的動作意義，功能是把主詞和「主詞補語」連接起來，說明主詞的狀態、性質或變化。**主詞補語通常是形容詞，而不是副詞**——因為補語是在描述「主詞」本身的狀態，不是在修飾動詞的動作方式。這是多益文法選項中「形容詞 vs. 副詞」考點的重要子類型，很多考生看到動詞後面有空格就直覺選副詞，結果誤觸連綴動詞陷阱。

*   The proposal **seems reasonable**. （reasonable 形容詞，描述 proposal 的狀態）
*   ~~The proposal seems reasonably.~~ （誤，副詞不能當補語）

## 1. 感官／狀態類連綴動詞
這類動詞描述主詞目前呈現的樣子或感受，常見有 seem, appear, look, feel, sound, taste, smell。

*   The client **seemed satisfied** with the revised quotation.
*   Her explanation **appeared convincing** to the entire panel.
*   The new packaging **looks professional** compared to the previous design.
*   The negotiation **sounds promising** after this morning's call.

## 2. 變化類連綴動詞
這類動詞描述主詞從一個狀態「變成」另一個狀態，常見有 become, get, grow, turn, go, come, fall。

*   After the merger, the workflow **became inefficient** for several departments.
*   The market **grew increasingly competitive** over the past decade.
*   The negotiations **turned tense** once the budget cap was mentioned.
*   The warehouse shelves **went empty** during the peak shopping season.

## 3. 維持／證明類連綴動詞
這類動詞描述主詞「保持」某狀態或「證明是」某狀態，常見有 remain, stay, keep, prove, turn out。

*   Despite the layoffs, employee morale **remained stable**.
*   The revised contract **proved acceptable** to both parties.
*   The new hire **turned out capable** far sooner than the manager expected.
*   Shipping costs **stayed manageable** even during the holiday rush.

## 4. 陷阱一：連綴動詞 vs. 一般動詞的雙重用法
有些動詞既可以當連綴動詞（後接形容詞），也可以當一般動詞（後接副詞修飾動作），要看語意判斷。

*   連綴動詞：The auditor **looked careful** while reviewing the ledger.（look=顯得，形容 auditor 本身）
*   一般動詞：The auditor **looked carefully** at every entry in the ledger.（look=看，副詞修飾「怎麼看」）
*   連綴動詞：The plan **grew complicated** as more stakeholders joined.（grow=變得）
*   一般動詞：The company **grew rapidly** after the acquisition.（grow=成長，副詞修飾成長速度）

## 5. 陷阱二：形容詞字尾長得像副詞
部分形容詞字尾也是 -ly，容易被誤判為副詞而排除，例如 timely, costly, orderly, friendly。這些字放在連綴動詞後面依然正確。

*   The delivery **proved timely**, arriving just before the client's deadline.
*   Replacing the entire fleet at once would **prove costly** for the logistics division.

## 6. 陷阱三：連綴動詞 + 形容詞 + 介系詞片語
連綴動詞後面常常先接形容詞，再接一個介系詞片語修飾前面的形容詞，不要被介系詞片語誤導成需要副詞。

*   The revenue forecast **remains uncertain** despite the latest projections.
*   The candidate **seemed nervous** during the final interview round.

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

### 連綴動詞本身
*   **seem** (v. 不及物) 似乎、顯得 | The offer *seemed* fair to everyone at the table.
*   **appear** (v. 不及物) 顯得、看起來 | The figures *appear* accurate at first glance.
*   **look** (v. 不及物，連綴用法) 看起來 | The renovated office *looks* impressive.
*   **sound** (v. 不及物，連綴用法) 聽起來 | The proposal *sounds* feasible.
*   **feel** (v. 不及物，連綴用法) 感覺起來 | The workload *feels* unmanageable this quarter.
*   **taste** (v. 不及物，連綴用法) 嚐起來 | (常見於產品描述情境，非本課重點但同類動詞)
*   **become** (v. 不及物) 變成 | The process *became* simpler after automation.
*   **turn** (v. 不及物，連綴用法) 變得 | The tone of the meeting *turned* hostile.
*   **grow** (v. 不及物，連綴用法) 逐漸變得 | Client demands *grew* more specific over time.
*   **go** (v. 不及物，連綴用法) 變得（多用於負面狀態） | The account *went* dormant after the client left.
*   **remain** (v. 不及物) 保持 | The budget *remained* unchanged despite inflation.
*   **stay** (v. 不及物，連綴用法) 保持 | Team performance *stayed* consistent all year.
*   **keep** (v. 連綴用法) 保持（常搭配現在分詞當補語） | The printer *keeps* jamming during rush hour.
*   **prove** (v. 不及物，連綴用法) 證明是 | The strategy *proved* effective within a month.
*   **turn out** (phr. v.) 結果證明是 | The candidate *turned out* highly qualified.

### 常見補語形容詞
*   **reasonable** (adj.) 合理的 | The refund policy is *reasonable*.
*   **convincing** (adj.) 有說服力的 | Her argument *seemed* convincing.
*   **plausible** (adj.) 看似合理的 | The excuse *sounded* plausible.
*   **stagnant** (adj.) 停滯的 | Sales *remained* stagnant for two quarters.
*   **resilient** (adj.) 有韌性的 | The supply chain *proved* resilient during the shortage.
*   **volatile** (adj.) 不穩定的、易變動的 | The stock price *turned* volatile after the announcement.
*   **lucrative** (adj.) 有利可圖的 | The new territory *appeared* lucrative to investors.
*   **obsolete** (adj.) 過時的 | The equipment *became* obsolete within a few years.
*   **redundant** (adj.) 多餘的、冗員的 | Several positions *became* redundant after the merger.
*   **credible** (adj.) 可信的 | The witness *seemed* credible to the board.
*   **turbulent** (adj.) 動盪的 | The industry *stayed* turbulent throughout the recession.
*   **viable** (adj.) 可行的 | The expansion plan *proved* viable after the pilot test.
*   **counterproductive** (adj.) 適得其反的 | The strict policy *turned out* counterproductive.
*   **imminent** (adj.) 迫近的 | A price hike *seemed* imminent given rising costs.
*   **transparent** (adj.) 透明的 | The bidding process *remained* transparent throughout.

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **形補判斷法**：連綴動詞後面若空格考「形容詞 vs. 副詞」，直接選形容詞，因為補語描述的是主詞，不是動詞本身的動作方式。
2.  **口訣「五感+變化+維持」**：seem/appear/look/sound/feel（五感類）＋ become/turn/grow/go（變化類）＋ remain/stay/keep/prove（維持證明類），背熟這三組就能秒認連綴動詞。
3.  **雙重用法動詞先看語意**：look、sound、grow、smell 等字同時有「一般動詞」用法，此時可接副詞；判斷關鍵在於空格後是否還有受詞或介系詞片語描述「動作方式」。
4.  **-ly 形容詞陷阱**：看到 -ly 結尾的字不要直覺當副詞刪除，timely、costly、orderly、friendly、likely 都是形容詞，能直接接在連綴動詞後面。
5.  **介系詞片語不影響主結構**：連綴動詞 + 形容詞 + 介系詞片語是固定結構，介系詞片語只是修飾語，不會把答案變成需要副詞。
6.  **prove/turn out 的兩種補語**：prove 和 turn out 後面可接形容詞（prove effective）或 to be + 形容詞（prove to be effective），意思相同，考題中兩種都可能出現。
7.  **become 不能省略 to be**：become 後面直接加形容詞或名詞，不需要也不能加 to be，例如 become popular（正確）、become to be popular（錯誤）。
8.  **keep 當連綴動詞常接分詞**：keep + 主詞補語時，常見補語是現在分詞（keep running）或形容詞（keep quiet），要根據語意判斷用哪一種。
9.  **remain/stay 的否定變化**：remain unchanged、stay unaffected 這類「remain/stay + 過去分詞當形容詞」的組合，過去分詞在此當形容詞用，一樣要選形容詞形式而非副詞。
10.  **句首倒裝干擾**：有些較難題型會把連綴動詞放在較長的子句或倒裝結構中，先找出真正的主詞和連綴動詞，再判斷補語詞性，不要被句子長度混淆。
11.  **avoid 選項含副詞字尾的形容詞**：作答時看到選項組是「原形/ly 副詞/形容詞/名詞」四選一，先確認動詞是否為連綴動詞，是的話直接刪掉明顯的副詞選項。
12.  **對比記憶法**：把「一般動詞+副詞」和「連綴動詞+形容詞」的例句成對背誦，例如 grow rapidly（副詞）vs. grow complicated（形容詞），能有效避免混淆。

## 💪 練習題（15 題）

**1.** The client's feedback on the redesigned website ______ overwhelmingly positive.
(A) seemed (B) seeming (C) seemingly (D) to seem

**2.** After the budget cuts, staff morale in the accounting department gradually ______ .
(A) declining (B) declined (C) decline (D) declines

**3.** Even after three rounds of negotiation, the terms of the contract ______ unclear to both parties.
(A) remain (B) remainder (C) remaining (D) remainly

**4.** The revised marketing plan ______ far more persuasive than the original draft.
(A) sound (B) sounds (C) sounding (D) soundly

**5.** Although the prototype passed every test, the manufacturing cost ______ prohibitively high once mass production began.
(A) proving (B) proved (C) proof (D) proves

**6.** The negotiations, which had dragged on for months, finally ______ productive after the new mediator stepped in.
(A) turn (B) turns (C) turned (D) turning

**7.** Despite repeated warnings from the safety inspector, the factory floor ______ hazardous throughout the audit.
(A) stayed (B) stays (C) staying (D) stayly

**8.** The consultant's proposal, though unconventional, ______ remarkably effective once implemented company-wide.
(A) proved out (B) proved (C) proving (D) prove

**9.** After the merger announcement, investor confidence ______ increasingly fragile.
(A) grew (B) grows (C) growing (D) grow

**10.** The updated onboarding process ______ far more efficient than the one used last year, according to new-hire surveys.
(A) appears (B) appearing (C) appearance (D) appearingly

**11.** No matter how the accounting team recalculated the figures, the quarterly report ______ inconsistent with the audited statements.
(A) kept (B) keeping (C) keeps to (D) keep

**12.** Although the vendor's samples looked promising, the finished product ______ disappointing to the procurement team.
(A) turned out (B) turned out to (C) turning out (D) turns out being

**13.** The shipment delay, though initially minor, ______ costly once penalty clauses in the contract were triggered.（第一空）Management later admitted the situation ______ largely avoidable.（第二空）
第一空：(A) proved (B) proving (C) proves (D) prove
第二空：(A) seemed (B) seeming (C) seems (D) to seem

**14.** The office renovation, originally expected to take two weeks, ______ far more disruptive than anyone had anticipated.
(A) become (B) becoming (C) became (D) becomes

**15.** Every candidate who reached the final round ______ genuinely enthusiastic about relocating to the regional office.
(A) seemed (B) seeming (C) seemly (D) to seem
