# 12_疑問詞與間接問句

## 核心概念

### 1. 直接問句 vs. 間接問句的語序差異
直接問句（direct question）需要主詞動詞倒裝，句尾用問號；間接問句（indirect question）則是把疑問詞子句嵌入另一個句子中作受詞或主詞，此時**恢復「疑問詞＋主詞＋動詞」的直述句語序**，不倒裝，句尾通常也不用問號（除非整句本身是問句）。

- 直接問句：Where **is** the manager? （倒裝）
- 間接問句：Could you tell me where **the manager is**? （不倒裝）

多益最常考的錯誤選項就是在間接問句裡誤植倒裝語序，例如寫成 "tell me where is the manager"，這是典型陷阱。

### 2. 疑問詞子句作名詞子句的三種功能
疑問詞（who, what, when, where, why, how，以及 which/whose）引導的子句可以在句中擔任：
- **受詞**：The supervisor asked **what time the shipment would arrive**.
- **主詞**：**Who will replace the retiring director** has not yet been decided.
- **補語**：The main issue is **why the delivery was delayed**.

整個 wh-子句在句中就是一個名詞單位，動詞要用單數（視為單一事實/問題）。

### 3. whether/if 與疑問詞子句的分工
當要問「是不是」而非「什麼/誰/何時」等具體資訊時，用 **whether**（正式，可用於各種位置）或 **if**（口語，通常只能作動詞受詞，不可接在介系詞後、也不建議放句首作主詞）。whether 後面可加 "or not"。

- The manager is not sure **whether** the client will accept the offer.
- Please confirm **whether or not** the meeting has been rescheduled.
- ~~If the client will accept~~ 作主詞是不自然的，應改用 Whether。

### 4. 疑問詞 + 不定詞（wh- + to V）
當主詞或情境明確，且子句要表達「該做什麼／該如何做」的建議、指示時，可簡化為「疑問詞 + to V」，常見於 know, decide, explain, ask, wonder 等動詞之後。

- The new hire wasn't sure **how to submit** the expense report.
- HR will explain **what to do** if a conflict of interest arises.

### 5. 常見疑問詞的語意與用法辨析
- **who** 問人（主格）／**whom** 問人（受格，正式書面較常見，介系詞後必用 whom）
- **whose** 問所有格，後面直接加名詞：whose signature, whose department
- **which** 用於有限選項中做選擇；**what** 用於開放式問題
- **how** 可搭配形容詞/副詞組成程度疑問：how soon, how often, how many, how much
- **why** 引導的間接問句常接在 explain, wonder, ask 之後，表達「原因」

### 6. 間接問句常出現的句型骨架
多益考題中，間接問句常藏在以下句型裡，考生要能辨識子句邊界：
- Please let us know + wh-子句
- I wonder/was wondering + wh-子句
- It is unclear/uncertain + wh-子句（wh-子句作真主詞，it 是虛主詞）
- Can/Could you tell me + wh-子句
- The report did not specify + wh-子句

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

### 疑問詞與間接問句核心字彙
*   **inquire** (v.) 詢問、查詢 | The client called to *inquire* when the replacement parts would be shipped.
*   **specify** (v. 及物) 明確指出、詳述 | The memo did not *specify* who would chair the next meeting.
*   **clarify** (v. 及物) 釐清、澄清 | Please *clarify* whether the discount applies to bulk orders.
*   **wonder** (v.) 想知道、納悶 | The intern was *wondering* how the reimbursement process worked.
*   **determine** (v. 及物) 判定、確定 | The committee has yet to *determine* which candidate best fits the role.
*   **confirm** (v. 及物) 確認 | We need to *confirm* whether the venue is available on that date.
*   **query** (n./v.) 疑問；質詢 | The auditor raised a *query* about who approved the transaction.
*   **uncertain** (adj.) 不確定的 | Management remains *uncertain* how the new tariff will affect pricing.
*   **unclear** (adj.) 不明確的 | It is still *unclear* why the shipment was held at customs.
*   **undecided** (adj.) 尚未決定的 | The board is *undecided* on when the merger should be announced.
*   **rationale** (n.) 理由、根據 | The email explained the *rationale* behind why the office would close early.
*   **whereabouts** (n.) 下落、所在位置 | Security could not confirm the *whereabouts* of the missing package.
*   **discretion** (n.) 判斷、裁量權 | It is left to the manager's *discretion* who attends the client dinner.
*   **outline** (v.) 概述、說明 | The handbook *outlines* what employees should do in an emergency.
*   **elaborate** (v.) 詳細說明 | Could you *elaborate* on how the new policy will be enforced?
*   **assess** (v. 及物) 評估 | The team is trying to *assess* how much the delay will cost the project.
*   **prompt** (v. 及物) 促使、引發 | The complaint *prompted* an investigation into why the refund was delayed.
*   **query the reason** 質疑原因 | Several employees decided to *query the reason* behind the sudden policy change.
*   **look into** 調查、了解 | HR agreed to *look into* who filed the anonymous complaint.
*   **figure out** 弄清楚、想出 | The technician couldn't *figure out* why the system kept crashing.
*   **be at a loss** 不知所措、毫無頭緒 | The assistant was *at a loss* as to whom she should contact first.
*   **make it clear** 講清楚、表明 | The CEO *made it clear* who would be responsible for the transition.
*   **shed light on** 闡明、使清楚 | The internal report *shed light on* why productivity had dropped.
*   **be in the dark about** 對……一無所知 | Most staff were *in the dark about* when the layoffs would take place.
*   **get to the bottom of** 徹底查明 | Auditors are trying to *get to the bottom of* who authorized the payment.
*   **weigh in on** 就……表達意見 | Several directors were asked to *weigh in on* which supplier to choose.
*   **narrow down** 縮小範圍 | The panel needs to *narrow down* which applicants to interview.
*   **the extent to which** ……的程度 | It is unclear *the extent to which* the recall will affect sales.
*   **as to** 關於（引導間接問句常見介系詞） | There was some confusion *as to* how the refund would be processed.

## 📚 補充秒殺公式

1.  **語序判斷法**：看到 tell/ask/know/wonder/explain 後面接 wh-字，先確認是「疑問詞＋主詞＋動詞」順序，凡出現「疑問詞＋助動詞＋主詞」（倒裝）的選項一律刪除。
2.  **whether vs. if 三大場合**：(1) 介系詞後只能用 whether，if 不行；(2) 句首作主詞只能用 whether；(3) 後面接 or not 緊貼在一起時只能用 whether or not；一般動詞受詞位置兩者皆可。
3.  **虛主詞 it + be + 形容詞 + wh-子句**：It is unclear/uncertain/unknown + who/what/when...，wh-子句是真正主詞，動詞永遠用單數。
4.  **wh- + to V 判斷法**：句中若省略主詞，且語意是「該做的動作」而非「已發生的事實」，優先考慮 wh- + to V 這個更精簡的結構。
5.  **whose 後面一定接名詞**：whose signature／whose department／whose responsibility，看到 whose 選項先確認後面是否緊跟名詞。
6.  **which 用於有限選項，what 用於開放式**：題幹若提到「兩個以上特定對象中選一個」用 which；若問「什麼事/什麼東西」沒有限定範圍用 what。
7.  **who 與 whom 的位置判斷**：介系詞或動詞後面若為受詞角色且題目偏正式書面，選 whom；主詞角色一律選 who。
8.  **how + 形容詞/副詞固定搭配**：how soon（多快）、how often（多頻繁）、how long（多久）、how many/much（多少），依語意選對搭配詞而非只看 how 本身。
9.  **間接問句動詞單複數**：整個 wh-子句作主詞時，視為單一概念，動詞一律用第三人稱單數。
10. **雙格題常考組合**：wonder/ask + wh-子句，加上 whether...or not 的固定搭配，是雙格題最常見的組合方式，先鎖定不需疑問詞倒裝的選項。
11. **介系詞 + wh-子句**：as to, regarding 等介系詞後面可以直接接疑問詞子句，此時同樣不倒裝。
12. **陷阱選項辨識**：選項中若疑問詞後面直接接動詞（例如 who is responsible 用在間接問句中卻寫成 who's responsible is unclear 這種雙重倒裝），通常是誘答陷阱，需特別留意主詞是否重複出現。

## 💪 練習題（15 題）

**1.** Could you please tell me ______ the quarterly report is due?
(A) is when (B) when is (C) when (D) when does

**2.** The manager was not sure ______ the client had approved the revised proposal.
(A) if (B) what (C) who (D) whose

**3.** ______ chairs the next budget meeting has not yet been decided by the board.
(A) What (B) Who (C) Whom (D) Which

**4.** The technician couldn't figure out ______ the printer kept jamming.
(A) why (B) whom (C) which (D) whose

**5.** It is still unclear ______ the merger will be completed before the fiscal year ends.（第一空）The board also has not clarified ______ department will oversee the transition.（第二空）
(A) whether / which (B) if / who (C) whether / whose (D) that / who

**6.** New employees often ask ______ they should submit their expense reports.
(A) how to (B) how do (C) how they to (D) how does

**7.** The auditor wants to confirm ______ signature appears on the approved invoice.
(A) who (B) whose (C) whom (D) which is

**8.** Please let us know ______ you would prefer to reschedule the interview.
(A) when would you (B) when you would (C) when do you (D) when did you

**9.** The client is undecided ______ to accept the standard contract or negotiate custom terms.
(A) if (B) that (C) whether (D) what

**10.** The HR director explained ______ candidates would be shortlisted for the final round.
(A) how many (B) how much (C) how long (D) how often

**11.** No one on the team knew ______ was responsible for approving the overtime request.
(A) whom (B) who (C) whose (D) which

**12.** The email did not specify ______ the vendor would deliver the replacement parts.
(A) how soon (B) how (C) what (D) whose

**13.** As to ______ the negotiations broke down, the management remains tight-lipped.
(A) what (B) why (C) whom (D) which

**14.** The assistant wondered ______ documents she needed to bring to the client meeting.（第一空）She also wasn't sure ______ or not the meeting location had changed.（第二空）
(A) what / whether (B) which / if (C) what / that (D) whose / whether

**15.** It remains uncertain ______ candidate the panel will ultimately select for the regional post.
(A) what (B) which (C) whom (D) whose
