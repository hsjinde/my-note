# 01_綜合練習一

## 📝 答題策略 🌟
段落填空題（Part 6）除了單句文法，還要利用「上下文」判斷答案：
1.  **時態一致原則**：先觀察前後句子的時態，空格處的動詞時態通常要與文章敘事時間軸一致。
2.  **代名詞指代**：確認空格代名詞所指的對象是文章中提到的「人」還是「事物」、是單數還是複數。
3.  **連接詞/轉折詞題**：讀懂前後兩句的邏輯關係（因果、對比、遞進、舉例），再選出對應的連接詞或轉折副詞（however, therefore, in addition...）。
4.  **句子插入題**：找出空格前後句子的主題與代名詞線索，插入的句子必須銜接語意、承接前句主詞或呼應下句話題。
5.  **詞彙題**：留意同一段落中重複出現的關鍵字或主題詞，答案通常要與全文情境一致，而非只看單句語意。

## 短文一：公司內部公告

Notice to All Staff

Starting next Monday, the office will ______(1) to a new floor plan designed to improve collaboration between departments. The IT team has already begun relocating equipment and setting up new workstations. ______(2). Employees are encouraged to label their personal belongings before the move to avoid confusion. If you have any questions about your new seating assignment, please contact the facilities office. We appreciate your patience ______(3) this transition period. A detailed floor map ______(4) to everyone by email before the move.

### 題目 1
(A) move
(B) moving
(C) moved
(D) has moved

### 題目 2
(A) The cafeteria will remain open during the renovation.
(B) All desks and chairs must be packed into labeled boxes by Friday.
(C) The company was founded over twenty years ago.
(D) Please remember to bring your own lunch next week.

### 題目 3
(A) despite
(B) during
(C) although
(D) since

### 題目 4
(A) will be sent
(B) sends
(C) sent
(D) was sending

## 短文二：訂單確認信

Dear Mr. Alvarez,

Thank you for your recent order of office supplies from Bright Office Solutions. We are pleased to confirm that your order ______(5) processed and is currently being prepared for shipment. Please note that due to high demand this month, delivery may take three to five business days instead of the usual two. ______(6). If you need the items sooner, please contact our customer service team to discuss expedited shipping options. We ______(7) your continued business and look forward to serving you again. Should you have any questions regarding ______(8) order, feel free to reach out to us anytime.

### 題目 5
(A) has been
(B) is
(C) was
(D) will be

### 題目 6
(A) We regret any inconvenience this delay may cause.
(B) Our store hours have changed for the holiday season.
(C) The new catalog will be released next spring.
(D) Thank you for visiting our showroom last week.

### 題目 7
(A) appreciate
(B) appreciated
(C) will appreciate
(D) have appreciated

### 題目 8
(A) you
(B) your
(C) yours
(D) yourself
