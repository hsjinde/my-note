### 文章一：品牌活動新聞稿

**Riverside Outfitters Launches "Wear It Forward" Campaign**

Riverside Outfitters, a regional outdoor apparel retailer, announced today the launch of its "Wear It Forward" campaign, a month-long initiative encouraging customers to trade in gently used clothing for store credit. Beginning this Friday, shoppers who bring in at least two used garments to any Riverside Outfitters location will receive a $10 store credit per item, redeemable toward any purchase in-store or online.

The donated clothing will be cleaned, repaired if necessary, and distributed to local community shelters through a partnership with the nonprofit organization CityCare Network. According to the company's marketing director, the campaign was designed both to reduce textile waste and to strengthen the brand's connection with environmentally conscious customers.

In addition to the trade-in offer, Riverside Outfitters will run a series of in-store events throughout the campaign, including styling workshops and a raffle for shoppers who participate in the donation program. Customers interested in learning more can visit the company's website or speak with a sales associate at their nearest location.

The campaign will run through the end of next month, after which the company plans to evaluate its impact before deciding whether to make it an annual event.

### 文章二：門市經理內部備忘錄

To: All Assistant Managers
From: District Manager, Sarah Nguyen
Subject: Updated Customer Complaint Procedure

As part of our ongoing effort to improve customer satisfaction, we are introducing a revised procedure for handling in-store complaints, effective immediately.

When a customer raises a concern, the assistant manager on duty should first listen to the full complaint before offering a solution. If the issue involves a product defect, the customer should be offered a full refund or exchange, whichever they prefer. If the complaint involves service quality, the assistant manager should document the details in the new Customer Feedback Log located at the service counter.

All complaints must be logged within 24 hours, regardless of how minor they may seem. This information will be reviewed weekly by the regional office to identify recurring issues and improve staff training. Assistant managers are also asked to follow up with customers who provided contact information, within three business days of the original complaint, to confirm that the issue was resolved to their satisfaction.

Please review this procedure with your team during your next staff meeting. If you have questions about specific cases, feel free to contact the district office directly.

Thank you for your continued commitment to excellent customer service.
