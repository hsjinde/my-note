# 06_雙受詞動詞

## 核心概念

某些動詞可以同時接兩個受詞：**間接受詞（人，IO）**與**直接受詞（物，DO）**，句型為：

**S + V + IO（人）+ DO（物）**

例如：The manager gave the assistant a new laptop.（經理給了助理一台新筆電）

這類動詞稱為「授予動詞」（ditransitive verbs），常見的有 give、offer、send、show、tell、lend、buy、bring、teach、pay、promise、ask、hand、award、assign、grant 等。

## 改寫成介系詞片語

雙受詞句型幾乎都可以改寫成「V + DO + 介系詞 + IO」的形式，介系詞依動詞不同分為 **to 類**與 **for 類**：

- **to 類**（強調「傳遞」的方向性）：give, offer, send, show, tell, lend, teach, hand, pay, promise, bring, mail, deliver, forward, wire, award, grant
  例：The HR department will send new employees a welcome packet. → The HR department will send a welcome packet **to** new employees.

- **for 類**（強調「代為做某事、利益歸屬」）：buy, order, make, find, cost（例外，cost 不能改寫）, get, reserve, save
  例：The supervisor bought the team a round of coffee. → The supervisor bought a round of coffee **for** the team.

⚠️ 特殊動詞 **ask** 接雙受詞時，直接受詞常是問題或請求（ask + IO + a question / a favor），改寫時介系詞用 **of**：The client asked the consultant a difficult question. → The client asked a difficult question **of** the consultant.

⚠️ **cost、charge、owe、envy** 等動詞雖然也接雙受詞，但**無法**改寫成介系詞片語：The renovation cost the company nearly two million dollars.（不能說 cost … to the company）

## 常見誤用陷阱

1. **被動語態選擇**：雙受詞動詞的被動式可以以「人」或「物」當主詞，但意思略有差異。The employees were given a bonus.（人當主詞，較自然）／A bonus was given to the employees.（物當主詞，需加 to）。
2. **不可省略介系詞**：改寫成介系詞片語後，若直接受詞在前，介系詞絕對不能省略，否則句子不合文法。
3. **suggest、explain、describe、announce、introduce、mention** 這類動詞**不能**直接接雙受詞（不可說 explain someone something），必須用「V + 物 + to + 人」：The manager explained the new policy to the staff.（不可說 explained the staff the new policy）。這是多益常考的陷阱動詞群。
4. **代名詞受詞的順序偏好**：當直接受詞是代名詞（it, them）時，母語人士傾向用介系詞片語而非雙受詞句型：Please send it to me.（優於 Please send me it.）

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

*   **offer** (v. 及物) 提供、給予 | The company will *offer* qualified candidates a signing bonus.
*   **buy** (v. 及物) 買、購買給 | The firm decided to *buy* its top performers new laptops.
*   **bring** (v. 及物) 帶來 | The intern was asked to *bring* the manager the signed contract.
*   **teach** (v. 及物) 教導 | The consultant will *teach* the interns effective negotiation skills.
*   **pay** (v. 及物) 支付給 | The accounting department will *pay* the vendor the outstanding balance by Friday.
*   **ask** (v. 及物) 詢問、要求 | The auditor *asked* the finance team several pointed questions.
*   **cost** (v. 及物) 使花費 | The production delay *cost* the company a considerable amount of revenue.
*   **issue** (v. 及物) 發給 | The HR office will *issue* every new hire an identification badge.
*   **mail** (v. 及物) 郵寄給 | Please *mail* the applicant a formal rejection letter within two weeks.
*   **deliver** (v. 及物) 遞送給 | The courier will *deliver* the office a fresh batch of supplies tomorrow.
*   **forward** (v. 及物) 轉寄給 | Could you *forward* me the client's original email?
*   **wire** (v. 及物) 電匯給 | The headquarters agreed to *wire* the branch office the remaining funds.
*   **extend** (v. 及物) 給予、延伸 | The bank chose to *extend* the small business a lower interest rate.
*   **allot** (v. 及物) 分配（時間、名額等） | The organizer will *allot* each speaker only fifteen minutes.
*   **quote** (v. 及物) 報價給 | The supplier *quoted* the buyer an unusually competitive price.
*   **charge** (v. 及物) 向...收費 | The consulting firm *charges* clients an hourly rate for on-site visits.

## 📚 補充秒殺公式

1.  **雙受詞公式**：看到「動詞 + 人 + 物」的句子結構，先確認動詞是否屬於授予動詞家族（give/offer/send/show/tell/lend/buy/teach/pay/promise/award/assign 等），這類動詞後面可以連續接兩個名詞。
2.  **to vs for 判斷法**：若動詞強調「把東西傳送到某人手上」，用 to（give, send, show, tell, lend, hand, pay, mail, deliver, forward, wire, award, grant）；若動詞強調「代替某人做、為某人的利益」，用 for（buy, make, find, order, reserve, save）。
3.  **不可雙受詞動詞黑名單**：explain、describe、suggest、announce、introduce、mention、say、report、recommend、propose 一律用「V + 物 + to/for + 人」，絕不能直接接雙受詞，這是選擇題的高頻陷阱。
4.  **cost/charge/owe 例外公式**：這三個動詞雖然接雙受詞，但**沒有對應的介系詞改寫形式**，看到選項出現 to/for 接在這三個動詞後面的物件時可直接刪去。
5.  **被動語態雙選擇**：授予動詞的被動式，人或物都能當主詞，考題常給「be + p.p. + 人」或「be + p.p. + to + 人」兩種正確形式，判斷時看主詞是人還是物。
6.  **代名詞受詞後移公式**：當直接受詞是 it/them 這類代名詞時，句子傾向改寫成「V + it/them + to/for + 人」，而非「V + 人 + it/them」，選項若出現「gave me it」通常不是最自然的答案。
7.  **ask 的特例公式**：ask 雙受詞改寫用 of 不用 to（ask a favor of somebody），與其他授予動詞不同，考題常混淆。
8.  **搭配轉折線索**：句中若出現「in exchange for」「on behalf of」等介系詞片語，通常暗示句子本身已用改寫句型，不需要再選雙受詞動詞。
9.  **同義動詞辨識**：present、grant、issue、allocate、assign 這組動詞常互相替換作為誘答選項，需依語境判斷「頒發／給予資格」還是「分配資源」的細微差異。
10. **時態與雙受詞並用**：雙受詞句型不影響時態判斷，遇到「by the time」「since」等時間標記時，仍須先確認時態，再確認動詞是否能接雙受詞。
11. **reimburse 固定搭配**：reimburse 接雙受詞時，物通常是費用類名詞（expenses, costs, fees），若後面接動作或事件名詞，則需搭配 for。
12. **guarantee/extend 商業慣用**：這兩個動詞在財務、契約情境中常接雙受詞表示「保證給予／提供優惠條件」，是財務類閱讀測驗常見詞彙。

## 💪 練習題（15 題）

**1.** The regional manager will ______ the top-performing branch a special recognition award at the annual meeting.
(A) offer (B) explain (C) suggest (D) announce

**2.** The vendor quoted a surprisingly low price ______ the procurement team during the negotiation.
(A) of (B) to (C) for (D) with

**3.** Please make sure to ______ the client a copy of the revised contract before the meeting ends.
(A) mention (B) describe (C) send (D) explain

**4.** The finance department will reimburse ______ for all approved business travel expenses.
(A) employees' expenses (B) for employees (C) to employees (D) employees

**5.** The bank has agreed to extend the small manufacturer ______ favorable repayment terms for the new equipment loan.
(A) unusually (B) especially (C) particularly (D) exclusively

**6.** It is company policy that managers must explain any policy changes ______ their team members in writing.
(A) with (B) to (C) for (D) at

**7.** The unexpected equipment failure ended up costing the factory nearly a week of lost production time, ______ the maintenance team could repair the machine.
(A) despite (B) because of (C) before (D) in case of

**8.** The consulting firm ______ its corporate clients a flat monthly retainer instead of an hourly rate.
(A) explains (B) suggests (C) describes (D) charges

**9.** The outgoing supervisor was asked to ______ his replacement all the pending project files before his last day.
(A) hand over (B) explain (C) mention (D) recommend

**10.** After the shortlisting process, the committee will award the winning proposal a grant ______ fifty thousand dollars.
(A) to (B) of (C) for (D) with

**11.** The customer requested that the retailer refund ______ the full amount, since the item arrived damaged.
(A) to her (B) for her (C) her (D) of her

**12.** The HR office plans to issue ______ new photo identification badges before the building renovation is complete.
(A) to all staff (B) all staff's (C) for all staff (D) all staff

**13.** The lead auditor asked several difficult questions ______ the accounting team during the year-end review.
(A) of (B) to (C) for (D) with

**14.** Rather than sending a written notice, the supervisor chose to ______ the new schedule directly to each team member.
(A) mention (B) explain (C) describe (D) allocate

**15.** The board decided to allocate the research division ______ additional funding for next year's projects.
(A) considerately (B) consideration (C) considerable (D) considerably
