### 文章一：自動化倉儲系統上線公告

**Notice: Automated Warehousing System Now Live**

Meridian Logistics is pleased to announce that its new automated warehousing system, WareBot, has been fully activated at our central distribution center as of this week. The system uses robotic sorting arms and real-time inventory scanners to process incoming and outgoing shipments with greater speed and accuracy.

Since going live, WareBot has reduced average order processing time by nearly 40 percent and virtually eliminated manual sorting errors. The system also generates automatic alerts whenever stock levels for any item fall below the reorder threshold, allowing the procurement team to place replenishment orders before shortages occur.

All warehouse staff will receive hands-on training on operating alongside WareBot during the next two weeks. Employees who previously handled manual sorting duties will be reassigned to oversee quality control and system monitoring. No layoffs are planned as part of this transition.

For questions about the new system or the upcoming training schedule, please contact the operations office at ext. 208.

### 文章二：供應商貨運時程調整通知信

Dear Valued Supplier,

We are writing to inform you of an upcoming change to our receiving schedule at the Chiayi Distribution Center. Beginning next month, all incoming deliveries must be scheduled through our new online booking portal at least 48 hours in advance. Deliveries arriving without a confirmed appointment may be turned away or subject to significant delays.

This change is part of our effort to reduce dock congestion and improve turnaround times for both inbound and outbound shipments. Once you submit a delivery request through the portal, you will receive a confirmation email with an assigned loading dock number and estimated unloading time.

Please note that the receiving dock will no longer accept deliveries between 12:00 p.m. and 1:00 p.m. due to staff shift changes. Suppliers who repeatedly fail to comply with the new scheduling requirements may be subject to a review of their vendor agreement.

If you have any questions about the new booking system or need assistance registering for portal access, please contact our vendor relations team at vendors@meridianlogistics.com.

Thank you for your cooperation as we work to streamline our receiving operations.

Sincerely,
Vendor Relations Team
Meridian Logistics
