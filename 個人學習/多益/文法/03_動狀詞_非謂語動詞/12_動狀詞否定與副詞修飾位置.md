# 12_動狀詞否定與副詞修飾位置

## 核心概念

### 一、動狀詞的否定形式

不定詞、動名詞、分詞若要否定，一律將 **not（或 never）直接放在動狀詞之前**，不可像限定動詞那樣用 don't / doesn't / didn't。

- 不定詞否定：**not to V**
  例：The supervisor asked employees **not to** discuss salary details in public.
- 動名詞否定：**not V-ing**
  例：The manager appreciated the intern's **not** interrupting the client during the call.
- 分詞（分詞構句）否定：**not V-ing / not p.p.**
  例：**Not** having received a reply, the vendor sent a follow-up email.

常見錯誤是把 not 放在 to 之後（to not V）或放在句首當作句子的否定詞，這在正式商用英文書面測驗中通常被視為次佳選項，秒殺原則是「not 緊貼在動狀詞正前方」。

### 二、副詞修飾不定詞的位置

副詞修飾不定詞時，理論上可以放在 to 與動詞之間（即所謂 split infinitive，分裂不定詞），但在正式商業文件與 TOEIC 測驗中，更常見、更保守的位置是放在整個不定詞片語之前或之後：

- The committee decided **to carefully review** every proposal.（分裂不定詞，口語可接受）
- The committee decided **to review every proposal carefully**.（副詞置於片語尾端，最保守安全）
- **To effectively manage** a remote team, a supervisor must communicate clearly.

TOEIC 選項中若同時出現「副詞置於 to 與 V 之間」和「副詞置於動詞片語之後」兩個看似合理的選項，通常以「不拆散慣用搭配動詞片語」為準，例如 to thoroughly review the contract 優於 to review thoroughly the contract（因為 review the contract 是固定搭配，不宜被副詞硬生生插入）。

### 三、副詞修飾動名詞與分詞的位置

- 修飾動名詞：副詞通常放在動名詞之前，或放在動名詞片語之後（不可放在動名詞與其受詞之間）。
  例：**Carefully reviewing** the budget helped the team avoid overspending.
  例：Reviewing the budget **carefully** helped the team avoid overspending.
  誤：Reviewing carefully the budget...（副詞硬生生插在動詞與受詞之間，破壞搭配）

- 修飾分詞（形容詞用法或分詞構句）：副詞置於分詞之前。
  例：The **newly hired** analyst impressed the finance director.
  例：**Having thoroughly reviewed** the contract, the lawyer approved the deal.

### 四、否定副詞與動狀詞的組合

當句子同時需要否定與副詞修飾時，順序通常是：否定詞（not）+ 副詞（可選）+ 動狀詞。

例：The consultant recommended **not immediately implementing** the new policy without further testing.

### 五、常見混淆點整理

1. not to V vs. to not V：優先選 not to V。
2. never 也可用於否定不定詞或分詞構句，語氣更強：**Never having missed** a deadline, the team earned the client's trust.
3. 分詞構句的否定詞永遠放在分詞前，即使邏輯主詞不同也不例外。
4. 副詞不可插在及物動詞與其受詞之間（無論該動詞是限定動詞還是動狀詞），這是本章與詞序相關的核心考點。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

*   **negate** (v. 及物) 使無效、否定 | The new evidence did not *negate* the original agreement between the two firms.
*   **negation** 名詞 否定 | The clause's *negation* clearly excludes part-time staff from the bonus program.
*   **refrain** (v. 不及物，後接 from) 克制、避免 | Employees are asked to *refrain* from using personal devices during client meetings.
*   **abstain** (v. 不及物，後接 from) 戒絕、棄權 | Two board members chose to *abstain* from voting on the merger proposal.
*   **withhold** (v. 及物) 保留、不給予 | The finance department decided to *withhold* the bonus until the audit was complete.
*   **decline** (v. 及物/不及物) 婉拒 | The vendor politely *declined* to lower the price further.
*   **omit** (v. 及物) 省略、遺漏 | Please do not *omit* any required field on the reimbursement form.
*   **exclude** (v. 及物) 排除 | The policy explicitly *excludes* interns from the profit-sharing plan.
*   **prohibit** (v. 及物) 禁止 | Company rules *prohibit* staff from disclosing client information externally.
*   **restrain** (v. 及物) 抑制、約束 | The manager had to *restrain* himself from interrupting the presentation.
*   **discourage** (v. 及物) 勸阻、使不願 | The steep fee is meant to *discourage* customers from canceling last minute.
*   **hesitant** 形容詞 猶豫的 | The board remained *hesitant* about not renewing the current supplier's contract.
*   **reluctantly** 副詞 不情願地 | The director *reluctantly* agreed not to expand the department this year.
*   **thoroughly** 副詞 徹底地 | The auditor *thoroughly* examined every invoice before signing off.
*   **promptly** 副詞 立即地 | Staff are expected to respond to client emails *promptly* each morning.
*   **diligently** 副詞 勤勉地 | The new analyst worked *diligently* to finish the forecast ahead of schedule.
*   **cautiously** 副詞 謹慎地 | The CFO proceeded *cautiously* before approving the overseas investment.
*   **explicitly** 副詞 明確地 | The contract *explicitly* states that late deliveries will incur a penalty.
*   **deliberately** 副詞 刻意地 | The negotiator *deliberately* paused before presenting the counteroffer.
*   **inadvertently** 副詞 無意間地 | The clerk *inadvertently* omitted a required signature on the form.
*   **repeatedly** 副詞 反覆地 | The supplier *repeatedly* failed to meet the agreed delivery schedule.
*   **rigorously** 副詞 嚴格地 | All applicants are *rigorously* screened before receiving a job offer.
*   **consciously** 副詞 有意識地 | Managers were asked to *consciously* avoid favoring any single vendor.
*   **fully** 副詞 完全地 | The team has not *fully* addressed the client's concerns yet.
*   **barely** 副詞 幾乎不 | The department *barely* managed to meet the quarterly quota.
*   **strictly** 副詞 嚴格地 | Visitors are *strictly* prohibited from entering the server room unaccompanied.
*   **unintentionally** 副詞 非故意地 | The assistant *unintentionally* sent the confidential memo to the wrong list.
*   **outright** 副詞 直接地、完全地 | The board *outright* rejected the proposal to relocate headquarters.
*   **flatly** 副詞 斷然地 | The supplier *flatly* refused not to raise prices next quarter.

## 📚 補充秒殺公式

1.  **not to V 是唯一安全解**：看到不定詞否定，優先選 not to V，不選 to not V 或 don't to V。
2.  **not V-ing 否定動名詞**：not 直接貼在動名詞前，不放在句子開頭當獨立否定詞。
3.  **分詞構句否定放最前**：Not / Never + V-ing / p.p.，整個分詞構句的否定詞永遠在最前端。
4.  **副詞不可拆散動詞＋受詞**：review the contract thoroughly 或 thoroughly review the contract 皆可，但 review thoroughly the contract 錯誤。
5.  **分裂不定詞可接受但非首選**：to carefully review 文法上合法，但若選項有「副詞放在片語尾端」通常是更保守、更常見的商用寫法。
6.  **副詞修飾分詞放分詞前**：newly hired、recently promoted、carefully drafted 這類「副詞+分詞」組合幾乎都是副詞在前。
7.  **never 比 not 語氣更強**：強調「從未」時選 never，且一樣緊貼在動狀詞前。
8.  **否定+副詞連用時，副詞夾在否定詞與動狀詞之間**：not immediately V-ing、not fully understanding 皆為正確語序。
9.  **refrain from / abstain from 後面一律接動名詞**：這是否定語意動詞片語的固定搭配，不接 to V。
10. **discourage/prohibit + 受詞 + from + V-ing**：這組搭配的否定意味已內建在動詞語意中，不需另加 not。
11. **flatly refuse / decline 是加強語氣的副詞＋動詞搭配**：副詞放在該動詞之前，不放在 to V 之間。
12. **看到選項出現「副詞插在及物動詞與受詞中間」，直接刪除**：這是本章最常見的陷阱誘答選項設計。

## 💪 練習題（15 題）

**1.** The HR director asked all supervisors ______ salary figures with anyone outside the finance team.
(A) to not discuss (B) not to discuss (C) not discuss (D) discussing not

**2.** The auditor's report criticized the branch for ______ approving expense claims without receipts.
(A) routinely not (B) to not routinely (C) not routinely (D) not to routinely

**3.** ______ received a response after three follow-up emails, the vendor finally called the procurement office directly.
(A) Having not (B) Not to have (C) To not having (D) Not having

**4.** The consultant advised the firm ______ implement the new pricing model until the pilot test concluded.
(A) not to immediately (B) to not immediately (C) immediately not to (D) to immediately not

**5.** Employees are strongly encouraged ______ personal errands during scheduled work hours.
(A) to not run (B) not to run (C) not running (D) running not

**6.** The technician was praised for ______ the malfunctioning server before it caused a full outage.
(A) identifying quickly the (B) quickly to identify (C) quickly identifying (D) identify quickly

**7.** ______ any objections from the board, the CFO proceeded with the acquisition.
(A) Not having received (B) Having not received (C) Not received having (D) Received not having

**8.** The new intern was told ______ interrupt senior staff during client calls.
(A) to never (B) never not to (C) not never to (D) never to

**9.** To ______ handle the surge in customer inquiries, the call center hired ten additional agents.
(A) effective (B) effectively (C) effectiveness (D) effectively be

**10.** The supplier's decision ______ raise prices this quarter surprised most of the retailers.
(A) to not (B) not for to (C) not to (D) for not to

**11.** The manager reviewed the contract ______ before forwarding it to legal counsel for final approval.
(A) thorough (B) thoroughness (C) thoroughly be (D) thoroughly

**12.** The committee agreed ______ discuss the layoffs publicly until an official statement was prepared.
(A) not to (B) to not (C) not for (D) for not

**13.** The regional director's memo, ______ drafted late last night, contained several factual errors.
(A) hasty (B) hastily (C) haste (D) hastening

**14.** 第一空：The auditor recommended ______ finalizing the report until all receipts were verified. 第二空：By ______ rushing the process, the team avoided several costly errors.
(A) not / to not (B) to not / not (C) not / not (D) never to / to not

**15.** The vendor flatly refused ______ the delivery schedule despite repeated requests from the client.
(A) adjusting (B) adjust (C) to adjusting (D) to adjust
