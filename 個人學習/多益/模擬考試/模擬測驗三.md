### 文章一：內部職缺公告

**Internal Job Posting: Employee Relations Specialist**

Meridian Business Group is currently seeking an Employee Relations Specialist to join our growing Human Resources team at the corporate office in Denver. The successful candidate will work closely with department managers to resolve workplace concerns and support a positive work environment.

**Responsibilities:**
- Investigate and mediate employee complaints and workplace disputes
- Advise managers on company policy and disciplinary procedures
- Assist in updating the employee handbook on an annual basis
- Coordinate exit interviews for departing staff

**Requirements:**
- A minimum of three years of experience in human resources or a related field
- Strong conflict-resolution and interpersonal skills
- Familiarity with employment law is preferred but not required
- Bachelor's degree in human resources, business administration, or a related discipline

This is an internal-only posting open to current full-time employees who have completed at least one year with the company. Interested employees should submit an updated resume along with a brief statement of interest to hr-internal@meridianbg.com by August 15. Selected candidates will be invited to a first-round interview with the HR director the following week.

### 文章二：員工福利公告信

Dear Team Members,

This is a reminder that the annual open enrollment period for employee benefits will run from October 1 to October 15. During this time, all employees have the opportunity to review and make changes to their health insurance, dental coverage, and retirement plan contributions for the upcoming year.

Employees who do not wish to make any changes to their current elections are not required to take any action; existing selections will automatically carry over into the new plan year. However, employees who wish to add a dependent, switch insurance providers, or adjust their retirement contribution percentage must submit an updated enrollment form through the HR portal before the deadline.

A series of informational sessions will be held in the main conference room on October 3 and October 8 to answer questions about plan options and any changes to premium rates for the coming year. Attendance at these sessions is optional but strongly recommended for employees considering changes to their coverage.

Please note that enrollment forms submitted after October 15 will not be processed, and employees who miss the deadline will automatically be enrolled in their current plan for another year. If you have questions about your benefits options, please contact the benefits team at extension 310.

Thank you for your attention to this important matter.

Sincerely,
Benefits Administration Team
