# 08_數量詞與數詞用法

## 核心概念
本章延伸 07 章「冠詞與數量詞」尚未深入處理的兩塊：一是可數/不可數名詞的**進階**數量詞辨析（less vs. fewer、精確數字 vs. 不定大量表達），二是**數詞 (numerals)** 本身在商業英文中的用法——基數詞、序數詞、分數、百分比、倍數。這些是新制多益 Part 5 詞性/搭配題的常見考點，也常出現在 Part 6 的財報、公告、調查報告類短文中。

## 1. less vs. fewer：可數/不可數的比較級陷阱 🌟

| 比較級 | 搭配對象 | 範例 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **fewer** | 可數名詞（複數）的比較級 | **fewer** *errors*, **fewer** *complaints* |
| **less** | 不可數名詞的比較級 | **less** *time*, **less** *paperwork* |

*   多益考試依循書面英文的嚴格規則：可數名詞一律用 `fewer`，即使日常口語中母語人士常誤用 `less`。
*   判斷方法：先看空格後面的名詞是否能加 -s 變複數，能加 → `fewer`；不能加 → `less`。

## 2. 精確數字 vs. 不定大量表達 🌟

| 用法 | 結構 | 範例 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **精確數字** | 數字 + 名詞（hundred/thousand/million 恆為單數、不加 -s） | **two hundred** *units*, **three million** *dollars* |
| **不定大量** | hundreds/thousands/millions **of** + 複數名詞 | **hundreds of** *applicants*, **millions of** *dollars* |

*   易混淆點：`two hundreds` 或 `two hundred of` 皆為錯誤寫法；精確數字後面**直接**接名詞，不可加 `-s` 也不可加 `of`。
*   反之，表達「數不清、約略很多」時才用 `hundreds/thousands/millions of`，此時 `hundred/thousand/million` 必須加 `-s` 且後面接 `of`。

## 3. 基數詞、序數詞與複合形容詞的商業用法 🌟

| 詞性/結構 | 用法 | 範例 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **序數詞 (ordinal)** | 置於冠詞與名詞之間，表「第幾個」 | the **third** *quarter*, the **second** *attempt* |
| **This is the + 序數詞 + time** | 表達「這是第幾次發生」 | This is the **third** *time* the shipment has been delayed. |
| **數字 + 名詞 → 複合形容詞** | 連字號連接，名詞恆用**單數** | a **five-year** *contract*, a **10-page** *report* |

*   複合形容詞最常見的錯誤是把名詞寫成複數（five-years、10-pages），這在多益選項中是典型的誘答陷阱。

## 4. 分數與百分比的主詞動詞一致 🌟

| 結構 | 動詞單複數判斷依據 | 範例 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **分數/百分比 + of + 名詞** 作主詞 | 動詞依 `of` 後面的名詞決定 | **Two-thirds of** the *employees* **have** completed the survey. / **One-third of** the *report* **is** finished. |
| **percent** | 前面接數字時恆為單數形式，不加 -s | The rate rose by 15 **percent**（不可寫成 15 percents）。 |
| **One/Each out of + 複數名詞** 作主詞 | 動詞依 `One/Each` 決定，恆為單數 | **One** out of five customers **was** dissatisfied. |

## 5. 倍數表達 (multiplicative expressions) 🌟

| 結構 | 用法 | 範例 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **倍數 + as + 形容詞/副詞 + as** | 比較兩者的倍數關係 | The new venue attracted **twice as many** *visitors* **as** last year. |
| **double / triple / quadruple 作動詞** | 直接表示「變成幾倍」 | Revenue **doubled** within two years. |
| **twofold / threefold 作形容詞或副詞** | 抽象倍數，不直接接名詞複數 | The benefit is **twofold**: lower cost and higher efficiency. |

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **less vs. fewer**：`fewer` 修飾可數複數名詞（fewer errors），`less` 修飾不可數名詞（less time）；多益依書面文法嚴格規則作答。
2.  **精確數字 + 名詞 vs. 不定大量表達**：數字明確時 `hundred/thousand/million` 維持單數、不加 -s（two hundred dollars）；表示「不確定的大量」時要用複數並加 of（hundreds of applicants, thousands of dollars, millions of users）。
3.  **序數詞的位置與句型**：置於冠詞與名詞之間，如 the third quarter；`This is the + 序數詞 + time` 是固定句型，表示「這是第幾次」。
4.  **基數詞 + 名詞構成複合形容詞**：數字與名詞之間加連字號，且名詞恆用單數，如 a five-year contract、a 10-page report，不可寫成 five-years 或 10-pages。
5.  **分數 (fraction) 作主詞時的動詞一致**：`分數 + of + 名詞` 當主詞，動詞單複數依 of 後面的名詞決定，如 two-thirds of the staff are..., one-third of the report is...。
6.  **percent 為不可數且形式固定，前接數字時恆為單數不加 -s**：15 percent（不可寫成 15 percents）；`percentage point` 則專指「百分點」的差異，與 percent 不可混用。
7.  **倍數表達 twice/three times + as + 形容詞/副詞 + as**：twice as many applicants as last year、three times as expensive as the previous model。
8.  **倍數動詞用法：double / triple / quadruple 可直接作及物或不及物動詞使用**：Sales doubled within a year. / The merger will triple the company's market share.
9.  **One/Each + out of + 複數名詞 作主詞時，動詞依 One/Each 決定為單數**：One out of five customers was dissatisfied with the service.
10. **amount to（片語動詞）表「總計達到」**：動詞單複數依主詞（通常是總額本身，為單數）決定，如 The total cost amounts to nearly two million dollars.
11. **twofold / threefold 作形容詞或副詞，表達抽象的「雙重／三倍」概念，不直接接名詞複數**：The advantage is twofold: cost savings and improved efficiency.
12. **quota / ratio / proportion 等統計類名詞常搭配介系詞 of 或 to 表示比例關係**：the ratio of managers to staff members、a daily quota of ten units。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語
### 數量與大小相關
*   **abundant** 形容詞 豐富的；充裕的 | The company enjoys an *abundant* supply of raw materials from its regional suppliers.
*   **ample** 形容詞 充足的 | The new warehouse provides *ample* storage space for the upcoming holiday inventory.
*   **an array of** 片語 一系列的 | The vendor showcased *an array of* products at the trade show booth.
*   **a fraction of** 片語 一小部分的 | Only *a fraction of* the budget has been spent so far this quarter.
*   **the bulk of** 片語 大部分的 | *The bulk of* the shipment will arrive by sea freight next week.
*   **countless** 形容詞 數不清的 | The project manager fielded *countless* questions from anxious stakeholders.
*   **dozens of** 片語 數十個的 | *Dozens of* applicants responded to the job posting within a single day.
*   **minority** 名詞 少數 | Only a *minority* of shareholders opposed the proposed acquisition.
*   **scarce** 形容詞 稀少的 | Skilled technicians have become increasingly *scarce* in the local labor market.
*   **sizable** 形容詞 相當大的 | The startup secured a *sizable* investment from a venture capital firm.

### 數詞、統計與百分比相關
*   **digit** 名詞 數字；位數 | Please enter the six-*digit* confirmation code sent to your email.
*   **double** 動詞/形容詞 使加倍；兩倍的 | The firm's profits *doubled* after the merger was finalized.
*   **figure** 名詞 數字；金額 | The sales *figure* for October exceeded all previous quarterly records.
*   **fraction** 名詞 分數；一小部分 | Only a small *fraction* of employees opted out of the new insurance plan.
*   **gross** 形容詞 總計的（未扣除前） | The company's *gross* revenue reached ten million dollars last fiscal year.
*   **innumerable** 形容詞 無數的 | The audit uncovered *innumerable* discrepancies in the expense reports.
*   **multiple** 形容詞 多個的 | The candidate had received *multiple* job offers before accepting this position.
*   **net** 形容詞 淨值的 | After taxes and expenses, the *net* profit was lower than projected.
*   **numeral** 名詞 數字符號 | The invoice number must be written using Arabic *numerals* only.
*   **overall** 形容詞/副詞 整體的；整體而言 | The *overall* customer satisfaction rating improved this quarter.
*   **per capita** 片語 每人平均的 | The report compared *per capita* spending across different regional offices.
*   **percentile** 名詞 百分位數 | Applicants scoring above the ninetieth *percentile* were invited for an interview.
*   **proportion** 名詞 比例 | The *proportion* of remote workers has grown steadily since last year.
*   **quantity** 名詞 數量 | The supplier offers discounts for orders placed in large *quantity*.
*   **quota** 名詞 配額；定額 | Each sales representative must meet a monthly *quota* to qualify for the bonus.
*   **ratio** 名詞 比率 | The manager tracks the *ratio* of complaints to total orders each week.
*   **statistic** 名詞 統計數據 | The latest *statistic* shows a decline in average delivery times.
*   **tally** 名詞/動詞 計數；合計 | The clerk had to *tally* the inventory items by hand after the system crashed.
*   **threefold** 形容詞/副詞 三倍的 | Demand for the product increased *threefold* after the advertising campaign launched.
*   **twofold** 形容詞/副詞 兩倍的；雙重的 | The benefits of the new system are *twofold*: faster processing and fewer errors.

## 💪 練習題（15 題）

**1.** Despite ___ market research, the marketing team still struggled to predict consumer demand accurately.
(A) several (B) much (C) many (D) a number of

**2.** A large ___ of paperwork must be processed before the merger can be finalized.
(A) number (B) several (C) amount (D) many

**3.** Since automating the invoicing process, the accounting team has handled ___ errors than before.
(A) less (B) least (C) few (D) fewer

**4.** Two-thirds of the employees ___ already completed the mandatory compliance training.
(A) have (B) has (C) is (D) was

**5.** Sales rose by approximately 15 ___ compared to the same period last year.
(A) percentile (B) percent (C) percentages (D) percentage

**6.** This is the ___ time the supplier has missed the delivery deadline this year.
(A) three (B) threefold (C) third (D) thirdly

**7.** The new office building offers ___ parking space for employees who commute by car.
(A) numerous (B) many (C) a few (D) plenty of

**8.** The warehouse recorded ___ minor defects last month, though the exact figure was not disclosed in the report.
(A) hundreds of (B) hundred (C) two hundreds (D) hundreds

**9.** This year's trade show attracted almost twice ___ visitors as last year's event.
(A) as much (B) as many (C) so many (D) many as

**10.** The corporation has invested ___ dollars in renewable energy projects over the past five years, and the figure continues to climb each quarter.
(A) million (B) two millions (C) millions of (D) millions

**11.** The company's revenue has nearly ___ since it expanded into the Asian market three years ago.
(A) double (B) doubling (C) doubles (D) doubled

**12.** One out of every five customers surveyed ___ dissatisfied with the delivery time.
(A) was (B) were (C) are (D) have been

**13.** The two companies signed a ___ contract to jointly develop the next generation of packaging materials.
(A) five-years (B) five-year (C) five year (D) fifth-year

**14.** According to the latest estimate, the total cost of the renovation ___ to nearly two million dollars.
(A) amount (B) amounting (C) amounts (D) have amounted

**15.** The annual survey found that ___ percent of employees reported feeling satisfied with the new benefits package, a considerable increase from last year.（第一空）___ of the respondents, however, said they would like more flexible working hours.（第二空）
第一空：(A) eighty (B) eightieth (C) eighty's (D) the eighty
第二空：(A) Much (B) Little (C) Amount (D) A number
