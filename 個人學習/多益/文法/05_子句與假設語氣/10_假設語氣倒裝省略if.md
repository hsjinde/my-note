# 10_假設語氣倒裝省略if

## 核心概念
在正式書面英文（尤其是商業書信、合約、公告）中，假設語氣的 if 子句經常「省略 if，並將助動詞移到主詞前面」，形成倒裝句。這種寫法比一般 if 子句更正式、更精簡，是多益閱讀與填空的高頻考點。三種倒裝各自對應一種假設語氣的時態：

| 原句 (含 if) | 倒裝句 (省略 if) | 對應時態 |
|---|---|---|
| If S + were... | **Were** + S... | 與現在事實相反 |
| If S + had + p.p.... | **Had** + S + p.p.... | 與過去事實相反 |
| If S + should + V... | **Should** + S + V... | 未來極小可能 / 萬一 |

## 1. Were 倒裝（與現在事實相反）
公式：`Were + S + (to V) / 補語, S + would/could/might + V`
*   **例句**：**Were** the budget approved today, the project **would launch** next quarter. (若預算今天通過，專案下季就會啟動——但目前尚未通過)
*   **例句**：**Were** I in charge of procurement, I **would renegotiate** the contract.

## 2. Had 倒裝（與過去事實相反）
公式：`Had + S + p.p., S + would/could/might + have + p.p.`
*   **例句**：**Had** the vendor **shipped** the parts on time, production **would not have stopped**. (若供應商當時準時出貨，生產線當時就不會停擺——但當時沒準時)
*   **例句**：**Had** management **been informed** earlier, the recall **could have been avoided**.

## 3. Should 倒裝（未來極小可能 / 萬一）
公式：`Should + S + V, 祈使句 / S + 助動詞 + V`
*   **例句**：**Should** the shipment **be delayed**, please notify the client immediately.
*   **例句**：**Should** the auditor **request** additional records, the finance team will provide them within 24 hours.

## 4. 三種倒裝的辨識訣竅
判斷該用哪一個倒裝助動詞，關鍵在於**看主要子句的動詞時態**，而不是先入為主套公式：
*   主要子句是 `would/could + have + p.p.` → 過去事實相反 → 用 **Had**
*   主要子句是 `would/could + V` (原形，非完成式) → 現在事實相反 → 用 **Were**
*   主要子句是**祈使句**或一般助動詞（will/can/should + V），且語意帶有「萬一」→ 用 **Should**

## 5. 倒裝句不能逆推成一般子句的陷阱
倒裝句中的 Had / Were / Should 是「移位後的助動詞」，句子裡**不能再出現 if**。以下為錯誤示範：
*   ❌ Had if the report been submitted... （if 與倒裝不能並存）
*   ✅ **Had** the report been submitted on time, the board would have reviewed it sooner.

同時要與「真實過去完成式問句」或「條件副詞子句連接詞」區分開：Had 開頭若後面接的是完整直述句（無倒裝語序），則多半只是單純的過去完成式敘述，而非假設語氣倒裝，需靠上下文（是否與事實相反）判斷。

## 6. 與 Without / But for 的比較
Without / But for + N 也能表達「要不是因為...」，效果類似 Had 倒裝，但結構是介系詞片語而非子句：
*   **Without** the emergency loan, the company **would have gone** bankrupt.
*   等同於：**Had** it not **been** for the emergency loan, the company would have gone bankrupt.

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

### 商業情境動詞
*   **finalise** (v. 及物, 英式拼法) 敲定 | **Had** the agenda **been finalised**, the meeting would have started on time.

### 商業情境名詞
*   **liability** (n.) 責任、負債 | **Had** the clause **been omitted**, the firm would bear no **liability**.
*   **shortfall** (n.) 短缺、不足額 | **Were** there a **shortfall** in funding, the launch would be postponed.
*   **surplus** (n.) 盈餘 | **Had** the quarter ended with a **surplus**, bonuses would have been higher.

### 假設/邏輯關聯詞與片語
*   **in that case** (片語) 如果那樣的話 | **Had** funding fallen through, **in that case** the project would have been shelved.
*   **conceivably** (adv.) 可想而知地、有可能地 | **Were** demand to spike, the factory could **conceivably** run three shifts.
*   **under such circumstances** (片語) 在這種情況下 | **Should** the merger fail, **under such circumstances** both firms would seek new partners.
*   **retroactively** (adv.) 追溯地 | **Had** the policy **taken effect** retroactively, all claims would have been reprocessed.
*   **preemptively** (adv.) 先發制人地、預先地 | **Were** risks identified early, management could act **preemptively**.
*   **at one's earliest convenience** (片語) 盡快 | **Should** you have concerns, please respond **at your earliest convenience**.

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **看主要子句判斷倒裝助動詞**：主要子句出現 `have + p.p.` → 選 **Had**；主要子句是原形動詞（無 have）→ 選 **Were**；語意「萬一」且常接祈使句 → 選 **Should**。
2.  **倒裝句不可與 if 並存**：選項若同時出現 if 又要求倒裝語序，一定是錯誤陷阱。
3.  **Were 倒裝也可接 to V**：`Were + S + to V` 表示「假使發生某事」，語氣更委婉、更假設性，常見於未來極小可能的正式書信。
    *   **Were** the board **to reject** the proposal, management would revise the budget.
4.  **Should 倒裝的祈使句慣用結尾**：後半句常以 please + V 或 be sure to + V 收尾，是辨識句型的視覺線索。
5.  **Had it not been for = Without / But for**：三者可互換，皆表示「要不是因為」，但 Had it not been for 是完整子句（可接完整句子），Without/But for 只接名詞片語。
6.  **主詞與助動詞的一致**：倒裝後助動詞仍須與主詞的人稱/單複數一致（Were 用於所有人稱，Had/Should 不隨人稱變化）。
7.  **倒裝句中的否定**：若要表達「若非...」的雙重否定強調語氣，用 `Had + S + not + p.p.`，not 放在主詞之後、動詞之前。
    *   **Had** the team **not intervened**, the incident would have escalated.
8.  **與名詞子句連接詞 whether 的區別**：`Should` 開頭的倒裝句不能替換成 `whether`，兩者結構功能完全不同，考題常混淆。
9.  **與真實條件句（unless/if）的語意差異**：倒裝句仍保有「與事實相反」或「發生機率低」的語氣，不同於 unless 引導的真實條件句。
10. **合約與公告文體偏好倒裝**：TOEIC 閱讀測驗的合約條款、人資公告、財務備忘錄常用 Should/Had 倒裝取代 if，是辨識文體的線索。
11. **Were 倒裝後接形容詞或分詞補語**：`Were + S + adj./p.p.` 也是常見變體，不一定接完整動詞片語。
    *   **Were** the results **inconclusive**, the study would be extended.

## 💪 練習題（15 題）

**1.** ______ the merger approved by regulators, the two firms would combine their supply chains immediately.
(A) Were (B) Had (C) Should (D) If

**2.** ______ the vendor shipped the components earlier, the assembly line would not have stopped last week.
(A) Should (B) Had (C) Were (D) Unless

**3.** ______ any discrepancy arise during the audit, please contact the finance office without delay.
(A) Were (B) Had (C) Should (D) Being

**4.** ______ the CFO been informed of the shortfall sooner, the board could have adjusted the budget in time.
(A) Should (B) Were (C) Does (D) Had

**5.** ______ I in the procurement manager's position, I would renegotiate the supplier contract before it expires.
(A) Were (B) Had (C) Should (D) Was

**6.** ______ the board to reject this year's proposal, management would have to revise the entire budget plan.
(A) Had (B) Were (C) Should (D) Does

**7.** ______ it not been for the emergency loan approved last quarter, the company would have gone bankrupt.
(A) Should (B) Were (C) Had (D) Was

**8.** ______ the invoice been incomplete, the payment department would have withheld the reimbursement.
(A) Should (B) Were (C) If (D) Had

**9.** 第一空：______ the client request rush delivery, our logistics team will expedite the shipment within 24 hours; 第二空：______ that occur, an additional fee will apply.
第一空：(A) Should (B) Were (C) Had (D) Does
第二空：(A) Were (B) Should (C) Had (D) Does

**10.** ______ the error not been rectified before the report was published, the firm would have faced regulatory penalties.
(A) Should (B) Were (C) Had (D) Does

**11.** ______ the audit reveal any irregularities, the CFO must notify the board within 48 hours.
(A) Were (B) Had (C) Being (D) Should

**12.** ______ the results of the pilot program inconclusive, the research team would extend the trial by another quarter.
(A) Were (B) Should (C) Had (D) Does

**13.** ______ management anticipated the surge in demand, the factory would have stocked sufficient raw materials in advance.
(A) Should (B) Had (C) Was (D) Were

**14.** 短文填空：Under the revised contract, (1)______ either party breach the confidentiality clause, the agreement will become null and void immediately. (2)______ the breach been discovered earlier, the affected party could have sought damages sooner.
第一空：(A) Were (B) Had (C) Should (D) If
第二空：(A) Should (B) Were (C) Does (D) Had

**15.** ______ the office to relocate before the lease expires, employees would receive a relocation allowance under company policy.
(A) Were (B) Should (C) Had (D) Does
