# 11_同位語與插入語結構

## 核心概念
同位語 (Appositive) 是緊接在一個名詞後面、用來「重新命名」或「補充說明」該名詞的另一個名詞或名詞片語；插入語 (Parenthetical) 則是用一對逗號、一對破折號或括號插入句子中間的補充資訊，把它整段拿掉，句子文法仍然完整。這兩種結構在多益 Part 5、6 都常考，考點集中在「空格該填名詞還是子句」「插入的轉承語怎麼選」以及「分詞構句作插入語時動詞形式怎麼選」。

## 1. 同位語 (Appositive) 的定義與基本用法 🌟
同位語**本身是名詞或名詞片語**，不含主詞+動詞的完整子句，直接放在被說明的名詞後面，兩者指的是同一個人事物。
*   The manager, **a former accountant**, reviewed the budget himself. (a former accountant = the manager)
*   多益考點：空格前後若已經是「逗號 + 名詞 + 逗號」的結構，且前面已經有主詞和動詞，空格通常只需要填**名詞**，不能填動詞或形容詞子句。

## 2. 同位語 vs. 形容詞子句的差異
同位語與非限定形容詞子句（, which / , who...）都可以用逗號插入句中補充說明，但**同位語沒有動詞，形容詞子句一定要有主詞+動詞**。
| 結構 | 範例 | 說明 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 同位語 | The CEO, **a Harvard graduate**, spoke first. | 純名詞片語，無動詞 |
| 形容詞子句 | The CEO, **who graduated from Harvard**, spoke first. | who + 動詞，有完整子句 |

**多益陷阱**：逗號後面若同時出現「which/who + be 動詞」又出現「名詞」，通常只能二選一，不可以疊加成 `, which a compact laptop` 這種缺動詞或缺連接詞的雜燴結構。

## 3. 限定同位語（無逗號）：頭銜／職稱 + 專有名詞
當同位語是「職稱、頭銜」直接加在「人名」前面，且去掉它會影響句意的辨識時，**中間不加逗號**，這叫限定同位語。
*   **Economist** Dr. Emily Wu will lead the workshop. (economist 直接修飾 Dr. Emily Wu，無逗號)
*   對比：Dr. Emily Wu, **a renowned economist**, will lead the workshop. (改成後位、非限定同位語則要加逗號)

## 4. 同位語的引導標記詞：namely / or / that is / in other words
這些字詞專門用來引出同位語或補充說明，意思是「也就是、換句話說」：
*   **namely**：後面直接接名詞（列舉、具體化）。The plan has one goal, **namely** growth.
*   **or**：X, **or** Y，用「或」來重新命名同一件事，不是「二選一」的意思。the CFO, **or** chief financial officer,
*   **that is** / **in other words**：後面接子句或名詞，重新陳述前面的內容。

## 5. 插入語 (Parenthetical) 與轉承副詞
轉承副詞（however, moreover, therefore, in fact...）插入主詞和動詞之間時，前後都要用逗號成對隔開；插入語拿掉後，句子文法仍必須完整。
*   The vendor, **however**, agreed to extend the deadline. (拿掉 however，句子仍完整)

## 6. 分詞構句與獨立結構作插入語
分詞片語 (V-ing / p.p.) 或「With + N + p.p./adj.」的獨立分詞構句，常被逗號或句首位置插入，補充說明主詞的伴隨動作或狀態。
*   The CFO, **having submitted** the final report, left early. (having + p.p. 表示「先完成」的動作，早於主要動詞 left)
*   **With** the merger agreement signed, both companies began integrating their systems. (With + N + p.p. 表示伴隨狀態，常放句首)

## 📝 多益秒殺解題技巧
1.  **先刪插入語，再判斷句型**：遇到一長串被逗號或破折號夾住的內容，先整段刪除，剩下的主詞、動詞才是判斷單複數一致與句型的依據。
2.  **同位語只能是名詞**：空格前後已是完整的「逗號…逗號」插入結構、且缺的是名詞角色時，動詞、形容詞選項可直接刪除。
3.  **that 不能引導逗號後的插入子句**：`, that is a compact laptop` 這種寫法文法錯誤，`that` 不能接在逗號後面當非限定子句的引導詞。

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **同位語的定義**：緊接在名詞後面、重新命名或補充說明該名詞的另一個名詞（片語），前後通常用逗號隔開，本身沒有動詞。
2.  **同位語 vs. 形容詞子句**：同位語是純名詞片語（無動詞）；形容詞子句一定要有「關係代名詞 + 動詞」，兩者不可混用或疊加。
3.  **限定同位語不加逗號**：職稱／頭銜直接放在專有名詞前面時（如 `economist Dr. Wu`）不加逗號，屬於限定性同位語。
4.  **破折號同位語**：句中已經有逗號時，可改用一對破折號 (—) 來標示同位語或插入語，避免與句中原有逗號混淆。
5.  **插入語必須成對出現**：無論是逗號、破折號或括號，插入語的標點一定要「前後各一個」成對出現，只寫一個會造成句子斷裂或誤讀。
6.  **同位語標記詞 namely**：`namely` 後面直接接名詞，用來具體列舉或點名前面提到的內容，不能接完整子句。
7.  **同位語標記詞 or**：`X, or Y,` 的 `or` 是「換句話說、也就是」的意思，用來重新命名同一件事物，不是「二選一」。
8.  **that is / in other words**：用來重新陳述、換句話說前面提到的內容，後面可接子句或名詞，常見於句中插入或句首。
9.  **轉承副詞插入語**：`however, moreover, therefore` 等轉承副詞插入主詞動詞之間時，前後都要有逗號，拿掉後句子文法仍完整。
10. **完成式分詞構句 having + p.p.**：插入語中若動作發生在主要動詞之前，用 `having + p.p.` 表示「先做完…之後」，不可直接用原形動詞。
11. **獨立分詞構句 With + N + p.p./adj.**：`With the report finished, ...` 這種「With + 名詞 + 分詞/形容詞」結構，常放句首補充伴隨狀況，是插入語的一種變化型。
12. **同位語不影響主詞動詞一致**：插入的同位語或插入語本身不是句子的主詞，動詞的單複數仍要依照原本主詞決定，不受插入內容干擾。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語
### 同位語與插入語核心詞彙
*   **apposition** 名詞 同位語結構 | The two nouns stand in *apposition* to each other in the sentence.
*   **appositive** 名詞 同位語 | "The manager, a former accountant," contains an *appositive*.
*   **parenthetical** 形容詞 插入的、附加說明的 | The *parenthetical* remark did not change the sentence's main meaning.
*   **insert** 動詞 插入 | Please *insert* the clarification between the two commas.
*   **elaborate** 動詞 詳細說明 | The manager *elaborated* on the new refund policy during the meeting.
*   **clarify** 動詞 澄清 | The memo was revised to *clarify* the reporting deadline.
*   **specify** 動詞 明確指出 | The contract failed to *specify* which party bears the shipping cost.
*   **restate** 動詞 重新陳述 | The consultant *restated* the proposal in simpler terms for the board.
*   **rephrase** 動詞 改述 | She *rephrased* the instructions so new hires could follow them easily.

### 插入標記詞與轉承副詞
*   **namely** 副詞 亦即、也就是 | The budget covers two items, *namely* travel and lodging.
*   **incidentally** 副詞 順帶一提 | *Incidentally*, the supplier also raised its shipping rates this quarter.
*   **notably** 副詞 值得注意地 | Two departments, *notably* sales and marketing, missed their targets.
*   **particularly** 副詞 尤其 | The client was *particularly* concerned about the delivery schedule.
*   **specifically** 副詞 明確地 | The audit *specifically* targeted overseas transactions.
*   **additionally** 副詞 此外 | *Additionally*, the vendor agreed to waive the late fee.
*   **furthermore** 副詞 此外、而且 | *Furthermore*, the report recommends hiring two more analysts.
*   **moreover** 副詞 再者 | The plan is costly; *moreover*, it requires board approval.
*   **nonetheless** 副詞 儘管如此 | The launch was delayed; *nonetheless*, sales exceeded expectations.
*   **nevertheless** 副詞 然而、儘管如此 | The market is competitive; *nevertheless*, the firm expanded overseas.
*   **meanwhile** 副詞 同時 | The design team, *meanwhile*, finalized the packaging.
*   **likewise** 副詞 同樣地 | The Tokyo office adopted the new system; the Seoul office did *likewise*.
*   **similarly** 副詞 同樣地 | Revenue rose in Q1; *similarly*, profits climbed in Q2.
*   **indeed** 副詞 的確 | The proposal was, *indeed*, the most cost-effective option.
*   **formerly** 副詞 先前、以前 | Mr. Kato, *formerly* the finance director, now advises the board.

### 描述人物與補充身分的形容詞
*   **renowned** 形容詞 著名的 | A *renowned* consultant was hired to redesign the workflow.
*   **prominent** 形容詞 顯著的、知名的 | A *prominent* investor joined the company's advisory board.
*   **distinguished** 形容詞 卓越的 | The keynote speaker is a *distinguished* professor of economics.
*   **veteran** 名詞/形容詞 資深人士；資深的 | Mr. Lin, a *veteran* of the logistics industry, oversees the new hub.
*   **aforementioned** 形容詞 上述的 | The *aforementioned* clause applies to all overseas branches.
*   **colleague** 名詞 同事 | Her *colleague*, the head of procurement, co-signed the request.

(A) that is a compact laptop (B) a compact laptop (C) which being a compact laptop (D) whose compact laptop

**3.** The seminar covered three core skills, ______ negotiation, persuasion, and active listening.
(A) despite (B) unless (C) namely (D) although

**4.** The department's productivity dropped for one simple reason — ______, the new software had never been properly installed.
(A) even though (B) as if (C) provided that (D) that is

**5.** The vendor, ______, agreed to extend the payment deadline by two weeks.
(A) however (B) unless (C) so that (D) whereas

**6.** ______ the merger agreement finally signed, both companies began integrating their accounting systems.
(A) Despite (B) With (C) Unless (D) Because

**7.** The CFO, ______ the final budget report, left the meeting early to catch her flight.
(A) submit (B) submitted (C) having submitted (D) to submit

**8.** Renowned ______ Dr. Emily Wu will lead tomorrow's workshop on supply-chain analytics.
(A) economy (B) economic (C) economically (D) economist

**9.** The regional director, Mr. Alvarez, ______ that the branch had exceeded its sales target.（第一空）The results, ______, were shared with all department heads during the town hall.（第二空）
第一空：(A) announced (B) announce (C) announcing (D) to announce
第二空：(A) although (B) moreover (C) unless (D) despite

**10.** The company's CFO, ______ chief financial officer, approved the new expense policy this morning.
(A) but (B) yet (C) or (D) so

**11.** Ms. Rivera, ______ of the operations team for over a decade, was recently promoted to vice president.
(A) heads (B) heading (C) who heads (D) a head

**12.** The training manual, updated ______ last month to reflect new safety protocols, is now available on the intranet.
(A) as of (B) in case (C) even if (D) so that

**13.** The pilot program was, ______, a trial run before full implementation across all branches.
(A) in addition (B) in other words (C) in case (D) as though

(A) supply (B) supplying (C) supplier (D) supplied
