# 09_時態與條件句搭配辨識

## 核心概念
本章與 [[05_子句與假設語氣/04_假設語氣]] 是一組互補章節：該章專注於「與事實相反」的假設語氣（第二、三類條件句與倒裝),而本章聚焦於**真實條件句（零、第一類）**以及**條件句中主副子句時態是否互相搭配**的辨識能力——也就是看到一個條件句時，先判斷它究竟屬於「真實／習慣」還是「與事實相反」，再檢查主副子句的時態組合是否合乎邏輯，而不是死背单一公式。多益的合約、政策公告、退換貨條款、會員規則等篇章題，最常出現這類「條件子句」。

## 1. 零條件句（Zero Conditional）：恆常事實與公司規定
表達**永遠成立的事實、規定、慣例**，副子句與主子句**都用現在簡單式**，與時間無關。
*   If a shipment **arrives** damaged, the receiving clerk **files** an incident report immediately. (公司固定流程)
*   Whenever the inventory level **drops** below the threshold, the system **generates** a reorder alert automatically.

## 2. 第一類條件句（Real / Future Conditional）：未來可能發生的真實情況
副子句用**現在簡單式**代替未來，主子句用 **will / can / may / must + V** 或**祈使句**。
*   If the client **signs** the contract by Friday, the project **will begin** the following Monday.
*   If the invoice **is** not paid within 30 days, a late fee **will be applied**.
*   若主子句是要求對方行動：If you **have** any questions about the policy, please **contact** the HR office. (祈使句)

## 3. 常見時態誤用陷阱：真實條件句 vs. 假設語氣混用
多益最愛的誘答方式，是把「真實條件句」與「與事實相反的假設語氣」的動詞形式**混搭**，造成邏輯錯誤。判斷關鍵：先看語意——句子談的是「未來仍有可能發生」還是「與現在／過去事實相反」？
*   ❌ If the shipment **arrived** late, we **will** notify the customer. (副子句用過去式卻搭配 will，時態不一致)
*   ✅ If the shipment **arrives** late, we **will** notify the customer. (真實條件句：現在式 + will)
*   ❌ If she **submits** the form today, she **would receive** a refund within a week. (現在式卻搭配 would，屬於假設語氣的主子句形式，兩邊不搭)
*   ✅ If she **submits** the form today, she **will receive** a refund within a week.

## 4. unless、provided that、as long as、only if：真實條件句的替代連接詞
這些連接詞在**語意屬於「真實條件」**時，副子句同樣遵守「現在式代未來」原則，主子句用未來式或祈使句，**不可**因為連接詞比較「正式」就誤用假設語氣動詞形式。
*   **unless**（＝ if...not）：Unless the supplier **confirms** the shipping date by tomorrow, the launch **will be** postponed.
*   **provided (that) / providing (that)**（條件是...）：The refund **will be processed** provided that the item **is returned** within 14 days.
*   **as long as**（只要）：Employees may work remotely as long as they **meet** their weekly targets.
*   **only if**（唯有...才）：The warranty **remains valid** only if the product **is registered** within 30 days of purchase.
*   **on condition that**（條件是...，較正式）：The vendor agreed to the discount on condition that the order **exceeds** 500 units.

## 5. in case：語意與 if 不同，時態邏輯也不同
in case 表示「以防萬一」，強調**預先採取行動以應對可能發生的事**，主子句動作**通常先於**副子句所描述的情況，兩者常同時用現在式或主子句用現在式／祈使句。
*   Please keep a copy of the receipt in case the item **needs** to be exchanged later.
*   The company keeps backup servers running in case the main system **fails**.

## 6. 條件子句與時態一致性的檢查步驟
遇到條件句選填題時，建議依序檢查：
1. 先判斷語意是「真實條件」還是「與事實相反」。
2. 若為真實條件，副子句一律用現在簡單式（即使講未來），主子句才可用 will/can/may/must 或祈使句。
3. 若為與事實相反，才回頭套用 [[05_子句與假設語氣/04_假設語氣]] 的退一格公式。
4. 檢查連接詞語意（unless、provided that、in case…）是否與句意相符，再確認前後子句時態是否互相呼應。

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **零條件句公式**：If/Whenever + S + 現在式, S + 現在式。用於公司規定、自動化流程、恆常事實，前後兩個子句都不受時間影響。
2.  **第一類條件句公式**：If + S + 現在式, S + will/can/may/must + V 原形。副子句絕不可用 will，即使語意明顯指向未來。
3.  **祈使句作為主子句**：If + S + 現在式, 祈使句（V 原形開頭）。常見於通知客戶或員工「符合條件時該怎麼做」。
4.  **辨識真實 vs. 假設語氣的第一步**：先看主子句助動詞是 will/can/may（真實）還是 would/could/might（假設），再回頭檢查副子句時態是否搭配。
5.  **unless = if...not**：unless 子句本身已經隱含否定，子句中不可再加 not，且同樣遵守「現在式代未來」。
6.  **provided/providing (that) 與 on condition that**：語意等同 if，但語氣更正式、更常見於合約與政策條款，時態規則與第一類條件句完全相同。
7.  **as long as 的雙重身分**：可表「條件」（只要）也可表「時間長度」（長達），需依上下文判斷；表條件時遵守真實條件句時態規則。
8.  **only if 提前時的倒裝**：Only if + 條件子句放句首時，主子句需部分倒裝（助動詞提前）；若 only if 子句在句中則不需倒裝。
9.  **in case 不等於 if**：in case 強調「預先防範」，中文常誤譯成「如果」，但邏輯是「以防萬一」，不可與 unless 或 provided that 互換。
10. **政策篇章題關鍵詞**：eligible, entitled, applicable, valid 等詞常與條件子句連用，描述「符合條件才享有的權利」，需連貫判讀整段條件邏輯。
11. **時態混搭陷阱總表**：現在式副子句 + would/could 主子句（錯）；過去式副子句 + will 主子句（錯）；這兩種組合是選項中最常見的誘答。
12. **否定條件句的常見寫法**：If + S + do/does not + V ＝ Unless + S + V（肯定式），改寫時注意連接詞與動詞肯定/否定同時互換，不可只改其中一個。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

### 條件、資格與規定
*   **eligibility** n. 資格、合格 | Applicants must confirm their *eligibility* before submitting the form.
*   **entitlement** n. 應得權利 | Employees lose their vacation *entitlement* if they resign before the anniversary date.
*   **stipulate** v. (及物) 明文規定 | The contract *stipulates* that payment must be made within 30 days.
*   **clause** n. 條款 | Please review the termination *clause* before signing.
*   **provision** n. 條款、規定 | The agreement includes a *provision* for early cancellation.
*   **proviso** n. 附帶條件 | The offer stands, with the *proviso* that stock remains available.
*   **criteria** n. (複數) 標準、條件 | Candidates who meet all the *criteria* will be shortlisted.
*   **threshold** n. 門檻 | Orders that exceed the *threshold* qualify for free shipping.
*   **subject to** phr. 視...而定、須經 | The proposal is *subject to* approval by the board.
*   **applicable** adj. 適用的 | This discount is *applicable* only to first-time customers.

### 契約與條款相關動詞
*   **breach** v./n. 違反（契約） | Late delivery would *breach* the terms of the agreement.
*   **waive** v. 放棄（權利）、豁免 | The landlord agreed to *waive* the late fee this once.
*   **waiver** n. 棄權書、豁免 | Customers must sign a *waiver* before joining the trial program.
*   **forfeit** v. 喪失（權利、款項） | Guests who cancel late will *forfeit* their deposit.
*   **void** v./adj. 使無效；無效的 | Any unauthorized change will *void* the warranty.
*   **null and void** phr. 完全無效 | The clause becomes *null and void* if either party breaches the agreement.
*   **revoke** v. 撤銷 | The company reserves the right to *revoke* access at any time.
*   **amend** v. 修訂 | Management may *amend* the policy with prior notice.
*   **amendment** n. 修正案 | The latest *amendment* takes effect next quarter.
*   **binding** adj. 具約束力的 | Once signed, the contract is legally *binding* on both parties.

### 政策與流程情境用語
*   **specify** v. 明確指定 | Please *specify* the reason for the return.
*   **default** v./n. 預設、違約 | Unless changed, the system will *default* to monthly billing.
*   **contingency** n. 應變措施、意外情況 | The department created a *contingency* plan for supply shortages.
*   **safeguard** v. 保障、防護 | These measures *safeguard* customer data in case of a breach.
*   **reimburse** v. 償還、報銷 | The company will *reimburse* travel expenses provided receipts are submitted.
*   **non-refundable** adj. 不可退還的 | The registration fee is *non-refundable* after the deadline.
*   **opt out** phr. 選擇退出 | Employees may *opt out* of the program at any time.
*   **unless otherwise specified** phr. 除非另有說明 | All prices include tax *unless otherwise specified*.

## 💪 練習題（15 題）

**1.** If the warehouse ______ an order before 3 p.m., the item usually ships out the same day.
(A) receives (B) will receive (C) received (D) would receive

**2.** Unless the vendor ______ the revised invoice by Friday, the payment will be delayed until next month.
(A) sent (B) sends (C) would send (D) had sent

**3.** The refund policy states that customers ______ a full refund provided that the product is returned within 14 days in its original packaging.
(A) receive (B) received (C) would have received (D) will have received

**4.** As long as employees ______ their quarterly targets, they remain eligible for the performance bonus.
(A) met (B) meet (C) would meet (D) had met

**5.** If a supplier fails to deliver on time, the procurement team ______ contact the backup vendor immediately.
(A) would (B) will (C) had (D) were to

**6.** In case the printer ______ during the presentation, please keep a backup copy of the slides on a USB drive.
(A) malfunctioned (B) malfunctions (C) would malfunction (D) had malfunctioned

**7.** Only if the shipment passes the final quality check ______ the product cleared for distribution.
(A) is (B) will be (C) was (D) would be

**8.** The lease automatically renews unless either party ______ written notice at least 60 days in advance.
(A) provided (B) provides (C) would provide (D) had provided

**9.** According to the membership terms, points expire if a member ______ them within 24 months of issuance.
(A) does not redeem (B) will not redeem (C) would not redeem (D) had not redeemed

**10.** On condition that the merger receives regulatory approval, both companies ______ their operations by the third quarter.
(A) would combine (B) will combine (C) combined (D) had combined

**11.**（雙格題）第一空：If the client ______ additional revisions after Friday, the delivery date will shift accordingly.（第一空）Providing that the new deadline ______ acceptable to both parties, no penalty clause will apply.（第二空）
第一空：(A) requests (B) will request (C) would request (D) had requested
第二空：(A) is (B) will be (C) would be (D) were

**12.** The IT department will restore access within an hour if the outage ______ a routine maintenance issue rather than a security breach.
(A) proves (B) will prove (C) would prove (D) had proven

**13.** Provided that the auditors ______ no discrepancies in the accounts, the annual report will be published on schedule.
(A) find (B) found (C) would find (D) will have found

**14.** Whenever a customer's order ______ the free-shipping threshold, the checkout system applies the discount automatically.
(A) exceeded (B) exceeds (C) would exceed (D) had exceeded

**15.**（雙格題）Unless the committee ______ the proposal before the end of the month, the budget for next year cannot be finalized.（第一空）In that case, the finance director ______ an emergency meeting to discuss alternative funding.（第二空）
第一空：(A) approves (B) will approve (C) would approve (D) had approved
第二空：(A) calls (B) will call (C) would call (D) had called
