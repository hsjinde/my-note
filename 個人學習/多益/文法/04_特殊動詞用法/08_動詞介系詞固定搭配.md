# 08_動詞介系詞固定搭配

## 核心概念
多益文法題中有一大類考點是「動詞＋固定介系詞」的搭配，這類搭配**沒有邏輯規則可推導，必須整組記憶**。常見出題方式是給一個動詞，要求選出正確的介系詞；或給一個介系詞空格，要求判斷前面動詞的固定搭配。這類題目也常混入詞性題（動詞 vs. 名詞 vs. 形容詞）一起考。

## 1. 「因果／來源」類動詞介系詞 🌟
表示「起因於、導致、歸因於」的動詞片語：
*   `stem from`（源自於）、`arise from`（起因於）、`account for`（說明、佔…比例）
*   `Delays in shipping often **stem from** poor communication with suppliers.`
*   `The rising cost of raw materials **accounts for** the price increase.`

## 2. 「依賴／依循」類動詞介系詞 🌟
*   `rely on`、`depend on`（依賴）、`adhere to`（遵守）、`conform to`（符合）
*   `The production schedule **relies on** timely delivery from overseas suppliers.`
*   `All contractors must **adhere to** the safety guidelines outlined in the manual.`

## 3. 「處理／應對」類動詞介系詞
*   `cope with`、`deal with`（應付、處理）、`resort to`（訴諸於）
*   `The customer service team had to **deal with** an unusually high volume of complaints.`
*   `When negotiations failed, both companies **resorted to** legal action.`

## 4. 「參與／投入」類動詞介系詞
*   `engage in`、`participate in`（參與）、`embark on`（展開、著手）、`specialize in`（專精於）
*   `The firm plans to **embark on** a major restructuring project next quarter.`
*   `Our consulting team **specializes in** cross-border tax compliance.`

## 5. 「反應／回應」類動詞介系詞
*   `react to`、`respond to`（對…做出反應）、`apologize for`（為…道歉）
*   `Management **responded to** the shareholders' concerns with a detailed statement.`
*   `The airline **apologized for** the inconvenience caused by the delayed flight.`

## 6. 雙受詞動詞介系詞（A + 動詞 + B + for/on/from）
這類動詞需要兩個受詞，中間夾著固定介系詞：
*   `blame A for B`（因 B 責怪 A）、`congratulate A on B`（因 B 恭喜 A）、`prevent A from B`（阻止 A 做 B）、`distinguish A from B`（區分 A 和 B）
*   `The CEO **congratulated** the sales team **on** exceeding this year's target.`
*   `Strict access control **prevents** unauthorized staff **from** entering the server room.`

## 7. 秒殺提示
遇到「動詞 + ______ + 名詞」的題目，先看動詞本身，再判斷後面是否為固定介系詞搭配；若四個選項都是介系詞（in/on/at/for/from/with...），代表這是純粹的搭配記憶題，語意分析幫助有限，直接背誦是唯一解法。

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **account for 的雙重意思**：可表示「說明、解釋」(He couldn't account for the missing funds.)，也可表示「佔…比例」(Overseas sales account for 40% of total revenue.)，兩種意思在多益都常考，須依上下文判斷。
2.  **depend on / rely on 幾乎可互換**：兩者都表「依賴」，後面接名詞或 V-ing，介系詞固定用 on，不可誤用 in 或 upon 以外的字（upon 是 on 的正式版，也可接受）。
3.  **adhere to vs. conform to**：adhere to 偏向「堅持遵守規定/原則」，conform to 偏向「符合規範/標準」，兩者介系詞都固定用 to，不可用 with。
4.  **cope with / deal with 幾乎同義**：兩者都接「難題、困境」作受詞，介系詞固定用 with，常與 difficulty, problem, situation, complaint 等名詞搭配。
5.  **resort to 之後只能接名詞或 V-ing**，不可直接接原形動詞；resort 若當名詞則意思是「渡假勝地」，考詞性題時要小心區分。
6.  **specialize in / excel at 的介系詞不同**：specialize 固定搭配 in，excel 固定搭配 at，容易被誤植互換，是常見陷阱。
7.  **雙受詞動詞的介系詞是整組固定的**：blame...for、congratulate...on、prevent...from、distinguish...from，考題常把介系詞挖空，只要記住動詞就能秒選。
8.  **prevent/stop + 受詞 + from + V-ing**：from 之後一律接動名詞，不可用不定詞，這是常見的文法陷阱。
9.  **contribute to 的 to 是介系詞不是不定詞記號**：後面必須接名詞或動名詞，不能直接接原形動詞，例如 The new policy contributed to **improving** efficiency.（正確）。
10. **refer to 的多重意思**：可表示「提及、指的是」或「查閱、參照」，例如 Please **refer to** the attached document for details.
11. **benefit from / suffer from**：兩者都表「因…而得利／受害」，介系詞固定用 from，主詞通常是承受影響的一方。
12. **apply for vs. apply to**：apply for 表「申請（職位、資格）」，apply to 表「適用於」，介系詞不同、意思也不同，是常見的混淆點。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

### 因果／來源類
*   **stem from** (v. phr.) 源自於 | The delay **stemmed from** a shortage of raw materials.
*   **arise from** (v. phr.) 起因於 | Several complaints **arose from** the confusing billing system.
*   **account for** (v. phr.) 說明、佔比例 | Manufacturing costs **account for** nearly half of the total budget.
*   **culminate in** (v. phr.) 以…告終、最終導致 | Years of research **culminated in** the launch of the new product.

### 依賴／依循類
*   **rely on** (v. phr.) 依賴 | The logistics team **relies on** accurate forecasts to plan shipments.
*   **depend on** (v. phr.) 依賴、取決於 | Whether the project succeeds **depends on** proper funding.
*   **adhere to** (v. phr.) 遵守、堅持 | Employees must **adhere to** the company's code of conduct.
*   **conform to** (v. phr.) 符合 | All products must **conform to** international safety standards.
*   **correspond to** (v. phr.) 符合、相當於 | The figures in the report **correspond to** last quarter's data.
*   **pertain to** (v. phr.) 關於、與…有關 | Please read the section that **pertains to** overtime pay.

### 處理／應對類
*   **cope with** (v. phr.) 應付、處理 | The support team struggled to **cope with** the surge in orders.
*   **deal with** (v. phr.) 處理、應付 | The manager will personally **deal with** the client's complaint.
*   **resort to** (v. phr.) 訴諸於 | The airline had to **resort to** offering vouchers to stranded passengers.
*   **succumb to** (v. phr.) 屈服於 | The vendor eventually **succumbed to** pressure and lowered the price.
*   **yield to** (v. phr.) 屈服、讓步於 | Management refused to **yield to** the union's demands.

### 參與／投入類
*   **engage in** (v. phr.) 從事、參與 | The company **engages in** several charitable activities each year.
*   **participate in** (v. phr.) 參加 | All staff are encouraged to **participate in** the training program.
*   **embark on** (v. phr.) 展開、著手進行 | The firm is about to **embark on** an ambitious expansion plan.
*   **specialize in** (v. phr.) 專精於 | This law firm **specializes in** intellectual property disputes.
*   **excel at** (v. phr.) 擅長於 | The new hire **excels at** building client relationships.
*   **thrive on** (v. phr.) 靠…蓬勃發展、樂於 | Some employees **thrive on** working under tight deadlines.
*   **capitalize on** (v. phr.) 利用、趁機獲利 | The startup **capitalized on** the growing demand for remote work tools.

### 反應／回應類
*   **react to** (v. phr.) 對…作出反應 | Investors **reacted to** the earnings report with cautious optimism.
*   **respond to** (v. phr.) 回應 | The department promised to **respond to** all inquiries within 24 hours.
*   **apologize for** (v. phr.) 為…道歉 | The manager **apologized for** the miscommunication during the launch.

### 雙受詞（A + 動詞 + B + 介系詞）類
*   **blame** (v. 及物) 責怪 | The report **blamed** the outage **for** the loss in sales.
*   **congratulate** (v. 及物) 恭喜 | The board **congratulated** the team **on** winning the contract.
*   **prevent** (v. 及物) 阻止 | The new firewall **prevents** hackers **from** accessing the database.
*   **distinguish** (v. 及物) 區分 | It is important to **distinguish** genuine leads **from** spam inquiries.

### 其他常考搭配
*   **benefit from** (v. phr.) 從…中受益 | Small businesses **benefit from** the new tax incentive.
*   **suffer from** (v. phr.) 遭受、受…之苦 | The retailer **suffered from** a sharp drop in foot traffic.
*   **contribute to** (v. phr.) 促成、貢獻於 | Poor communication **contributed to** the project's delay.

## 💪 練習題（15 題）

**1.** The sudden drop in production efficiency largely ______ from an outdated inventory system.
(A) stems (B) results (C) accounts (D) arises

**2.** Employees at the branch are required to ______ to the updated dress code starting next month.
(A) rely (B) conform (C) apply (D) adhere

**3.** The customer support hotline was overwhelmed and could barely ______ with the number of incoming calls.
(A) deal (B) cope (C) manage (D) handle

**4.** Facing a shortage of skilled technicians, the factory eventually ______ to hiring temporary contractors.
(A) resorted (B) conformed (C) adhered (D) referred

**5.** The regional office plans to ______ in a series of community outreach programs this fall.
(A) participate (B) engage (C) embark (D) specialize

**6.** The design studio ______ in creating packaging for luxury cosmetics brands.
(A) excels (B) specializes (C) thrives (D) engages

**7.** 第一空：Rather than waiting for a crisis, the marketing team decided to ______ on the trend early. 第二空：This early move ultimately ______ to the brand's rapid growth in the region.
(A) capitalize / contributed (B) capitalize / accounted (C) thrive / stemmed (D) resort / resulted

**8.** How quickly a company can ______ to market changes often determines its long-term survival.
(A) react (B) reply (C) answer (D) respond

**9.** The airline's spokesperson publicly ______ for the technical failure that grounded dozens of flights.
(A) regretted (B) apologized (C) blamed (D) accounted

**10.** The auditor ______ the accounting error for the discrepancy discovered in the quarterly report.
(A) blamed (B) apologized (C) distinguished (D) prevented

**11.** The board formally ______ the sales division on achieving record revenue this quarter.
(A) blamed (B) prevented (C) congratulated (D) responded

**12.** New access badges ______ unauthorized visitors from entering the manufacturing floor.
(A) cope (B) resort (C) yield (D) prevent

**13.** Local restaurants have ______ from the recent surge in tourism following the festival.
(A) benefited (B) suffered (C) contributed (D) resulted

**14.** 第一空：The logistics department ______ heavily on real-time tracking software to schedule deliveries. 第二空：Without it, dispatchers would struggle to ______ with sudden route changes.
(A) depends / resort (B) relies / cope (C) adheres / deal (D) conforms / react

**15.** It can be difficult to ______ a genuine market trend from a temporary spike in demand.
(A) prevent (B) blame (C) distinguish (D) congratulate
