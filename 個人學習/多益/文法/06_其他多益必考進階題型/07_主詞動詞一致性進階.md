# 07_主詞動詞一致性進階

## 核心概念
本章延續「主動詞一致性」的基礎規則，聚焦於三種進階陷阱：(1) 集合名詞在不同語境下的單複數判斷、(2) 不定詞片語／動名詞片語／子句當主詞時動詞恆為單數、(3) there be 句型與「假複數連接詞」（如 together with, along with, as well as）插入主詞和動詞之間時，動詞仍須配合**原本的主詞**，而不是插入語裡的名詞。

## 1. 集合名詞的語境判斷
集合名詞（如 crowd, panel, workforce, delegation）在多益中預設視為**單數整體**，但當句子強調「成員個別行動」時，可能出現複數動詞，需依上下文語意判斷，而非死背清單。

*   The **panel** **has** reached a unanimous verdict on the vendor selection. (視為單一整體)
*   The **workforce** **is** expected to grow by 10 percent next fiscal year.

## 2. 片語與子句當主詞 ➔ 恆視為單數
不定詞片語、動名詞片語、或名詞子句作為句子主詞時，即使片語內含複數名詞，動詞仍須用**單數**，因為主詞是「整個動作/概念」，不是片語裡的某個名詞。

*   **To relocate** the regional offices **requires** approval from headquarters.
*   **Reviewing** all the vendor contracts **takes** longer than expected this quarter.
*   **Whether the merger proceeds** **depends** on shareholder approval.

## 3. there be 句型：配合「緊接在後」的名詞
there be 句型中，動詞單複數取決於 be 動詞之後**緊接出現的第一個名詞**，尤其當清單中同時列出單數與複數名詞時。

*   **There is** a projector and several laptops in the conference room. (緊接的 a projector 為單數)
*   **There are** several vendors and one main supplier competing for the contract. (緊接的 several vendors 為複數)

## 4. 假複數連接詞：插入語不改變主詞單複數 🌟
`together with, along with, as well as, accompanied by, in addition to, besides, no less than, rather than` 這些片語**不是對等連接詞**，即使意思接近「和」，它們只是修飾主詞的插入語（常用逗號隔開），動詞仍須配合**原本的主詞**，不受插入語內名詞的單複數影響。

*   The **CFO**, together with two senior accountants, **is** reviewing the annual budget.
*   The **branch manager**, along with several supervisors, **has** approved the new schedule.
*   The **shipment**, as well as the accompanying invoices, **was** delayed by customs.

對照：若用 `and` 連接，則動詞須改為複數。
*   The CFO **and** two senior accountants **are** reviewing the annual budget.

## 5. by / with 介系詞片語插入主詞與動詞之間
被動語態或描述性子句中，`by`、`with` 引導的片語常插入主詞和動詞之間，考生容易誤把片語裡的名詞當成主詞。判斷方法與基礎章節相同：先刪除介系詞片語，再看剩下的主詞。

*   The **proposal**, submitted by three department heads, **was** approved unanimously.
*   The **warehouse**, equipped with the latest tracking system, **reduces** shipment errors significantly.

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **不定詞/動名詞片語當主詞**：動詞恆單數，不看片語內名詞單複數。
2.  **that/whether/what 子句當主詞**：動詞恆單數。
3.  **there be + 清單**：動詞配合緊接在 be 動詞後的第一個名詞，不是配合整份清單。
4.  **together with / along with / as well as / accompanied by / in addition to / besides**：視為插入語，動詞配合原主詞，不因插入語變複數。
5.  **rather than 插入語**：功能同上，動詞仍配合原主詞，例如 The **director**, rather than her assistants, **is** attending the summit.
6.  **no less than + 數字 + N**：整體視為單數，強調數量之大，例如 **No less than** ten thousand dollars **was** spent on renovations.
7.  **集合名詞語境判斷**：預設單數，但若句意明顯強調成員個別分歧行動，可用複數（如英式用法 The panel are divided）。
8.  **被動分詞片語插入（p.p. 修飾）**：找出被修飾的名詞才是主詞，插入的 by/with 片語只是補充資訊。
9.  **現在分詞片語插入（V-ing 修飾）**：同上，判斷邏輯不變。
10. **倒裝句中的 there/here**：動詞仍須配合倒裝後方的真正主詞單複數，不可直接套用 is/are。
11. **同位語插入（逗號夾住的補充說明）**：同位語不影響主詞單複數，例如 The **consultant**, a former auditor herself, **understands** the risks well.
12. **多重插入語疊加**：句子中若同時出現介系詞片語與 as well as 片語，仍只看最前面的核心名詞決定動詞單複數。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

### 集合名詞與整體概念
*   **panel** (n. 評審／委員小組) | The **panel is** currently interviewing the final round of candidates.
*   **workforce** (n. 全體勞動力) | The company's **workforce has** doubled in the past decade.
*   **delegation** (n. 代表團) | A **delegation** from the parent company **is** visiting the branch next week.
*   **crowd** (n. 群眾) | The **crowd was** eager to hear the CEO's announcement.
*   **coalition** (n. 聯盟) | The **coalition** of suppliers **has** agreed on a unified pricing policy.

### 假複數插入語（不改變主詞單複數）
*   **together with** (phr. 連同) | The **manager**, together with her assistants, **is** finalizing the proposal.
*   **along with** (phr. 隨同) | The **contract**, along with two appendices, **was** sent for legal review.
*   **as well as** (phr. 以及) | The **director**, as well as the marketing team, **plans** to attend the launch.
*   **accompanied by** (phr. 伴隨) | The **CEO**, accompanied by two board members, **arrives** tomorrow.
*   **in addition to** (phr. 除了...之外) | The **budget**, in addition to the revised forecast, **needs** approval.
*   **besides** (prep. 除了) | The **supervisor**, besides two engineers, **reviews** the site every morning.
*   **rather than** (phr. 而不是) | The **assistant**, rather than the manager, **handles** routine inquiries.
*   **no less than** (phr. 不少於) | **No less than** a million dollars **is** allocated for the expansion.

### 動詞／動作片語
*   **relocate** (v. 遷移) | The firm plans to **relocate** its headquarters next spring.
*   **finalize** (v. 定案) | The board will **finalize** the merger terms by Friday.
*   **allocate** (v. 分配) | Management will **allocate** additional funds to the R&D team.
*   **coordinate** (v. 協調) | The project lead must **coordinate** schedules across departments.
*   **dispatch** (v. 派遣、發送) | The logistics team will **dispatch** the shipment before noon.
*   **oversee** (v. 監督) | The supervisor **oversees** all quality control procedures.
*   **compile** (v. 彙整) | The intern was asked to **compile** the survey responses.
*   **streamline** (v. 使流程化簡) | The new software helps **streamline** the approval process.

### 片語主詞相關
*   **undertaking** (n. 任務、承擔的工作) | **Undertaking** such a large project **requires** careful planning.
*   **outsourcing** (n. 外包) | **Outsourcing** the customer service department **has reduced** operating costs.
*   **verdict** (n. 裁決、結論) | The panel's **verdict** on the vendor dispute **surprised** everyone.
*   **appendix** (n. 附錄) | The **appendix**, attached with the report, **lists** all raw data.
*   **forecast** (n. 預測) | The quarterly **forecast**, prepared by the analysts, **indicates** steady growth.
*   **counteroffer** (n. 還價、反提案) | The supplier's **counteroffer**, along with a revised timeline, **was** rejected.
*   **stipend** (n. 津貼) | The training **stipend**, together with travel expenses, **covers** most costs.
*   **liaison** (n. 聯絡人、聯繫窗口) | The regional **liaison** works closely with overseas partners.
*   **taskforce** (n. 專案小組) | The **taskforce** assigned to the audit **meets** every Monday.
*   **subsidiary** (n. 子公司) | The **subsidiary**, along with its parent company, **reports** quarterly earnings.
*   **turnout** (n. 出席人數、出席率) | The **turnout** for the recruitment fair **exceeded** expectations.

(A) Coordinate (B) Coordinated (C) Coordinating (D) To have coordinated

**3.** The **workforce** at the manufacturing plant ______ expected to expand significantly next quarter.
(A) is (B) are (C) were (D) have been

**4.** There ______ a printer and two scanners available for staff use on the third floor.
(A) are (B) is (C) were (D) have been

**5.** The regional **director**, accompanied by three senior consultants, ______ the new distribution center tomorrow.
(A) visit (B) visits (C) are visiting (D) have visited

**6.** The **shipment**, along with all supporting customs documents, ______ delayed at the border for two days.
(A) were (B) was (C) are (D) have been

**7.** **Whether the two firms will merge** ______ still uncertain, according to the latest press release.
(A) remain (B) remains (C) are remaining (D) have remained

**8.** There ______ several vendors and one preferred supplier bidding for the logistics contract.
(A) is (B) was (C) are (D) has been

**9.** The **assistant manager**, rather than the branch supervisors, ______ responsible for approving overtime requests.
(A) are (B) is (C) were (D) have been

**10.** **Outsourcing** the payroll function to a third-party firm ______ reduced administrative costs by 15 percent.
(A) have (B) has (C) are (D) were

**11.** The company's **appendix**, attached with the audit report, ______ detailed figures for every branch.
(A) list (B) lists (C) are listing (D) have listed

**12.** ______ ten thousand dollars was set aside for the employee wellness program this year.
(A) No less than (B) Not until (C) As well as (D) Rather than
