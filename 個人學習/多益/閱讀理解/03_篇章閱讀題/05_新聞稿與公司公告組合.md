# 05_新聞稿與公司公告組合

## 📝 答題策略 🌟
1.  **新聞稿看標頭與第一段**：新聞稿（Press Release）通常在標頭註明發布地點與日期，第一段就交代「誰、做了什麼、為何重要」，是主旨題的黃金位置。
2.  **公司公告先辨識對象與生效日**：內部公告（Announcement/Memo）常在開頭點出適用對象（全體員工／特定部門）與生效日期，這兩者是細節題常考重點。
3.  **數字與人名要對應職稱**：新聞稿常出現受訪者的職稱與引言，作答時務必把「誰說了什麼」對應清楚，避免張冠李戴。
4.  **字義題代入語境**：同一個字在新聞稿與公告中可能有不同用法，須依上下文判斷，而非套用最熟悉的字義。
5.  **推論題留意语氣與立場**：新聞稿通常語氣正面、強調效益；公告則偏中性、著重程序，推論時要符合文體語氣，不可過度延伸。
6.  **善用段落定位**：兩篇文章各自獨立，作答前先確認題目對應哪一篇，避免把另一篇的細節誤植進答案。

## 文章：新聞稿

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

Harborview Foods Unveils Plant-Based Snack Line

Portland, March 3 — Harborview Foods, a regional snack manufacturer based in Portland, announced today the launch of its first plant-based snack line, branded "GreenBite." The new products, including roasted chickpea crisps and lentil crackers, will be available in grocery stores across the Pacific Northwest starting April 1.

"We have spent the past two years refining these recipes to make sure they taste as good as they are nutritious," said Marcus Delgado, Harborview's head of product development. "GreenBite reflects our commitment to offering snacks that customers can feel good about eating."

According to the company, the new line was developed in response to rising customer interest in plant-based options, with an internal survey showing that nearly 60 percent of Harborview's regular customers had asked for lower-sodium, allergen-free snack choices. To meet this demand, GreenBite products are produced in a dedicated facility that does not process peanuts, tree nuts, or dairy, making them suitable for customers with common food allergies.

Harborview plans to introduce three additional flavors later this year and is exploring distribution partnerships with national retailers. The company will host a tasting event at its Portland headquarters on March 20, open to the public by reservation.

## 文章：公司公告

TO: All Staff, Riverstone Logistics
FROM: Facilities Management
RE: Relocation of Second-Floor Offices

This notice is to inform all staff that the second-floor offices currently housing the Accounting and Human Resources departments will be relocated to the newly renovated third floor beginning May 12. The move is part of the company's ongoing effort to consolidate departments that frequently collaborate, and it will also allow the second floor to be converted into additional meeting space.

Employees in the affected departments should pack personal belongings into the labeled boxes provided by Facilities Management no later than May 8. Boxes will be transported by the moving crew over the weekend of May 10–11, and staff should expect their new workstations to be ready for use by 9:00 A.M. on May 12. Employees are asked not to move heavy furniture or equipment themselves; any such requests should be directed to Facilities Management in advance.

During the transition period, phone lines and network access for the Accounting and Human Resources departments may be temporarily unavailable. Staff in other departments who need urgent assistance from these teams during this time should contact them by email rather than by phone.

We appreciate your patience and cooperation as we complete this transition. Questions about the relocation should be directed to the Facilities Management office at extension 214.
