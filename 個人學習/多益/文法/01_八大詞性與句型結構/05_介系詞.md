# 05_介系詞

## 核心概念
介系詞用來表達時間、空間或邏輯關係。介系詞後面**永遠只能接名詞或動名詞 (V-ing)**。

## 1. to 的用法：是不定詞還是介系詞？ (影片重點) 🌟
多益極愛考 `to` 的判斷，因為 `to` 有兩種身分：
1.  **不定詞的 to** ➔ 後面接 **原形動詞 (V)**
    *   I want **to** *go* home. (我想回家)
2.  **介系詞的 to** ➔ 後面接 **名詞 (N)** 或 **動名詞 (V-ing)**
    *   I look forward **to** *seeing* you. (我期待見到你)

### 必背介系詞 to 片語 (後接 V-ing/N)
*   **look forward to** (期待)
*   **be opposed to / object to** (反對)
*   **be used to / be accustomed to** (習慣於)
*   **be devoted to / be dedicated to / be committed to** (致力於 / 奉獻於)
*   **in addition to** (除了...之外)
*   **prior to** (在...之前 = before)

## 2. 時間介系詞 (in, on, at)
*   **at**：特定精確的時間點 (at 3:00 PM, at noon)
*   **on**：特定的日子、星期 (on Monday, on October 10th)
*   **in**：較長的時間 (月份、季節、年份) (in 2023, in summer, in October)
*   **within**：在...期間內 (within 30 days)

## 3. 空間介系詞
*   **at**：明確地點 (at the station)
*   **on**：在表面上、街道上 (on the desk, on Main Street)
*   **in**：在大空間、城市、國家內 (in the office, in Tokyo)

## 📝 多益秒殺解題技巧
1.  看到空格前的動詞片語是 `look forward`, `dedicated`, `accustomed` 等等，後方的 `to` 是介系詞，秒選 **V-ing** 結尾的選項。
2.  如果沒有背過的特殊片語，一般的 `to` 多數是不定詞，後面接 **原形動詞** (如 in order to V, decide to V)。

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **for vs. since 時間介系詞**：`for + 一段時間`(如 for three years)，`since + 特定時間點`(如 since 2020)，常與完成式連用，是時態題的重要輔助線索。
2.  **by vs. until 的差別**：`by`(在...之前，動作在該時間點前**完成**一次) vs. `until`(直到...為止，動作**持續**到該時間點)，常考「一次性動詞 vs. 持續性動詞」搭配。
3.  **介系詞 + 關係代名詞**：`in which, on which, for which` 等固定搭配，常見於較長閱讀句子，判斷先看介系詞習慣搭配的名詞。
4.  **複合介系詞片語**：`in accordance with`(依照)、`with regard to / regarding`(關於)、`in charge of`(負責)，多益閱讀與詞彙題常考這類三字或多字片語。
5.  **among vs. between**：`between`(兩者之間)，`among`(三者以上之間)，是介系詞題中常見的數量陷阱。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語
### 時間介系詞
*   **during** 介系詞 在...期間 | Sales increased *during* the holiday season.
*   **since** 介系詞 自從 | The company has grown rapidly *since* 2015.
*   **by** 介系詞 在...之前（截止） | Please submit the report *by* Friday.
*   **within** 介系詞 在...範圍內 | The refund will be processed *within* 7 business days.

### 邏輯/因果介系詞
*   **due to** 介系詞 由於 | The delay was *due to* a technical error.
*   **in accordance with** 片語 依照 | The refund is issued *in accordance with* company policy.
*   **regarding / concerning** 介系詞 關於 | Please contact us *regarding* your account.

### 常考固定片語
*   **be responsible for** 片語 負責 | She *is responsible for* managing the budget.
*   **be familiar with** 片語 熟悉 | New staff should *be familiar with* the safety guidelines.
*   **in charge of** 片語 負責、掌管 | He *is in charge of* the marketing department.
*   **on behalf of** 片語 代表 | She signed the contract *on behalf of* the company.
*   **as of** 片語 自...起 | *As of* next month, the office will relocate.

## 💪 練習題（5 題）

**1.** The new policy has been in effect ___ the beginning of this year.
(A) for (B) since (C) during (D) by

**2.** All applications must be submitted ___ the end of this month.
(A) until (B) by (C) since (D) within

**3.** The committee is ___ reviewing all budget proposals before the fiscal year ends.
(A) responsible to (B) responsible for (C) responsible of (D) responsible with

**4.** ___ the two candidates, Ms. Lin has more relevant experience.
(A) Among (B) Between (C) Except (D) Besides

**5.** The manager signed the agreement ___ the entire department.（第一空）Employees are expected to be ___ the updated procedures before the new system launches.（第二空）
第一空：(A) in charge of (B) on behalf of (C) regarding (D) due to
第二空：(A) familiar to (B) familiar with (C) familiar for (D) familiar of
