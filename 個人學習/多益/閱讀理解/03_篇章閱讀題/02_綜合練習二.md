# 02_綜合練習二

## 📝 答題策略 🌟
1.  **雙篇/三篇閱讀先看題目來源**：題目若同時涉及兩篇文章的內容，通常是「交叉比對題」，需分別在兩篇文章找線索再整合。
2.  **時刻表/表格類文章看欄位標題**：先確認每一欄代表什麼（時間、地點、價格、負責人），再依題目需求對應查找。
3.  **廣告/通知類文章抓「條件與限制」**：留意日期、數量、資格等限制字眼（only, must, before, unless），這些常是細節題考點。
4.  **消去法搭配文章段落標記**：把每個選項與文章對應段落逐一核對，明確與文章矛盾或未提及的選項可先刪除。
5.  **留意「NOT mentioned」題型**：這類題目通常要找出唯一「文章沒有提到」的選項，其餘三個選項都能在文章中找到依據。

## 文章：活動通知與時刻表

Annual Tech Conference – Workshop Schedule

Bright Future Tech Conference will be held at the Lakeside Convention Center on September 10. Below is the schedule for the afternoon workshop sessions. Please arrive at least 15 minutes early to check in, as seating is limited and registration closes once each session is full.

| Time | Workshop | Speaker | Room |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1:00 P.M. – 2:00 P.M. | Introduction to Cloud Computing | Dr. Amanda Reyes | Room A |
| 2:15 P.M. – 3:15 P.M. | Data Privacy in the Digital Age | Mr. Kevin Zhao | Room B |
| 3:30 P.M. – 4:30 P.M. | Building Effective Remote Teams | Ms. Sarah Lin | Room A |
| 4:45 P.M. – 5:45 P.M. | AI Tools for Small Businesses | Dr. Amanda Reyes | Room C |

All workshop materials will be distributed digitally after the conference; printed handouts will not be provided this year to reduce paper waste. Attendees who wish to receive a certificate of participation must sign in at the registration desk for each session they attend. Light refreshments will be available in the lobby between sessions, but a full lunch will not be provided, so attendees are encouraged to eat beforehand.

For any changes to the schedule, please check the conference website, as updates will not be announced via email.

### 題目 1（細節題）
Who is scheduled to give more than one workshop?

(A) Mr. Kevin Zhao
(B) Ms. Sarah Lin
(C) Dr. Amanda Reyes
(D) All of the speakers

### 題目 2（推論題）
What can be inferred about attendees who want a certificate of participation?

(A) They must pay an additional registration fee.
(B) They need to sign in at each session they attend.
(C) They must attend all four workshops.
(D) They will receive the certificate automatically by mail.

### 題目 3（NOT mentioned 題）
What is NOT stated about the conference?

(A) Seating is limited for each session.
(B) Printed handouts will not be distributed.
(C) A full lunch will be provided to attendees.
(D) Schedule changes will be posted on the website.

### 題目 4（細節題）
According to the notice, where can attendees find light refreshments?

(A) In each workshop room
(B) In the lobby
(C) At the registration desk
(D) At a nearby restaurant
