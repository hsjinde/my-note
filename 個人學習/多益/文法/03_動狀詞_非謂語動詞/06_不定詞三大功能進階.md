# 06_不定詞三大功能進階

## 核心概念
《01_不定詞》已介紹過不定詞的三大功能（名詞、形容詞、副詞）與必接不定詞的動詞清單。本章更進一步，聚焦三大功能「進階版」最容易考錯的三個地方：
1. **形容詞用法**：不定詞後位修飾名詞時，名詞跟不定詞之間到底是「主動」還是「被動」關係？前面介係詞去哪了？
2. **副詞用法**：目的、原因、結果三種語意如何用不同句型表達，而不只是死記 `in order to`。
3. **名詞用法**：不定詞片語當主詞/受詞/補語時的位置偏好，以及跟「假受詞 it」搭配的固定句型。

## 1. 形容詞用法進階：不定詞修飾名詞的邏輯關係

不定詞放在名詞後面修飾該名詞時，這個名詞其實是不定詞動作的「隱藏受詞」或「隱藏主詞」，必須判斷清楚，否則會漏寫介係詞或誤判語態。

### A. 名詞是不定詞動作的邏輯受詞 → 若動詞後面本來就需要介係詞，介係詞不能省略
*   She needs a colleague **to rely on**. (rely on colleague，介係詞 on 不可省)
*   The manager is looking for a assistant **to work with**. (work with assistant)
*   This is not an easy problem **to deal with**. (deal with problem)

### B. 名詞是不定詞動作的邏輯主詞 → 不加介係詞
*   He is the only candidate **to meet** all the requirements. (candidate 是 meet 的主詞)
*   The first team **to finish** the audit will present its findings.

### C. 名詞地位是「該做的事」，不定詞須用被動語態 (to be p.p.)
當被修飾的名詞不會主動執行動作，而是被動被處理時，要用 `to be p.p.`。
*   There are still several contracts **to be signed** before Friday. (合約不會自己簽，是被簽)
*   The documents **to be reviewed** are on your desk.

> 秒殺提醒：看到「名詞 + to V」時，先問「這個名詞會不會自己做這個動作？」會 → 主動 to V；不會（是被處理的對象）→ to be p.p.。

## 2. 副詞用法進階：目的、原因、結果三分法

多益考題常把「表目的」跟「表結果」的不定詞句型混在一起考，必須分清楚三種語意各自對應哪個句型。

### A. 表目的（為了）
*   直接用 to V：We hired extra staff **to handle** the holiday rush.
*   加強語氣：**in order to** V / **so as to** V（正式文體，常見於書信、公告）
    *   Management revised the schedule **in order to** *reduce* overtime costs.
*   否定目的：**in order not to** V / **so as not to** V（不可寫成 not to 直接放句首修飾整句）
    *   The clerk double-checked every entry **so as not to** *make* an error.

### B. 表原因（情緒形容詞 + to V）
不定詞說明「為什麼會有這種情緒」，此用法與情緒動詞的過去分詞形容詞（如 disappointed, surprised）連用。
*   The board was **pleased to** *see* the strong quarterly results.
*   Staff were **surprised to** *learn* about the sudden relocation.

### C. 表結果（only to V / such...as to V）
*   **only to V**：表示「結果卻…」，通常是出乎意料、令人失望的結果。
    *   The technician rushed to the site, **only to find** the issue had already been resolved.
*   **such + adj/n + as to V**：表示程度高到足以造成某結果，等同 so...that。
    *   The delay was **such** a serious issue **as to** *require* immediate escalation to senior management.

## 3. 名詞用法進階：主詞、受詞、補語的位置偏好

### A. 當主詞：真主詞 vs. 假主詞 it
不定詞片語直接放句首當主詞在正式文體中可行，但多益更常考「假主詞 it + 真正主詞（不定詞片語）後移」的句型，尤其搭配 for/of + 邏輯主詞。
*   **To finalize** the contract before the deadline is essential. (直接當主詞，較生硬)
*   **It** is essential **for the legal team to finalize** the contract before the deadline. (較自然，多益高頻)
*   **It** was thoughtful **of the director to acknowledge** every team member's contribution. (of + 人，表評價人的性格)

> 判斷 for 還是 of：形容詞是在評價「事情本身」(important, essential, necessary) 用 for + 人；形容詞是在評價「這個人的特質」(kind, thoughtful, careless, rude) 用 of + 人。

### B. 當受詞：與「假受詞 it」搭配的固定句型
某些動詞（find, make, consider, think）接受詞 + 受詞補語時，如果受詞是不定詞片語（過長），必須用假受詞 it 先佔位，真正的受詞（不定詞片語）移到句尾。
*   ~~The committee found to schedule the review difficult.~~（錯誤，不定詞片語太長不可直接當受詞）
*   The committee found **it** difficult **to schedule** the review before the fiscal year ends. (正確)
*   Management considers **it** essential **to notify** all stakeholders in advance.

### C. 當主詞補語：說明主詞的本質或目標
*   Our top priority **is to secure** the client's approval before month-end.
*   The purpose of this meeting **is to clarify** the new reimbursement policy.

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **名詞 + to V 的介係詞判斷**：先確認不定詞原本是不是「介係詞動詞」（rely on, deal with, work with），如果是，修飾名詞時介係詞不能消失，要留在動詞後面。
2.  **to be p.p. 陷阱**：句中名詞若是「尚待處理」的事物（合約、文件、議程項目），且該名詞不會主動做這個動作，選項要選被動 **to be p.p.**，而非主動 to V。
3.  **in order to vs. so that**：`in order to + V` 後面接原形動詞；`so that + S + V` 後面要接完整子句（常搭配 can/may/will）。兩者不可混用文法結構。
4.  **so as not to 的位置**：否定的目的用 so as not to / in order not to，not 一律放在 to 前面，不可放在句首單獨修飾全句。
5.  **only to V 的語感**：只要句子前段描述「努力/前往/嘗試」，後段接 only to V 且語意是「事與願違」，幾乎可以直接秒選。
6.  **such...as to 與 so...that 的轉換**：`such + N + as to V` 可以改寫成 `so + adj + that + S + V`，考題常要求判斷兩者哪一個文法正確銜接。
7.  **情緒形容詞 + to V vs. 情緒動詞 + Ving**：be pleased/surprised/disappointed **to V** 表示「原因」；而動名詞子句（Ving）通常是描述持續的狀態，不表原因，選項出現情緒形容詞空格後方，優先考慮 to V。
8.  **假主詞 it + for/of 判斷**：看到 It is + 形容詞 + ___ + to V 的句型，先判斷形容詞評價的是「事」還是「人」，決定用 for 還是 of。
9.  **假受詞 it 三步驟**：動詞（find/make/consider/think）+ it + 形容詞/名詞 + 真正受詞（to V 片語）——凡看到「find/make/consider + it + adj」的固定框架，空格處理原則是先確認 it 是否已出現，若已出現則後面直接接 to V。
10. **不定詞當補語 vs. 動名詞當補語**：`S + be + to V` 常表達「未來要做的目標/計畫」（our goal is to expand），而 `S + be + Ving` 則描述「正在進行的事」（the issue is delaying the launch），語意不同不可互換。
11. **雙不定詞片語並列**：句中若同時出現兩個不定詞片語（一個表目的、一個作受詞），先分別判斷各自的功能，不要因為都是 to V 就誤以為是對等連接需要 and。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

### 動詞（常見於「be + p.p. + to V」被動修飾名詞句型）
*   **finalize** (v. 及物) 敲定 | The team needs one more day **to finalize** the budget proposal.
*   **notify** (v. 及物) 通知 | It is essential for the supervisor **to notify** all staff of the change.
*   **secure** (v. 及物) 確保、獲得 | Our goal is **to secure** a long-term contract with the supplier.
*   **escalate** (v. 及物) 上呈、升級 | Any unresolved complaint should be **escalated to** the regional director.
*   **stagger** (v. 及物) 錯開安排 | Managers decided **to stagger** shifts in order to reduce congestion in the break room.
*   **outsource** (v. 及物) 外包 | The firm plans **to outsource** its data entry tasks next quarter.
*   **streamline** (v. 及物) 簡化流程 | The new software was designed **to streamline** the approval process.
*   **withhold** (v. 及物) 保留、扣留 | The finance office chose **to withhold** payment until the invoice was corrected.

### 形容詞（評價人 vs. 評價事，用於 it is adj of/for sb to V）
*   **thoughtful** 形容詞 體貼的（評價人） | It was **thoughtful of** the director *to acknowledge* every team member.
*   **considerate** 形容詞 體諒的（評價人） | It was **considerate of** her *to reschedule* the call for overseas clients.
*   **wise** 形容詞 明智的（評價事或人皆可） | It would be **wise for** the firm *to diversify* its suppliers.
*   **prudent** 形容詞 審慎的 | It is **prudent for** managers *to review* every contract clause carefully.
*   **feasible** 形容詞 可行的（評價事） | It is not **feasible for** the team *to finish* the audit by Friday.
*   **vital** 形容詞 至關重要的（評價事） | It is **vital for** every employee *to understand* the safety procedures.
*   **inconsiderate** 形容詞 不體貼的（評價人） | It was **inconsiderate of** him *to cancel* the meeting without notice.

### 情緒形容詞（+ to V 表原因）
*   **relieved** 形容詞 放心的 | The auditor was **relieved to** *find* no discrepancies in the accounts.
*   **astonished** 形容詞 驚訝的 | Investors were **astonished to** *hear* about the sudden merger.
*   **reluctant** 形容詞 不情願的 | The supplier was **reluctant to** *lower* its prices further.
*   **content** 形容詞 滿足的 | The client seemed **content to** *wait* for the revised proposal.
*   **anxious** 形容詞 焦慮／渴望的 | The intern was **anxious to** *make* a good impression during her first week.

### 片語與句型
*   **only to V** 片語 結果卻（表意外結果） | The courier arrived early, **only to find** the office still closed.
*   **such...as to V** 句型 如此…以致於 | The backlog was **such** a burden **as to** *delay* every downstream department.
*   **so as not to V** 片語 為了不…（否定目的） | The clerk verified the total twice **so as not to** *overcharge* the customer.
*   **in order not to V** 片語 為了不…（正式） | The firm postponed the launch **in order not to** *clash* with a competitor's event.
*   **be reluctant to V** 片語 不情願做… | The vendor was **reluctant to** extend the payment deadline.
*   **be entitled to V** 片語 有權做… | Full-time staff **are entitled to** *request* additional leave.
*   **it is up to sb to V** 片語 由某人決定要做… | **It is up to** the finance director **to** *approve* the extra spending.

## 💪 練習題（15 題）

**1.** She needs a mentor experienced enough ______ during the transition period.
(A) advise (B) advising (C) to advise (D) advised

**2.** There are still several agreements ______ before the merger can be finalized next month.
(A) signing (B) to sign (C) to be signed (D) signed

**3.** The logistics team stayed late ______ the shipment error before the client noticed.
(A) correct (B) correcting (C) to correct (D) corrected

**4.** ______ the board's approval, the finance director quietly revised the projected budget figures.
(A) So as not to delay (B) So that not delay (C) In order to not delay (D) Not in order to delay

**5.** The courier rushed across town to deliver the package, ______ that the office had already closed for the holiday.
(A) only finding (B) only to find (C) only found (D) only find

**6.** It was rather ______ of the supervisor to reassign the workload without consulting anyone.
(A) careless (B) careful (C) necessary (D) essential

**7.**（雙格題）The delay was ______ a significant obstacle ______ several vendors to miss their contractual deadlines.
第一空：(A) such (B) so (C) too (D) enough
第二空：(A) as to cause (B) that cause (C) as causing (D) to cause

**8.** The audit committee considers ______ crucial to document every transaction before submitting the report.
(A) that (B) it (C) this (D) them

**9.** Our current objective ______ market share in the southeast region before the next fiscal year begins.
(A) is expanding (B) expands (C) is to expand (D) expand

**10.** The new intern was genuinely ______ to receive positive feedback after only two weeks on the job.
(A) surprising (B) surprise (C) surprised (D) to surprise

**11.** This drafting software is not an easy program ______ without prior training.
(A) work with (B) to work with (C) working with (D) to be worked

**12.** ______ for the procurement office to verify every vendor's certification before signing a contract.
(A) It is standard practice (B) That is standard practice (C) There is standard practice (D) This standard practice is

**13.** The two departments merged their teams ______ reduce redundant administrative tasks.
(A) for (B) so that (C) in order to (D) so as

**14.** Management found ______ necessary to reassign three analysts to the compliance project.
(A) that (B) it (C) so (D) this

**15.**（雙格題）The pilot results were ______ impressive ______ the regional director approve a company-wide rollout immediately.
第一空：(A) so (B) such (C) too (D) enough
第二空：(A) as to (B) that (C) to (D) as causing
