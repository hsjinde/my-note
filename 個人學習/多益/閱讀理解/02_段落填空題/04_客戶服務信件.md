# 04_客戶服務信件

## 📝 答題策略 🌟
1. **先辨識信件的目的**：是回覆客訴、通知售後服務,還是說明保固政策，掌握目的後再判斷空格的語氣與時態。
2. **詞彙題看「情境搭配」**：客服信件常出現 refund（退款）、replace（更換）、malfunction（故障）等固定搭配詞，答案須符合客服情境而非單純字面相近。
3. **句子插入題找「因果與安撫語氣」**：客訴回覆信常見「致歉→說明處理方式→提供補償」的順序，插入句須銜接這個邏輯鏈。
4. **語態與時態一致**：留意動作的執行者是「客戶」還是「公司/產品」，並確認與前後句時態（現在完成式、未來式）是否呼應。
5. **關係代名詞與連接詞題**：判斷空格後方是完整子句還是名詞片語，並依語意選出合適的關係代名詞或轉折詞。

## 短文一：客戶服務回覆信

Dear Ms. Patterson,

Thank you for contacting our customer service team regarding the blender you purchased last month. We are sorry to hear that the appliance ______(1) stopped working properly after only a few uses. Based on the description you provided, our technicians believe the motor ______(2). ______(3). In the meantime, we would like to send you a replacement unit at no additional cost, along with a prepaid return label for the defective item. Once we receive the faulty blender, our quality team will inspect it to determine ______(4) caused the malfunction, so that we can prevent similar issues in future production batches.

### 題目 1
(A) has
(B) have
(C) had
(D) having

### 題目 2
(A) is defective
(B) defective
(C) defect
(D) defects

### 題目 3
(A) We are pleased that the product met your expectations.
(B) A certified repair technician will contact you within two business days to arrange a free inspection.
(C) The blender was designed by our award-winning product team.
(D) Please remember to register your product online for warranty coverage.

### 題目 4
(A) what
(B) that
(C) which
(D) whom

## 短文二：售後服務通知

Dear Valued Customer,

We are writing to inform you that, starting next month, all products purchased from GreenHome Appliances ______(5) an extended two-year warranty instead of the standard one-year coverage. This change applies automatically to any item bought after the effective date listed above. ______(6). If your appliance requires servicing, simply present your original receipt at any authorized service center nationwide. We understand that a malfunctioning appliance can disrupt your daily routine, ______(7) we have also introduced a 24-hour hotline for urgent repair requests. Should you have any questions about ______(8) new policy, our support team is ready to assist you at any time.

### 題目 5
(A) will include
(B) included
(C) including
(D) has included

### 題目 6
(A) The warranty extension does not apply to products purchased before this date.
(B) Our showroom will be closed for renovations next week.
(C) We recently launched a new line of kitchen appliances.
(D) Customers are encouraged to leave reviews on our website.

### 題目 7
(A) so
(B) but
(C) because
(D) although

### 題目 8
(A) our
(B) ours
(C) us
(D) we
