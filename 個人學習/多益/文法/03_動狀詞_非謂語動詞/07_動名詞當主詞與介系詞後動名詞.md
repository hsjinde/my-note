# 07_動名詞當主詞與介系詞後動名詞

## 核心概念
本章聚焦兩個多益高頻但常被忽略的細節：① 動名詞作「句子主詞」時的進階陷阱（含完成式、被動式、否定式動名詞），② 介系詞後面的**進階固定搭配片語**（不只是 look forward to，還有 in addition to、when it comes to 等），這些片語中的 to／for／of 都是介系詞，後面一律接 V-ing。

## 1. 動名詞作主詞的進階型態
動名詞當主詞時視為單數，但它本身仍可帶有時態與語態的變化：

*   **一般式**：*Filing* the expense report *is* Ms. Grant's responsibility every month.
*   **完成式（having + p.p.）**：表示動名詞的動作發生在主要動詞之前。
    *   *Having reviewed* the contract twice *does* not guarantee that every clause is error-free.
*   **被動式（being + p.p.）**：強調主詞是動作的承受者。
    *   *Being promoted* ahead of schedule *surprised* the entire sales team.
*   **否定式（not + V-ing）**：not 放在動名詞前面，不是放在助動詞後面。
    *   *Not submitting* the form on time *resulted* in a delayed reimbursement.

## 2. 主詞位置的陷阱：動名詞 vs 不定詞 vs 名詞子句
句首空格若考的是「當主詞」，選項通常混雜原形動詞、不定詞、動名詞、名詞。多益偏好動名詞當句子主詞，尤其當空格後直接接介系詞片語或受詞而非 be 動詞時：
*   *Coordinating* schedules across three time zones *proved* to be the biggest challenge.
*   （✗ To coordinate 在此類直述句主詞位置較少見；✗ Coordinate 不能當主詞）

## 3. 介系詞後必接 V-ing 的進階片語 🌟
以下片語結尾的 to／of／for／in／at 都是**介系詞**，不是不定詞的 to，後面一律接動名詞：

*   **in addition to + V-ing**（除了...之外）
    *   *In addition to* *managing* the budget, she also trains new hires.
*   **when it comes to + V-ing**（說到...）
    *   *When it comes to* *negotiating* contracts, Mr. Diaz has no equal.
*   **with a view to + V-ing**（以...為目的）
    *   The firm restructured its logistics *with a view to* *cutting* delivery time.
*   **apart from / aside from + V-ing**（除了...）
    *   *Apart from* *approving* invoices, the controller also audits expense claims.
*   **in favor of + V-ing**（贊成...）
    *   The board voted *in favor of* *outsourcing* the customer service line.
*   **on / upon + V-ing**（一...就...）
    *   *Upon* *arriving* at the site, the inspector immediately flagged three violations.
*   **by + V-ing**（藉由...的方式）
    *   The team cut costs *by* *renegotiating* the supplier contract.
*   **in + V-ing**（在...的過程中／因為...）
    *   *In* *drafting* the proposal, the analyst consulted last year's figures.

## 4. cannot help、no use、no point 等特殊句型
*   **cannot help + V-ing**（忍不住...）：help 在此是及物動詞，後接動名詞受詞。
    *   The auditor *could* not *help* *noticing* the discrepancy in the ledger.
*   **It's no use / It's no good + V-ing**（...是沒有用的）
    *   *It's no use* *complaining* about the delay; we need a solution instead.
*   **There's no point in + V-ing**（...沒有意義）
    *   *There's no point in* *rescheduling* the meeting again this week.

## 5. 動名詞的邏輯主詞
動名詞前若出現所有格（或受格），是在強調動作的執行者，不影響動名詞本身仍是句子主詞或介系詞受詞的身分：
*   The board appreciated *the manager's* *volunteering* to lead the audit.

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **句首空格 + 逗號後接動詞 → 選動名詞當主詞**：若空格在句首，後面緊跟受詞或介系詞片語，再接主要動詞，選 V-ing。
2.  **not + V-ing**：否定動名詞的 not 一律緊貼在動名詞前面，選項中 not doing 優於 does not do。
3.  **having + p.p. 表「先於主要動詞發生」**：句子主要動詞是過去式或現在式，動名詞若強調「先前已完成」則用完成式動名詞。
4.  **being + p.p. 表被動動名詞**：若動名詞的邏輯主詞是動作的「被...」對象，一律用 being p.p.，不能只用 p.p.。
5.  **介系詞 + V-ing 是鐵律**：看到 in addition to、when it comes to、with a view to、apart from、in favor of、on/upon、by、in 後面出現空格，直接刪掉原形動詞與不定詞選項。
6.  **when it comes to 的 to 常被誤判為不定詞**：凡是「動詞 + to」的固定片語，若後面接的是名詞/動名詞而非原形動詞，to 就是介系詞。
7.  **cannot help + V-ing vs cannot help but + V**：cannot help 後接動名詞；cannot help but 後面才接原形動詞，兩者不可混用。
8.  **there's no + V-ing**：此句型的 V-ing 是主詞補語兼固定用法，不可替換為 to V。
9.  **by + V-ing 表示方法**：題幹若問「藉由什麼方式達成」，答案空格幾乎必為 V-ing。
10. **upon/on + V-ing 表示時間先後**：常與句意中的「一...就...」（as soon as）互換，用來測驗考生是否認得 on 是介系詞而非副詞。
11. **主詞位置動名詞優先於 to V**：多益在直述句主詞空格的選項設計上，動名詞的正確率通常高於不定詞，除非句意明顯強調「未來目的」。
12. **所有格 + V-ing 不影響句子結構判斷**：所有格只是動名詞的邏輯主詞標記，考生仍應把整個「所有格＋V-ing」視為單一名詞單位來分析句子成分。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

### 動名詞主詞句型常見動詞
*   **file** (v. 及物) 歸檔；提交 | *Filing* the expense report accurately *saves* the accounting team time.
*   **coordinate** (v. 及物) 協調 | *Coordinating* three departments *requires* constant communication.
*   **audit** (v. 及物) 稽核 | *Auditing* every invoice manually *is* no longer practical.
*   **renegotiate** (v. 及物) 重新協商 | *Renegotiating* the lease *helped* the company save 10 percent annually.
*   **flag** (v. 及物) 標記；提出示警 | *Flagging* irregular transactions *is* the compliance officer's daily task.

### 進階介系詞片語（+ V-ing）
*   **in addition to** 片語 除了...之外 | *In addition to* reviewing contracts, the lawyer also drafts new clauses.
*   **when it comes to** 片語 說到... | *When it comes to* pricing strategy, the CFO has final say.
*   **in favor of** 片語 贊成... | Most shareholders voted *in favor of* merging the two divisions.
*   **as for** 片語 至於... | *As for* extending the deadline, the director remains undecided.
*   **get around to** 片語 終於抽空做... | The manager finally *got around to* updating the employee handbook.
*   **look into** 片語 調查；研究 | The committee agreed to *look into* streamlining the approval process.
*   **count on** 片語 依賴；指望 | The department *counts on* receiving the report before Friday.
*   **rely on** 片語 依靠 | The plant *relies on* recycling materials to cut waste.
*   **focus on** 片語 專注於 | The new hire should *focus on* mastering the booking system first.
*   **succeed in** 片語 成功做到... | The negotiator *succeeded in* lowering the shipping fee.
*   **engage in** 片語 從事於 | Employees are prohibited from *engaging in* soliciting clients for personal gain.
*   **specialize in** 片語 專精於 | The firm *specializes in* auditing small manufacturing businesses.
*   **excel at** 片語 擅長於 | The candidate *excels at* resolving customer disputes quickly.
*   **keen on** 片語 熱衷於 | The regional manager is *keen on* expanding into overseas markets.
*   **fond of** 片語 喜愛 | The design lead is *fond of* experimenting with new packaging.
*   **tired of** 片語 厭倦 | Several staff members are *tired of* repeating the same onboarding steps.
*   **proud of** 片語 為...感到自豪 | The director is *proud of* mentoring junior analysts.
*   **devoted to** 片語 致力於 | The nonprofit is *devoted to* improving supply chain transparency.
*   **prone to** 片語 容易發生... | Outdated machines are *prone to* causing production delays.
*   **opposed to** 片語 反對 | Some managers are *opposed to* adopting the new scheduling software.
*   **resort to** 片語 訴諸於 | The vendor had to *resort to* offering discounts to clear inventory.
*   **adapt to** 片語 適應於 | New employees must *adapt to* working under tight deadlines.
*   **participate in** 片語 參與 | All branch managers are expected to *participate in* reviewing the annual budget.
*   **take pride in** 片語 以...為榮 | The workshop *takes pride in* delivering custom orders on time.
*   **cannot help but** 片語 忍不住（後接原形動詞） | The reviewer *could* not *help but* praise the clarity of the report.
*   **far from** 片語 遠非；一點也不 | The onboarding process is *far from* being finished.

## 💪 練習題（15 題）

**1.** *Coordinating* schedules across three time zones ______ the biggest obstacle for the remote team.
(A) is (B) are (C) being (D) to be

**2.** *Not* ______ the deadline resulted in a formal warning from the compliance office.
(A) meet (B) meeting (C) to meet (D) met

**3.** ______ reviewed the supplier's contract twice, the legal team still requested a third check.
(A) Have (B) Having (C) To have (D) Had

**4.** In addition to ______ the quarterly budget, the finance director also oversees payroll approvals.
(A) manage (B) managed (C) managing (D) to manage

**5.** When it comes to ______ prices with overseas suppliers, Mr. Alvi has no equal in the department.
(A) negotiate (B) negotiated (C) to negotiate (D) negotiating

**6.** The plant upgraded its filtration system with a view to ______ emissions before the new regulations take effect.
(A) reduce (B) reducing (C) reduced (D) to reduce

**7.** Apart from ______ customer complaints, the support lead also trains new agents.
(A) handle (B) handled (C) handling (D) to handle

**8.** The shareholders ultimately voted in favor of ______ the two logistics divisions.
(A) merge (B) merging (C) to merge (D) merged

**9.** Upon ______ at the warehouse, the inspector immediately noticed a labeling error.
(A) arrive (B) arrived (C) to arrive (D) arriving

**10.** The auditor could not help ______ the discrepancy between the two spreadsheets.
(A) notice (B) noticing (C) to notice (D) noticed

**11.** It's no use ______ about the shipping delay; the client already knows.
(A) complain (B) complained (C) complaining (D) to complain

**12.** There's no point in ______ the meeting again this week.
(A) reschedule (B) rescheduling (C) rescheduled (D) to reschedule

**13.** ______ promoted ahead of schedule surprised even the most senior members of the sales team.
(A) Be (B) Being (C) To be (D) Been

**14.** The board appreciated the manager's ______ to lead the internal audit without additional compensation. Aside from ______ the audit, she also revised the reporting template used company-wide.
第一空：(A) volunteer (B) volunteered (C) volunteering (D) to volunteer
第二空：(A) lead (B) led (C) leading (D) to lead

**15.** By ______ the supplier contract, the procurement team managed to cut annual costs by twelve percent.
(A) renegotiate (B) renegotiated (C) renegotiating (D) to renegotiate
