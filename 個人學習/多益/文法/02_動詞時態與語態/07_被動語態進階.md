# 07_被動語態進階

## 核心概念
本章延續「03_被動語態」的 `be + p.p.` 基礎，聚焦三個更進階、更容易混淆的被動語態子題：(1) 用 **get** 取代 be 的動態被動語態、(2) **使役動詞被動化的限制**（哪些動詞可以被動化、哪些不行）、(3) **雙受詞授予動詞轉被動時保留受詞** 的完整規則。這三塊是多益中高分考生最容易在「看起來都是被動」的選項中選錯的地方。

### 1. Get + p.p.：動態被動語態
除了 `be + p.p.`，英文還有另一種被動語態：**`get + p.p.`**。它和 be 被動語意相同，但**強調狀態「從無到有」的變化過程**，語感較為生動、口語，也常見於商業書信中描述突發或意外的轉變。
*   She **got promoted** to senior manager last spring. (她升遷了 — 強調「發生了升遷這件事」)
*   The shipment **got delayed** because of the customs inspection. (貨物被延誤了)

**最重要的陷阱**：get 被動句中，get 本身就已經帶有被動語意，**前面絕對不可以再加 be 動詞**。
*   ❌ The printer **was got** fixed.
*   ✅ The printer finally **got** fixed.

**與使役動詞 get 的區別**：get 除了可以作被動助動詞（後面不接受詞），也可以作使役動詞（`get + 受詞 + p.p.`，後面一定接受詞），兩者意思和結構完全不同：
*   被動語態（主詞自己被完成動作，後面沒有受詞）：The printer **got fixed**. (印表機被修好了)
*   使役語態（主詞安排別人完成動作，後面有受詞）：The technician **got** *the printer* **fixed**. (技術人員把印表機修好了)

### 2. 使役動詞被動化的限制
「03_被動語態」第 3 節已提過 make/let/have + O + 原形V 被動化時要補回 to，但事實上**不是每個使役動詞都能這樣被動化**：

| 動詞 | 主動句型 | 語意 | 能否被動化 | 被動時的正確講法 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **make** | make + O + V原形 | 強迫、促使 | ✅ 可以 | **be made to V** |
| **help** | help + O + V原形/to V | 幫忙 | ✅ 可以 | **be helped to V** |
| **have** | have + O + V原形 | 交代、吩咐 | ⚠️ 幾乎不用此結構被動化 | 改用 **be asked to V / be required to V** |
| **let** | let + O + V原形 | 允許、讓 | ⚠️ 幾乎不用此結構被動化 | 改用 **be allowed to V / be permitted to V** |

*   ✅ The intern **was made to** apologize in person. (make 可被動化)
*   ❌ Staff members **were let to** leave early. → ✅ Staff members **were allowed to** leave early.
*   ❌ The assistant **was had to** submit the report early. → ✅ The assistant **was asked to** submit the report early.

### 3. 雙受詞授予動詞轉被動：保留受詞的規則
「03_被動語態」第 4 節只點出 offer/give 這類動詞被動時「後面還會剩一個受詞」，這裡把授予動詞依可否保留裸受詞分成兩型，這是多益篇章題常見的誘答陷阱：

**Type A（真雙受詞動詞）**：主動句可以直接說「動詞 + 人 + 物」，不需要任何介系詞。這類動詞被動化時，**人和物都可以當主詞**。
*   常見動詞：give, offer, send, tell, show, teach, award, grant, assign, pay, promise, guarantee, hand, lend, sell, owe
*   主動：The board **awarded** *the employee* *a certificate*.
*   被動（人當主詞，物直接保留）：The employee **was awarded** a certificate.
*   被動（物當主詞，補回 to）：A certificate **was awarded** to the employee.

**Type B（須接介系詞的動詞）**：主動句中的「人」一定要搭配 to/for 才能出現，不能直接放在動詞後面（不能說 explain someone something）。這類動詞被動化時，**只能用「物」當主詞**，人絕對不能當被動主詞。
*   常見動詞：explain, describe, suggest, announce, recommend, introduce, propose, admit, mention, report, demonstrate
*   主動：The auditor **explained** the discrepancy *to the board*.
*   ✅ 被動：The discrepancy **was explained** to the board.
*   ❌ 被動：The board **was explained** the discrepancy. (explain 沒有這種用法)

**Type C（provide 類動詞，人當主詞須加 with）**：provide, supply, present, equip, entrust 這類動詞主動句常寫成「動詞 + 人 + with + 物」，被動化時若以人當主詞，**須保留 with**，不能直接留裸受詞。
*   主動：The company will **provide** *new employees* *with a laptop*.
*   ✅ 被動：New employees **are provided with** a laptop.
*   ❌ 被動：New employees **are provided** a laptop. (漏掉 with)

## 📝 多益秒殺解題技巧
1.  看到空格前後有 **be** 且選項出現 get 相關字：先判斷後面有沒有受詞——沒有受詞是「get 被動」，有受詞是「get 使役」。
2.  絕對不要選 **was/were got + p.p.**，這是雙重被動的錯誤結構，凡是這種選項直接刪除。
3.  遇到 have/let 的被動句選項（was had to / was let to），這兩者幾乎不存在，優先考慮同義替代詞 be asked to / be allowed to。
4.  遇到「授予類動詞 + 受詞」的被動句，先問自己：這個動詞主動時可不可以直接說「動詞 + 人 + 物」而不加介系詞？可以 → Type A（人可當主詞，物直接保留）；不行、一定要 to/for → Type B（只能物當主詞，保留 to/for）。
5.  看到 provide/supply/present/equip/entrust 被動句，人當主詞時記得檢查後面是否漏掉 with。

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **get 被動＝be 被動的動態版**：語意相同，但 get 強調狀態改變的過程，較口語生動，常搭配 finally, eventually, suddenly, gradually 等副詞。
2.  **get 被動不可疊加 be 動詞**：get 本身已內含被動語感，「was/were got + p.p.」是雙重被動的錯誤，凡看到直接刪除。
3.  **get 被動 vs get 使役的關鍵在受詞**：`get + p.p.`（無受詞）＝被動，主詞自己被完成動作；`get + 受詞 + p.p.`（有受詞）＝使役，主詞安排別人完成動作。
4.  **get 的過去分詞**：美式英文中常用 **gotten**（如 had gotten approved），尤其在現在完成式、過去完成式中；英式英文則多用 got，兩者多益皆可能出現。
5.  **助動詞 + get + p.p.**：will get approved、might get delayed、should get fixed，助動詞後一律接原形 get，不可加 to 或 -ing，和「助動詞 + be + p.p.」規則相同。
6.  **make/help 可被動化**：被動時動詞一律**補回 to**（was made to V、was helped to V），這點與一般 be 被動不同，是最常考的細節。
7.  **have/let 通常不用此結構被動化**：多益不會出現「was had to V」或「was let to V」，遇到這種選項可直接視為錯誤誘答，改選 be asked to / be required to（替代have）或 be allowed to / be permitted to（替代let）。
8.  **Type A 授予動詞判斷法**：能自然說出「動詞 + 人 + 物」（give him the report）就是 Type A，人和物都可以當被動主詞。
9.  **Type B 動詞判斷法**：一定要用 to/for 才能接人（explain it to him），這類動詞被動時**人絕對不能當主詞**，只能物當主詞並保留 to/for。
10. **Type C 動詞需保留 with**：provide, supply, present, equip, entrust 被動化時，人當主詞須搭配固定介系詞 with，物當主詞則搭配 to（present）或直接接受詞（supply/provide + 物 + to + 人也可）。
11. **同一動詞可能橫跨考點**：award、grant 等詞除了是 Type A 授予動詞，也常出現在一般 be 被動句（was awarded、was granted），須依句子結構判斷後面是否還有受詞。
12. **篇章題常混用多種被動**：同一封 email 或備忘錄中，get 被動、Type A/B 授予動詞被動、一般 be 被動經常一起出現，須逐句依動詞特性個別判斷，不能套用單一公式。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

### get + p.p. 常見動態被動搭配
*   **get promoted** phr. 獲得升遷 | She **got promoted** to senior manager within two years.
*   **get rejected** phr. 被拒絕 | His loan application **got rejected** due to insufficient credit history.
*   **get injured** phr. 受傷 | A warehouse worker **got injured** while operating the forklift.
*   **get delayed** phr. 被延誤 | The shipment **got delayed** because of the customs inspection.
*   **get approved** phr. 獲得核准 | The renovation budget **got approved** after several revisions.

### 支援 get 被動的核心動詞
*   **approve** v. 核准 | The finance director must **approve** all purchases over $5,000.
*   **reject** v. 拒絕 | The committee **rejected** the merger proposal unanimously.
*   **promote** v. 升遷、晉升 | The company plans to **promote** two supervisors this quarter.
*   **terminate** v. 終止（僱用關係） | The firm decided to **terminate** the underperforming contractor.
*   **injure** v. 使受傷 | The faulty equipment **injured** several factory workers last year.
*   **select** v. 挑選、選出 | The panel will **select** the winning proposal next week.

### 雙受詞授予動詞（Type A，保留受詞被動）
*   **assign** v. 指派 | The manager will **assign** each team member a specific task.
*   **award** v. 頒發、授予 | The board decided to **award** the employee a certificate of excellence.
*   **promise** v. 承諾 | The supplier **promised** the client a full refund.
*   **guarantee** v. 保證 | The warranty **guarantees** customers a free repair within one year.
*   **hand over** phr. 移交、交出 | The outgoing director **handed over** his responsibilities to the new hire.
*   **lend** v. 借出 | The bank **lent** the startup a significant amount of capital.
*   **owe** v. 欠、歸功於 | The company **owes** its early success to a small but loyal customer base.

### 須接介系詞、不可省略 to/for 的動詞（Type B）
*   **explain** v. 解釋 | The auditor will **explain** the discrepancy to the board.
*   **describe** v. 描述 | The witness **described** the incident to the investigators in detail.
*   **introduce** v. 介紹 | The host will **introduce** the keynote speaker to the audience.
*   **propose** v. 提議 | The consultant **proposed** a new pricing model to management.
*   **admit** v. 承認 | The manager **admitted** the mistake to the entire department.
*   **mention** v. 提及 | She **mentioned** the delay to her supervisor during the meeting.
*   **demonstrate** v. 示範、證明 | The engineer will **demonstrate** the new feature to potential investors.

### provide 型動詞（Type C，需搭配 with）與其他相關動詞
*   **provide** v. 提供 | The company will **provide** new employees with a laptop.
*   **supply** v. 供應 | The vendor **supplies** the factory with raw materials every month.
*   **present** (v. 及物) 呈現、贈送 | The chairman will **present** the retiring staff with a farewell gift.
*   **deny** v. 拒絕給予、否認 | The landlord **denied** the tenant permission to sublet the unit.
*   **entrust** v. 委託、託付 | The CEO **entrusted** the project to her most experienced manager.

**1.** The technician finally ______ the printer fixed yesterday after three failed attempts.
(A) was got (B) got (C) has gotten (D) getting

**2.** The printer in the sales office finally ______ fixed after being reported broken for two weeks.
(A) was got (B) has got (C) got to (D) got

**3.** Shipments bound for the coastal warehouse often ______ delayed during typhoon season.
(A) get (B) gets (C) is getting (D) got

**4.** If the proposal is not revised by Friday, it will likely ______ rejected by the board.
(A) getting (B) gets (C) get (D) got

**5.** By the time the crisis management team intervened, the factory's safety record had already ______ badly damaged in the eyes of regulators.
(A) get (B) gotten (C) got to (D) getting

**6.** After repeated complaints from a client, the intern ______ apologize in person by the marketing director.
(A) was made to (B) was made (C) made to (D) was making

**7.** Because of the storm warning, staff members ______ to leave the office two hours early yesterday.
(A) were let (B) let (C) were allowed (D) were letting

**8.** The assistant ______ to submit the expense report a day earlier than usual after the schedule changed.
(A) was had (B) had (C) was having (D) was asked

**9.** The new hires ______ to navigate the internal software system during their first week of onboarding.
(A) helped (B) were helped (C) were helping (D) had help

**10.** Even though the supervisor ______(1)______ the two clerks reorganize the filing cabinets, neither of them was formally ______(2)______ to do so under their job description.
第一空：(A) was made (B) making (C) made (D) to make
第二空：(A) required (B) require (C) requiring (D) requires

**11.** The top salesperson ______ a bonus trip to Hawaii for exceeding the quarterly target.
(A) awarded (B) was awarding (C) awards (D) was awarded

**12.** A generous severance package ______ to every employee who was laid off during the restructuring.
(A) offered (B) was offered (C) was offering (D) offers

**13.** The new expense policy ______ to all department heads during Monday's meeting.
(A) was explained (B) explained (C) was explaining (D) explains

**14.** All new hires ______ with a laptop and a company handbook on their first day.
(A) provided (B) are providing (C) are provided (D) provide
