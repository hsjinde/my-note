# 13_不可數名詞陷阱進階

## 核心概念
多益單字題和文法題中，有一群「集合性不可數名詞」專門用來考驗考生：它們在中文裡感覺是「可以算的東西」（一件設備、一件行李、一件家具），但在英文文法上卻**永遠不可加 -s，也永遠不可加 a/an**。本章專門整理這批陷阱字，並對照它們各自的「可數替身」，讓你在考場上看到選項就能秒判。

## 1. 集合不可數名詞 vs. 可數替身 🌟
這些名詞表示「一整類東西的集合」，本身不可數；如果句子明確要講「一件、一項」，必須換成對應的可數名詞。

| 不可數集合名詞（❌ 不可加 -s / a-an） | 中文 | 對應可數替身（✅ 可加 -s / a-an） |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **equipment** | 設備 | a piece of equipment / a device / a machine |
| **machinery** | 機械 | a machine |
| **furniture** | 家具 | a piece of furniture / a chair, a desk |
| **staff / personnel** | 員工（集合） | a staff member / an employee / a worker |
| **merchandise** | 商品 | an item / a product |
| **luggage / baggage** | 行李 | a piece of luggage / a bag, a suitcase |
| **stationery** | 文具 | a pen, a notebook |
| **jewelry** | 珠寶 | a piece of jewelry / a ring |
| **cash** | 現金 | a bill, a coin |
| **cutlery** | 餐具 | a fork, a knife |
| **clothing** | 服裝（集合） | a garment, an item of clothing |
| **traffic** | 交通（車流） | a vehicle |
| **artwork** | 藝術品（集合） | a work of art |
| **homework** | 作業 | an assignment |
| **correspondence** | 往來信件 | a letter, an e-mail |
| **feedback** | 意見回饋 | a comment, a suggestion |
| **garbage / trash** | 垃圾 | an item of garbage |
| **freight / cargo** | 貨運、貨物 | a shipment |
| **produce**（n. 農產品） | 農產品 | a fruit, a vegetable |
| **software / hardware** | 軟體／硬體 | a program / a device |
| **scenery** | 風景 | a view |
| **footwear** | 鞋類（集合） | a pair of shoes |

**判斷口訣**：只要句子想表達「一項/一件」，看到選項是這些不可數名詞加了 `-s` 或前面配了 `a/an`，直接刪掉；正確答案通常是「a piece of + 不可數名詞」或直接換成右欄的可數替身。

## 2. 動詞單複數呼應陷阱 🌟
不可數集合名詞當主詞時，動詞一律用**單數**；就算中文語感是「員工們」「機械們」，動詞也絕不能用複數。

*   **The equipment ___ (is / are) inspected every month.** → 選 **is**（equipment 不可數，恆單數）。
*   **The company's staff ___ (was / were) informed of the change.** → 英式用法常視為複數概念（集合視為多人），但美式多益考題傾向以**單數字面**判斷，優先看題幹給的動詞線索；重點是 `staff` 前不可加 `a`，也不可寫成 `staffs`。
*   **All the merchandise ___ (has / have) been shipped.** → 選 **has**。

## 3. 常見錯誤選項型態，一眼識破 🚫
1.  `equipments`、`furnitures`、`staffs`、`luggages`、`baggages`、`machineries` —— 這些**加了 -s 的形式在正式多益文法中一律視為錯誤**，看到直接刪。
2.  `an equipment`、`a furniture`、`a luggage` —— 不可數名詞前面配 `a/an` 同樣是陷阱，除非搭配 `a piece of`。
3.  `many equipment`、`many furniture` —— `many` 只能接可數複數，遇到不可數集合名詞要改用 `much` 或 `a great deal of`。

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **a piece of 萬用公式**：equipment、furniture、luggage、advice、information、news 前面想表達「一件」時，一律用 `a piece of N`，而不是加 `a/an` 或 `-s`。
2.  **staff/personnel 恆單數形**：`staff`、`personnel` 本身已是集合名詞，前面不可再加 `a`，字尾也不可加 `-s`；要講「一位員工」用 `a staff member` 或 `a member of staff`。
3.  **merchandise 只有單數形**：`merchandise` 沒有複數形，句中若要表達多樣商品，用 `various merchandise` 而非 `merchandises`。
4.  **luggage/baggage 二選一皆不可數**：兩字意思幾乎相同，皆不可加 -s；「一件行李」固定講 `a piece of luggage/baggage`，不能說 `a luggage`。
5.  **software/hardware 的可數陷阱**：`software` 不可數，但「一套軟體」可用 `a piece of software` 或 `a software program`；`hardware` 同理。
6.  **traffic 恆不可數**：`traffic` 指車流整體，不可加 -s；「一輛車」要用 `a vehicle` 或 `a car`。
7.  **cash 不可數但 money 亦同**：`cash` 前不可加 `a`，「一張鈔票」用 `a bill`，「一枚硬幣」用 `a coin`。
8.  **feedback / correspondence 恆單數**：兩字都是集合抽象概念，前面配 `much` 而非 `many`，不可加 -s。
9.  **clothing 集合 vs. clothes 複數**：`clothing` 不可數（單數動詞），`clothes` 恆複數（複數動詞）——注意兩者動詞呼應不同，是選項中常見的混淆陷阱。
10. **数量詞判斷不可數集合名詞的三步驟**：①看到空格後面是這批集合字 → ②刪掉字尾有 -s 的選項 → ③刪掉前面配 a/an 的選項 → ④剩下的原形或「a piece of + 名詞」通常就是答案。
11. **jewelry 與 jewel 的差異**：`jewelry`（不可數，泛指珠寶飾品）vs. `jewel`（可數，指單顆寶石）；考題常混用兩者字尾。
12. **produce（農產品）與 product（產品）發音／詞性易混**：`produce` 作不可數名詞時重音在前（唸法同動詞不同），指未加工的農產品整體；`product` 則是一般可數名詞，指單一產品。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語
### 集合不可數名詞
*   **machinery** 名詞 機械（集合、不可數） | The factory replaced all its outdated *machinery* last quarter.
*   **merchandise** 名詞 商品（集合、不可數） | The warehouse stores seasonal *merchandise* before it reaches retail stores.
*   **luggage** 名詞 行李（不可數） | Passengers are asked to label their *luggage* before boarding.
*   **baggage** 名詞 行李（不可數，與 luggage 同義） | The airline charges extra for overweight *baggage*.
*   **stationery** 名詞 文具（集合、不可數） | The office manager ordered new *stationery* for the entire floor.
*   **jewelry** 名詞 珠寶（集合、不可數） | The store displays its finest *jewelry* in a locked case.
*   **currency** 名詞 貨幣（不可數，指某國通貨） | The invoice must specify the *currency* used for payment.
*   **cutlery** 名詞 餐具（集合、不可數） | The restaurant ordered new *cutlery* for its renovated dining hall.
*   **clothing** 名詞 服裝（集合、不可數） | The factory produces protective *clothing* for warehouse workers.
*   **cash** 名詞 現金（不可數） | Customers may pay in *cash* or by credit card.
*   **traffic** 名詞 交通、車流（不可數） | Heavy *traffic* delayed the delivery truck by two hours.
*   **artwork** 名詞 藝術品（集合、不可數） | The gallery insured all the *artwork* before the exhibition opened.
*   **homework** 名詞 作業、功課（不可數） | The training manual assigns *homework* for new hires to complete at home.
*   **correspondence** 名詞 往來信件（不可數） | All official *correspondence* must be archived for at least five years.
*   **feedback** 名詞 意見回饋（不可數） | The manager reviewed the customer *feedback* before revising the policy.
*   **garbage** 名詞 垃圾（不可數） | The cleaning crew removes *garbage* from the office every evening.
*   **freight** 名詞 貨運、貨物（不可數） | The company ships its *freight* by rail to reduce costs.
*   **cargo** 名詞 貨物（不可數，多指船運/空運） | The vessel carried industrial *cargo* across the Pacific.
*   **produce**（n.） 名詞 農產品（不可數） | The supplier delivers fresh *produce* to local restaurants twice a week.
*   **software** 名詞 軟體（不可數） | The IT department installed new *software* on every workstation.
*   **hardware** 名詞 硬體（不可數） | The technician upgraded the *hardware* in the server room.
*   **scenery** 名詞 風景（不可數） | Visitors praised the *scenery* surrounding the new resort.
*   **footwear** 名詞 鞋類（集合、不可數） | The retailer expanded its *footwear* section this year.
### 可數替身與相關詞
*   **staff** 名詞 員工（集合，恆單數形） | The entire *staff* attended the mandatory safety briefing.
*   **item** 名詞 一件物品（可數） | Each *item* on the invoice must match the shipping list.
*   **device** 名詞 裝置（可數） | The clinic purchased a new diagnostic *device* last month.
*   **product** 名詞 產品（可數） | The company launched a new *product* in the Asian market.
*   **garment** 名詞 一件衣物（可數） | Every *garment* must pass quality inspection before shipment.

## 💪 練習題（15 題）

**1.** The maintenance crew inspects all the factory ______ every Friday afternoon.
(A) equipments (B) equipping (C) an equipment (D) equipment

**2.** Before the client's visit, the office manager rearranged several pieces of ______ in the conference room.
(A) furniture (B) furnitures (C) a furniture (D) furnished

**3.** The logistics team confirmed that ______ for the trade show had already left the warehouse.
(A) a merchandise (B) merchandises (C) the merchandise (D) merchandising

**4.** Passengers traveling with more than one piece of ______ will be charged an additional fee.
(A) luggages (B) a luggage (C) luggage (D) luggaging

**5.** According to the survey, the entire ______ was satisfied with the newly renovated break room.
(A) staff (B) staffs (C) a staff (D) staffing

**6.** The IT department is replacing outdated ______ on every computer in the accounting office.
(A) softwares (B) a software (C) softwaring (D) software

**7.** The purchasing department ordered new ______ for the reception desk, including pens and notepads.
(A) stationery (B) stationeries (C) a stationery (D) stationed

**8.** Customers who prefer to pay in ______ should visit the counter instead of using the online checkout.
(A) a cash (B) cashes (C) cash (D) cashing

**9.** The delivery was delayed because heavy ______ blocked the main route into the city.（第一空）As a result, several ______ arrived at the warehouse nearly an hour behind schedule.（第二空）
第一空：(A) traffics (B) a traffic (C) traffic (D) trafficking
第二空：(A) vehicle (B) vehicles (C) a vehicle (D) vehicling

**10.** The gallery's insurance policy covers all the ______ displayed during the annual exhibition.
(A) artwork (B) artworks (C) an artwork (D) artworked

**11.** Employees are encouraged to submit ______ about the new scheduling system through the online form.
(A) a feedback (B) feedbacks (C) feedback (D) feeding back

**12.** The shipping company confirmed that the ______ would arrive at the port by Thursday morning.
(A) cargoes (B) a cargo (C) carrying (D) cargo

**13.** All official ______ regarding the merger must be forwarded to the legal department immediately.
(A) correspondences (B) correspondence (C) a correspondence (D) corresponding

**14.** The supplier guarantees that its fresh ______ will be delivered to every branch within twenty-four hours.（第一空）Restaurant managers are asked to inspect each ______ carefully upon arrival for signs of spoilage.（第二空）
第一空：(A) produce (B) produces (C) a produce (D) producing
第二空：(A) items (B) item (C) an items (D) itemize

**15.** The manufacturer replaced most of its aging ______ to increase the plant's overall output.
(A) machineries (B) a machinery (C) machinery (D) mechanized
