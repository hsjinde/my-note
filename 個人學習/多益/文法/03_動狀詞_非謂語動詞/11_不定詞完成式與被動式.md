# 11_不定詞完成式與被動式

## 核心概念

不定詞（to V）除了單純的「to V」原形之外，還有時態與語態的變化，用來精確表達動作發生的**先後順序**與**主被動關係**。多益閱讀與句子填空經常利用這兩種變化來測試考生是否真正理解「不定詞所指的動作，相對於主要動詞是先發生、還是被動承受」。

不定詞總共有四種主要型態：

| 型態 | 結構 | 意義 |
|---|---|---|
| 簡單主動式 | to V | 動作與主要動詞同時或在其之後發生 |
| 簡單被動式 | to be p.p. | 動作被動發生，與主要動詞同時或之後 |
| 完成主動式 | to have p.p. | 動作發生在主要動詞**之前** |
| 完成被動式 | to have been p.p. | 動作被動發生，且發生在主要動詞**之前** |

## 一、不定詞的被動式：to be p.p.

當不定詞的邏輯主詞（句子的主詞）是動作的**承受者**而非**執行者**時，必須使用被動不定詞 to be p.p.。

*   The proposal needs **to be approved** by the finance committee before Friday.（提案需要「被」核准）
*   Employees expect **to be informed** of any schedule changes in advance.

判斷技巧：把不定詞後面的受詞位置補回主詞，看主詞是「做」這個動作還是「被做」這個動作。例如 "The contract is ready to sign."（合約準備好可以簽了，contract 是被簽的對象，也可寫成 to be signed，但慣用語 ready to V 允許省略 be；正式書面則常見 to be signed）。

## 二、不定詞的完成式：to have p.p.

當不定詞所表達的動作，發生在主要動詞（句子的真正時態）**之前**時，使用 to have p.p.，藉此在不使用子句的情況下表達時間的先後關係。

*   The supplier claims **to have shipped** the goods on Monday, although the warehouse has no record of it.（宣稱「已經」出貨，早於 claims 的時間點）
*   The auditor appears **to have overlooked** a discrepancy in the year-end statement.

常與這類「敘述型」動詞連用：claim, seem, appear, report, be said, be believed, be thought, be rumored 等，因為這些動詞本身表達「現在對過去某件事的判斷／陳述」。

## 三、不定詞的完成被動式：to have been p.p.

當「先於主要動詞發生」與「被動承受」兩種意義同時成立時，兩者合併使用。

*   The shipment is believed **to have been delayed** by the customs inspection last week.（被認為「已經被」延誤了）
*   The vendor is reported **to have been notified** of the price change two months ago.

## 四、易混淆情境：完成式 vs. 簡單式的時間差

考生最容易出錯的地方，是句子裡沒有明顯的時間副詞，但邏輯上動作必須早於主要動詞。判斷方法：找出主要動詞的時態，再問「不定詞的動作，是跟主要動詞同時，還是已經發生完畢？」

*   The manager is glad **to have resolved** the dispute before the client's visit.（glad 是現在，resolved 早於現在 → 完成式）
*   比較：The manager is glad **to resolve** the dispute.（若動作與 glad 同時或稍後，用簡單式）

## 五、易混淆情境：主動 vs. 被動的邏輯主詞判斷

某些句子表面上看起來動詞後面直接接不定詞，但邏輯主詞其實是承受者，必須用被動式，這種題型常出現在正式信函與合約用語中。

*   All new equipment is scheduled **to be inspected** before installation.（equipment 不會自己 inspect，而是被檢查）
*   The candidate selected for the position is expected **to be notified** by phone rather than email.

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

### 敘述／推測動詞（常接 to have p.p.）

*   **allegedly** adv. 據稱地 | The manager **allegedly** authorized the payment without approval.
*   **purportedly** adv. 據說地 | The document was **purportedly** signed by the former director.
*   **supposedly** adv. 據說；照理說 | The missing shipment was **supposedly** lost in transit.
*   **rumored** (v. 及物, p.p.) 被謠傳 | The company is **rumored** to have been in merger talks.
*   **documented** (v. 及物, p.p.) 被記錄 | The firm's growth is well **documented** to have started five years ago.

### 表達檢查、核准、通知等（常用被動不定詞）

*   **inspect** v. 及物 檢查 | Every shipment must be scheduled to be **inspected** upon arrival.
*   **sanction** v. 及物 批准；核可 | The expansion plan is expected to be **sanctioned** by the board.
*   **notify** v. 及物 通知 | Clients are expected to be **notified** within 48 hours of any delay.
*   **streamline** v. 及物 使精簡化 | The approval process is set to be **streamlined** by next Monday.
*   **refund** v. 及物 退款 | Deposits are supposed to be **refunded** within two weeks of cancellation.
*   **relocate** v. 及物 搬遷 | The headquarters is scheduled to be **relocated** next spring.
*   **audit** v. 及物 稽核 | All financial records are due to be **audited** annually.
*   **indemnify** v. 及物 賠償 | Affected customers are expected to be **indemnified** for the inconvenience.
*   **withhold** v. 及物 扣留；保留 | Payment may be **withheld** pending final inspection.
*   **overhaul** v. 及物 徹底檢修 | The outdated system needs to be **overhauled** before the merger.

### 表達完成、達成等（常用於 to have p.p.）

*   **accomplish** v. 及物 完成；達成 | The team is proud to **have accomplished** the target ahead of schedule.
*   **outpace** v. 及物 超越；領先 | The department claims to **have outpaced** its quarterly quota.
*   **overlook** v. 及物 忽略 | The reviewer seems to **have overlooked** a key clause in the contract.
*   **underestimate** v. 及物 低估 | The manager admitted to **have underestimated** the project's complexity.
*   **breach** v. 及物 違反 | The supplier is alleged to **have breached** the delivery agreement.
*   **withdraw** v. 及物 撤回 | The bidder is said to **have withdrawn** its offer at the last minute.

### 句型連接詞彙

*   **be said to V** 片語 據說 | The vendor **is said to have** raised its prices last quarter.
*   **be believed to V** 片語 被認為 | The delay **is believed to have been** caused by a software glitch.
*   **be thought to V** 片語 被認為 | The proposal **is thought to have been** rejected by the board.
*   **be poised to V** 片語 蓄勢待發；即將 | The audit **is poised to be** completed by Friday.
*   **be expected to V** 片語 被期待要 | Staff **are expected to be** briefed before the site visit.
*   **be liable to V** 片語 可能會；有責任 | Long-term employees **are liable to be** reimbursed for relocation costs.
*   **be due to V** 片語 應該要；預計 | The contract **is due to be** renewed next month.
*   **be reported to V** 片語 據報導 | The factory **is reported to have** resumed operations.
*   **turn out to V** 片語 結果證明 | The invoice **turned out to have been** duplicated by mistake.

## 📚 補充秒殺公式

1.  **看到句意「已經」但沒有明顯過去式動詞**：優先考慮 to have p.p.，尤其主要動詞是 claim, seem, appear, report, be said/believed/thought 這類「陳述現在對過去的看法」動詞。
2.  **看到不定詞前的名詞是動作的承受者**：一律改成 to be p.p.，常見於 need to be done、is scheduled to be reviewed、is expected to be notified 等句型。
3.  **「先發生」+「被動」同時成立**：合併成 to have been p.p.，這是四種型態中最容易被忽略的一種，通常出現在被動語態句的後半段。
4.  **glad / pleased / proud / sorry + to have p.p.**：情緒形容詞後接完成式不定詞，表示「對已經發生的事感到（情緒）」，若動作與情緒同時發生則用簡單式。
5.  **the first / last / only + 名詞 + to have p.p.**：表達「第一個／唯一一個已經完成某事的人」，常考句尾修飾語。
6.  **need / want / require + to be p.p.**：這三個動詞後方若接不定詞表示「被動需要」，也可用動名詞主動形式（need doing）表達相同被動意義，是常見改寫題。
7.  **is supposed to be + p.p.**：表示「應該要被（做）」，強調外部規範或期待，而非動作執行者的意願。
8.  **選項同時出現 to V / to be V-ing / to have p.p. / to have been p.p. 四種型態時**：先判斷主被動（找邏輯主詞是否為受詞角色），再判斷時間先後（是否早於主要動詞），兩步驟缺一不可。
9.  **claim to V vs. claim to have p.p.**：前者表示「宣稱將要做」，後者表示「宣稱已經做過」，時態意義完全不同，考題常利用此對比出雙格題。
10. **be said to have been p.p. 是四種型態中資訊密度最高的句型**：同時包含被動語態指標詞（be said）＋完成不定詞（to have）＋被動分詞（been p.p.），拆解時建議由後往前確認每個成分。
11. **正式書面中，ready / eager / anxious + to V 通常維持主動形式**，即使邏輯上帶有被動意涵（如 ready to sign 指合約準備好被簽），這是慣用語法的例外，不可機械化套用被動公式。
12. **獨立於句意判斷之外，四個選項若字尾一致（都是 signed／delayed／approved 等 p.p.）而差在 to V 結構本身**，通常是在考「時態先後＋主被動」的雙重理解，而非單字意義。

## 💪 練習題（15 題）

**1.** The consultant claims ______ the entire risk assessment before the board meeting last Tuesday.
(A) to have completed (B) to complete (C) to be completed (D) completing

**2.** All shipping containers are scheduled ______ before they leave the port.
(A) to inspect (B) inspecting (C) to be inspected (D) having inspected

**3.** The negotiation is believed ______ by a disagreement over the delivery timeline.
(A) to have delayed (B) to delay (C) delaying (D) to have been delayed

**4.** Ms. Park was pleased ______ the client's approval before the deadline she had set for herself.
(A) to receive (B) to be received (C) to have received (D) receiving

**5.** According to the internal memo, the merger agreement, which industry analysts (第一空) ______ finalized weeks ago, is now expected (第二空) ______ announced publicly next month.
(A) is said to have been / to be (B) is said to be / to have been (C) said to have been / to have been (D) said to be / to be

**6.** New hires are required ______ of all confidentiality policies before accessing internal systems.
(A) to inform (B) informing (C) to have informed (D) to be informed

**7.** The technician is the only staff member ______ the malfunction before it caused a full outage.
(A) to have identified (B) to identify (C) to be identified (D) identifying

**8.** The invoice, which was originally sent to the wrong department, appears ______ without anyone noticing.
(A) to correct (B) to have been corrected (C) to be corrected (D) correcting

**9.** All expense claims must ______ by a supervisor before being submitted to finance.
(A) approve (B) have approved (C) be approved (D) approving

**10.** The supplier is rumored ______ its production capacity significantly over the past year, though no official statement has confirmed this.
(A) to expand (B) to be expanded (C) expanding (D) to have expanded

**11.** The warehouse manager wants the damaged inventory ______ before the year-end audit begins.
(A) to be removed (B) to remove (C) to have removed (D) removing

**12.** Analysts note that the company's revenue, (第一空) ______ underestimated in the earlier forecast, is now (第二空) ______ well ahead of last year's figures.
(A) said to be / believed to have been (B) said to have been / believed to be (C) saying to have been / believing to be (D) to say / to believe

**13.** The contractor is reported ______ several safety regulations during the renovation, prompting an official investigation.
(A) to violate (B) to be violated (C) violating (D) to have violated

**14.** The proposal is due ______ by the executive committee no later than Friday.
(A) to review (B) to be reviewed (C) reviewing (D) to have reviewed

**15.** The director seems ______ the risks of the overseas expansion before presenting the plan to shareholders.
(A) to underestimate (B) to be underestimated (C) to have underestimated (D) underestimating
