# 02_綜合練習二

## 📝 答題策略 🌟
1.  **先看空格再讀全文**：段落填空題不需要通篇精讀，先掃過空格前後一到兩句即可作答大部分題目。
2.  **詞彙題看「主題詞一致性」**：文章談的是人事異動還是產品發表，答案詞彙要符合該主題語境，不能只看字面意思相近。
3.  **句子插入題找「線索詞」**：注意人稱代名詞（he/she/it/they）、指示詞（this/that/these）、時間副詞是否與前後句呼應。
4.  **語態題**：判斷空格的動詞是主動執行或被動承受，留意主詞是「人」還是「物」。
5.  **副詞轉折題**：however（轉折）、therefore（因果）、in addition（遞進）、for example（舉例）需依上下文邏輯選擇。

## 短文一：人事異動公告

To: All Department Heads
From: Human Resources

We are pleased to announce that Ms. Laura Kim ______(1) the position of Regional Sales Manager, effective August 1st. Ms. Kim has been with the company for six years and has consistently exceeded her sales targets. ______(2). Please join us in congratulating her on this well-deserved promotion. Her previous responsibilities ______(3) be temporarily distributed among the current sales team until a replacement is hired. If you have any questions regarding this transition, please contact HR ______(4) the end of this week.

### 題目 1
(A) accepts
(B) accepted
(C) has accepted
(D) will accept

### 題目 2
(A) She previously worked at a competing firm before joining us.
(B) The sales department will move to the third floor next month.
(C) Our annual company picnic has been rescheduled.
(D) The new employee handbook is now available online.

### 題目 3
(A) will
(B) are
(C) have
(D) had

### 題目 4
(A) by
(B) since
(C) for
(D) at
