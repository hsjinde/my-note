# 08_不定詞動名詞意義差異動詞

## 核心概念
有一群動詞後面**既可以接不定詞 (to V)，也可以接動名詞 (V-ing)**，但兩者意義完全不同，甚至時間先後關係相反。這類題目是多益 Part 5 的經典陷阱：選項文法都對，唯一的判斷依據是「語意邏輯」。本章聚焦 `01_不定詞.md` 只簡略帶過的 remember/forget/stop/try 之外，更完整地整理 regret、mean、go on、quit、deserve、can't help、used to 家族等辨析動詞。

## 1. 「未來動作」vs「過去/已發生動作」型
這類動詞的規則是：**to V 指向動作發生在主要動詞之後（尚未做）；V-ing 指向動作發生在主要動詞之前或同時（已經做過）**。

| 動詞 | to V（未做，指向未來） | V-ing（已做，指向過去） |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **remember** | remember *to lock* the door（記得去鎖） | remember *locking* the door（記得曾鎖過） |
| **forget** | forget *to attach* the file（忘記要附檔） | forget *attaching* the file（不記得曾附過檔） |
| **regret** | regret *to inform* you（很遺憾要通知，正式用語） | regret *signing* the contract（後悔曾簽了約） |

*   The clerk **forgot to attach** the invoice before sending the email.
*   We **regret to inform** you that your application has been declined.
*   She later **regretted signing** the lease without reading the fine print.

## 2. 「主動去做」vs「動作中斷」型：stop
`stop to V` 中的 to V 是**副詞片語（表目的）**，意思是「停下正在做的事，去做另一件事」；`stop V-ing` 的 V-ing 才是真正被停止的動作，意思是「停止做...」。
*   The auditor **stopped to check** the receipts. （停下手邊工作，去核對收據）
*   The factory **stopped producing** the discontinued model last month. （停止生產）

## 3. 「嘗試但方法/結果不同」型：try
`try to V` 強調「努力嘗試但未必成功」；`try V-ing` 強調「試著採取某種做法，看看有沒有效果」。
*   The technician **tried to fix** the server for hours but eventually gave up.
*   If the printer jams again, **try restarting** the machine before calling IT support.

## 4. 「表示打算」vs「意味著」型：mean
`mean to V` 是「打算、故意要做某事」；`mean V-ing` 則是「（某件事）意味著、會導致某個結果」，主詞通常不是人。
*   I didn't **mean to interrupt** your presentation.
*   Missing the shipping deadline would **mean losing** the client's trust.

## 5. 「繼續」相關動詞：go on
`go on to V` 表示「（做完一件事後）接著去做另一件不同的事」；`go on V-ing` 表示「繼續做原本正在做的同一件事」。
*   After finishing the sales report, Mr. Huang **went on to present** the marketing forecast.
*   Despite the criticism, the team **went on revising** the proposal until midnight.

## 6. 「停止做某事」的另一組：quit
`quit V-ing` 表示徹底停止某項習慣性動作，日常口語與商用英文皆常見，**quit 後面一律接 V-ing，不接 to V**，常與 stop V-ing 互換，但語氣更強烈、更口語。
*   The department **quit using** the outdated filing system after the audit.

## 7. 「值得/該得到」：deserve
`deserve to V` 與 `deserve V-ing`（主動形式表被動）皆可，後者較正式，常見於商業評鑑語境：deserve doing = deserve to be done。
*   The proposal **deserves to be reviewed** carefully.
*   This complaint **deserves looking into** immediately.（值得被調查）

## 8. 「忍不住」：can't help
`can't help V-ing`（無法克制不去做）為固定句型，help 在此當「避免」解，後方**只能接 V-ing**，不可接 to V。
*   The staff **couldn't help laughing** when the projector malfunctioned during the pitch.

## 9. used to 家族三兄弟（最容易混淆）
*   **used to + V**：過去的習慣或狀態（現在已不是）。→ The company **used to outsource** its accounting.
*   **be used to + V-ing/N**：習慣於（現在的狀態，to 是介系詞）。→ The staff **are used to working** overtime during peak season.
*   **get used to + V-ing/N**：逐漸習慣於（強調變化過程）。→ New hires need time to **get used to using** the internal system.

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **看到 remember / forget / regret + 空格**：先判斷語意是「還沒做、要去做」（未來）還是「已經做過、記得曾經」（過去）── 未來選 to V，過去選 V-ing。
2.  **看到 stop + 空格**：句意若是「停下來去做另一件事」選 to V；句意若是「停止進行中的動作」選 V-ing，且 stop V-ing 的頻率遠高於 stop to V。
3.  **quit 後面一律接 V-ing**，沒有 quit to V 這種用法，看到 quit 直接秒選 V-ing。
4.  **can't help 後面一律接 V-ing**，這是固定片語，不需判斷語意。
5.  **used to V（過去習慣）vs be used to V-ing（現在習慣）**：關鍵在 be 動詞有沒有出現──有 be 動詞（is/are/was/were）就是「習慣於」，接 V-ing；沒有 be 動詞、直接 used to 接原形動詞，才是「過去曾經」。
6.  **get used to** 強調「正在適應、逐漸習慣」的過程，常與 gradually, over time 等副詞連用，後方一樣接 V-ing 或名詞。
7.  **try to V vs try V-ing**：句子若在描述「排除故障、換個方法試試看」的情境，優先選 V-ing；描述「努力但過程辛苦、未必成功」則選 to V。
8.  **go on to V vs go on V-ing**：句中若出現 after + V-ing 或 then 等表示「換下一件事」的線索，選 to V；若強調「持續同一動作」則選 V-ing。
9.  **deserve V-ing 具有被動語意**：This issue deserves addressing = This issue deserves to be addressed，看到 deserve + V-ing，不要誤以為文法錯誤。
10. **mean 的主詞是「事件/情況」而非「人」時，後方多接 V-ing**（表示結果／意味著）；主詞是「人」且描述意圖時，後方接 to V。
11. **陷阱**：regret to inform / regret to say 是商業書信固定用語（表示「很遺憾地通知」），千萬不要因為看到 regret 就直覺選 V-ing。
12. **can't afford to V 與 can't help V-ing 是兩組不同片語**，afford 後面固定接 to V，help 後面固定接 V-ing，考題常把兩者放在同一組選項中混淆考生。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

### 動詞（意義隨 to V / V-ing 改變）
*   **remember** 及物動詞 記得 | Please **remember to** *submit* the timesheet before Friday.
*   **forget** 及物動詞 忘記 | He will never **forget** *closing* the biggest deal of his career.
*   **regret** 及物動詞 後悔；遺憾 | We **regret to** *announce* that the branch will be closed permanently.
*   **stop** 及物/不及物動詞 停止 | The supplier **stopped** *shipping* products without prior notice.
*   **try** 及物動詞 嘗試 | Employees should **try** *contacting* the help desk before escalating the issue.
*   **mean** 及物動詞 意味著；打算 | Cutting the budget would **mean** *reducing* the marketing headcount.
*   **quit** 及物動詞 戒除；停止 | The board **quit** *funding* the unprofitable division.
*   **deserve** 及物動詞 應得；值得 | The proposal **deserves** *further discussion* at the next meeting.
*   **attempt** 及物動詞 嘗試 | The technician **attempted to** *reboot* the system twice before calling support.
*   **cease** 及物/不及物動詞 停止（正式） | The manufacturer will **cease** *producing* the older model by year end.
*   **recall** 及物動詞 回想起 | She could not **recall** *meeting* the client at last year's expo.
*   **imagine** 及物動詞 想像 | It is hard to **imagine** *running* the logistics center without the new software.
*   **appreciate** 及物動詞 感激；欣賞 | We would **appreciate** *receiving* your feedback by Monday.
*   **delay** 及物動詞 延遲 | The vendor **delayed** *delivering* the parts due to a customs inspection.
*   **resist** 及物動詞 抵抗；忍住不 | The negotiator could barely **resist** *accepting* the counteroffer immediately.
*   **tolerate** 及物動詞 容忍 | The firm will not **tolerate** *falsifying* expense reports.
*   **discontinue** 及物動詞 中止；停產 | The retailer **discontinued** *stocking* the seasonal item after low sales.
*   **undertake** 及物動詞 承擔；著手進行 | The consultancy **undertook to** *restructure* the entire supply chain.
*   **request** 及物動詞 請求 | Clients frequently **request** *rescheduling* their appointments at short notice.
*   **dread** 及物動詞 害怕；恐懼 | Many staff **dread** *presenting* figures to senior executives.
*   **risk** 及物動詞 冒著...的風險 | The company would **risk** *losing* its reputation by ignoring the complaint.
*   **practice** 及物動詞 練習 | New agents **practice** *handling* difficult customer calls during training.

### 片語
*   **can't help + V-ing** 片語 忍不住 | The interviewer **couldn't help smiling** at the candidate's confident answer.
*   **used to + V** 片語 過去習慣（現在已非如此） | The department **used to submit** paper forms before the system was digitized.
*   **get used to + V-ing** 片語 逐漸習慣於 | It took the new hires weeks to **get used to using** the inventory system.

## 💪 練習題（15 題）

**1.** Realizing the deadline had passed, the assistant regretted ______ the shipment earlier that morning.
(A) not confirming (B) to not confirm (C) not confirm (D) not to confirming

**2.** The maintenance crew stopped ______ the elevator so that a resident could get on.
(A) holding (B) to hold (C) hold (D) held

**3.** After reviewing the complaint, the manager admitted ______ the customer's email for over a week.
(A) to ignore (B) ignore (C) having ignored (D) ignores

**4.** If the printer keeps jamming, try ______ the paper tray before contacting IT.
(A) realign (B) to realigning (C) realigned (D) realigning

**5.** The vendor never meant ______ the client, but the shipping label was printed with the wrong address.
(A) to mislead (B) misleading (C) mislead (D) having misled

**6.** The accounting team quit ______ spreadsheets manually once the new automated system went live.
(A) update (B) updating (C) to update (D) updated

**7.** This recurring billing error deserves ______ before the next invoicing cycle begins.
(A) to address (B) address (C) addressing (D) addressed

**8.** Whenever the workload spikes, the receptionist can't help ______ overwhelmed by the number of calls.
(A) to feel (B) feel (C) felt (D) feeling

**9.** The regional office ______ file quarterly reports on paper before headquarters switched to a cloud platform.
(A) used to (B) is used to (C) gets used to (D) was used to

**10.** After months of remote work, most employees have finally gotten used to ______ video conferencing tools for daily meetings.
(A) use (B) using (C) used (D) to using

**11.** Having completed the budget review, the finance director went on ______ the revised figures to the board. Later, she went on ______ the same presentation to the regional managers as well.
第一空：(A) presenting (B) present (C) to present (D) presented
第二空：(A) present (B) presented (C) presenting (D) to present

**12.** The supplier attempted ______ the shipment twice, but customs delays prevented both deliveries.
(A) reroute (B) rerouting (C) rerouted (D) to reroute

**13.** Given the ongoing supply shortage, the factory will likely cease ______ this particular component next quarter.
(A) producing (B) produce (C) to producing (D) produced

**14.** The auditor recalled ______ a similar discrepancy in last year's accounts before flagging it again this year.
(A) to notice (B) noticing (C) notice (D) having notice

**15.** The board couldn't afford to overlook the risk, so it insisted on ______ the merger terms before signing.
(A) reexamine (B) to reexamine (C) reexamining (D) reexamined
