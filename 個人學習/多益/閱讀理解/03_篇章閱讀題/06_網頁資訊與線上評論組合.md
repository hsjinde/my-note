# 06_網頁資訊與線上評論組合

## 📝 答題策略
1. **網頁介紹文先抓賣點**：公司網頁（About Us / 服務介紹）通常在第一段就點出核心賣點與目標客群，掌握這點可快速鎖定主旨題答案。
2. **分清事實與感受**：線上評論文章中，細節題常考評論者具體提到的「事實」（時間、地點、價格、服務項目），要與評論者的個人感受區分開來。
3. **推論題看整體語氣**：評論的推論題常考「評論者整體態度」或「是否會再度光顧／推薦」，需綜合全文語氣與具體描述判斷，而非只看單一句子。
4. **字義題代入語境**：同一單字在不同句子中常有不同意思，須以該字所在句子的上下文為準，而非直接套用字典最常見的釋義。
5. **善用專有名詞定位**：方案名稱、地點、價格、星級評分都是細節題常考的關鍵字，直接回文章對應段落核對即可。

## 文章：公司網頁

About Us — Riverside Coworking Hub

Located just two blocks from Central Station, Riverside Coworking Hub offers a spacious, light-filled workspace designed for freelancers, remote employees, and small teams who want more than just a desk. Our facility includes six private meeting rooms, a quiet zone for focused work, high-speed fiber internet, and a fully equipped coffee lounge where members can recharge between tasks.

We offer three flexible membership options to fit different working styles. The Day Pass ($15) grants access to the open workspace and coffee lounge for a single day. The Monthly Plan ($120) includes unlimited access to the open workspace, two free hours of meeting room use per week, and priority registration for our monthly networking events. The Corporate Plan, designed for teams of five or more, includes a dedicated set of desks, unlimited meeting room bookings, and a personal account manager who handles billing and scheduling on the company's behalf.

All members receive a keycard that grants building access from 7:00 A.M. to 10:00 P.M. on weekdays; Corporate Plan members additionally receive 24-hour access, including weekends, at no extra charge. We also host a free networking event on the first Friday of every month, where members can meet other professionals in a relaxed, informal setting.

Whether you need a single day of focused work or a permanent home base for your growing team, Riverside Coworking Hub is built to help your business thrive.

## 文章：顧客線上評論

★★★★☆ (4/5) — Posted by Daniel R.

I visited Blue Basil Bistro last weekend to celebrate a friend's promotion, and overall it was a pleasant experience. The restaurant's interior has a warm, inviting ambiance, with dim lighting and soft music that made it easy to hold a conversation. We started with the roasted eggplant appetizer, which was flavorful and generously portioned. My main course, the herb-crusted salmon, was cooked perfectly, though my friend felt her pasta dish was slightly bland compared to the description on the menu.

Service was attentive throughout the meal; our server checked in regularly without being intrusive, and any request for extra bread or water was handled promptly. The only drawback was the wait time — we had a reservation for 7:00 P.M. but were not seated until nearly 7:25, with no explanation offered for the delay.

Prices are on the higher side for the portion sizes, so I would not recommend this place for a casual weekday lunch, but it is well suited for a special celebration. I would return for the salmon alone, though I would call ahead to confirm my table will be ready on time.
