# 04_廣告與訂購單組合

## 📝 答題策略 🌟
1.  **廣告類文章先找「賣點」與「行動呼籲」**：廣告通常在開頭點出產品/服務的核心賣點，並在結尾要求讀者採取行動（訂購、造訪網站、撥打電話），主旨題答案多半圍繞這兩處。
2.  **表格與清單資訊要逐欄核對**：訂購單、確認單常以清單或表格呈現品項、數量、金額，作答前先確認每一欄位代表的意義，避免把「單價」看成「總價」。
3.  **促銷條件看清楚適用範圍**：廣告中的折扣、贈品常附帶條件（限量、限時、需輸入代碼），題目常考「什麼情況下才能享有優惠」。
4.  **確認單類文章留意「例外狀況」**：訂購確認信常說明「若商品缺貨/延遲會如何處理」，這類段落是推論題與細節題的常見出處。
5.  **字義題代入語境**：不要只選字面上最常見的意思，須看該字在句子中實際描述的對象與動作。
6.  **兩篇文章各自獨立作答**：本篇的兩篇短文分屬不同情境（廣告、訂購確認單），彼此沒有關聯，不需要交叉比對。

## 文章：產品廣告

**Introducing the DeskEase Monthly Supply Box**

Tired of running out of printer paper right before a big presentation, or realizing your stapler is empty during a client meeting? DeskEase delivers a curated box of essential office supplies to your doorstep every month, so your team never has to make an emergency trip to the store again.

Each box is tailored to your office size and includes items such as printer paper, pens, sticky notes, and cleaning wipes. Subscribers can adjust the contents of their box at any time through our online dashboard, and unused items can be rolled over to the following month at no extra charge.

For the month of September only, new subscribers who sign up using the code FRESH25 will receive a free ergonomic wrist rest with their first box, while supplies last. This offer cannot be combined with any other promotion and is limited to one per household.

DeskEase subscriptions can be paused or canceled at any time with no penalty; however, requests must be submitted at least five business days before the next scheduled shipment to take effect for that cycle. To sign up or learn more, visit www.deskease.com or call our customer line at 555-0148.
