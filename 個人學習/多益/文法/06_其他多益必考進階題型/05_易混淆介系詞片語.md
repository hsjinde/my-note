# 05_易混淆介系詞片語

## 核心概念
多益 Part 5、Part 6 常考「固定搭配的介系詞片語」(Preposition Collocations)。這類題目**沒有文法邏輯可推導**，必須直接背誦「動詞/形容詞 + 特定介系詞」的固定用法。多益陷阱常設計「意思相近但介系詞不同」的選項互相混淆。

## 1. 「負責 / 相關」意義的易混淆片語 🌟

| 片語 | 意思 | 例句 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **be responsible for** | 為...負責 | She **is responsible for** the marketing budget. |
| **in charge of** | 負責掌管 | He **is in charge of** the entire project. |
| **be in favor of** | 贊成 | The board **is in favor of** the merger. |
| **be subject to** | 須遵守 / 可能會有 | Prices **are subject to** change without notice. |
| **be eligible for** | 有資格獲得 | Employees **are eligible for** the bonus after one year. |

## 2. 「依照 / 符合」意義的易混淆片語 🌟

| 片語 | 意思 | 例句 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **in accordance with** | 依照、與...一致 | The refund was issued **in accordance with** company policy. |
| **in compliance with** | 遵守 | The factory operates **in compliance with** safety regulations. |
| **comply with** (無 in) | 遵守 (動詞片語，無須 in) | All staff must **comply with** the new guidelines. |
| **according to** | 根據 (資料來源) | **According to** the report, sales rose 10%. |

## 3. 「取代 / 除了」意義的易混淆片語

| 片語 | 意思 | 例句 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **instead of** | 代替、而不是 | We chose email **instead of** a phone call. |
| **in place of** | 取代 | The intern will attend the meeting **in place of** the manager. |
| **in addition to** | 除了...之外 (還有) | **In addition to** her salary, she receives a housing allowance. |
| **regardless of** | 不論、不管 | The policy applies to everyone, **regardless of** position. |

## 4. 常見「動詞 + 介系詞」固定搭配 🌟

| 動詞片語 | 意思 | 例句 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **consist of** | 由...組成 | The committee **consists of** five members. |
| **result in** | 導致 | The delay **resulted in** significant losses. |
| **result from** | 起因於 | The delay **resulted from** a shipping error. |
| **refrain from** | 克制不做 | Please **refrain from** using cell phones during the meeting. |
| **benefit from** | 從...中受益 | Small businesses **benefit from** the new tax policy. |
| **account for** | 說明 / 佔比例 | Exports **account for** 30% of total revenue. |

> [!note]
> `result in` 與 `result from` 方向相反：**原因 result in 結果**；**結果 result from 原因**。
> - The **storm** *resulted in* flight delays. (暴風雨 ➔ 導致 ➔ 誤點)
> - The **delay** *resulted from* the storm. (誤點 ➔ 起因於 ➔ 暴風雨)

## 📝 多益秒殺解題技巧
1.  看到 `be subject to`, `be eligible for`, `in accordance with` 等固定搭配 ➔ 直接背誦整組片語，不要用一般文法邏輯推導介系詞。
2.  `result in` (導致) vs `result from` (起因於) ➔ 看清楚**主詞是原因還是結果**再選介系詞方向。
3.  `comply with`（動詞+with，不加 in）vs `in compliance with`（名詞片語，需要 in）➔ 注意詞性不同，介系詞用法也不同。
4.  遇到「意思相近、介系詞不同」的選項（如 instead of / in place of / in addition to）➔ 先判斷句意是「取代」還是「附加」，再選對應片語。

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **be dependent on / rely on / depend on**：都表示「依賴」，皆固定接 on，不要誤用 depend of/for。
2.  **be aware of / be capable of**：形容詞後接 of，常與 be subject to (接 to) 混淆，注意介系詞不同。
3.  **at the request of / at the discretion of**：固定用 at 開頭，表示「應...要求 / 由...自行決定」。
4.  **陷阱：exempt from vs exempt to**：exempt (豁免) 固定與 from 搭配，選項常故意放錯誤的 to 或 of 混淆考生。
5.  **prior to vs before**：prior to 是較正式的書面用語，意思等同 before，但 prior to 後面**不可直接接子句**，只能接名詞/動名詞。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

### 形容詞 + 介系詞
*   **be dependent on** (adj. 依賴於) | Success **is dependent on** teamwork.
*   **be aware of** (adj. 知悉、意識到) | Please **be aware of** the new safety rules.
*   **be capable of** (adj. 有能力做到) | She **is capable of** managing the entire department.
*   **be exempt from** (adj. 被豁免) | Non-profit organizations **are exempt from** this tax.
*   **be entitled to** (adj. 有權獲得) | Full-time staff **are entitled to** paid leave.

### 動詞 + 介系詞
*   **comply with** (v. 遵守) | All vendors must **comply with** the contract terms.
*   **consist of** (v. 由...組成) | The package **consists of** three components.
*   **refrain from** (v. 克制不做) | Visitors should **refrain from** taking photos.
*   **result from / result in** (v. 起因於 / 導致) | The error **resulted from** a system glitch.

### 片語
*   **in accordance with** (phr. 依照) | Payment will be made **in accordance with** the agreement.
*   **in compliance with** (phr. 遵守) | The building was constructed **in compliance with** fire codes.
*   **at the discretion of** (phr. 由...自行決定) | Bonuses are given **at the discretion of** management.
*   **regardless of** (phr. 不論) | All applicants are considered **regardless of** experience level.
*   **on behalf of** (phr. 代表) | She signed the document **on behalf of** the director.

**1.** All employees are required to __________ with the company's code of conduct.
(A) comply
(B) compliance
(C) complying
(D) compliant

**2.** The intern attended the conference __________ the marketing director, who was on leave.
(A) instead
(B) in place of
(C) in addition to
(D) regardless of

**3.** Part-time workers are not __________ the health insurance benefits offered to full-time staff.
(A) eligible to
(B) eligible for
(C) eligible with
(D) eligible of

**4.** The delay in production __________ from a shortage of raw materials.
(A) resulted
(B) resulted in
(C) caused
(D) led

**5.** 請閱讀以下短文並選出最適合填入空格的答案。

All shipments must be packaged ______(1)______ international safety standards. Employees who fail to follow these guidelines may be held responsible ______(2)______ any resulting damage.

(1)
(A) in accordance with
(B) instead of
(C) regardless of
(D) in addition to

(2)
(A) of
(B) for
(C) with
(D) to
