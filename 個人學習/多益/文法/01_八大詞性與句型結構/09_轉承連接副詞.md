# 09_轉承連接副詞

## 核心概念
轉承連接副詞（conjunctive adverbs / transitional adverbs）是用來銜接**兩個獨立句子**之間邏輯關係的副詞，例如 however、therefore、moreover、nevertheless。它們在意義上很像連接詞，但**文法身分是副詞，不是連接詞**——這正是 Part 5、Part 6 的核心陷阱：轉承副詞**不能單獨把兩個完整句子接在一起**，只能靠標點符號（分號、句號）搭配使用，否則會造成 comma splice（逗號連綴）的文法錯誤。

## 1. 三種標準句型 🌟

| 句型 | 公式 | 範例 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **分號句型** | S + V; **轉承副詞**, S + V. | The flight was delayed; *however*, passengers were offered meal vouchers. |
| **句號句型** | S + V. **轉承副詞** (字首大寫), S + V. | The flight was delayed. *However*, passengers were offered meal vouchers. |
| **插入語句型** | S, **轉承副詞**, + V.（副詞插入單一子句中） | The flight, *however*, departed only twenty minutes late. |

*   **常見錯誤（陷阱）**：`The flight was delayed, however passengers were calm.` ← 只用逗號連接兩個完整子句是錯的，必須改成分號或句號。
*   插入語句型中，主詞和動詞仍屬於**同一個子句**，轉承副詞只是被逗號夾在中間，並沒有連接兩個子句。

## 2. 語意分類 🌟
Part 6 短文填空最常考「依上下文邏輯選出正確的轉承副詞」，因此必須先分清楚每個副詞屬於哪一種邏輯關係：

| 邏輯關係 | 常見轉承副詞 |
| :--- | :--- |
| 轉折／對比 | however, nevertheless, nonetheless, in contrast, on the other hand, conversely |
| 遞進／補充 | moreover, furthermore, in addition, additionally, besides |
| 因果／結果 | therefore, thus, hence, consequently, as such |
| 舉例／說明 | for instance, for example, namely, in other words, that is to say |
| 相似／類比 | similarly, likewise |
| 時間／同時 | meanwhile, in the meantime, afterward |
| 強調／事實 | in fact, indeed |
| 替代／取代 | instead, alternatively |

## 3. 轉承副詞 vs. 從屬連接詞（易混淆）
`09_轉承連接副詞` 與 `04_連接詞` 的差別在於：從屬連接詞（although, because, unless...）後面直接接子句、不需要標點分隔；轉承副詞則需要分號或句號才能連接兩個子句。

| 判斷方式 | 連接詞 (although, because) | 轉承副詞 (however, therefore) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 能否直接連接兩子句 | 可以，`S+V, 連接詞 + S+V.` | 不行，需搭配分號或句號 |
| 在句中的位置 | 只能放在子句最前面 | 可放句首、插入句中，甚至句尾 |
| 詞性 | 連接詞 | 副詞 |

*   秒殺技巧：只要看到空格前後各是一個「完整子句」，且中間只有逗號，選項中若有連接詞（although/because/unless）與副詞（however/instead），**優先看標點**——逗號前只能接連接詞，不能接副詞。

## 📚 補充秒殺公式
1.  **分號 + 轉承副詞 + 逗號**：`S+V; therefore, S+V.` 是最常考的固定公式，分號前後都必須是完整子句。
2.  **句號 + 轉承副詞（大寫）+ 逗號**：句號後轉承副詞開頭要大寫，後面照樣接逗號。
3.  **Comma Splice 陷阱**：轉承副詞前面只有逗號、沒有分號或句號時，該選項通常是錯的（除非副詞是插入語，被夾在同一子句的主詞和動詞之間）。
4.  **插入語用法**：`however`、`therefore` 等可以插入單一子句中間（`S, however, V...`），此時不涉及連接兩個子句，純粹當語氣詞。
5.  **moreover / furthermore / in addition 互換**：三者都表示「此外、而且」，語意上可互換，差別只在語域（furthermore 較正式）。
6.  **likewise / similarly 語意一致原則**：使用這兩個字時，前後兩句的內容必須是「同一類、同方向」的敘述，若前後句是對比關係就不能選這兩個字。
7.  **instead 的替代關係**：instead 表示「以此取代彼」，常與前一句的否定敘述（not, fail to, could not）呼應。
8.  **namely / that is to say 具體化原則**：這兩者用來對前面提到的名詞或概念做**進一步具體說明**，後面通常會列出對應的細節或例子。
9.  **in fact / indeed 加強語氣，非轉折**：這兩個字是「強調、加強前述內容」的功能，常接在 not merely/not only 之類的鋪陳句之後，不是拿來表達對比。
10. **meanwhile 表同時發生**：連接兩個平行發生的動作或事件，常用於描述兩個部門、兩個地點同時進行的工作。
11. **Part 6 選詞先看邏輯關係再看語域**：先判斷前後句是「轉折/因果/遞進/舉例/相似」哪一種關係，縮小選項範圍後，才比較語域正式度做最終選擇。
12. **as such 的因果用法**：as such 意為「因此、照此情況」，用於總結前文已成立的事實所導致的結果，語感比 therefore 更正式、更書面。

## 🔤 相關單字和片語

### 轉折／對比
*   **however** 副詞 然而 | The proposal was well received; *however*, the budget committee rejected it.
*   **nevertheless** 副詞 儘管如此 | The report contained several errors; *nevertheless*, management approved it.
*   **nonetheless** 副詞 儘管如此 | Profits declined this quarter; *nonetheless*, the board approved a dividend payout.
*   **in contrast** 副詞片語 相對地 | Overseas sales rose sharply; *in contrast*, domestic sales remained flat.
*   **on the other hand** 副詞片語 另一方面 | Hiring more staff would speed up production; *on the other hand*, it would raise labor costs.
*   **conversely** 副詞 相反地 | The east warehouse is fully stocked; *conversely*, the west warehouse is running low on supplies.

### 遞進／補充
*   **moreover** 副詞 此外、而且 | The candidate has strong references; *moreover*, she has five years of relevant experience.
*   **furthermore** 副詞 此外 | The new system reduces errors; *furthermore*, it cuts processing time in half.
*   **in addition** 副詞片語 此外 | The firm expanded its product line; *in addition*, it opened two new retail outlets.
*   **additionally** 副詞 此外 | The contractor finished ahead of schedule; *additionally*, the project came in under budget.
*   **besides** 副詞 此外 | The vendor offers competitive prices; *besides*, its delivery record is excellent.

### 因果／結果
*   **therefore** 副詞 因此 | The client missed the payment deadline; *therefore*, the account was suspended.
*   **thus** 副詞 因此 | Demand exceeded supply; *thus*, the company raised its prices.
*   **hence** 副詞 因此 | The merger was finalized last month; *hence*, the two teams now report to one director.
*   **consequently** 副詞 因此、結果 | The server crashed twice this week; *consequently*, the IT department is upgrading the hardware.
*   **as such** 副詞片語 因此、照此情況 | The position requires travel; *as such*, applicants must hold a valid passport.

### 舉例／說明
*   **for instance** 副詞片語 例如 | Several policies were revised; *for instance*, employees may now work remotely twice a week.
*   **for example** 副詞片語 舉例來說 | The firm has cut several expenses; *for example*, it switched to a cheaper courier service.
*   **namely** 副詞 也就是說 | Two departments missed their targets, *namely* marketing and logistics.
*   **in other words** 副詞片語 換句話說 | The client wants a full refund; *in other words*, the contract will be terminated.
*   **that is to say** 副詞片語 也就是說 | The plant will run at half capacity; *that is to say*, only one shift will operate.

### 相似／類比
*   **similarly** 副詞 同樣地 | The Taipei office adopted flexible hours; *similarly*, the Tainan branch introduced the same policy.
*   **likewise** 副詞 同樣地 | The finance team streamlined its approval process; *likewise*, HR simplified its onboarding forms.

### 時間／同時
*   **meanwhile** 副詞 同時、與此同時 | The auditors reviewed the accounts; *meanwhile*, the staff prepared for the site inspection.
*   **in the meantime** 副詞片語 在此同時 | The replacement part will arrive next week; *in the meantime*, technicians will use a temporary fix.
*   **afterward** 副詞 之後 | The board reviewed the proposal; *afterward*, they scheduled a vote for the following meeting.

### 強調／事實
*   **in fact** 副詞片語 事實上 | The project was not merely completed on time; *in fact*, it finished two weeks early.
*   **indeed** 副詞 的確、事實上 | Customer complaints did not increase; *indeed*, satisfaction scores improved.

### 替代／取代
*   **instead** 副詞 反而、取而代之 | The supplier could not meet the order; the buyer, *instead*, contacted a backup vendor.
*   **alternatively** 副詞 或者、換個方式 | Staff may attend the workshop in person; *alternatively*, they can join via video conference.

## 💪 練習題（15 題）

**1.** The vendor's quotation came in significantly higher than the budget allowed; ______, the procurement team requested bids from two additional suppliers.
(A) therefore (B) moreover (C) likewise (D) meanwhile

**2.** Ms. Alvarez had already reviewed the contract twice. ______, she asked the legal department to check it once more before signing.
(A) For example (B) Nevertheless (C) Meanwhile (D) Namely

**3.** The redesigned packaging reduces shipping costs; ______, it is more environmentally friendly than the previous version.
(A) otherwise (B) instead (C) moreover (D) as a result

**4.** The factory failed to meet its production quota this quarter. ______, the plant manager was summoned to headquarters for a review meeting.
(A) Similarly (B) For instance (C) In other words (D) Consequently

**5.** The marketing budget, ______, remained unchanged even though the company expanded into three new overseas markets.
(A) however (B) for example (C) namely (D) meanwhile

**6.** Several departments were asked to cut discretionary spending. The marketing team, ______, canceled its annual trade-show sponsorship.
(A) nevertheless (B) for instance (C) likewise (D) instead

**7.** The Taipei branch increased its quarterly sales by 12 percent. ______, the Kaohsiung branch also reported double-digit growth for the same period.
(A) Instead (B) However (C) Similarly (D) Namely

**8.** The supplier could not deliver the parts on schedule; the operations manager, ______, sourced the components from a local distributor.
(A) likewise (B) namely (C) in fact (D) instead

**9.** The finance team spent the morning reconciling last quarter's accounts. ______, the auditors began reviewing the inventory records in the warehouse.
(A) Meanwhile (B) Namely (C) Therefore (D) Conversely

**10.** The consultant's proposal was not merely acceptable; ______, it exceeded every requirement outlined in the contract.
(A) meanwhile (B) in fact (C) likewise (D) otherwise

**11.** All employees are required to submit expense reports by the fifth of each month. ______, receipts for any purchase over NT$1,000 must be attached to the report.（第一空）Employees who miss the deadline will lose reimbursement eligibility for that month; ______, the finance department strongly encourages staff to set a personal reminder.（第二空）
第一空：(A) However (B) Instead (C) In addition (D) Otherwise
第二空：(A) for example (B) likewise (C) namely (D) therefore

**12.** Online sales grew by 20 percent this quarter; ______, in-store sales declined for the third consecutive quarter.
(A) in contrast (B) similarly (C) additionally (D) subsequently

**13.** The client requested a full refund; the company ______ agreed to replace the defective unit at no additional charge.
(A) although (B) instead (C) because (D) unless

**14.** The negotiations lasted more than six hours ______ the two sides still could not reach an agreement.
(A) however, (B) , however (C) ; however, (D) but however,

**15.** The audit uncovered several minor bookkeeping errors; ______, the overall financial statements were found to be accurate.
(A) moreover (B) for example (C) meanwhile (D) nevertheless
